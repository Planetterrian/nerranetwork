Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions about Nerra Network, our shows, and how we operate. Don't see your question? Get in touch.

Are Nerra Network episodes AI-generated?
Yes. We use AI tools for research, draft generation, and narration. Humans govern the editorial process — deciding topics, verifying accuracy, and enforcing standards. Read the full AI Disclosure.
Who hosts the shows?
Nerra Network shows are produced by founder Patrick Novak in Vancouver, Canada. Individual show pages describe each show's editorial focus. See the About page.
How often are new episodes released?
Most shows publish daily. A few — Fascinating Frontiers, Planetterrian Daily, Omni View, Models & Agents — alternate even/odd days. Each show page lists its exact schedule.
Are there ads?
No. Nerra Network is independent, self-funded, and 100% ad-free. No host-read ads, no programmatic mid-rolls, no sponsored segments.
How can I support the shows?
Subscribe on Apple Podcasts or Spotify, leave a review (reviews significantly help discoverability), share an episode you liked with a friend, and join the weekly newsletter.
Can I download episodes for offline listening?
Yes. Every major podcast app supports offline downloads. You can also download MP3s directly from each episode page on nerranetwork.com.
Do you have transcripts?
Yes. Every episode has a written article counterpart on the blog, with full transcripts, source links, and cited references. Look for the Read transcript link on any summaries page.
What's your editorial stance?
We prioritize fresh content over length, transparent sourcing over anonymous claims, and calm daily delivery over breaking-news panic. Each show has its own editorial focus, but the network-wide goal is signal over noise.
How do I report a factual error?
Email hello@nerranetwork.com with the episode number and the specific claim. We correct errors in the blog article and issue audio corrections in the following episode when the error is significant.
Can I reuse Nerra Network content?
Short quotes with clear attribution to Nerra Network are welcome and encouraged. Full-episode republishing requires written permission. Details in the Terms of Service.
Does Nerra Network track me?
We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation for aggregate metrics, and consent banners for any advertising pixels. You can decline both. Read the privacy policy.
Still have questions? Contact us.
