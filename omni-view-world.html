Omni View Top World News

The world's ten most consequential stories today, ranked — one question argued both ways, one sign of progress, about twelve minutes.

Daily ~11-13 min

Omni View Top World News ranks the ten stories that matter most anywhere in the world today — by how many people they change something for, how much and how soon — drawing on the world's newsrooms and the network's five regional desks, which publish earlier each morning. Then one contested question with the strongest case on each side, and one measurable sign of progress. Read by Mira, the Nerra Network's AI host — the stories are chosen and written by software from named newsrooms, every item names its source, and every claim is attributed.

listeners anywhere who want the ten stories that matter most today, told straight, in one sitting

About Omni View Top World News

The whole world in about twelve minutes: the ten most consequential stories today, ranked, then both sides of one contested question. Mira, the network's AI host, reads it and says so at the top of every episode. It names its sources, attributes every claim and never takes a side.

Hosted by Mira, the Nerra Network's AI host.

Who makes this

Patrick Novak

Patrick Novak created the Nerra Network and runs it from Vancouver. He chooses what the shows cover, writes the instructions they are made from, and listens to what comes out. No episode of anything here carries an ad or a sponsor.
