Rules:
- "guest_pitch": someone (a publicist, agent, or the guest themselves) proposing a person as a podcast guest. "guest_followup": a reply or nudge on a pitch that was already discussed in this thread.
- "sponsor_or_sales": advertising, sponsorship, paid placement, link exchanges, services being sold to Patrick.
- "platform_notice": automated mail from a platform or vendor (Apple, Spotify, YouTube, Google, GitHub, Cloudflare, Supabase, Voximplant, billing systems).
- "personal_or_business": real correspondence from a person that is not a guest pitch (partners, contracts, invoices, friends, family, legal).
- "newsletter_or_noise": bulk mail, digests, marketing blasts, spam.
- guest_name is the proposed GUEST, not the sender, when they differ. publicist_name / publicist_email are the sender when the sender is pitching someone else; null when the guest pitches themselves.
- recommended_show is only set for guest_pitch / guest_followup: "age_of_ai" when the pitch's substance is AI, agents, automation, AI inside an industry, or AI investing; otherwise "nerra_voices". null for every other category.
- pitched_show is the daily show the sender explicitly asked to appear on, mapped to its slug; null if none was named or it is one of the interview shows.
- mentions_money_or_legal is true if the email talks about payment, fees, contracts, lawyers, legal claims, or anything Patrick must decide on financially.
- Be conservative with confidence: below 0.75 when the email is ambiguous, truncated, or you are guessing.
