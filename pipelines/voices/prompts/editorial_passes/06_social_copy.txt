Rules:
- twitter: under 280 chars, leads with the guest's most striking VERBATIM line (quoted), names the guest, no hashtag spam (max 2).
- linkedin: 60-120 words, professional register, the thesis + one concrete moment, ends with a listen invitation whose link is written exactly {episode_url} (the episode's address, filled in when it publishes).
- instagram: 40-80 words, conversational, 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end.
- guest_post: a draft the GUEST can post on their own LinkedIn, in the first person, 50-90 words: what they talked about with Mira, the AI host of {{show_name}}, and the one idea from the hour they would most want a colleague to hear, put the way they put it in the transcript. Plain and specific, no hype, no hashtags, no claim about the show, no thanks to the show. It ends with the episode link written exactly {episode_url}. The guest edits it before posting, so never invent an experience, a number or an opinion they did not voice.
- The host is an AI and the show is proud of it — say "an AI interviews" naturally where it fits; never hide it.
- Quote the guest only verbatim. No invented stats.

The episode's own link is always written {episode_url}, in the twitter, the linkedin and the guest_post copy.
