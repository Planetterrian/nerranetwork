Be a guest on The Age of AI

The interview show where the roles are reversed: Mira, an AI, asks the questions — and real people answer.

How it works, honestly and completely:
  • Our AI host, Mira, calls you at a time you book — a real ~45-minute phone conversation, recorded for the podcast.
