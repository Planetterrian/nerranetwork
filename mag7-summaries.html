Ep 3: Six hundred forty-four million dollars in premiums did not get Tesla Insurance into New York.
Download Read transcript

MAG 7 Daily

Six hundred forty-four million dollars in premiums did not get Tesla Insurance into New York.

What You Need to Know: New York rejected Tesla General Insurance’s plan to cover only Tesla vehicles, after the company’s insurance entities reported $644.2 million in direct written premiums in the first half of 2026. Google is putting Gemini on the phone for ordinary business calls and giving enterprise Gemini Live a lip-synced face. Forty-two state attorneys general are lining up consumer-protection rules for Meta’s chatbots, and a Washington filing lists 277 Microsoft cuts in the Seattle region.

Top News

New York blocks Tesla’s VIN-only auto filing: InsuranceNewsNet

The New York Department of Financial Services rejected a filing from Tesla General Insurance Inc. that would have limited eligibility to Tesla vehicles identified by vehicle identification number. Regulators raised concerns under Section 2324 of New York Insurance Law before reviewing the filing’s actuarial merits, according to an analysis from S&P Global Market Intelligence. Tesla’s insurance entities generated $644.2 million in direct written premiums in the first half of 2026: California $477.8 million, Texas $60.4 million, Nevada $23.3 million, and Maryland $15 million. New York produced no direct written premium in that period even though Tesla Insurance Co. is licensed in the state, S&P said. Source: [insurancenewsnet.com](https://insurancenewsnet.com/innarticle/tesla-insurance-blocked-as-ny-regulators-examine-connected-car-coverage)

Gemini will place the business call, then stay on the line: Notebookcheck

Google is rolling out “Call for me” as an early preview on Pixel 11, initially only in the United States, so Gemini handles the conversation rather than only dialing. The support page lists product-availability checks, quote requests, reservations, phone menus, and time on hold. Emergency calls, marketing calls, and payments are excluded, and credit card numbers, passwords, and health information must not be shared. Before the call, the app shows the number and the information Gemini will give or request; Gemini then identifies itself as the user’s AI assistant and says the call is recorded. Users can follow a live transcript, listen in, or take over, and afterward get a summary. Notebookcheck reports that transcripts are stored on Google’s servers, audio stays on the device, and neither is used to train Google’s models. The feature is separate from Agentic Calling, which since 2025 has placed certain business calls from Search or AI Mode rather than free-form tasks in the Gemini app. Source: [notebookcheck.net](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Google-puts-Gemini-on-the-phone-AI-handles-calls-for-questions-and-reservations.1408327.0.html)

Forty-two states move to police Meta’s chatbots and agents: simplywall.st

Meta is facing a multistate push in which 42 state attorneys general are coordinating oversight of its AI systems. The coalition aims to enforce safeguards for chatbots and AI agents through state consumer-protection and civil-rights laws. That would extend scrutiny beyond existing federal oversight into state enforcement. Source: [simplywall.st](https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/media/nasdaq-meta/meta-platforms/news/meta-platforms-meta-draws-42-state-push-to-police-ai-chatbot)

A state filing lists 277 Microsoft cuts in the Seattle region: GeekWire

A Washington state filing lists 277 positions Microsoft eliminated in the Seattle region, one job title at a time. Art, game production, game design, technical art, and audio design account for 77 of them. Software engineering is the largest single category, at 60. Source: [geekwire.com](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/artists-game-designers-and-producers-hit-hard-as-microsoft-cuts-277-jobs-in-washington-state/)

Apple and Qualcomm renew a global patent license: 9to5Mac

Qualcomm and Apple have renewed their global patent licensing agreement. The renewed deal extends a license that had been set to expire in 2027. Source: [9to5mac.com](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/24/apple-and-qualcomm-renew-global-patent-licensing-agreement/)

Company Desk

Gemini 3.8 Live grows a face that lip-syncs and keeps talking: Digital Trends

A week after Google launched Gemini 3.8 Live and Live Extended Thinking on September 15, Live Avatar adds near real-time generated video so the agent can lip-sync, use facial expressions, and answer without a freeze between turns. Google is keeping the feature inside Gemini Enterprise for now, for uses such as customer service and interactive walkthroughs, with 97 languages and speech, lip movements, and expressions that adjust without restarting the conversation. Preset avatars are available, and approved enterprise customers can build custom ones from a reference image that preserve likeness, style, or character identity. The avatar can still call tools and fetch information in the background. Generated audio and video carry SynthID watermarking. TechRadar reported that Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking outperformed GPT-Live-1 Astra and Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 across multiple benchmarks while remaining cheaper to run per hour of input audio. Source: [digitaltrends.com](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/google-has-given-gemini-3-8-live-a-face-that-can-lip-sync-react-and-use-tools-in-the-background/)

Golden Gate drops Intel Macs, Boot Camp, and AFP: MacRumors

macOS Golden Gate is the first macOS that runs only on Apple silicon; Intel Macs cannot run it and stay on macOS Tahoe. Boot Camp Assistant is unsupported. There is no AFP client, Time Capsule no longer works with Golden Gate Macs, and AirPort Utility is no longer included in the install. Upgrading removes Rosetta 2, though it can be reinstalled if an installed app needs it, and Golden Gate is the last macOS with full Rosetta support. A warning appears when a Rosetta app runs, and affected apps are listed under Settings, General, About, Intel-Based Apps. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/24/macos-golden-gate-features-removed/)

Suing lawyers say Meta stamped privilege on too much: The Verge

In ongoing lawsuits over alleged harm to teens’ safety and mental health, Meta’s lawyers have argued that certain evidence should be withheld as attorney-client privilege. Lawyers suing the company said this week that the label has been applied far too broadly. They pointed to a recently unsealed document, and The Verge reported that employees ordered “attorney/client privilege” hats while those child-safety disclosures were being fought. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000370/meta-instagram-attorney-client-privilege-hats)

Microsoft drops the Copilot Plus PC name: The Verge

About two and a half years after Microsoft pushed “Copilot Plus PCs” as the machines with enough on-device AI, The Verge reports that Microsoft is killing off the brand. Windows Central’s Zac Bowden is the reporter The Verge cites. The Verge writes that Microsoft and Qualcomm seem to be admitting the brand is already dead. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000495/microsoft-is-killing-off-the-copilot-plus-pc-brand)

The Counterpoint

The book grew, and it was never built for a broad market: InsuranceNewsNet

The New York block lands on a business S&P measured as still expanding, and on a product its own analyst says is not a broad-market insurer. Previous S&P research found Tesla generated $1.37 billion in direct written premiums in 2025, up 40.7% from $970.8 million a year earlier. Tesla-affiliated insurers wrote 75.9% of that business directly in 2025, compared with 32.6% in 2024. California remained the largest market, nearly 70% of 2025 premium volume, at $725 million. Tim Zawacki, head of financial institutions research at S&P Global Market Intelligence, said the purpose “is not to be a competitive source of insurance coverage for a broad segment of the market” and is “to essentially eliminate a point of friction in the Tesla sales process.” He said the shift into affiliated carriers emerged partly because some insurers were reluctant to write electric-vehicle business or charged more because of repair costs and loss severity. Source: [insurancenewsnet.com](https://insurancenewsnet.com/innarticle/tesla-insurance-blocked-as-ny-regulators-examine-connected-car-coverage)

The Thread: Ten billion dollars on a chip still years out

DensityAI, founded a year ago by former leaders of Tesla’s Dojo supercomputer program, is in advanced talks to raise hundreds of millions of dollars in a round that would value it at $10 billion, The Information reported, citing two people with knowledge of the discussions. The valuation is high for a company whose chips those people described as early in development and potentially years from mass production. The company’s leaders have told potential investors they have an agreement in which Amazon Web Services would purchase eventual chips from DensityAI if the chips meet certain performance requirements, the people said. The buy, as those investors were told it, is conditional on performance.

Elon Musk said Colossus 2, an AI computing cluster built by his xAI business, may more than double its current Nvidia chip count by the end of the year, Bloomberg reported. That is a volume target on chips already in the cluster, set against a startup valuation on chips that are still early. They share a calendar. DensityAI’s founders left Tesla’s Dojo effort a year ago and are now describing an Amazon purchase that triggers only if performance requirements are met, while the chips remain years from mass production. Musk’s year-end aim sits on Nvidia parts Colossus 2 already uses, and the scale he named is more than double that current count. For Amazon, the DensityAI clause is optional supply if a new chip qualifies. For Nvidia, Colossus 2 is demand on the parts shipping now. Tesla’s name in the account is the Dojo program those founders left, not the buyer. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/startup-founded-ex-tesla-dojo-leaders-nears-10-billion-valuation)

Apple still plans security updates for Intel Macs through fall 2028. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/24/macos-golden-gate-features-removed/)

MAG 7 Daily

Hundreds of thousands of merchants can now offer Prime on their own sites at no extra cost.

What You Need to Know: Hundreds of thousands of merchants on Amazon’s U.S. fulfillment network can now turn on fast, free Prime delivery on their own websites at no added cost to them or their shoppers. Google is rolling Gemini audio models out to developers the same day its DeepMind chief says Gemini 4 is in refinement. Apple priced a two-phone number handoff, and Meta put a pocket Muse device and a hundred-gram headset on a launch path.

Top News

Merchants switch on Prime delivery on their own sites: About Amazon

Merchants using Amazon Multichannel Fulfillment in the U.S. can now offer fast, free Prime delivery on their own websites at no additional cost to them or their shoppers. Hundreds of thousands of merchants can activate it in minutes, with no changes to checkout, payments, or store policies. Through the new MCF Preferred Pricing Program, merchants can save up to 25% on fulfillment fees across their own websites, marketplaces, and social channels, with no contracts required. Source: [aboutamazon.com](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-mcf-prime-delivery-merchant-websites?utm_source=rss)

Gemini Audio models roll out to Studio, Notebook, and the API: Google

Google said new Gemini Audio models are rolling out across Google AI Studio, Gemini Notebook, and the Gemini API. The linked post is titled around Gemini 3.8 text-to-speech. Google said the models are coming soon to Google Vids and Gemini Enterprise. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/Google/status/2102781492644413566)

iPhone Handoff gets a monthly price and a short carrier list: MacRumors

iPhone Handoff in iOS 27 lets the same number sit on two iPhones, active on only one at a time, and it is separate from the existing Handoff that continues an app on another Apple device. Calls, voicemail, messages — including iMessage, RCS, SMS, and MMS — and Find My location move to the phone that is unlocked, with a “Switched to this iPhone” confirmation. T-Mobile in the United States is charging customers 5 per month; Deutsche Telekom is charging €6.95 per month, but only on plans that do not already include multi-SIM, while MagentaMobil L includes one multi-SIM and XL includes two. The feature is available through T-Mobile and Telekom; Verizon and EE will support it later this year, and AT&T has not committed, with a spokesperson pointing to existing eSIM activation and transfer options. Both phones need iOS 27 or later, Wi-Fi, the same Apple Account, Messages in iCloud, and FaceTime and iMessage on that account. Stopping the service means contacting the carrier; deleting an eSIM may not stop the charge. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/iphone-handoff-how-it-works-carrier-support-cost/)

DeepMind chief says Gemini 4 is in refinement: The Verge

Koray Kavukcuoglu, in his first media appearance as leader of Google’s DeepMind division, told The Information that Gemini 4 is in its refinement stage. The Verge reports Google is nearing the launch after trailing rival developers on flagship AI releases. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/999802/google-deepmind-gemini-4-timeline-koray-kavukcuoglu)

Apple sets an early-next-week fix for iPhone 18 Pro reboots: 9to5Mac

Apple confirmed to 9to5Mac that a software update is coming early next week for iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max phones that freeze and reboot after a failed Face ID attempt. 9to5Mac had reported the failures on Monday and read them as a software glitch rather than a hardware fault. Source: [9to5mac.com](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/23/apple-iphone-18-pro-rebooting-bug-software-update-fix/)

The Thread: A hundred-gram headset against a shelved pendant

A hundred grams is the weight MacRumors gives Meta’s new VR glasses, five times lighter than the Meta Quest 3, with full-color passthrough and a 5K display. The same report says Apple has shelved plans to launch an AI pendant, while Mark Zuckerberg used a “one more thing” slot at Connect to unveil Muse Charm, a puck about the size of an Apple Watch with a tamagotchi-like avatar for talking to Muse. Zuckerberg said Meta packed the real-time voice and avatar stack into something that fits on a keychain, started by a tap on a fingerprint sensor in the corner, without unlocking a phone or opening an app, and that Charm is aimed at sale by the holiday season, with more to share later this year. The VR glasses are set to launch in the spring at a starting price of 1,300. Meta says they offer cinema-grade clarity with Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, up to three hours of continuous high-resolution media playback on a single charge, every game available for Quest headsets, Xbox Cloud Gaming, and a virtual keyboard and trackpad on a desk when connected to a PC or Mac. For context, MacRumors notes an M5 Apple Vision Pro weighs 750–800 grams depending on configuration. An audio-only Ray-Ban model, presented against the “pervert glasses” label, supports media playback and calls and runs for up to 12 hours, while the charging case adds up to 48 hours. The refreshed camera Ray-Ban Meta is slimmer, adds a button that activates Meta AI, and is said to have nine hours of battery versus eight on the second-generation model, plus a six-microphone array Meta says can cut out over 90 percent of background noise. The 12MP camera supports 3K video, with Dolby Atmos spatial audio support coming soon, and prices start at 449. An FDA-cleared hearing enhancement is due later this year, as part of a Meta One subscription or a one-time cost of 149, with a 30-day free trial. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/24/meta-did-a-one-more-thing-and-its-an-ai-tamagotchi/)

Top News

U.S. government urges Supreme Court to reverse Apple contempt ruling over App Store commission: 9to5Mac

The U.S. government and several legal and business groups filed briefs with the Supreme Court urging reversal of at least part of the civil contempt ruling against Apple in the Epic Games lawsuit. The district court held Apple in contempt after the company allowed external purchase links yet imposed a 27 percent commission on those transactions. Apple has since removed the commission entirely. The company maintains that the original injunction text did not prohibit charging a commission and that lower courts improperly relied on the spirit of the order rather than its wording. The injunction states that Apple is permanently restrained from prohibiting developers from including buttons, external links or other calls to action that direct customers to purchasing mechanisms outside In-App Purchasing. The Supreme Court agreed in late June to hear Apple’s argument on this point. Source: [9to5mac.com](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/22/u-s-government-urges-supreme-court-to-reverse-apple-contempt-ruling-over-app-store-commission/)

Google and the Gates Foundation to bring AI resources to 200 million farmers across the Global South.: Google Blog

Google partnered with the Gates Foundation to direct $100 million toward organizations that scale AI-powered climate and crop insights. The program targets 200 million smallholder farmers in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, an increase from the current 50 million reached. Smallholder farmers produce nearly 35 percent of the world’s food across more than 500 million farms yet often lack access to satellite data, financial services and public support needed for unpredictable weather. The combined funding includes dedicated technical support from Google researchers to deploy real-time climate, agricultural and language AI tools. Source: [blog.google](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/google-org/partnering-with-the-gates-foundation-to-bring-ai-resources-to-200-million-farmers-across-the-global-south/)

The steeper discounts apply when customers commit to specific feature usage levels. The company is simultaneously preparing an AI super app for launch. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/exclusive-microsoft-boost-copilot-discounts-launches-ai-super-app)

The Thread: AI tool launches and partnerships
