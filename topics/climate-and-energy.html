Topic

Climate and energy

Two different jobs share this subject. One is regulatory: what Canadian environmental rules actually require, province by province, in a form you can forward to a team. The other is progress: the clean-energy build-out, reported with numbers instead of adjectives.

The compliance brief exists because a consultation today is a gazetted rule next year, and almost nobody covers that gap.

The 3 shows that cover it

Tesla Shorts Time

Daily · ~15 min

Shorting Tesla? Time's Up.

Environmental Intelligence

Weekly — Mondays · ~10 min

Environmental regulatory and compliance briefing.

The DP Pod: The Do Positive Podcast

Weekly — Mondays · ~10 min

The club for people who do something about it — good news, honest numbers, one action a week.
