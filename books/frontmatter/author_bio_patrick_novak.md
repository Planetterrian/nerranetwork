Patrick Novak runs the Nerra Network, an independent podcast network
based in Vancouver that produces daily shows on technology, science,
markets, and the history of ideas — including *Unintended Consequences*
and *First Principles Daily*, the two series these books grew out of.

The stories in this collection were researched and directed by the
author and drafted with AI assistance. Every factual claim that carries
a source was checked against that source before publication, and the
Sources section at the back of this book lists them — the same standard
the network applies to every episode it publishes.

Hear the shows, read the story trackers, and find the rest of the
catalogue at nerranetwork.com.
