The skipper: Scott Shawyer

President and skipper. Came to the sport from engineering and business (26 years leading JMP Solutions). Holds the double-handed course record for the RORC Transatlantic Race; 4th in the 2024 New York Vendée, his first solo IMOCA race. Collingwood, Ontario.

Be Water Positive (water-scarcity awareness) + a Youth Pathway Programme for young Canadian sailors.

The Canadian lineage

  • Mike Birch — Won the very first Route du Rhum in 1978 — 47 years old, in the 12-metre trimaran Olympus Photo, by 98 seconds after 23 days at sea. He died in 2022, aged 90.
  • Gerry Roufs — Lying second in the 1996–97 Vendée Globe when his last message from the South Pacific described 15-metre seas and 80-knot winds. Never heard from again; wreckage of Groupe LG 2 reached Chile months later.
  • Derek Hatfield — Campaigned Spirit of Canada through the round-the-world races of the 2000s and started a Vendée Globe; no Vendée finish. Died in 2016.
  • Scott Shawyer — Chapter four. No Canadian has ever completed the Vendée Globe.
