Guest Picks

Every interview on The Age of AI and Nerra Voices ends the same way. Mira asks for a book worth reading, the best advice the guest was ever given, who has inspired them most, the show's own closing question, and who she should interview next. These are their answers, in their words.

Books

The bookshelf

The one book each guest would put in your hands.

Maximum Trading Gains With Anchored VWAP

Brian Shannon

There isn't really a book that goes over this. A good one on volume-type trading is Brian Shannon's Anchored VWAP book, which I just picked up recently.

Scott Pulcini, Nerra Voices Ep3

Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

Robert B. Cialdini

A great book for everyone in recruitment and sales on how to tell stories better, because it is all about story and how you communicate it. Recruitment is a sales-driven people business, so the better you are at telling the story, the better you communicate your vision and goals in life.

Thor Hesselberg, The Age of AI Ep12

The E-Myth

Michael E. Gerber

It is about working on the business versus in the business, and the difference between being an entrepreneur and self-employed.

Jason Fishman, The Age of AI Ep11

Zero to One

Peter Thiel

It helped me start building the solution and start building the systems.

Sameer Ranjan, The Age of AI Ep8

Future Shock

Alvin Toffler
John Capobianco, The Age of AI Ep5

The best advice they were ever given

“Lose your head, lose your ass. When you lose your head, you will lose your ass, meaning your money.”

From my grandfather
Scott Pulcini, Nerra Voices Ep3

“Focus on pleasing the 80-year-old you over yourself. Everything in between is building up to that sequence.”

Thor Hesselberg, The Age of AI Ep12

“Never give up.”

Jason Fishman, The Age of AI Ep11

Who inspires them

Dr. Brett Steenbarger

He wrote a number of trading psychology books. I'm in one of them, Enhancing Trader Performance, and he kept me grounded during very turbulent times.

Scott Pulcini, Nerra Voices Ep3

My mom

She was kind, gullible to some extent, but always positive even when we were falling down. It is not about your success; it is how you learn from your failures, because the more you fall, the stronger you get.

Thor Hesselberg, The Age of AI Ep12

My dad

Jason Fishman, The Age of AI Ep11

The bets they can't prove yet

The Age of AI asks every guest: “What's the one bet you're making for the next twelve months that you cannot prove yet?”

No hiring process in the future will happen without trust being established before you go into a screening call. We're building a new category and this is not built overnight.

Thor Hesselberg, The Age of AI Ep12

More issuers will enter the marketplace as investor success stories, companies we've worked with going public and shareholders having liquidity events, translate into more investor interest, more successful campaigns, and more companies looking to issue shares through these vehicles.

Jason Fishman, The Age of AI Ep11

I think we're going to see a whole restructuring in how teams are done. We're moving out of practicing real estate as an island toward a business-by-osmosis model where people work together, share knowledge, stay in the parts that bring them passion, and build apprenticeship and mentorship that doesn't take advantage of newer or older agents.

Roddy de la Garza, The Age of AI Ep10

I really do think there is something to the hype of agentic AI, even though an OpenClaw setup is still extremely clunky and I spend about as much time debugging it as doing real work. I'm hoping that in twelve months that research pays off in more production from my existing team without adding people, hybrid teams of people and agents, and using agents to prototype work and then turn that into deeper, more deterministic automation.

Sheldon Poon, The Age of AI Ep9

Most of the people are getting tired and overworked in the age of AI. I have not proved it yet, but I wish to prove it soon.

Sameer Ranjan, The Age of AI Ep8

There will be market disruption and labor disruption as AI advances, but I'm betting the stock market stays intact and that I'll have a job for the next twelve months, that it's still my best bet to stay in the market. People have always managed, and I don't expect society to collapse in the next twelve months.

Vincent Rylan, The Age of AI Ep7

The bet I'm anticipating is that most AI content creation will be regulated to the point where IP protection is less of a concern, and that nearly all AI content platforms will follow an approach that supports the artists behind the work, because that will become table stakes for what people choose to consume.

Hogan Shrum, The Age of AI Ep4

In six months, Mira will get much better at developing a conversation instead of cutting it off early, turning a short topic into a longer one, and pulling on more strings so it keeps going.

Dan Perra, The Age of AI Ep2

The bet is that there is space for this and people will want this. I like growing it organically, but we need a wider audience and their feedback to make something really great, so the open question is whether to put resources into marketing.

Patrick Novak, The Age of AI Ep1

What they wish more people understood

Nerra Voices asks every guest: “What's the one thing about your work that you wish more people understood?”

All you have to do is identify the two most important things in trading, volume and volatility. Stop-iceberg data is the most important data I've ever seen; chasing shiny new indicators instead of concentrating on volume events is not going to work.

Scott Pulcini, Nerra Voices Ep3

The relationship we're building is not a one-off intervention. This is a lifetime relationship, and we take everything we do to heart, thinking about, worrying about, and sharing in that pathway with every patient.

Dr. Michael Brandt, Nerra Voices Ep2

We sit between the merchants and the processors, and we don't charge merchants anything, no subscription fees and no onboarding fees. We charge processors a nominal 10 cents per successful transaction, so there are different options for everybody and it can be a win-win across the board.

Viktor Popovic, Nerra Voices Ep1
