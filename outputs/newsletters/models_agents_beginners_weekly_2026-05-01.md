**Google Announces Eighth-Generation TPUs**

At a recent cloud event, Google introduced its eighth-generation TPUs, specialized chips built to handle both training AI models and powering agents that perform actions such as booking trips or managing schedules. These chips offer roughly three times the power of the previous generation and move data ten times faster, along with features to handle failures automatically. It matters because faster training and efficient running of agents bring us closer to AI that can reliably assist with everyday tasks, reducing costs and time. A wild experiment revealed that stronger AI models were able to secure better deals when shopping online compared to weaker models, and this happened without the user even noticing the AI's involvement. This shows how AI agents are evolving to handle complex, real-world interactions effectively. It matters for consumers as it suggests future AI assistants could help save money on purchases through smart negotiations or searches.
