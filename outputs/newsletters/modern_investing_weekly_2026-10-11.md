## Strategy of the Week

**Volume confirmation** — the filter that would have saved three of those four marks ([▶ Episode 195 · 2026-10-09](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep195.html)).

1. **What it is.** You do not enter a news, earnings, or partnership trade unless that session's volume clears the 20-day average. No spike, no position. Full stop.
2. **When to use it.** Any time a headline is doing the selling — an AMD–Samsung meeting, a Plaid partnership, an AI-power story, a guidance raise. Use it *now*, in a rotation regime, when sector flows can overwhelm a perfectly decent single-stock story. Skip it for slow accumulation of a 30-year dividend grower you're building for income, not a pop.
3. **How to set it up.** Fifteen minutes, one of three platforms:
   - **Wealthsimple:** Open the name → Chart → daily. Eyeball today's volume bar against the last month. For the actual 20-day average, open TradingView in a browser tab (free), type the ticker, add `SMA(20)` on the volume pane. Wealthsimple won't do the math — TradingView will.
   - **Questrade:** IQ Edge or web chart. Add volume, then a 20-period average on that pane. Compare before you size.
   - **IBKR:** TWS or Client Portal. Volume + SMA 20. Today's bar prints above that line or you don't click buy.

Pair it with a valuation screen and you get this week's Dollarama setup. Ignore it and you get META's Plaid headline fading on a quiet tape ([▶ Episode 196 · 2026-10-10](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep196.html)).

## Canadian Corner

Three TFSA mechanics, no fluff.

**Where the US stuff lives.** [▶ Episode 192 · 2026-10-06](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep192.html) told you to review US equity allocations in TFSAs at S&P all-time highs. Do it with the tax map in mind: the IRS does **not** recognize the TFSA, so US-listed dividends leak **15% withholding** you cannot recover. The RRSP *is* recognized, so US dividend payers belong there. Growth names with tiny yields — the GOOGL/META crowd, and Vanguard's **VGT / MGK / VONG** tech tilts from [▶ Episode 195 · 2026-10-09](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep195.html) — are the ones that actually make sense in a TFSA. You're not leaking much. You *are* still taking CAD/USD risk.

**Where the Canadian cash flow lives.** Enbridge at **5.9%**, with 98% of earnings from regulated utilities and long-term contracts and 80% of that inflation-indexed, is a TFSA native — no withholding, tax-free compounding, 30-year dividend-growth DNA ([▶ Episode 196 · 2026-10-10](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep196.html)). Both get more interesting when mortgage rates are punching duration in the face, *if* you can live with the overlay. Buy HDIV on Wealthsimple, Questrade, or IBKR like any TSX ETF; DRIP the distributions.
