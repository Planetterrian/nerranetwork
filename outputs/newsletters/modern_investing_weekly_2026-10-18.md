## Strategy of the Week

**The five-year P/E screen with a 20-day volume gate.**

1. **What it is:** Only add risk in quality names trading below their five-year average P/E, and only when same-day volume beats the 20-day average — a buffer when momentum rotates out ([▶ Episode 204 · Oct 18](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep204.html)).
2. **When to use it:** After inflation shocks, EM outflows, or tech washouts like this week, when high-multiple names get punished and cash-flow stocks go on sale. It shone in 2022’s rate-hike tape. Skip the volume check and you get IAG.TO.
3. **How to set it up:**
   - **Wealthsimple:** Open the screener (or your watchlist), flag P/E below the five-year average, then compare today’s volume to the 20-day average on the chart. If you’re in Canadian energy and tanker rates are extreme, cut the add in half — don’t flatten it ([▶ Episode 198 · Oct 12](https://nerranetwork.com/blog/modern_investing/ep198.html)).
   - **Questrade:** Pull the quote, check P/E versus history, overlay 20-day average volume. Same half-size rule when Gibson-style freight costs sit in last year’s 90th percentile.
   - **IBKR:** Run TWS Scanner plus the Volume Scanner for P/E below the five-year average *and* volume above the 20-day average, then cross-check sector rotation before you click.

If you do nothing else: run that scan once this weekend and write down three tickers that clear both gates.
