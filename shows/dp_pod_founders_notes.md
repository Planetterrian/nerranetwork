## PATRICK (real, on-air-safe)

- Vancouver-based. Physical chemist by training (BSc Chemistry, University
  of Alberta). Spent nearly twenty years in environmental analytical
  chemistry — was VP and co-owner of CARO Analytical Services, which became
  British Columbia's leading environmental lab. His lab pioneered cannabis
  testing when Canada legalized, and urgently stood up COVID PCR testing
  during the public-health crisis.
- A builder by compulsion: runs a Vancouver startup incubator
  (Planetterrian Ventures) whose projects include the Nerra Network,
  Bill Saved (an AI that waits on hold with your phone company and fights
  billing disputes for you), Avvizo (an AI daily check-in phone call for
  seniors and people living alone — no app needed), Loans Expert (mortgage
  rate comparison for Canadians), and Lilwords (a mobile word game he built
  himself in Unity over two years).
- Also built a food-tech venture making a carbon-negative vegan protein
  from upcycled brewery spent yeast and spent soybeans — the goal: feed
  more people without growing more crops.
- Angel investor whose fund focuses on science, sustainability, and
  female founders.
- Published science-fiction novelist; writes at planetterrian.substack.com
  ("Writings for Enrichment and Interest") and on Medium. On X:
  @patricknovak.
- THE STORY (use for grit / action-precedes-motivation themes): in 2015
  Patrick raced the Yukon River Quest — a multi-day ultra-distance paddle
  race — in a double kayak named "Scientia Navus" with his friend Ryan, as
  team fifty-seven, "CARO Water Quest", with essentially no training.
  Twenty-six hours in he was physically and mentally broken and completely
  convinced their race was over — proud, even, to be quitting, having
  already paddled longer than he thought he could. They kept going and
  finished fourth in the men's tandem kayak class. He'd first paddled the
  Yukon with his dad twenty-five years earlier, as a kid.
- Genuine beliefs: individuals are not powerless; the feeds profit from
  making you feel otherwise. Numbers beat vibes. A caveat stated honestly
  is more optimistic than hype.
