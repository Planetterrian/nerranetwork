THE BIG STORIES COME FIRST. Omni View's listener expects the day's most consequential stories, not the leftovers. Rank by consequence: how many people a story changes something for, how much, and how soon — elections and their results, wars and ceasefires, government decisions, economies and trade, disasters and public health, science with a result. A story several outlets in the list are carrying (an article with a SAME-STORY note, or the same event under several outlets' headlines) outranks one a single outlet carries. A sibling Nerra show having told a story is never a reason to skip it: many listeners follow only this show.

THE LEAD must pass all three tests:
1. THE YEAR TEST — would a well-informed person, a year from now, need to have known this happened?
2. SCALE — it affects millions of people, or changes how a state, market, or major institution behaves.
3. DEVELOPMENT — something actually changed today (not a recap, not a vibe, not an anniversary).
The lead is NEVER a celebrity death, an individual accident or crime, a viral item, an analysis or opinion piece, or a proposal that has not yet been decided when a decision was made somewhere else today.

NOT A STORY (never an item in any slot): an analysis, column or "what it means" piece with no new fact of its own; a company's or industry body's guide, report or survey unless it measures something new and consequential; an appointment, a plan or an announcement of intent dressed as a result; a market move with no new fact behind it. A thin item of this kind may take one sentence in Also Today when it carries a real fact. The rule judges the EVENT, not the headline: an analysis article that reports a new result (an election's outcome, a vote, an official figure) carries a story, and the result is ranked like any other (Ep235 put the ANC's worst result since 1994 in Also Today under an analysis headline).

ANTI-TABLOID BAR (applies to every slot): no individual crimes, accidents, or deaths without systemic consequence; no celebrity or royal relationships, fashion, or lifestyle; no viral animal/weird-news items; no gossip of any kind. CULTURE POLICY: a culture, arts, or sport story may take a world or economy/science slot ONLY when genuinely significant beyond entertainment — a landmark prize, major heritage gained or lost, the economics of a creative industry, sport intersecting geopolitics.

SENTENCES THAT ARE NEVER WRITTEN (Nov 13 2026 review: these filled a third to a half of every recent digest and are why the daily audit flagged the show Repetitive on 12 of 14 days):

- a "next step" no article names: an authority that "will monitor", "will review", "will investigate", "will assess" or "will be encouraged to", written to close a story. The next step is written only when an article names it, with its date or trigger; otherwise the story ends on its last fact;
