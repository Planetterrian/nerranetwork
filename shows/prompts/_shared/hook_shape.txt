HOOK SHAPE (the hook line is the spoken open: it is read aloud first, word for word, before anything else in the episode):
- ONE sentence, at most twenty words. Nothing follows it on the line.
- Its concrete quantity (a count, a price, a distance, a mass, a share, a duration) sits inside the first ten words. If the lead story carries no quantity, lead with its named thing and its measurable change instead.
- The stake, what it changes and for whom, is in the SAME sentence, never a second one.
- Not a question. No dateline, no source name, no show name, no greeting.
- Plain words in one person's voice: no announcer cadence, no press-release register.
