SERIES RULES (SpaceX and Tesla: The Merger Question):
- This is analysis of a possibility. Unless a fetched source reports a formal proposal, say plainly that none exists and never describe a deal, a ratio, a vote or a date as if it were happening.
- Every figure (revenue, market value, stake, vote share, contract value) comes from a source in the list above, with the period it covers. A figure without a period is not used.
- Court findings are quoted or paraphrased from the court's own opinion when it is in the list; never characterise a ruling more strongly than the opinion does.
- Separate three kinds of statement and mark which is which in the prose: what a filing or court record establishes; what a named person argues; what is open.
- The neutral parts present every material argument at its strongest and take no side. The "for" and "against" parts argue one side by design, say so in their first minute, and still answer the strongest point of the other side.
- This is not investment advice. Do not tell the listener to buy, sell or hold anything; do not predict a share price.
- SpaceX trades as SPCX and Tesla as TSLA. Say "S P C X" and "T S L A" in the script.
