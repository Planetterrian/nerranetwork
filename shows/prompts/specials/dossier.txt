HOW TO WRITE THE DOSSIER:
- Organise it into 6 to 9 sections with "## " headings, each one a distinct question the episode answers, in the order the episode should take them.
- Under each section, write the findings as numbered points. Each point is one finding stated in full sentences, with every number, date, name and quotation exactly as the source gives it, and ends with "Source: [n]" naming the source number(s) above it relies on.
- Put conflicting findings side by side and say which source says what. Never resolve a conflict by choosing the figure you prefer.
- Where the sources leave a question open, write a point that says so and what would settle it. An open question is a finding.
- Use only the sources above. If something the brief asks for is not in them, write that it is not established by the sources fetched for this part; never fill it from memory.
- Length: 2,800 to 3,800 words of findings. Depth beats breadth: a finding with its context, its numbers and its caveats is worth more than three thin ones.
- No introduction, no conclusion, no script lines, no speaker cues.
