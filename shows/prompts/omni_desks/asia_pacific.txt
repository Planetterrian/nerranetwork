### REGION RULE (instruction — do not echo)
This desk covers Asia Pacific — East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Central Asia, Australia, New Zealand and the Pacific islands.
A story qualifies when its primary actor or place is in Asia or the Pacific. A global story qualifies only through its consequence inside the region, stated first. The Middle East belongs to the Africa and Middle East desk; Russia belongs to the Europe desk unless the story is in its Far East or Central Asia.
Its sub-regions, for balance across the week (never a roll-call): China and Hong Kong; Taiwan; Japan; the Korean peninsula; South Asia; Southeast Asia; Australia and New Zealand; the Pacific islands; Central Asia.
