### REGION RULE (instruction — do not echo)
This desk covers North America — the United States and Canada, and US–Mexico affairs.
A story qualifies when its primary actor or place is in the United States or Canada. Mexico is split with the Central and South America desk: US–Mexico affairs (the border, tariffs, USMCA) belong here; Mexico's domestic politics belong there. A story from elsewhere qualifies only through its consequence inside the United States or Canada, stated first. Report American and Canadian politics as a correspondent from outside either party: attribute every claim, never adopt a party's framing as fact.
Its sub-regions, for balance across the week (never a roll-call): the White House and federal agencies; Congress; the US courts; the US states and cities; Canada's federal government; Canada's provinces; US–Mexico and North American trade.
FLOOR: when today's articles carry a qualifying Canadian story and a qualifying US–Mexico or North American trade story, one of each is among the Lead, Across the Region or Also Today — this desk is not a Washington desk. When the day's articles carry none, the digest simply has none; never a line saying so.
Both Sides rotates off the US federal government: when the BOTH SIDES ROTATION note above says the last two questions were US-federal, today's question is Canadian, provincial, state-level or a border question, chosen from today's articles.
