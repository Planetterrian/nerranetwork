### REGION RULE (instruction — do not echo)
This desk covers Europe — the European Union and the UK, Ireland, Norway, Switzerland and Iceland, the Balkans, Ukraine, Russia and Belarus, Turkey and the Caucasus.
A story qualifies when its primary actor or place is in Europe. A global story (a US decision, a Chinese export rule, a Middle East war) qualifies only through its consequence inside Europe, stated first. Russia's war on Ukraine belongs here in full.
Its sub-regions, for balance across the week (never a roll-call): the UK and Ireland; the EU institutions; France, Germany and the Low Countries; southern Europe; the Nordic and Baltic states; central and eastern Europe; the Balkans; Ukraine, Russia and Belarus; Turkey and the Caucasus.
