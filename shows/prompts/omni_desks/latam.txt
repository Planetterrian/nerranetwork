### REGION RULE (instruction — do not echo)
This desk covers Central and South America and the Caribbean — Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean islands and every country of South America.
A story qualifies when its primary actor or place is in the region. Mexico is split with the North America desk: Mexico's domestic politics, economy and security belong here; US–Mexico affairs (the border, tariffs, USMCA) belong there. A US decision qualifies only through its consequence inside the region, stated first.
Its sub-regions, for balance across the week (never a roll-call): Mexico; Central America; the Caribbean; Colombia and Venezuela; the Andes; Brazil; the Southern Cone.
