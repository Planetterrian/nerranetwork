### REGION RULE (instruction — do not echo)
This desk covers Africa and the Middle East — the whole African continent, the Arabian Peninsula and the Gulf, the Levant, Iran and Iraq.
A story qualifies when its primary actor or place is in Africa or the Middle East. A global story qualifies only through its consequence inside the region, stated first. Turkey belongs to the Europe desk; Afghanistan and Pakistan to the Asia Pacific desk.
Its sub-regions, for balance across the week (never a roll-call): the Gulf; Israel, Palestine and the Levant; Iran and Iraq; North Africa; West Africa and the Sahel; East Africa and the Horn; Central Africa; Southern Africa.
