Why Nerra

Built different

⚡

Sourced, Not Vibes

Every episode is built from dozens of named sources, and every article links to them. A source-integrity gate checks that cited claims actually appear where we say they do. See how an episode is made.

🎧

Zero Ads. Zero Sponsors.

No ad reads, no sponsor segments, no clickbait. Every episode on every show is free; Nerra Personal is the optional upgrade for people who want one custom feed.

🌎

Multi-Perspective Coverage

Omni View argues each side of a story in its strongest form. Across the network we pull from diverse sources to present balanced, nuanced takes — not echo chambers.

📅

A Cadence You Can Set Your Morning By

Most shows publish every day; the rest weekly or when an interview is ready, and each show page says which. Build a daily habit you can rely on.

🇧🇨

Made in Canada

Independently produced in Vancouver by Patrick Novak. No corporate backing, no editorial interference.

🔊

Four Listening Languages

English originals with Français, Русский and 中文 audio tracks, plus two shows made natively in Russian. Pick your audio language with the globe in the menu.

Our Shows

Daily intelligence on the topics that matter

Every show is ad-free and independently produced; most publish every day. Pick one, or let Nerra Daily hand you the whole network in one episode. Three of them are hosted by Mira, who also does the network's interviews herself.

Nerra Daily
Hosted by Mira Daily ~2 hours
Nerra Daily is the Nerra Network's combined daily edition. Every morning, once the day's English shows have published, Mira assembles them into one continuous listen, in a fixed order: SpaceX Daily, Tesla Shorts Time, Fascinating Frontiers, Models & Agents, Planetterrian Daily and Omni View lead, then Modern Investing Techniques, the narrative shows and the beginner track, closing on The DP Pod's good news. On Mondays the weekly shows join the rundown, and midway through Mira adds a short field note of her own. Every segment is the full, unabridged episode from that show's own feed; what Nerra Daily adds is the through-line — Mira's introductions, the connections between stories, and one place to hear it all. Subscribe to Nerra Daily for everything, or pick up any individual show from its own feed at nerranetwork.com.
For: Listeners who want the entire Nerra Network — news, markets, science and ideas — in a single daily episode
Sources: Every English Nerra Network show, unabridged, Chapter markers per segment, Anchored by Mira (The Age of AI)

Nerra Network is an independent podcast network of 31 shows covering Tesla, SpaceX, AI for experts and beginners, modern investing, balanced world news, space and astronomy, science and longevity, environmental policy, first-principles thinking, the unintended consequences of big decisions, good news with something you can do about it (The DP Pod), Canada's offshore energy story (Offshore North), live AI-hosted interviews (The Age of AI), and financial literacy and language learning in Russian — plus Nerra Daily, the whole network in one daily edition. All produced in Vancouver, Canada by Patrick Novak, with AI doing the writing and narration and a human owning the standards. How we use AI.

Our mission: stimulating conversation, supporting positive developments that benefit all, and providing unbiased coverage with varying perspectives.
