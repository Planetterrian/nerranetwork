Apply to The Age of AIApply to Nerra Voices

The Age of AI and Nerra Voices are the first interview shows of their kind: an AI asks the questions, and the human guest decides whether the conversation is published at all.

What is unusual is not that the host is an AI — it is the terms. Mira holds a live, unscripted conversation rather than reading a script at a recording. She discloses on air that the host is a machine. A human editor reviews every episode before release, and that review has no timer that expires into publication. And the guest gets their own transcript before anything is assembled, with a week to approve it, cut anything from it, or refuse it outright: what they cut is removed from the audio, not bleeped, and a takedown stays available after publication. Plenty of shows use AI to write, narrate or edit, and some now let it interview. We have not found another that hands the guest the final say.

If you know of an interview show that was already doing this, we would genuinely like to hear about it — write to hello@nerranetwork.com and this page will say so.

Latest corrections

When we get something wrong we say so on the episode's page and in the next episode's show notes.

  1. Tesla Shorts Time Oct 16

    Correction to episode 634: The episode's headline and cold open said Tesla's China sales dropped 12 percent year over year in September. CPCA September retail for Tesla in China was down 9.35 percent year over year, not 12. The Model Y topping the monthly sales chart is unchanged.

  2. Unintended Consequences Oct 11

    Correction to episode 139: This episode said the Argentine government licensed the Patagonian toothfish fishery off South Georgia and that Argentine-flagged boats worked it. South Georgia is a British Overseas Territory; its fishery is licensed by the Government of South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands under CCAMLR rules, not by Argentina. The episode also said the licences cut orca calf survival there. We could not find a source for that finding and the episode cited none, so treat the case as unsupported.

  3. Tesla Shorts Time Oct 8

    Correction to episode 627: The episode's opening line and title said Giga Storage's move into Germany widens the path for Tesla Megapack deployments across Europe. The Energy-Storage.News report the story was written from connects the project to neither Tesla nor Megapack. That link was ours, not the source's.

About Nerra Network

More about us →

Feed your curiosity, not your anxiety.

News and ideas from 26 free shows on space, AI, markets, world news, science and more. Every story is sourced, every claim is checked against its source, and you can read the article or hear the episode it came from.

Nerra Network is an independent news and podcast network of 26 free shows covering world news, space and astronomy, science, AI for experts and beginners, AI chips and data centres, markets and modern investing, the Magnificent Seven, prediction markets, SpaceX, Tesla, longevity and peptide research, environmental policy, first-principles thinking, the unintended consequences of big decisions, good news with something you can do about it (The DP Pod), Canada's offshore ocean-racing story (Offshore North), local news from Vancouver and Collingwood, live AI-hosted interviews (The Age of AI and Nerra Voices), and financial literacy and language learning in Russian — plus Nerra Daily, the whole network in one daily edition. All produced in Vancouver, Canada by Patrick Novak, with AI doing the writing and narration and a human owning the standards. How we use AI.

Our mission: stimulating conversation, supporting positive developments that benefit all, and providing unbiased coverage with varying perspectives.

  • ⚡
    Sourced, not vibes. News episodes are built from named sources and each article links to them. A source-integrity gate checks cited claims against those sources and marks any it cannot verify — the public claims ledger shows the status. See how an episode is made.
  • 🎧
    Free, and independent. Every episode is free. Sponsors may support the shows; a sponsored message is always labelled as one, and no sponsor chooses, reviews or changes what a show covers. Nerra Personal is the optional upgrade for people who want one custom feed.
  • 🌎
    Every side, in its strongest form. Omni View argues each side of a story at its best, and across the network we draw on diverse sources rather than one echo chamber.
  • 📅
    A cadence you can set your morning by. Most shows publish every day; the rest weekly or when an interview is ready, and each show's card says which.
  • 🇨🇦
    Made in Canada. Independently produced in Vancouver by Patrick Novak. No corporate backing, no editorial interference.
  • 🔊
    3 listening languages. English originals with Français and Русский audio tracks, plus two shows made natively in Russian. Pick your audio language with the globe in the menu.
