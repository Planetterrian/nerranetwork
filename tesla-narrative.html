Active Strategic Programs

Optimus

Last deep coverage: Ep 482 (2026-05-20)

Giga Texas dedicated factory construction is underway. First visible production cells and humanoid training progress expected throughout 2026. Volume production still targeted for 2027+.

Key open questions:
  • When will we see meaningful volume production (hundreds of units per week)?
  • Cost per unit vs human labor breakeven economics
  • Initial target applications (internal Tesla deployment vs external sales)
TST confidence on major timelines: medium
Notable claims surfaced on the show:
Ep 461 (2026-04-15): "Factory will be visibly transformative by mid-2026" — Status: partially on track
Ep 482 (2026-05-20): "First production-intent cells expected before end of 2026" — Status: monitoring

Cybercab / Robotaxi

Last deep coverage: Ep 475 (2026-05-13)

Vehicle unveiled at We, Robot event. Unsupervised regulatory path and production timeline remain the primary gating items. No firm unsupervised deployment date announced yet.

Key open questions:
  • Regulatory approval timeline for unsupervised operation in the US and key markets
  • Production start date and initial fleet deployment size
  • Vehicle cost target and business model (Tesla-owned fleet vs owner-operated)
TST confidence on major timelines: low-medium

FSD Unsupervised

Last deep coverage: Ep 483 (2026-05-22)

Supervised FSD continues rapid expansion (v12 → v13 → v14 series). True city-street unsupervised operation remains gated by regulators. China version rebranded 'Assisted Driving' in 2026.

Key open questions:
  • When will unsupervised FSD receive regulatory approval in any major market?
  • HW5/AI5 dependency vs current HW4 fleet capability
  • Long-term impact of China naming/positioning change
TST confidence on major timelines: medium

HW5 / AI5 + Dojo

Not yet deeply covered on the show

Next-generation inference hardware in active development. Expected to deliver a major leap in capability for both unsupervised FSD and Optimus training/inference.

Key open questions:
  • Performance multiplier vs current HW4
  • Production timeline and vehicle integration path
  • Dojo supercomputer scaling and training throughput
TST confidence on major timelines: medium

Next-Gen / Affordable EV (Redwood)

Last deep coverage: Ep 470 (2026-05-05)

Development program ongoing with aggressive cost-reduction targets. Still in pre-production; no firm customer launch window announced.

Key open questions:
  • Final vehicle price target (sub-$30k equivalent or better)
  • Launch timing and initial geographic markets
  • Battery chemistry, manufacturing approach, and structural integration
TST confidence on major timelines: low

4680 Cells + Structural Battery Pack

Last deep coverage: Ep 478 (2026-05-18)

Production scaling at Giga Texas and Nevada. Structural pack already shipping in some vehicles. Cell yield, cost curve, and energy density continue to improve.

Key open questions:
  • Cost per kWh trajectory and path to internal targets
  • Energy density gains in future 4680 generations
  • Volume impact on Cybertruck and future vehicle platforms
TST confidence on major timelines: medium
