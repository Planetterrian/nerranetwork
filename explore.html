Nerra Daily
Hosted by Mira Daily ~2 hours
Nerra Daily is the Nerra Network's combined daily edition. Every morning, once the day's English shows have published, Mira assembles them into one continuous listen, in a fixed order: SpaceX Daily, Tesla Shorts Time, Fascinating Frontiers, Models & Agents, Planetterrian Daily and Omni View lead, then Modern Investing Techniques, the narrative shows and the beginner track, closing on The DP Pod's good news. On Mondays the weekly shows join the rundown, and midway through Mira adds a short field note of her own. Every segment is the full, unabridged episode from that show's own feed; what Nerra Daily adds is the through-line — Mira's introductions, the connections between stories, and one place to hear it all. Subscribe to Nerra Daily for everything, or pick up any individual show from its own feed at nerranetwork.com.
For: Listeners who want the entire Nerra Network — news, markets, science and ideas — in a single daily episode
Sources: Every English Nerra Network show, unabridged, Chapter markers per segment, Anchored by Mira (The Age of AI)

First Principles Daily
Daily ~10-12 min
A daily narrative podcast that takes one idea seriously: most of the world runs on reasoning by analogy, and reasoning from first principles — building up from raw materials and physics — is how the biggest leaps actually happen. Episodes alternate between a concrete example of this thinking in action — historical breakthroughs like the moving assembly line, Bessemer steel, and the shipping container, modern cost curves like solar and batteries, and occasionally one of Musk's teams — and a deep look at an industry whose Idiot Index is begging to be attacked.
For: engineers, builders, founders, and the endlessly curious — anyone who wants to understand WHY something costs what it costs and where the next 10x is hiding.
Sources: Primary engineering accounts, Company technical disclosures, Reputable trade and science press, First-principles cost reasoning

Offshore North
Weekly — Mondays ~12 min
Offshore North is the Nerra Network's weekly guide to offshore ocean racing. One of the great endurance sports on earth is reported almost entirely in French, which leaves English-speaking fans with the results but not the meaning. Every Monday, Dan closes that gap: what Canada Ocean Racing did on the road to the Vendée Globe and what it actually changes, the week in the wider IMOCA fleet — races, new boats, rule changes, and the money behind all of it — and Plain Sailing, one concept explained properly for people who love this sport without living inside it. Sources named in every episode and linked in the notes. No hype, no play-by-play, and nothing stated that the sources do not support.
For: English-speaking offshore racing fans, Canadian sailors and club racers, and anyone who watched a Vendée Globe documentary and wanted to keep following the sport afterward
Sources: Canada Ocean Racing, IMOCA class + race organizers, Tip & Shaft, Scuttlebutt, Sail-World
