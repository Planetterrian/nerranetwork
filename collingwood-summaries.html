Ep 3: Collingwood voters gained internet ballots at ten Thursday, ahead of October twenty-six.
Download Read transcript

Collingwood Weekly

Collingwood voters gained internet ballots at ten Thursday, ahead of October twenty-six.

What You Need to Know: Collingwood voters gained internet ballots at ten Thursday, ahead of October twenty-six, and paper ballots are planned at more than one point through the rest of the month. Clearview’s window opens Monday, October 12, and runs to October 26, online, by phone, or at an Election Services Centre, using the voter information letters mailed in September. A Simcoe County Transit Service Delivery Review describes a split county-and-town network, Collingwood marked Probus Month for more than 2,300 residents in 10 local clubs, and the County of Simcoe says the Simcoe County Museum stays open Thanksgiving Monday while the archives close.

The Week's Stories

Voting starts today in Collingwood. Here's what you need to know: CollingwoodToday

CollingwoodToday reports that internet voting in the town opened at 10 a.m. on October 8. The same account says multiple paper voting options are planned throughout the month, so a paper ballot is not limited to a single day. Election day is October 26. The opening of internet voting on October 8 is the dated start of the town’s voting period that runs to that election day. Paper options sit alongside the internet ballot for the remainder of the month. Residents who want a paper ballot have those planned options before October 26, and residents who want the internet ballot have had that route since 10 a.m. Thursday. Source: [collingwoodtoday.ca](https://www.collingwoodtoday.ca/2026-municipal-election-news/voting-starts-today-in-collingwood-heres-what-you-need-to-know-12856541)

Councillors push for expanded transit links across Simcoe County: CollingwoodToday

CollingwoodToday reports that councillors are pushing for expanded transit links across Simcoe County. The push follows a new Simcoe County Transit Service Delivery Review. That review says the region’s mix of county and municipal transit systems is fragmented. The review names three results of that split: gaps in coverage, separate fare structures, and inconsistent accessibility standards. Coverage gaps are the places the mixed systems do not reach as one network. Separate fare structures mean a rider can meet a different fare when the trip crosses from a county service to a municipal service, or the other way. Inconsistent accessibility standards mean the access rules are not the same from one of those systems to the next. The councillors’ push, as CollingwoodToday frames it, is for links that reach farther across the county than that fragmented mix now does. On October 2 this show was still on county roads and services as an open question; today’s new fact is the review’s finding of fragmentation, named with those three results, and the councillors’ push for wider links. Source: [collingwoodtoday.ca](https://www.collingwoodtoday.ca/local-news/councillors-push-for-expanded-transit-links-across-simcoe-county-12867312)

Probus month marked with flag raising in Collingwood: CollingwoodToday

Collingwood marked Probus Month with a flag raising, CollingwoodToday reports. More than 2,300 Collingwood-area residents are part of the 10 local clubs. The flag raising is the public mark of the month in town. The membership figure is the Collingwood-area total across those 10 clubs, not a count for a single club. The 10 clubs are the local set that holds that membership. The month being marked is Probus Month, and the place of the flag raising is Collingwood. Source: [collingwoodtoday.ca](https://www.collingwoodtoday.ca/local-news/probus-month-marked-with-flag-raising-in-collingwood-12872630)

Council & County

Clearview: Vote online, by phone or at an Election Services Centre Oct. 12 to 26: BarrieToday

BarrieToday reports that Clearview electors can vote online, by phone, or at an Election Services Centre from October 12 to October 26. That is a 15-day window that starts Monday and ends on the same election day Collingwood uses, October 26. Voter information letters mailed in September have everything electors need to vote. Those letters include voting credentials. They include the voting website. They include the telephone number. They include the credentials needed to cast a vote. An elector using the internet uses the website and the credentials in that September letter. An elector using the phone uses the telephone number and the credentials in that letter. An elector who wants a staffed place uses an Election Services Centre inside the October 12 to 26 window. The three routes — online, phone, and an Election Services Centre — are all open across that same stretch of days. Source: [barrietoday.com](https://www.barrietoday.com/2026-municipal-election-news/clearview-vote-online-by-phone-or-at-an-election-services-centre-oct-12-to-26-12868041)

The Weekend

Simcoe County Museum to open Thanksgiving Monday while archives close: BarrieToday

BarrieToday reports that the County of Simcoe is advising residents of service and facility schedule changes for Thanksgiving Day on Monday. The Simcoe County Museum will be open that Monday. The archives will be closed that Monday. The county’s advice covers service changes and facility schedule changes for the holiday, and the museum-open, archives-closed split is the change the report names. Thanksgiving Day this year falls on Monday, and that Monday is the day the museum stays open while the archives do not. Source: [barrietoday.com](https://www.barrietoday.com/local-news/simcoe-county-museum-to-open-thanksgiving-monday-while-archives-close-12870029)

Both Sides: Wasaga Beachwood Road housing

Wasaga Beach council approved amendments for a housing project on Beachwood Road after reassurance that flooding issues will be resolved. Simcoe.com reports the project is for 214 units. The developer will give land to the town for a channel to mitigate flooding. Staff found the project consistent with planning policies. The strongest case for the amendments is that staff finding: the project fits the planning policies already in place, and the land for a channel is the step offered to deal with flooding.

Residents raised concerns about environmental degradation, and a conservation authority raised concerns about environmental degradation as well. Nearly 2,800 people signed a petition against the plan. Some councillors had previously signed a petition opposing the project. The strongest case against it is that environmental concern, made by residents and by the conservation authority, alongside a petition of nearly 2,800 people and the fact that some councillors had themselves signed on against the plan.

The fact sitting with both arguments is the Beachwood Road project itself: 214 units, a staff finding that it is consistent with planning policies, and a petition against it that drew nearly 2,800 signatures. Council approved the amendments after reassurance that flooding issues will be resolved. The developer will give the town land for a channel to mitigate flooding. Source: [simcoe.com](https://www.simcoe.com/news/council/wasaga-beach-romanin-flooding/article_bc6c2a1d-a96d-5ce5-8d25-da8263e0ea54.html)

Ep 1: Police captured a bull moose in Collingwood and will move him north.

Collingwood Weekly

Police captured a bull moose in Collingwood and will move him north.

What You Need to Know: On Tuesday, police, firefighters and provincial staff tranquilized a young bull moose at Collingwood’s waterfront and loaded him for a move farther north. Simcoe County is planning a one-day controlled burn on about 5 hectares in the Nottawasaga Tract in Clearview, as early as late September. Highway 26 has nightly full closures in Clearview, and no weather warnings are in effect.

What This Show Covers

Every Friday morning, the week in Collingwood and the south Georgian Bay towns — Wasaga Beach, Clearview and The Blue Mountains — is gathered for year-round residents, weekenders, seasonal workers, business owners and parents who want to know what the town, the county and the bay did, and what the weekend holds. That means council and county decisions on budget, development, water, roads and services; the local economy when a number is attached; public safety from a named police or fire source; local sport results; and events a source names with a date and a place. Roads and weather come only from Ontario 511 and Environment Canada. When a local issue is contested, each side is given in its strongest form, best reason first, attributed by name, so a listener cannot tell which side the show prefers.

The round-up is read by Mira, the network's AI host. She says so. She does not claim to live here, to have skied the hill, walked the harbour or attended a meeting. She speaks as a well-informed host who read the week's local news so you do not have to. The stories are chosen and written by software from named local newsrooms and public data, and every item names its source. The one thing it will not do is speculate about people — no guesses about anyone's motives, guilt, health or private life.

Collingwood Weekly is part of the Nerra Network, an independent, ad-free network of shows at nerranetwork.com. You are welcome to look around if another show would be useful to someone you know.

The Week's Stories

Bull moose tranquilized in Collingwood, taken away in trailer: BarrieToday

After about three days of sightings reported to Collingwood and The Blue Mountains OPP, a young bull moose was located Tuesday morning in Harbourview Park and tranquilized by Bear Wise staff in the Arboretum at about noon. He ran into the water by the bridge at Harbourview Park, east of the Awen Gathering Circle, and collapsed from the dart. Bear Wise staff and OPP went into the water to pull him to shore; Collingwood firefighters and more officers, including detachment commander Insp. Loris Licharson, then loaded him on a plastic sled and used a winch to pull him up the bank into a trailer. Police nicknamed him Elliot. Bear Wise staff, who came from Midhurst, will take him farther north. Naturalist David Hawke, on scene with CollingwoodToday, confirmed it was a bull moose and said he had also been spotted in Thornbury the day before. Source: [barrietoday.com](https://www.barrietoday.com/local-news/bull-moose-tranquilized-in-collingwood-taken-away-in-trailer-12808515)

County of Simcoe to conduct controlled burn in Clearview: County of Simcoe

The County of Simcoe will conduct a controlled burn on about 5 hectares, or 12.5 acres, in the County Forest Nottawasaga Tract at 793455 County Road 124 in Clearview. It may start as early as conditions allow in late September or early October, is expected to take one day, and was delayed from spring 2026 by weather. The county says the burn responds to debris and brush left after salvage of trees damaged in the 2025 ice storm, so the site can be reached for reforestation; the property will be replanted and stay part of the county’s 34,000 acres of municipal forest. Lands and Forests Consulting is contracted to run the burn. Smoke is to disperse to the northeast, east or southeast, and possibly into the next day; the county says it does not anticipate road closures. Residents with asthma or chronic lung disease who live near the burn may wish to stay indoors, and anyone with health concerns should follow their health care provider. Source: [simcoe.ca](https://simcoe.ca/featured-news/county-of-simcoe-to-conduct-controlled-burn-in-clearview-2/)

Collingwood man arrested for armed robbery of a Circle K: CollingwoodToday

A 32-year-old Collingwood man was arrested on September 20. He is charged in connection with an armed robbery of a Circle K in Collingwood. Source: [collingwoodtoday.ca](https://www.collingwoodtoday.ca/police-beat/collingwood-man-arrested-for-armed-robbery-of-a-circle-k-12807033)
