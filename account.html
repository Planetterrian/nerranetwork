          Edition length

          When an edition would run long, Mira trims later stories from the end of your longest shows — only at a clean story break, never a show's lead story, never a whole show — and tells you what she trimmed. If it can't fit by those rules, it runs long. Every edition also remembers what you heard, so a story you already know comes back as an update.
