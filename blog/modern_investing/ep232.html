Strategy Spotlight

Tax-loss harvesting with ETFs lets investors realize capital losses to offset gains while keeping equivalent market exposure through a similar but not identical fund. The Barron's report shows this approach generated billions in reported tax losses last year by swapping holdings such as broad-market ETFs that track nearly identical indexes. An investor opens a taxable account at a Canadian broker, identifies two ETFs with overlapping holdings but different tickers, sells the loser to book the loss, then immediately buys the replacement. The step works best when the price difference is small and the swap happens inside the same trading day to avoid market drift. It has produced consistent tax savings in years with mixed equity returns but fails when both ETFs move in lockstep or when superficial-loss rules in Canada disallow the claim within 30 days. The technique requires tracking adjusted cost base in the account software and confirming the replacement ETF meets the same risk profile before execution. The approach is particularly useful for Canadian investors holding US-listed ETFs in non-registered accounts where capital gains are taxable at the inclusion rate. The billions in losses reported last year demonstrate the scale at which institutions and individuals apply this swap technique during volatile periods. When executing the trade, the investor must ensure the replacement ETF has similar beta and sector exposure to maintain the intended market position without triggering wash-sale rules in the US or superficial loss rules in Canada. Source: barrons.com

Investor Education: How Earnings Guidance Updates Shift Valuation Narratives

You hold Elanco Animal Health after its third-quarter revenue of US$1,265 million and net income of US$18 million; the company then raises full-year 2026 revenue guidance to the US$5,155 million to US$5,180 million range. The mechanism works because the new bracket becomes the operational yardstick analysts use to judge pipeline execution, margin progress, and foreign-exchange resilience over the next three quarters. When guidance rises, the bull case narrative tightens around higher innovation revenue and cleaner earnings conversion, yet the same update also highlights remaining risks such as swine-market volatility or pricing pressure that could still offset the top-line lift. A pro habit is to compare the updated range directly against the prior consensus midpoint within 24 hours of the release and note any narrowing of the band. The common misconception is that a guidance raise automatically lifts the stock multiple; the fix is to check whether the new range also tightens the risk factors listed in the same release before adjusting position size. The shift from a prior year quarterly loss to positive earnings combined with the higher full year revenue expectations highlights changing fundamentals that may affect how investors assess Elanco Animal Health’s earnings quality, cost discipline and product portfolio execution. The focus now turns to how Elanco Animal Health’s improved profitability and higher full year revenue guidance may influence its broader investment narrative. The biggest near term catalyst remains execution on the Big 6 portfolio and wider pet and farm franchises converting into cleaner earnings and cash flow.
