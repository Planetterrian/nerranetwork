Strategy Spotlight

Valuation screening identifies stocks trading below estimated fair value using discounted cash flow models and peer multiples. Today's market conditions make this relevant because several Canadian retailers reported earnings beats that widened the gap between current prices and analyst-derived fair values. To implement it, open a screener on your brokerage platform, filter for companies with positive earnings surprises and cash-rich balance sheets, then compare the last close to the reported fair value estimate. This approach worked well during the twenty twenty two rate-hike cycle when depressed multiples provided a buffer against further declines. Risks include model assumptions that prove too optimistic if revenue growth slows. For a TFSA holder, apply the screen to names already on your watchlist and size positions at one thousand dollars to maintain discipline. Investor Education: The Dot-Com Bubble Lessons for AI Investors

Imagine you bought a high-growth tech name last week after an AI-related announcement and watched it gap higher on volume. The mechanism that actually determines whether that move sustains is the difference between revenue-backed cash flow and pure narrative multiples, exactly as it was in nineteen ninety nine when many internet stocks traded at hundreds of times sales with no profits. A pro insight is to check the ratio of enterprise value to next-twelve-months revenue against the sector median before adding to a position; if the multiple sits more than fifty percent above peers with similar growth, the setup carries extra downside. The concrete misconception to avoid is assuming every AI company will compound like the survivors; the fix is to demand visible revenue and free-cash-flow traction before treating the name as a long-term holding. Survivors such as Amazon maintained real revenue streams while many others collapsed when funding dried up. Today's AI names differ because most already generate measurable sales, yet the valuation discipline lesson remains identical. Investors who applied the same check in twenty twenty two avoided the sharpest drawdowns in unprofitable growth names.
