Strategy Spotlight

Breadth readings act as an early filter for whether a market move has participation or is just a narrow handful of names carrying the index. Today's flip from negative to less-negative breadth on a down day for the S&P shows how sector rotation can produce quick reversals in leadership even when headline indices finish lower. The S&P itself fell 0.48 percent, yet the number of stocks beating the index rose from 30 on Friday to 53 on Monday while laggards fell from 115 to 74, moving net breadth from -85 to -21. Investors can track this by comparing the number of stocks above their 20-day average to those below it, or by watching the advance-decline line on the same platform that supplies their charts. The approach works best when a single session's breadth swing exceeds recent averages, as it did Monday, because that signals capital is rotating rather than exiting. Risk appears when breadth improves but volume stays below the 20-day average, leaving the move vulnerable to quick reversal. Platforms such as TradingView or Thinkorswim let users overlay breadth indicators directly on index charts for daily checks. Canadian investors can apply the same filter inside a TFSA by screening TSX names for the same advance-decline differential before committing new capital. Source: x.com

Investor Education: Naming Your Invalidation Before You Buy

Imagine you bought a data-centre name last week after it posted record quarterly sales and raised guidance. Your order filled at the open, yet the position is already down 4% because the broader tech rotation you assumed would support it never arrived. The mechanism is simple: without a written invalidation, every dip gets rationalized as noise until the loss grows large enough to force an exit that hindsight then labels a mistake. Professionals write the exact observable trigger first—such as “if the next two sessions close below the 50-day moving average on volume above the 20-day average, the rotation thesis is dead”—then set the stop at that level. Most retail investors skip this step and later describe the loss only as “it went down,” which prevents learning what actually broke. The concrete fix is to type the invalidation sentence into the same note where you record the entry thesis before the order is placed. This practice converts a loss from an emotional event into a data point about which assumption failed. It also forces the investor to define success and failure in observable market terms rather than in feelings about the name. Over repeated trades the habit reveals patterns in which types of invalidation trigger most often, allowing the investor to refine entry criteria before the next position is sized.

Профессионалы сначала записывают точный наблюдаемый триггер — например, если следующие две сессии закроются ниже пятидесятидневной скользящей средней на объёме выше двадцатидневной средней, — и ставят стоп на этом уровне.

Большинство розничных инвесторов пропускают этот шаг и потом описывают убыток только словами «оно упало», что мешает понять, что именно сломалось.

Конкретное решение — напечатать фразу с инвалидацией в той же заметке, где вы фиксируете тезис входа, ещё до того, как заявка будет выставлена.

Эта практика превращает убыток из эмоционального события в точку данных о том, какое допущение не сработало.

Она также заставляет инвестора определять успех и неудачу в наблюдаемых рыночных терминах, а не в чувствах к бумаге.

На повторных сделках привычка выявляет закономерности: какие типы инвалидации срабатывают чаще всего, — и позволяет уточнять критерии входа до того, как будет задан размер следующей позиции.
