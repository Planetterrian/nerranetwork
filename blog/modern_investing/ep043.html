Strategy Spotlight

The barbell portfolio pairs a concentrated set of high-conviction growth holdings with a sleeve of defensive, income-generating names to perform across market regimes. Today’s environment of elevated oil prices and selective AI infrastructure spending makes the approach relevant because it limits drawdowns when momentum fades while still capturing upside in data-center and networking names. Implementation starts by allocating roughly 60% to eight core positions—typically four growth and four defensive—then rebalancing quarterly using a simple moving-average filter on each sleeve. Historical data shows the structure delivered lower volatility than pure equity benchmarks during 2022’s rate-hike cycle while still participating in the subsequent recovery. The main risk is over-concentration in the growth leg if sector rotation stalls, so position sizing should respect a 5% single-name cap inside the TFSA. Platforms such as Wealthsimple or Questrade make the rebalancing step straightforward with low-cost limit orders. Source: bnnbloomberg.ca

Investor Education: How Insurance Float Powers Compounding at Scale

Imagine you bought a Berkshire Hathaway position last quarter after reading about its insurance operations. Your shares were acquired at the prevailing market price, yet the real engine behind the long-term return wasn’t the equity portfolio alone—it was the float generated by premiums collected upfront and invested for years before claims were paid. In 2025 the float exceeded $170 billion, effectively providing Berkshire with interest-free leverage that amplifies every percentage point of investment return. Most retail investors overlook this because they focus only on the stock holdings rather than the liability side of the balance sheet. The pro tip is to scan the footnotes of any insurance-heavy company’s 10-K for the “float” line item and compare its growth rate to the investment yield; when float expands faster than claims, the compounding advantage widens. The biggest misconception is treating float as free money without duration risk. Instead, always check the duration of the insurance liabilities before assuming the structure will work at smaller scales.

Tools & Techniques

TradingView Volume Profile Indicator: TradingView

The volume profile tool overlays historical volume at each price level so investors can identify high-volume nodes that often act as support or resistance. It gives an edge by revealing where institutional accumulation occurred rather than relying solely on moving averages. Active traders on the free or paid tiers can add it to any chart via the Indicators menu and set alerts at key nodes. Source: bnnbloomberg.ca

Portfolio Visualizer Backtest Module: Portfolio Visualizer

Users can upload custom portfolios and run rolling-window backtests against the NASDAQ to measure alpha under different regimes. The free tier supports up to ten assets and shows maximum drawdown and Sharpe ratios, helping TFSA holders stress-test barbell allocations before committing capital. Source: marketwatch.com

Quick Hits

Cerebras Shares Slip After IPO Debut

Shares of the AI chip designer fell in early trading following Thursday’s strong debut, illustrating how quickly momentum can fade once initial hype is absorbed.

Action: Avoid adding new AI hardware exposure until the stock stabilizes above its first-week low with rising volume. Source: cnbc.com

Clean Energy Viewed as Shock-Resistant

Corporate leaders noted that renewables face fewer supply disruptions than oil-linked assets amid ongoing geopolitical tensions.

Action: Add a broad clean-energy ETF such as ICLN to the defensive sleeve of any barbell allocation this quarter. Source: financialpost.com

Magnum Ice Cream Spikes on Takeover Speculation

The newly independent ice-cream maker jumped 18% on reports of private-equity interest just six months after its Unilever spin-off.

Action: Place a limit order 5% below today’s close to capture any post-rumor pullback while keeping position size under 2% of the TFSA. Source: cnbc.com

Chevron vs. TotalEnergies Valuation Gap

The two energy majors trade at different multiples despite similar upstream exposure, creating a relative-value opportunity.

Action: Consider swapping a portion of TotalEnergies into Chevron inside the RRSP if the valuation discount persists into next week. Source: fool.com
