Strategy Spotlight

Earnings beats in high-profile names like Nvidia often trigger immediate price pops, yet the real edge comes from requiring volume confirmation above the 20-day average before committing capital. Today’s market shows exactly why: without that filter, capital can rotate into higher-beta AI plays and leave earlier positions behind even when fundamentals remain solid. Implementation is straightforward on any platform with volume data—pull up the daily chart, compare today’s share volume to the 20-day average, and only size the position if the beat coincides with at least 150% of average turnover. This approach worked best during the 2023-2025 AI expansion when volume spikes reliably preceded multi-week runs; it fails when volume is light because the move is driven by short covering rather than new institutional buying. The recurring lesson from recent rotations is that multi-year visibility alone cannot protect against sector rotation—volume is the missing gatekeeper. Source: bnnbloomberg.ca

Investor Education: Canadian Discount Brokerages Compared

Imagine you just spotted a U.S. quantum-computing name you want to add to your TFSA on a dip. You click buy at market open and receive a fill 18 cents worse than the last quoted price because your broker’s routing added extra latency and a wider spread. That friction compounds across dozens of trades and is the exact reason brokerage choice matters more for active investors than most realize. Wealthsimple Trade offers commission-free trades and seamless TFSA/RRSP integration but limits advanced order types and real-time Level 2 data. Questrade charges low commissions with free ETF purchases and solid U.S. market access, making it the default for most intermediate Canadians who want both cost control and basic options. Interactive Brokers delivers the lowest margin rates and direct market access yet requires comfort with a more complex platform and occasional currency conversion fees. Bank-owned platforms such as TD Direct Investing or RBC Direct Investing bundle everything inside your existing banking app at the cost of higher commissions and slower execution. The biggest misconception is assuming “free trades” automatically means the best net outcome; instead, always compare total all-in cost including data fees, FX spreads, and order-routing quality before funding the account.
