Strategy Spotlight

Geopolitical premium investing involves overweighting assets that benefit from policy shifts such as tariffs or critical-mineral demand rather than broad market beta. Today’s proposed 10-12.5% duties on 60 trading partners and the Canadian antimony discovery highlight how quickly supply-chain security can reprice entire sectors. Investors can implement this by screening TSX-listed miners and processors for non-Chinese sources of defence-critical inputs, then sizing positions at 3-5% of a TFSA to limit single-name risk. The approach has historically delivered during trade-war periods when domestic content rules tighten, but it carries elevated volatility if diplomatic tensions ease. Use free government mineral lists and company filings to confirm jurisdiction before entry. Always layer in volume confirmation above the 20-day average before scaling. Source: bnnbloomberg.ca

Investor Education: How to Read an Earnings Report

Imagine you bought a healthcare name last week after it announced an overseas product launch. Your order filled at the open, but the real driver was whether management’s forward guidance exceeded consensus and whether free cash flow actually covered the expansion spend. Revenue growth shows top-line momentum while EPS reveals how efficiently costs are managed; a company can post higher revenue yet still miss on EPS if margins compress. Check the balance sheet for rising long-term debt relative to cash and compare operating cash flow to reported net income to spot aggressive accounting. The biggest mistake retail investors make is fixating only on the headline beat or miss. Instead, always read the management commentary section for explicit language on guidance changes and capital allocation before deciding to add or trim.
