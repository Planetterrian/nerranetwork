Strategy Spotlight

Valuation investing identifies companies trading below their intrinsic worth based on metrics like return on equity and capital allocation strength, then holds until the gap closes or fundamentals deteriorate. Hannover Re reported a 21.1 percent return on equity in its latest quarter on strong profitability, showing how reinsurance operators can compound value when underwriting discipline holds. The firm achieved this RoE through disciplined risk selection that kept combined ratios in check even as premium volumes grew. Screen for names with RoE above 15 percent and stable combined ratios using your broker's fundamental scanner, then compare the current price-to-book ratio against the five-year average to quantify the discount. This approach worked well in prior rate-cycle peaks when excess capital was redeployed into share buybacks rather than new risk. It fails when catastrophe losses spike faster than reserves, as seen in earlier industry drawdowns where RoE dropped below 10 percent for multiple quarters. Apply the screen inside a TFSA to defer tax on any re-rating gains while monitoring quarterly reserve releases for early warning signs of deterioration. Canadian investors can run the same screen on TSX-listed insurers to capture similar capital-allocation advantages without currency conversion costs. Source: investing.com

Investor Education: How National-Team ETF Buying Affects Liquidity

You hold a broad-market ETF in your TFSA and notice the bid-ask spread tightening sharply on a day when Chinese state funds report record purchases. The mechanism works because large, coordinated ETF inflows from government-linked vehicles absorb sell-side supply in one block, narrowing spreads and reducing intraday volatility for retail orders that follow. In the most recent week those funds deployed the largest single-week total on record, lifting the underlying index without requiring individual stock-by-stock accumulation. The coordinated buying creates a temporary liquidity buffer that allows smaller orders to execute closer to the mid-price rather than at the wider ask. The pro habit is to check the previous session's ETF flow data before market open using your platform's news feed; if flows exceed the prior 20-day average, tighten limit orders by one cent to capture the improved liquidity. This check takes less than two minutes on most Canadian broker dashboards and prevents paying an extra spread when the national-team bid is active. The common misconception is that all volume spikes reflect organic investor demand; the fix is to cross-reference flow reports before assuming the move will persist once the coordinated buying pauses. When the national-team purchases slow, spreads can widen again within a single session, catching traders who entered on the artificial liquidity without checking the source of the volume. Source: bloomberg.com

Tools & Techniques

Interactive Brokers Option Chains

The scanner surfaces covered-call premiums on holdings with yields above 15 percent annualized for 30-day expiries, giving income-focused accounts a concrete yield target. Log in, select the underlying, and filter for open interest above 500 contracts to ensure liquidity. This feature helps quantify opportunity cost on flat positions inside an RRSP. Investors can export the premium data into a spreadsheet to compare annualized yields across multiple holdings before deciding which name to write calls against. Source: bloomberg.com

Quick Hits

Bitcoin dips to $74k as regulatory uncertainty, ETF outflows weigh

Regulatory overhang and outflows are pressuring the largest crypto holding, increasing correlation risk with risk-off equity moves. Bitcoin fell to its lowest level since mid-August as ETF redemptions accelerated and policy clarity remained absent. The move raises the chance that crypto allocations inside registered accounts will continue to lag traditional risk assets until flow data stabilizes.

Action: Reduce any single-name crypto allocation to under 5 percent of TFSA risk budget until ETF flow data turns positive. Source: investing.com

Norway Halts Power Connections for New Data Centers

The suspension removes near-term electricity supply for additional AI infrastructure projects, slowing one growth driver for power and equipment suppliers. Norway cited grid-capacity constraints as the reason for pausing new reservations, directly affecting data-center operators planning European expansion. The decision adds to political and infrastructure hurdles already pressuring AI-related capital spending.

Action: Review any utility or data-center supply-chain holdings for exposure to European grid constraints and trim if over 3 percent of portfolio. Source: bloomberg.com

Ardian Raises $1 Billion for Global Semiconductor Investments

A new dedicated vehicle signals continued institutional capital allocation to the sector despite recent volatility. The French private-equity firm closed the fund targeting chip-related companies worldwide, providing a fresh pool of long-term capital that could support equipment and materials suppliers. The size of the commitment offers a benchmark for how much patient capital remains available even after the recent selloff.

Action: Use the commitment size as a benchmark when screening for listed semiconductor equipment names with similar long-term contracts. Source: globalbankingandfinance.com

Kingspan shares fall as core insulation sales miss estimates, guidance unchanged

The miss highlights cyclical pressure on construction materials even as guidance holds. Core insulation revenue came in below analyst forecasts while the company left its full-year outlook unchanged, suggesting management sees the shortfall as temporary. The share-price reaction creates a potential entry point for investors willing to wait for housing-market stabilization.

Action: Add the name to a watchlist for potential valuation entry if price-to-sales falls another 10 percent from current levels. Source: sg.finance.yahoo.com
