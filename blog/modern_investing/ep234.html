Strategy Spotlight

Dividend-income investors can screen for sustainable payouts by checking consecutive years of increases alongside cash-flow coverage from regulated or contracted assets. TC Energy reported 26 straight years of dividend growth and 98 percent of earnings from regulated assets or long-term take-or-pay contracts in its latest quarter, supporting the current $0.88 quarterly payout and 3.8 percent forward yield. The company placed $2.5 billion of projects into service during the first nine months while daily average flows rose 6 percent on Canadian pipelines, 3 percent on U.S. pipelines, and 8 percent on Mexican pipelines. Adjusted net income reached $1.1 billion or $0.94 per share, up 22.1 percent from the year-ago quarter, and adjusted EBITDA climbed 11.1 percent to $3 billion. To apply the screen, open your brokerage’s stock screener, filter TSX energy-infrastructure names for at least 20 years of dividend growth and adjusted EBITDA growth above 10 percent year-over-year, then review the most recent quarterly report for the percentage of earnings from contracted sources. The approach worked when TC Energy placed $2.5 billion of projects into service in the first nine months while flows rose 6 percent on its Canadian pipelines. It can fail when contract renewals come in at lower rates or when a large project faces cost overruns that pressure coverage ratios, as seen in prior infrastructure names during 2022 rate-hike volatility. Investor Education: Naming Your Invalidation Before You Buy

You bought MP Materials after its November 5 earnings beat, expecting the 41 percent year-over-year rise in neodymium-praseodymium production to drive further gains, yet the stock could still fall if fourth-quarter sold tons drop below the guided 1,000 metric tons. The mechanism at work is that an invalidation is a specific, observable threshold—here a quarterly sales figure—that proves the original thesis incorrect, rather than a vague price drop. MP Materials ended the quarter with $1.34 billion in cash and equivalents while guiding capital expenditures for the full year between $500 million and $600 million. Professional traders write the exact condition down before entry so the stop-loss can be derived directly from it and so post-trade review focuses on whether the data point actually arrived. One concrete habit that separates informed investors is checking the company’s own guidance table in the earnings release for the precise metric and quarter that would falsify the case. The common misconception is treating any decline as proof the thesis failed; the fix is stating the measurable event that must occur inside the holding window and exiting only if it does. NdPr production reached 1,018 metric tons in the third quarter while sales hit 1,020 metric tons, and the company expects fourth-quarter sold volumes to stay near 1,000 metric tons at roughly $100 per kilogram.

Quick Hits

India may limit leveraged stock bets

Regulators are considering tighter rules on leveraged equity positions to reduce volatility, which could dampen short-term trading volumes in Indian ADRs held by Canadian investors. The proposal targets excessive speculative activity that has contributed to recent market swings.

Action: Review any India-focused ETF exposure in your TFSA and consider trimming positions larger than 5 percent of the account.

ARK Invest buys 378,515 SpaceX shares

The purchase adds to ARK’s private-space allocation, signaling continued institutional interest in pre-IPO names that sometimes appear later via special-purpose vehicles. The transaction occurred on the same day multiple other private-market moves were reported across growth funds.

Action: If you hold ARKK or ARKX in a registered account, note the increased private weighting and rebalance if it exceeds your target allocation.

UK 30-year bonds attract retail buyers

UK retail investors are locking in yields above 5 percent in long-dated gilts, illustrating how non-Canadian fixed-income markets can offer tax-efficient capital-gains potential inside a TFSA when currency-hedged versions become available. The buying follows a period of sharp price declines earlier in the year.

Action: Monitor Canadian bond ETF screens for any new hedged long-duration products that reach similar yields.

Jim Cramer’s take on RDDT

Cramer noted Google algorithm changes cut Reddit traffic, highlighting how platform-risk events can pressure ad-dependent names even without an earnings miss. He compared the situation to earlier experiences at TheStreet where similar changes halved traffic numbers.

Action: If RDDT sits in your portfolio, set a price alert at its 50-day moving average and review position size against sector concentration limits. Source: x.com
