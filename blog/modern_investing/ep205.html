Strategy Spotlight

Auto loan delinquencies have reached the highest level since 2010, with one in five Americans behind on at least one loan and the overall market now totaling $1.7 trillion, larger than credit-card balances as a share of household debt. The average age of vehicles on U.S. roads has climbed to a record of nearly 13 years, indicating that replacement purchases are being postponed because of elevated financing costs and tighter credit standards. These figures matter for portfolio construction because sustained high delinquencies have historically preceded slower consumer spending and reduced demand for discretionary goods, creating headwinds for retail and auto-related holdings. Canadian investors can track the trend by reviewing monthly New York Fed delinquency data releases and comparing them against sector weights in any U.S.-exposed consumer names held inside a TFSA. Defensive utilities and staples have tended to outperform during similar periods of consumer-credit stress, while financials tied to auto lending and discretionary retailers face greater downside risk. Position sizing in any name deriving more than 20% of revenue from U.S. auto financing should be reduced until the delinquency series shows consistent improvement. The pattern typically provides a three-to-six-month lead time before broader consumer weakness appears in earnings reports, allowing time for reallocation toward rate-sensitive but stable sectors such as utilities. Monitoring volume confirmation alongside these macro indicators helps avoid entering names already caught in sector rotation. Source: x.com

Investor Education: Core-Satellite ETF Portfolio Construction

Picture holding XEQT as the core holding inside a TFSA while allocating a 10% satellite position to a utilities-focused ETF; after a rally lifts the satellite allocation to 14%, the pre-set rebalancing rule requires trimming back to the original target weight. The mechanism delivers broad global equity exposure at low cost through the single core ticker while permitting targeted bets on themes such as grid modernization or preferred-share income without requiring investors to rebuild the entire portfolio. Rebalancing occurs quarterly or whenever any satellite exceeds its band by more than three percentage points, keeping taxable events minimal inside registered accounts where contribution room is already limited. In non-registered accounts the same drift threshold applies, yet investors must also observe the superficial-loss rule when realizing gains or losses inside the 30-day window around the trade. October distributions from Desjardins fixed-income and preferred-share ETFs, including TSX:DCU with payment scheduled at month-end for unitholders of record in late October, demonstrate how income-oriented satellites can be layered on top of the equity core without disrupting overall asset allocation. Pro insight: set a recurring quarterly calendar reminder to compare current weights against the original targets rather than reacting to short-term price fluctuations. The misconception to avoid is assuming every satellite position will remain permanently; once the original thesis, such as a specific phase of the rate-cut cycle, ceases to apply, the holding should be sold or folded back into the core allocation. Source: kalkinemedia.com

На незарегистрированном счёте тот же порог дрейфа всё равно действует, но вы ещё должны соблюдать правило поверхностного убытка по прибылям или убыткам внутри тридцатидневного окна вокруг сделки.

Октябрьский доход может сесть сверху на это долевое ядро, не раздувая веса: облигационные и привилегированные И-Ти-Эф Desjardins, включая тикер Ди-Си-Ю на Ти-Эс-Экс, должны выплатить в конце месяца пайщикам на дату фиксации в конце октября.

Поставьте повторяющееся квартальное напоминание в календарь и сравнивайте текущие веса с исходными целями, а не гонитесь за движением прошлой недели.

Заблуждение в том, что каждый сателлит — это бумага навсегда; как только исходный тезис истекает — например, конкретная фаза цикла снижения ставки — вы продаёте его или складываете обратно в ядро.
