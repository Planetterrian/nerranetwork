Market Pulse: The S&P 500 closed at 7,176, down 2.0 percent, while the NASDAQ finished at 23,879, down 2.3 percent. The TSX Composite settled at 34,029, down 1.6 percent after a 549-point decline. Markets sold off even as Anthropic closed its first day of trading at US$97.17, rising more than 17 percent above its IPO price in the largest public stock offering ever. The next Bank of Canada decision is scheduled for December 9, with markets pricing in a modest rate increase of 18 to 24 basis points. Tech and consumer discretionary sectors lagged as broader rotation pressure hit growth names amid the AI selloff that also sent Nvidia and AMD shares lower.

Strategy Spotlight

Covered-call writing lets an investor collect premium income on shares they already own or are willing to buy, capping upside in exchange for yield while the position is held. Article 16 shows CrowdStrike rising on a peer's strong earnings report even as the broader market fell, creating a setup where an investor comfortable owning the name could sell calls against it. To implement, screen for liquid options chains on US-listed tech names, select the nearest monthly expiry, and sell the out-of-the-money call whose premium equals roughly 1 percent of the underlying price. The approach has delivered positive alpha in the record when volume confirmation was present but has lagged when sector rotation accelerated without it. The risk is early assignment or a sharp rally that leaves the shares called away above the strike. Canadian investors can execute this inside a TFSA or RRSP through any broker offering US options, such as Interactive Brokers or Questrade, by first confirming the name meets the five-session hold criteria and then writing the call on the same day as the share purchase. When the peer earnings catalyst is confirmed and the broader index is declining, the premium provides a buffer that improves the matched-window outcome versus simply holding shares. The technique fails when the invalidation fires inside the window, such as negative revisions from the same peer group that originally lifted the sector. Source: fool.com

Investor Education: How Bond Spreads Reveal AI-Capex Risk

You bought shares of a hyperscaler supplier after strong order flow data, expecting continued multiple expansion, yet the position stalled as credit spreads on the sector's bonds widened 15 basis points in two sessions. That widening occurs because investors demand extra yield to hold the debt when they perceive higher execution risk in the data-center build-out, even if equity flows remain positive. The mechanism is visible in real time on the Bloomberg terminal or your broker's fixed-income screener: compare the option-adjusted spread of a named issuer's 10-year note against the Treasury curve and watch for daily changes above five basis points. A practical check is to pull the most recent 10-K risk-factor language on power and chip supply before sizing the equity position. The common misconception is that equity momentum alone confirms the capex story; spreads often move first because bondholders price refinancing risk that shareholders overlook until dilution or guidance cuts appear. Fed staff economists separately noted that aggregate productivity effects from generative AI remain small so far and that the recent pickup likely reflects factors predating its diffusion, which further tempers expectations for immediate earnings leverage from the build-out. Investors monitoring these spreads can therefore treat a sustained widening above ten basis points as an early warning to reduce exposure or add a hedge before equity prices fully reflect the higher cost of capital. Source: x.com

Tools & Techniques

Interactive Brokers TWS Scanner

The scanner lets users filter US options chains by premium yield, open interest, and implied volatility rank in a single view, surfacing covered-call candidates that meet liquidity thresholds before the market opens. Active Canadian investors running multiple $1,000 positions can set alerts for names already in the portfolio and export the results directly into position-sizing worksheets. The feature is available on the desktop platform at the Pro tier and requires no additional subscription beyond the standard commission schedule. Source: bnnbloomberg.ca

Wealthsimple Options Chain Viewer

Wealthsimple's chain viewer displays weekly expiries and probability of profit for covered calls on US names with a one-click import into the TFSA or RRSP account view. It highlights the exact premium and capital at risk for a given strike, removing the need to switch platforms for quick income calculations. The tool is free for all account holders and updates in real time during market hours. Source: fool.ca
