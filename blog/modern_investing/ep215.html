Strategy Spotlight

Rate-sensitivity screening measures how much a stock's valuation compresses when the policy rate rises by comparing its current yield to the new risk-free rate and testing whether free-cash-flow growth can offset the higher discount. Take SmartCentres REIT from today's coverage: it trades at a 6.8 percent annualized yield paid monthly with same-property NOI up 2.6 percent year-over-year and occupancy at 98.1 percent. Run the screen by pulling the distribution yield, subtracting the new 2.5 percent overnight rate, then checking whether the resulting spread exceeds the five-year average; if it does, the name passes the first filter. Next, verify that funds from operations per unit held steady or rose while debt metrics stayed inside policy limits. The technique flags names whose cash flows can absorb the higher rate without forcing a distribution cut. It has worked when growth in rental renewals outpaced the rate move, as seen in the 12 percent rent increase on non-anchor leases, but fails when occupancy slips and the spread narrows below the historical median. The REIT also renewed 86 percent of leases scheduled to expire in 2026, delivering an impressive 12 percent rent increase excluding anchor tenants. These renewal metrics provide the concrete growth offset needed to clear the higher discount rate. Investors can implement the screen inside a TFSA by exporting the yield and FFO figures from their brokerage statements into a simple spreadsheet that subtracts the Bank of Canada overnight rate and flags any spread below the trailing five-year median. Source: fool.ca

Investor Education: How Policy Rate Changes Alter Dividend Reinvestment Math

You hold a Canadian dividend stock inside a TFSA that just announced a 3 percent distribution increase while the Bank of Canada lifted the overnight rate to 2.5 percent. The reinvestment math shifts because the new risk-free rate raises the opportunity cost of holding the equity even though the TFSA itself remains tax-free. The mechanism works through the discount rate applied to future distributions: each incremental 25-basis-point hike increases the rate used to value those cash flows, so the same distribution stream now supports a lower present value unless growth accelerates enough to compensate. A practical check is to recalculate the implied internal rate of return on the position using the updated overnight rate plus your required equity premium; if the new IRR falls below your target, the position's weight in the TFSA may need trimming even though no tax is due. Professional managers run this test daily by feeding the revised rate into their models before deciding whether to add to the holding on the next distribution date. The common misconception is that tax-free status makes rate moves irrelevant inside a TFSA; the fix is to rerun the IRR calculation after every Bank of Canada decision using the new overnight rate as the floor. When the overnight rate moves from 2.25 percent to 2.5 percent, the equity risk premium required to justify the same holding rises by exactly that 25 basis points, narrowing the margin of safety on any name whose distribution growth is already modest. Canadian investors can perform the recalculation in under ten minutes by taking the current yield, adding the expected annual distribution growth rate, and subtracting the new 2.5 percent overnight rate plus a 5 percent equity premium. The resulting figure shows whether the position still clears the investor's minimum required return after the policy shift. This adjustment matters most for high-yield holdings whose growth rates sit close to the overnight rate itself, because those names lose the largest amount of present value when the discount rate ticks higher. Source: bankofcanada.ca

В день, когда ключевая ставка только что переставила планку, эти сквозные заявки — та лента, по которой вы читаете, готовы ли деньги по-прежнему переплачивать в бумагах, чувствительных к ставке, и в бумагах с двойным листингом.

Четырёхшаговый контрольный список сделки под отчётность требует подтвердить дату отчёта, направленный уклон, цены опционов относительно исторической волатильности и выход в худшем случае до любого входа.
