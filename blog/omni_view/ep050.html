1) Trump and Xi open Beijing summit with trade, Iran and AI on the table

What happened (neutral): US President Donald Trump arrived at the Great Hall of the People for an opening ceremony and one-hour face-to-face session with Chinese leader Xi Jinping. The talks are scheduled to cover international trade, artificial intelligence, and the war involving Iran. Security was tightened across the capital ahead of the visit.

Perspectives: Outlets such as The Guardian frame the encounter as a high-stakes reset between the two powers, noting that both leaders have described their long acquaintance and the need to manage flashpoints. Chinese state media, cited in the same reports, stress that mishandling Taiwan could lead to conflict, reflecting Beijing’s priority on sovereignty issues. US coverage highlights Trump’s emphasis on securing deals from major companies, showing how domestic political optics shape the American approach. Both sides agree the agenda includes concrete economic and security items; they differ on which topic deserves first attention.
