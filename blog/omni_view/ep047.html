1) Iran's stalled peace talks weigh on European markets and defense stocks

What happened (neutral): European stocks traded mixed on Monday as investors reacted to the latest developments in U.S.-Iran negotiations. Defense shares declined while broader indices showed limited movement. Markets are monitoring any signs of progress or further delays in the talks.

Perspectives: CNBC frames the session as investors calmly digesting negotiation updates without major panic selling. Other market observers note that defense stocks often move inversely to perceived de-escalation chances because lower conflict risk reduces demand for military equipment. Both sides agree the talks remain the central variable; they differ on whether current stalling signals temporary posturing or deeper obstacles to agreement.
