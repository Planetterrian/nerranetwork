Top News

  1. Falcon 9 achieves record 32-hour pad turnaround on NROL-136

    SpaceX launched a Falcon 9 from Vandenberg Space Force Base Launch Complex-4E at 4:23 a.m. local time on November 22 carrying the classified NROL-136 payload for the National Reconnaissance Office. The interval from the prior launch off the same pad measured exactly 32 hours, 3 minutes and 21 seconds, beating the previous 45-hour record set at Cape Canaveral Pad 40. Booster B1097 completed its 14th flight and landed at Landing Zone 4 roughly seven and a half minutes after liftoff. SpaceX vice president of launch Kiko Dontchev credited the West Coast team and set the next target at a 24-hour turnaround. The mission marked the NRO’s sixth launch of 2026 and the fifteenth flight supporting its proliferated-architecture satellite network. The NRO has not disclosed how many satellites flew on NROL-136 or their technical details. The proliferated architecture places numerous small satellites in low Earth orbit so that satellites can take turns observing locations and provide backup if others fail. The booster separated about two minutes after liftoff and returned to the launch base. Kiko Dontchev posted that the team finished preparations several hours ahead of schedule. The prior record of 45 hours had been set at Pad 40 in Florida. All six NRO launches in 2026 have used Falcon 9 rockets to deliver classified payloads into low Earth orbit. The NRO began deploying the constellation in May 2024 with NROL-146; NROL-179 launched on June 19, 2026. Source: technology.org Source: biz.chosun.com Source: demokratis.co.id

Engineering Deep Dive

The V-2 engine that powered the first large liquid-propellant ballistic missile delivered 245 kN of sea-level thrust at a chamber pressure of only 14–15 bar while burning LOX and 75 % ethanol. Its turbopump ran on steam generated by hydrogen-peroxide decomposition, a cycle that limited both efficiency and reusability. In contrast, today’s Raptor 3 achieves roughly ten times that thrust—2 452 kN sea-level—inside a full-flow staged-combustion cycle that routes all propellant through the turbines before they reach the main chamber. The design raises chamber pressure to 300–350 bar, raises specific impulse to approximately 330 s at sea level and 355 s in vacuum, and still keeps engine mass at 1 525 kg for a thrust-to-weight ratio near 164.

Those numbers matter because specific impulse and chamber pressure directly govern how much payload can be placed into a given orbit for a fixed propellant load. The V-2’s low pressure and modest Isp forced it to carry an enormous fraction of its launch mass as propellant; Raptor’s higher figures shrink that fraction dramatically. Full-flow staged combustion also eliminates the separate gas-generator exhaust that wastes propellant on older engines, converting nearly all of the chemical energy into useful thrust. The V-2’s spherical combustion chamber and 18 small injector elements were state-of-the-art in 1944, yet they could not approach the mixing efficiency or thermal margins now routine in Raptor’s pintle injectors and actively cooled chambers.

The practical result is visible in Starship’s architecture: thirty-three Raptors on the Super Heavy booster can lift a vehicle whose dry mass is only a few percent of its propellant load, then return the booster for reuse within hours. The same physics that once made a 245 kN engine the state of the art now lets a single engine class deliver both the thrust needed for orbital-class missions and the reliability margins required for airline-style flight rates. Rapid pad turnaround on Falcon 9 already demonstrates how reuse economics scale when engines and structures survive multiple flights; Raptor’s design choices simply extend that logic to a vehicle an order of magnitude larger.

Le concept proliféré fait voler de nombreux petits satellites en orbite terrestre basse pour qu'ils se relaient à surveiller un lieu et prennent le relais si un autre tombe en panne, et le NRO n'a pas divulgué combien de satellites ont volé sur cette mission ni leurs détails techniques.

Le NRO a commencé à déployer la constellation en mai deux mille vingt-quatre avec NROL-146, NROL-179 a décollé le dix-neuf juin deux mille vingt-six, et le précédent record de rotation du pas de tir avait été établi au Pad 40 de Cape Canaveral.

Il emportait aussi une articulation de poignet défaillante du Canadarm2 pour que les ingénieurs au sol puissent l'inspecter, arrimée par l'astronaute de la NASA Jessica Watkins après que les équipages l'aient remplacée lors d'une sortie extravéhiculaire le trente juin.

La météo a retenu la capsule pendant trois jours, et elle a quitté la station le vingt novembre.

Cathie Wood chez ARK Invest, note Dealroom, dit que le calcul orbital pourrait casser les prix des centres de données terrestres d'ici deux ans, avec Starship projeté à trois cent cinquante-huit dollars le kilogramme en orbite en deux mille vingt-sept.

Sur le front de l'intelligence artificielle, Elon Musk a dit que le prix de la mémoire vive, la mémoire sur chaque carte, a poussé l'équipe à réduire le matériel AI4.5 de trente-deux gigaoctets à vingt-quatre gigaoctets par unité, parce que la bande passante mémoire empêche déjà d'exploiter pleinement la configuration plus large.

Cette réduction maintient la conception dans des limites de fonctionnement pratiques et rabote le coût des pièces.

La pression de chambre est le sujet de la plongée technique du jour, en commençant par le moteur du V-2 qui délivrait deux cent quarante-cinq kilonewtons de poussée au niveau de la mer à seulement quatorze à quinze bars, en brûlant de l'oxygène liquide et de l'éthanol à soixante-quinze pour cent, sa turbopompe tournant à la vapeur issue de la décomposition du peroxyde d'hydrogène, un cycle qui limitait le rendement et la réutilisabilité.

Le Raptor 3 atteint deux mille quatre cent cinquante-deux kilonewtons au niveau de la mer — à peu près dix fois deux cent quarante-cinq — dans un cycle de combustion étagée à flux complet qui achemine tout le propergol à travers les turbines avant la chambre principale, faisant monter la pression à trois cents à trois cent cinquante bars, environ vingt fois les quinze bars du V-2 quand on divise trois cents par quinze.

L'impulsion spécifique, les secondes de poussée tirées d'une unité de propergol, est d'environ trois cent trente secondes au niveau de la mer et trois cent cinquante-cinq dans le vide, et trois cent trente fois neuf virgule quatre-vingt-un mètres par seconde au carré, ça tombe de l'ordre de trois mille deux cents mètres par seconde de vitesse d'éjection.

La masse reste à mille cinq cent vingt-cinq kilogrammes, donc deux millions quatre cent cinquante-deux mille newtons divisés par mille cinq cent vingt-cinq fois neuf virgule quatre-vingt-un, ça donne un rapport poussée sur poids proche de cent soixante-quatre.

Une impulsion et une pression plus élevées réduisent la fraction de propergol qu'une fusée doit emporter, et le flux complet élimine l'échappement du générateur de gaz qui déverse par-dessus bord le flux de turbine non brûlé sur les cycles plus anciens, en convertissant presque toute l'énergie chimique en poussée utile.

La chambre sphérique du V-2 et ses dix-huit éléments d'injecteur étaient ce qu'il y avait de plus avancé en mille neuf cent quarante-quatre ; le Raptor utilise des injecteurs à pointeau et des chambres à refroidissement actif pour un rendement de mélange et une marge thermique que le V-2 ne pouvait pas atteindre.

Trente-trois de ces moteurs sur le booster Super Heavy soulèvent un véhicule dont la masse à vide n'est que de quelques pour cent de sa charge de propergol, puis ramènent ce booster Super Heavy pour le réutiliser en quelques heures, en étendant la physique à un véhicule plus grand d'un ordre de grandeur.

Кэти Вуд из АРК Инвест, отмечает Дилрум, говорит, что орбитальные вычисления могут обойти наземные дата-центры по цене в течение двух лет, а «Старшип» прогнозируют по триста пятьдесят восемь долларов за килограмм на орбиту в две тысячи двадцать седьмом.

Стоит держать руку на пульсе: кредиторы могут не дать Спейс-Экс вывести на орбиту передовые чипы искусственного интеллекта на миллиарды долларов, потому что ковенанты по финансированию, сообщает Эм-Эл-Кью, писались ещё до того, как появилась стратегия орбитальных вычислений.

«Раптор-3» выходит на две тысячи четыреста пятьдесят два килоньютона на уровне моря — примерно в десять раз больше, чем двести сорок пять, — в полнопоточном цикле ступенчатого сгорания, который гонит всё топливо через турбины до основной камеры, поднимая давление до трёхсот — трёхсот пятидесяти бар, примерно в двадцать раз выше пятнадцати бар у «Фау-2», если разделить триста на пятнадцать.

SpaceX cut Falcon 9 pad turnaround to thirty-two hours, beating the forty-five hour record and freeing pad capacity. This is SpaceX Daily, episode 171. Dragon's splashdown with station samples, xAI's Memphis cooling pause, and Starlink's block in China, plus an engineering deep dive on chamber pressure. SpaceX sent the classified NROL-136 payload for the National Reconnaissance Office, or NRO, off Vandenberg Space Force Base Launch Complex 4E at four twenty-three in the morning local time on November twenty-second, technology.org reports, three minutes and twenty-one seconds past the new pad mark. Booster B1097 completed its fourteenth flight. It separated about two minutes after leaving the pad and landed at Landing Zone 4 roughly seven and a half minutes after liftoff, back at the launch base. SpaceX vice president of launch Kiko Dontchev credited the West Coast team and posted that preparations finished several hours ahead of schedule, with the next target a twenty-four hour turnaround. The flight was the NRO’s sixth of two thousand twenty-six and the fifteenth supporting its proliferated-architecture satellite network, and every one of those six NRO missions this year flew on Falcon 9 with classified payloads into low Earth orbit. The proliferated design flies many small satellites in low Earth orbit so they can take turns watching a location and stand in if another fails, and the NRO has not disclosed how many satellites flew on this mission or their technical details. The NRO began deploying the constellation in May two thousand twenty-four with NROL-146, NROL-179 launched on June nineteenth two thousand twenty-six, and the previous pad-turnaround mark had been set at Cape Canaveral Pad 40. An uncrewed Dragon, SpaceQ reports, splashed down off California on November twenty-first with biology collected for cancer and aging research on the International Space Station. It also carried a failed Canadarm2 wrist joint so engineers on the ground can inspect it, stowed by NASA astronaut Jessica Watkins after crews replaced it on a June thirtieth spacewalk. Weather held the capsule three days, and it left the station on November twentieth. xAI stopped construction of a South Memphis wastewater recycling plant, Wisevoter reports, and now expects to restart in January. That stoppage hits the cooling schedule the Colossus-class training cluster depends on. Starlink service, Elon Musk posted, is unavailable in China because the system is illegal there. Elon Musk tied Starlink’s next expansion to unsupervised Full Self-Driving, vehicles that operate without a human at the wheel, and said coverage should arrive toward the end of next year to feed the high-bandwidth those cars need so passengers can work and watch entertainment. SciGuySpace posted on X that Tesla Full Self-Driving has become instantly intuitive and made driving measurably more pleasant, a personal observation rather than a company release. China put out its clearest timeline yet for putting astronauts on the Moon before two thousand thirty, NASASpaceflight reports, starting with three uncrewed precursor flights. Crewed flight out of Earth orbit is supposed to wait until those three robotic missions succeed. SciGuySpace observed that earlier NASA decisions prevented Orion from gaining low-lunar-orbit capability despite two decades of funding, his reading of that history rather than an agency confirmation. Rocket Lab’s chief financial officer, TradingView reports, noted stronger pricing power for Electron launches amid reduced availability of SpaceX rideshare opportunities. Electron demand remains firm even as the stock just posted its worst week on record. In the same window, a YouTube report not confirmed by the company says SpaceX is preparing Booster 24 and Ship 42 for Starship Flight 17 right after Flight 15, a test that could debut from Pad 39A in Florida. Cathie Wood at ARK Invest, Dealroom notes, says orbital compute could undercut terrestrial data centers within two years, with Starship projected at three hundred fifty-eight dollars per kilogram to orbit in two thousand twenty-seven. Worth keeping an eye on, lenders could block SpaceX from putting billions of dollars of advanced AI chips in orbit because their financing covenants, MLQ reports, were written before the orbital-compute strategy existed. Those terms could stop the deployment even after technical and regulatory hurdles fall. The way out is a debt renegotiation or a showing that orbital assets sit outside the current restrictions. A seventy-four billion dollar AI data-center plan still has to clear votes in Texas and Arizona on December ninth. On the AI front, Elon Musk said the price of RAM, the memory on each board, pushed the team to cut AI4.5 hardware from thirty-two gigabytes to twenty-four gigabytes per unit, because memory bandwidth already stops the larger configuration from being fully used. That cut holds the design inside practical operating limits and trims what the parts cost. Chamber pressure is the subject of today's engineering deep dive, starting with the V-2 engine that delivered two hundred forty-five kilonewtons of sea-level thrust at only fourteen to fifteen bar while burning liquid oxygen and seventy-five percent ethanol, its turbopump running on steam from hydrogen-peroxide decomposition, a cycle that limited efficiency and reusability. Raptor 3 reaches two thousand four hundred fifty-two kilonewtons at sea level — roughly ten times two hundred forty-five — in a full-flow staged-combustion cycle that routes all propellant through the turbines before the main chamber, raising pressure to three hundred to three hundred fifty bar, about twenty times the V-2's fifteen bar when you divide three hundred by fifteen. Specific impulse, the seconds of thrust from a unit of propellant, is approximately three hundred thirty seconds at sea level and three hundred fifty-five in vacuum, and three hundred thirty times nine point eight one meters per second squared is on the order of three thousand two hundred meters per second of exhaust velocity. Mass stays at one thousand five hundred twenty-five kilograms, so two million four hundred fifty-two thousand newtons divided by one thousand five hundred twenty-five times nine point eight one yields a thrust-to-weight near one hundred sixty-four. Higher impulse and pressure shrink the propellant fraction a rocket must carry, and full-flow eliminates gas-generator exhaust that dumps unburned turbine flow overboard on older cycles, converting nearly all of the chemical energy into useful thrust. The V-2's spherical chamber and eighteen injector elements were state of the art in nineteen forty-four; Raptor uses pintle injectors and actively cooled chambers for mixing efficiency and thermal margin the V-2 could not reach. Thirty-three of those engines on the Super Heavy booster lift a vehicle whose dry mass is only a few percent of its propellant load, then return that Super Heavy booster for reuse within hours, extending the physics to a vehicle an order of magnitude larger.
