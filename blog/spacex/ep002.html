  2. SpaceX Raptor Engine Explained: How Raptor 3 Became the World's Most Powerful Methane Engine — and What It Means for SPCX Investors - MEXC Exchange

    3. Raptor 3 achieves higher chamber pressure than any prior methalox engine through a full-flow staged combustion cycle that routes all propellant through both turbopumps. The redesign eliminates the gas generator and many valves, lowering part count and production time. Thrust now exceeds 280 tf while specific impulse remains competitive for both sea-level and vacuum variants. These gains directly address the open question of engine production rate versus Starship demand. Source: Google News

  3. SpaceX Launches Pre-Orders for Replica Raptor Engine Bell - Tesla North

    4. SpaceX opened pre-orders for a full-scale replica of the Raptor engine bell aimed at collectors and educational displays. The hardware matches the outer geometry of the flight article down to the nozzle contour and mounting flange. Production uses the same additive and subtractive processes as flight hardware, giving buyers a tangible reference for the engine’s scale. Deliveries are scheduled after the next production block clears flight units. Source: Google News

  4. SpaceX Leases Colossus 1 Data Center to Anthropic Following Grok AI Latency Issues - News and Statistics - IndexBox

    5. SpaceX has leased capacity in the Colossus 1 facility to Anthropic after xAI reported elevated inference latency on Grok workloads. The move reallocates a portion of the Memphis supercluster while xAI expands its own racks. Anthropic will use the GPUs for large-model training runs that require high-bandwidth interconnects already installed at the site. The lease terms remain confidential but are described as short-term bridge capacity. Source: Google News

  5. Eighty Residents Sue SpaceX, Saying Elon Musk's Constant Rocket Launches Are Literally Destroying Their Homes - Futurism

    6. Eighty South Texas residents filed suit claiming repeated Starship static fires and launches have caused structural damage to homes through vibration and overpressure. The complaint seeks injunctive relief and monetary damages tied to specific launch dates. SpaceX has not yet filed a response in the public docket. Resolution would likely require either negotiated mitigation or court-ordered limits on test cadence. Source: Google News

  6. SpaceX Made a Public Land Swap Deal with USFWS. Now Environmental Groups Are Suing - Outdoor Life

    7. Environmental organizations sued to block a land exchange between SpaceX and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service near Boca Chica. The swap would give SpaceX additional acreage for expanded launch infrastructure in return for conservation easements elsewhere. Plaintiffs argue the exchange violates National Environmental Policy Act review requirements. A preliminary injunction hearing is expected within weeks. Source: Google News

  7. In aerospace, AI isn’t replacing workers. It’s filling a shortage — SpaceNews

    8. Aerospace firms report that AI tools are being deployed to handle repetitive engineering tasks amid a persistent shortage of qualified technicians and analysts. The software assists with wiring harness routing checks and anomaly detection in test data rather than displacing headcount. SpaceX has not disclosed its internal usage but operates in the same labor market. Executives say the approach shortens review cycles without reducing staffing plans. Source: spacenews.com

  8. Update: SpaceX IPO raises US$75Bn for investment in LEO, AI and launchers - megaproject.com

    9. The IPO prospectus earmarks a portion of the $75 billion raised for low-Earth orbit infrastructure, artificial-intelligence compute, and additional launcher capacity. The Counterpoint

Blue Origin’s New Glenn prototype suffered a pad explosion during a recent Florida test, removing a near-term competitor from the heavy-lift manifest and shifting some NASA lunar cargo planning timelines. The incident highlights the technical risk still present in new booster development even after years of investment. SpaceX’s own Starship program continues to face similar unknowns around booster catch hardware and rapid turnaround. A successful New Glenn re-flight would be required to restore schedule confidence for affected missions.

AI & Compute

SpaceX leased Colossus 1 capacity to Anthropic after Grok inference latency rose on xAI workloads, showing how the Memphis cluster is being treated as shared infrastructure across the Musk ecosystem.

The IPO filing explicitly lists AI compute as one of three priority uses for the $75 billion proceeds alongside LEO constellation growth and launcher expansion.

Aerospace suppliers are adopting AI-assisted design review tools to address technician shortages, a trend that parallels SpaceX’s need to scale engineering output for orbital data-center satellites.

xAI has not yet disclosed the next Colossus expansion phase, leaving open how much of the IPO capital will flow directly into additional GPU racks versus Starlink backhaul upgrades.

Engineering Deep Dive

Raptor 3’s shift to a full-flow staged combustion cycle routes every molecule of methane and oxygen through the respective turbopumps before they reach the injector. This eliminates the separate gas-generator stage that previously dumped hot gas overboard, raising overall efficiency while removing several valves and manifolds. The resulting part-count reduction directly attacks the Idiot Index: where earlier Raptors carried an estimated 4–5× markup over raw materials and labor, the new architecture targets closer to 3× by collapsing assembly steps.

That lower index matters because each Starship needs dozens of engines; even a 20 % drop in engine cost multiplies across the booster and ship. The design choice also buys margin on chamber pressure without increasing turbopump stress, because the pre-burners now operate at the same mixture ratio as the main chamber.

Next, watch for the first integrated test of a full Raptor 3 set on a Ship or Booster static-fire stand. If the simplified plumbing survives the thermal environment at 280 tf, the program gains a credible path to the production rate required for monthly flight attempts.
