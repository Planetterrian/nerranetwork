    3. The V5 user terminal introduces a smaller phased-array aperture and integrated laser-link transceiver that raises downlink capacity while cutting power draw by roughly 15 percent. The retrofit uses existing fuselage hard points and adds minimal drag. Operational testing is scheduled for later this quarter. The antennas leverage the same phased-array technology already proven on Starlink platforms. Engineering Deep Dive

The engine-relight sequence planned for Starship Flight 13 must solve a fundamental fluid-dynamics problem: restarting a cryogenic Raptor in the vacuum of space after the vehicle has coasted for tens of minutes. In microgravity the remaining liquid oxygen and methane stratify inside the tanks, leaving vapor pockets at the feed lines. Without active settling thrust or a dedicated ullage motor, the turbopumps risk ingesting gas and cavitating on ignition. SpaceX’s approach uses a short RCS burn to settle the propellants, followed by a carefully timed helium purge that clears the lines before the igniter fires. The energy cost of that settling burn directly reduces the payload margin available for the first orbital refueling demonstration.

If the relight succeeds, the same settling technique becomes the baseline for every subsequent tanker flight, collapsing the propellant-transfer timeline from days to hours. The raw propellants themselves are cheap, yet the entire mission architecture hinges on whether a few kilograms of helium and RCS impulse can guarantee liquid at the pump inlet. A single failed relight forces either a heavier settling system or a redesign of the tank geometry, both of which multiply the per-flight cost far beyond the price of the fuel. The Idiot Index here is instructive because the finished capability of reliable vacuum restart costs orders of magnitude more than the propellant inputs alone.

Success on Flight 13 would validate the settling sequence under real orbital conditions and directly inform the refueling demonstrations required for Artemis III. Engineers will monitor tank-pressure decay rates and igniter performance across multiple attempts to build statistical confidence. Any deviation in helium consumption or RCS propellant use will be tracked against pre-flight models to refine future vehicle mass budgets. The outcome determines whether Starship can transition from suborbital hops to sustained orbital operations within the current architecture.

还有一个话题追踪到在墨西哥湾一艘商用船上安装的V5 Starlink终端的首次公开目击。

值得关注的是，根据跟踪数据和监管申报，Starlink卫星现在平均每周执行近一次避撞机动。

这一频率上升会消耗推进剂储备并缩短在轨寿命。

除非采用改进的协调机制或碎片减缓规则，否则Starlink星座和其他近地轨道交通的持续增长将进一步增加机动负担。

解决之道在于达成新的国际交通管理协议，或通过硬件升级降低每次机动的推进剂消耗。

早期演示飞行将验证机载加速器在轨道条件下的功率和热管理。

从工程角度看，星舰飞行13计划中的再点火序列必须解决一个根本的流体动力学难题：飞行器在太空中滑行数十分钟后，如何在真空环境下重新启动低温猛禽发动机。

在微重力环境下，剩余的液氧和甲烷会在贮箱内分层，在输送管路处留下蒸气气袋。

如果没有主动沉降推力或专用留余发动机，涡轮泵就有可能吸入气体并在点火时发生汽蚀。

SpaceX的方案是采用短暂RCS燃烧使推进剂沉降，随后进行精确计时的氦气吹扫，在点火器启动前清理管路。

沉降燃烧的能量消耗会直接减少首次轨道补给演示可用的有效载荷裕度。

如果再点火成功，同样的沉降技术将成为后续每一次油轮飞行的基准方案，将推进剂转移时间从数天缩短至数小时。

原始推进剂本身成本低廉，但整个任务架构能否成立，取决于几公斤氦气和RCS冲量能否确保泵入口处始终有液体。

一次再点火失败，就可能迫使采用更重的沉降系统或重新设计贮箱几何结构，这两种方案都会使单次飞行成本大幅增加，远超燃料本身的价格。

这里的白痴指数很有启发性，因为可靠真空再启动这一成品能力，成本比单纯的推进剂输入高出几个数量级。

飞行13的成功将验证真实轨道条件下的沉降序列，并直接为Artemis III所需的补给演示提供依据。

工程师将监测多次尝试中的贮箱压力衰减率和点火器性能，以建立统计置信度。

任何氦气消耗或RCS推进剂使用的偏差都将与飞行前模型进行比对，以优化未来飞行器质量预算。

这一结果将决定星舰能否在现有架构下从亚轨道跳跃过渡到持续轨道运行。
