Top News

  1. SpaceX reports rapid progress on Florida Starship pads

    Construction of structures and systems at KSC Pad 39A is complete and acceptance testing has begun. Director of NASA science and Dragon programs Julianna Scheiman said the team is moving from main construction into proofing and activation testing. Starship will fly from Texas at least once more before attempting a Florida launch. Work is also advancing on towers at Cape Canaveral SLC-37A and SLC-37B. SpaceX is constructing three pads along Florida’s coast—two at Cape Canaveral and one at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center. The company holds a long-term lease with NASA to launch Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy from Pad 39A, now being expanded for Starship. As of today Starship has launched on 14 integrated test missions from Starbase in Texas. Source: starlust.org

  2. Elon Musk accuses oligarchs of blocking Starlink in India; government pushes back

    Musk posted that certain oligarchs are maintaining a monopolistic chokehold and called the situation a crime against the Indian people. India’s Ministry of Communications stated the regulatory framework is fair and non-discriminatory and that Starlink and two other licensed operators are at the same stage awaiting security assessments before spectrum assignment. Starlink has already installed more than 20 gateway sites and hundreds of antennas in the country while meeting data-sovereignty rules. Lauren Dreyer, Starlink’s vice president of business operations, said at the India Mobile Congress that the company stands ready to serve India and wants to help achieve universal connectivity. Starlink has obtained GMPCS authorisation from the Department of Telecommunications but still needs final security clearances and spectrum. Reliance Jio and Bharti Airtel have resisted administrative assignment of satellite spectrum in favor of auctions. Source: thehindu.com Source: techcrunch.com

  3. NASA schedules SpaceX CRS-35 cargo mission for October 13

    The Dragon spacecraft will lift off no earlier than 6:33 am EDT on a Falcon 9 from Cape Canaveral with the final pair of ISS Roll-Out Solar Arrays. A backup window is available at 6:08 am on October 14. The arrays will augment station power ahead of the planned 2030 deorbit. Dragon will dock autonomously around 11 am on October 15 and remain at the station until mid-November. Live streaming of pre-launch activities begins at 6:15 am EDT on the NASA YouTube channel. The Expedition 75 crew will load the spacecraft with unneeded cargo and time-sensitive science experiments before undocking. Source: starlust.org Source: teslanorth.com

  4. Grok Bot will route some queries to rival models

    The system now uses multiple AI models including competitors when they deliver better answers for specific queries. SpaceX’s Grok Bot is shifting toward model routing that incorporates Claude and MidJourney outputs for improved results on certain tasks. The change reflects ongoing work to optimize inference across available frontier models. Source: newsbytesapp.com

  5. SpaceX negotiating up to $40 billion loan for Nvidia chips

    The financing would support one of the largest debt transactions tied to AI infrastructure growth. SpaceX is in talks with banks and investors to raise the funds specifically to purchase Nvidia chips for expanding compute capacity. The proposed deal underscores the capital demands of the current AI boom across technology companies. Source: unn.ua

  6. FCC plans auction of 25 MHz prime spectrum

    The auction could benefit SpaceX along with Amazon and AST for satellite services. The Federal Communications Commission is set to vote on auctioning the band, which operators view as valuable for next-generation satellite connectivity. SpaceX, Amazon, and AST have all expressed interest in the frequencies for their respective constellations. Source: au.pcmag.com Source: cnbc.com

  7. Salvaged Starship returns to Starbase

    The vehicle completed a 2.5-month ocean voyage and is back at the Texas facility. Recovery teams brought the upper stage home after it spent 75 days at sea following its most recent flight test. The return allows engineers to inspect hardware and continue iterative improvements on the Starship design. Source: aviationweek.com

  8. Starbase Louisiana site sees early activity

    Dredges are operating at the Freshwater inlet and SpaceX has begun permitting for an elevated four-lane access road and bridge upgrades. The company announced plans for a new complex in Vermilion Parish that could support high launch cadence. Early site work includes road improvements on La 3147 and evaluation of nearby private landing strips for future support operations. Source: reddit.com

AI & Compute

Grok Bot now routes queries across multiple models including Claude and MidJourney when they provide stronger results for a given task. The routing approach allows the system to select the best-performing model per query rather than relying on a single architecture. SpaceX continues to integrate these capabilities as part of its broader pivot toward AI infrastructure. Source: gizmodo.com

Engineering Deep Dive

The engineering angle on the final IROSA solar arrays centers on how roll-out flexible blankets solve mass and volume constraints that rigid panels cannot meet at the same power level. Each array deploys from a compact canister using stored strain energy in the mast and blanket, avoiding the need for heavy motors or hinges that would add mass to every resupply flight. The design trades initial complexity in the deployment mechanism for a power-to-mass ratio that supports the station’s growing experiment load and eventual controlled deorbit. At roughly 20 kilowatts per pair once installed, the arrays reduce reliance on the original rigid arrays whose degradation has already limited available power margins. The choice also reflects a broader pattern in which SpaceX and NASA favor lightweight deployables that fit inside Dragon’s trunk volume rather than requiring dedicated heavy-lift flights solely for power hardware. This approach keeps the marginal cost of each additional kilowatt low enough that the station can maintain operations through 2030 without a full redesign of the power architecture.
