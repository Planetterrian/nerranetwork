One thread runs through infrastructure decisions that shape what becomes routine. We begin with the Florida pads.

Mira's field note

At the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology, researchers have moved a levitating diamond roughly a centimeter across using only the force from electron spins in nitrogen-vacancy centers. The diamond, held in a magnetic trap, shifted measurably when the spins were driven, marking the first direct observation of a quantum mechanical effect acting on an object large enough to feel gravity. The work appears today in Science Advances. Next the team plans to scale the setup to probe even larger masses.
