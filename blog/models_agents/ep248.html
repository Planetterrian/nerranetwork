Shopify AI agent sales rose 3.3 times year-over-year on Black Friday, showing agent-driven purchases scaling in real commerce.

By Patrick November 28, 2026 Ep 248 8 min read Listen to podcast

What You Need to Know: Shopify reported AI agent sales running 3.3 times higher than last year during Black Friday, with merchants seeing peaks of $2.3 million to over $5 million per minute. Ant Group open-sourced LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560B-parameter MoE medical model with 25B active parameters, 1M-token context, and MIT license that leads open models on InclusionMed. Developers should watch agent payment flows and medical agent tooling this week as both see concrete production use.

Top Story

Shopify president Harley Finkelstein stated that sales through AI agents ran about 3.3 times higher than a year earlier during Black Friday 2026. He described it as the first real Black Friday where agentic commerce played a meaningful role, though no absolute dollar figure was released. Peak merchant sales reached roughly $2.3 million and 27,000 orders per minute on some dashboards, with others showing over $5 million and 46,000 orders per minute. Bain research cited in coverage puts AI-agent purchases at 0.4 percent of global ecommerce transactions and 0.2 percent of value, indicating the channel remains small despite fast growth. Accounts of the interview differ slightly on scope, with some tying the growth specifically to the Shop app and others describing agent sales more broadly. Builders testing agent checkout flows should size the actual base volume before assuming large revenue impact, as the starting point for agent-driven purchases stays low across the wider market. Source: startupfortune.com

Model Updates

Ant Group Releases LingSante-3.1-flash: An Open-Weight MoE Model with 560B Total Parameters and 25B Activations per Token: MarkTechPost

It leads open models on the InclusionMed benchmark and targets clinical reasoning, medical search, and tool-using agents. An MXFP4 quantized checkpoint is available, with reference SGLang setups using 8x H200 or 4x GB300 GPUs. The model mixes medical and general data through pretraining, mid-training, SFT, and RL post-training focused on reasoning and professional behavior. The architecture uses a hybrid-linear MoE design with 63 KDA linear attention layers and 9 gated MLA layers in a 7:1 ratio, 512 routed experts plus one shared expert, and eight routed experts active per token. Hidden size is 4096 with 64 attention heads and a vocabulary of 157184 tokens. The family also includes LingSante-3.1-air-VL at 124B total parameters with 5.5B active and 256K context. Three additional models remain closed: LingSante-Pulse for risk prediction across more than 150 conditions, LingSante-MedGuard for safety and bioethics screening, and LingSante-Live for real-time video consultation inside the Afu app that serves 150 million users and handles nearly 20 million daily health consultations. The model scores 65.72 on HealthBench Professional, the highest in the nine-model comparison on the model card. Source: marktechpost.com

From Alibaba to BharatGen, 10 Small Language Models That Rivalled LLMs in 2026: Analytics India Magazine

The article surveys ten small language models from labs including Alibaba and BharatGen that closed performance gaps with larger LLMs during 2026. It highlights efficiency gains in domains such as Indian languages and specialized tasks where parameter count stayed under 30B. Source: analyticsindiamag.com

Economic efficiency and climate mitigation of large language models depend on different levers: Nature

The paper examines how economic efficiency and climate mitigation for large language models rely on separate optimization levers rather than shared improvements. Source: nature.com

Agent & Tool Developments

Hired by an AI Agent: 2,000 $5 Payments Explained: explainx.ai

A Hacker News user reported being hired and paid by an AI agent, with the final invoice settled as roughly 2,000 separate $5 bank transactions. The thread notes that per-tool-call spend ceilings in agent harnesses can trigger micro-payment storms when batch settlement is absent. SkillDB, linked to the poster, markets structured skills for agents across 38 domains with more than 6300 skills listed on its about page. The experience was described as more pleasant and reasonable than typical commercial clients. No rate, project description, or duration appears in the original post, and the five-comment thread contains no named employer, agent product, or payment processor. Commenters point to spend-guardrail patterns seen in UK FCA guidance on agentic payments, where agents initiating transactions may require authorization while regulated firms retain responsibility. Source: explainx.ai

Switzerland Confirms First AI-Agent Cyberattacks; Four of Five Breaches Succeeded: BigGo Finance

Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre recorded the country's first autonomous AI-agent cyberattacks, with four of five incidents fully compromising targets. Three targeted critical infrastructure operators and two succeeded; attackers used agentic frameworks that select tools and pivot autonomously. The number of freely available agentic offensive tools nearly doubled since October. Small and medium-sized businesses were among the organizations hit, and those that came forward did so voluntarily. The NCSC advisory issued November 24 stated the agents had fully compromised victims' systems in every known case at that point. The agency cites ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI as examples of reachable agentic offensive frameworks, though it does not confirm either appeared in the Swiss incidents. No major service disruption has been reported, and the agency has not named victims or attributed the campaign. Source: finance.biggo.com

Speculative Decoding Makes Tool Calls Faster. Agents Need a Cache.: Towards Data Science

Benchmarks with Qwen3 and Llama on llama.cpp showed a draft model speeding up code, JSON, and tool calls by up to 1.7x. Uncached agent loops ran 6 percent slower, indicating caching is required to realize gains in repeated agent workflows. The draft model approach improves throughput on structured outputs while the lack of a cache in agent loops erodes the benefit across multiple turns. Source: towardsdatascience.com

Practical & Community

datasette-charts 0.4a0: Simon Willison

The datasette-charts 0.4a0 release adds a plugin hook for other plugins to define new chart types and fixes friction points identified during experimental use. A new site at charts.datasette.io includes a tutorial for plugin authors and a gallery linking to interactive demos on Datasette Lite. The release addresses design issues that surfaced when Claude Opus 5.5 built an experimental plugin to test the hook. Source: simonwillison.net

Can an SNES Run an LLM?: Hackster.io

Benj Edwards ran a 2.2-million-parameter LLM on an unmodified SNES using a Super FX 2 flash cart, producing somewhat coherent sentences. A larger ROM variant adds a fact book for better information retrieval, and an emulated version is available online for testing. The SNES CPU runs at 3.58 MHz with only 256 KB of RAM, making the Super FX 2 coprocessor essential for the workload. The pure version fits entirely on the flash cart while the fact-book version is approximately five times larger in ROM size. Source: hackster.io

Code Mode vs. Tool Calling Benchmarks Find a 16-Tool Crossover: Towards Data Science

Code Mode proved 120x cheaper and 18x faster than tool calling on 100-item fan-outs with many MCP integrations, though less reliable on single lookups. The crossover point sits near 16 tools. The benchmarks used Qwen3 and Llama models on llama.cpp and highlighted the cost advantage for high-volume structured workflows once the number of tools exceeds the threshold. Source: towardsdatascience.com

Salt Security's report found 49.8 percent of organizations confirmed or suspected an AI agent performed an unintended action in the prior year, with only 12.5 percent able to trace complete agent sequences. Source: webwire.com

Blending Unsupervised Clustering Tasks with Scikit-LLM: KDnuggets

Scikit-LLM enables blending unsupervised clustering with language model capabilities for practical text analysis workflows. Source: kdnuggets.com

How ServiceNow aims to keep AI agents secure and under control: Techzine Global

ServiceNow outlines controls for keeping autonomous agents within defined operational and security boundaries. Source: techzine.eu

Under the Hood: Self-Hosting Open-Weight Models

Self-hosting open-weight models lets organizations keep data inside their perimeter and avoid per-token cloud fees once volume is high enough. The core tradeoff starts with hardware: consumer Blackwell GPUs such as the RTX 5090 deliver 3.5-4.6x higher throughput than the 5060 Ti on RAG workloads while cutting latency dramatically. NVFP4 quantization further improves this by giving 1.6x throughput over BF16 at 41 percent lower energy use and only 2-4 percent quality loss. At moderate sustained volume the electricity cost alone falls to $0.001-0.04 per million tokens, 40-200x below budget cloud APIs, with hardware payback in under four months. The practical limit appears when frontier capability is required; most teams therefore keep a hybrid pattern of local inference for routine or sensitive traffic and cloud calls for the hardest tasks. The gotcha that bites most teams is underestimating ongoing ops cost once context lengths and concurrency rise beyond the initial benchmark numbers. Systematic evaluations across 79 configurations spanning quantization formats, context lengths from 8k to 64k, and workloads including RAG, multi-LoRA agentic serving, and high-concurrency APIs show that budget GPUs achieve the highest throughput-per-dollar for API workloads with sub-second latency. The RTX 5090 stands out for RAG and multi-LoRA serving, but the energy and cost advantages of NVFP4 hold across model sizes from Qwen3-8B to GPT-OSS-20B. Source: arxiv.org

Things to Try This Week

  • Test LingSante-3.1-flash on medical reasoning or agent tasks using the MXFP4 checkpoint and SGLang on 4-8 H200 GPUs to see how the 1M context performs on long clinical documents.
  • Run the datasette-charts 0.4a0 plugin on your own data and explore the new chart-type hook at charts.datasette.io to add custom visualizations without writing everything from scratch.
  • Compare Code Mode versus standard tool calling on your MCP-heavy workflows once you pass roughly 16 tools to measure the 120x cost reduction reported in the benchmarks.
  • Try the SNES LLM ROM on real hardware or the online emulator to experience the extreme low end of local inference and understand where coherence breaks.
  • Explore the full LingSante family details in the model card to plan for the upcoming phased releases of the vision-language and safety-focused variants.

On the Horizon

  • Continued growth in agent-driven Black Friday and holiday sales data as more platforms report final figures.
  • Further agent security guidance from regulators following the Swiss NCSC incidents and Salt Security survey results.
  • Additional small-model releases from labs focused on regional languages and efficiency.
