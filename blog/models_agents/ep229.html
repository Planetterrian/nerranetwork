Models & Agents Blog

LG just opened free API access to its 750B K-EXAONE 2.5 model for agentic workloads.

By Patrick November 09, 2026 Ep 229 11 min read Listen to podcast
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top Story Model Updates Agent & Tool Developments Practical & Community Also Today Things to Try This Week On the Horizon

What You Need to Know: LG AI Research released free hosted access to K-EXAONE 2.5, a 750B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model focused on Korean and agent tasks. It improves on the prior version across software development, web browsing, SaaS automation, and agent problem-solving benchmarks. Builders working on Korean-language agents or tool-calling workflows can now test it without cost.

Top Story

LG AI Research opened free API access to K-EXAONE 2.5, a 750B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model trained further from the August K-EXAONE 2.0 release. The model targets agentic workloads with strong Korean-language support and handles long context plus complex tool calls. Internal benchmarks show gains over the prior version on software development, web information retrieval, SaaS automation, and agent problem-solving tasks. Developers and startups can obtain an API key immediately through the EXAONE platform for service building and research. The release positions the model as a technology-validation step toward a frontier-level system planned for January. Source: mk.co.kr

Model Updates

Telecom Companies Give AI Agents More Responsibility: PYMNTS.com

Telecom operators are assigning AI agents expanded roles in network operations, customer service, and predictive maintenance. The shift reflects growing trust in agent reliability for high-stakes infrastructure tasks. Source: pymnts.com

Compression of Emotion in Language Model Representations: arXiv NLP

Seven LLMs tested on the GoEmotions dataset show consistent over-compression of 27 fine-grained emotion categories into broader positive-negative splits in their hidden representations. Linear probes still recover fine-grained information despite the unsupervised geometry favoring coarser groupings. Source: arxiv.org

Contextuality and Contradictions: Exploring Real-World Identifications of AI-Generated Writing: arXiv NLP

Wikipedia editors and students identify AI text using diverse evidence types that prove contradictory and context-dependent rather than fixed textual markers. The study finds pragmatic infelicity, not specific features, often drives these judgments. Source: arxiv.org

RL-ATTACK: A Generalized Black-Box Adversarial Attack on Language Models via Reinforcement Learning: arXiv NLP

A reinforcement-learning attacker trained against target models achieves lower post-attack accuracy and better query efficiency than baselines on natural language inference and generative QA tasks. The approach learns tailored transformations directly from the target without external knowledge. Source: arxiv.org

Guardrail Erosion in Domain Fine-Tuned Large Language Models: A Paired Audit of Knowledge-Centric Abstention in Biomedical LLMs: arXiv NLP

A paired audit of 25 biomedical fine-tuned models against their base checkpoints found appropriate abstention rates on MedAbstain dropped from a median of 8.3% to 4.2% after fine-tuning. The change was heterogeneous across model pairs. Source: arxiv.org

Agent & Tool Developments

AI agent writing process: copy-paste when it fits: Simon Willison (AI builder) (X)

Simon Willison reports selectively copying sentences generated by agents into his own writing when they match his intended meaning exactly. The practice treats agents as collaborative drafting tools rather than full replacements. Source: x.com

Hiding AI PR slop with details/summary tags: Simon Willison (AI builder)

Simon Willison now collapses lengthy AI-generated GitHub PR descriptions behind HTML details/summary tags to reduce reader fatigue while keeping the content available. GitHub Markdown supports the tags, making the pattern immediately usable. Source: simonwillison.net

From Procedures to Permissions: Learning Precise Subagent Authorization from Agent Skills: arXiv NLP

The method extracts authorization requirements directly from Agent Skills procedural descriptions to train subagent permission policies. On ClawArena-Team it raised tool permission precision by 13.4 points and workspace permission precision by 24.1 points for Qwen3.5-35B-A3B. Source: arxiv.org

MARS: Training Multilingual Search Agents for Retriever-Free Evidence Acquisition: arXiv NLP

MARS trains multilingual search agents that interact directly with raw corpora via shell commands instead of relying on learned retrievers. Corpus-native entity grounding and redundancy-aware rewards improve trajectory quality and cross-lingual generalization. Source: arxiv.org

Practical & Community

Twitter preview cards can't handle HTML tags in titles: Simon Willison (AI builder) (X)

Simon Willison discovered that Twitter preview cards strip or break HTML tags placed in post titles. The finding affects anyone embedding formatted text in social sharing metadata. Source: x.com

AI chatbots aren’t therapists. But can they finally get men to open up?: STAT

The article examines whether AI chatbots can lower barriers for men seeking mental health support despite not being licensed therapists. It focuses on conversational patterns that encourage disclosure. Source: statnews.com

Structuring Survey Histories for LLM Digital Twins: A Controlled Study of Background, Decision Procedure, and Evaluation Profiles: arXiv NLP

A fixed Background-Decision procedure-Evaluation schema improved held-out answer prediction accuracy over raw transcripts by 1.27 points on gpt-5.4-nano and 1.19 points on gpt-5.4-mini. Adding the Evaluation component drove most of the gain. Source: arxiv.org

Richness Is Not Evenness: Ordered Renyi Crossings under Self-Training: arXiv NLP

Recursive self-training of GPT-2 on its own outputs produces distinct crossing points where different Renyi entropy measures shift from loss to gain as sampling temperature rises. The ordering holds across multiple measurement protocols and languages. Source: arxiv.org

Also Today

On the Incompleteness of do-calculus for Universal Identifiability: arXiv NLP: The paper shows do-calculus remains sound but loses completeness when identifying causal effects from arbitrary mixtures of observational and interventional distributions. Source: arxiv.org

Beyond Truncation? Entailment-Licensed Elision and the Limits of Semantic Preservation in LLM Decoding: arXiv NLP: An entailment-based elision controller removes surplus tokens while preserving content better than simple truncation on three model families, though 59% of sampled elisions still harmed answer quality. Source: arxiv.org

Language, Not Code Abstraction: Natural-language Training Shapes Brain-aligned Geometry: arXiv NLP: GPT-2 models trained on TinyStories produced higher brain alignment scores than code-trained counterparts on Pereira-243 and Tuckute2024, with the advantage tied to natural-language training rather than abstract structure. Source: arxiv.org

Failure Profiling and Reachable Trajectory Selection for Reasoning Distillation: arXiv NLP: The FPRT method profiles student failure modes and selects teacher trajectories reachable by the student, achieving state-of-the-art results across five reasoning benchmarks on two student backbones. Source: arxiv.org

Does Reasoning Improve Psychological Depth in Large Language Models?: arXiv NLP: Human raters found no consistent advantage for reasoning-enhanced models on short-story psychological depth, while an LLM-as-a-Judge showed strong pro-reasoning bias. Source: arxiv.org

The Invisible Gap: Romanized Indic Generation Fails Where Automatic Metrics Succeed: arXiv NLP: Automatic metrics failed to detect script spillage and English collapse in romanized Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada generation, while native speakers rated outputs differently across four frontier models. Source: arxiv.org

AlienBench: A Benchmark of Structural Novelty in Language Model Outputs: arXiv NLP: The benchmark scores model descriptions of alien creatures by counting departures from Earth-animal features, with open-weight models averaging higher novelty than proprietary ones. Source: arxiv.org

PhoPunct: A Large-Scale Benchmark of Transformer Architectures for Vietnamese Punctuation Restoration: arXiv NLP: On 22.1 million tokens, plain Transformer classifiers matched or beat Bi-LSTM-CRF variants in seven of eight matched comparisons, showing recurrent and structured heads add little under fixed training budgets. Source: arxiv.org

Calibrated concept profiles: making the reading of a model's neurons a measurement: arXiv NLP: The method turns direct neuron reading into a repeatable measurement by sampling a declared stimulus distribution and reporting activation in units of the neuron’s own spread. Source: arxiv.org

Representational drift in a code model: behaviour settles, which neurons carry it does not: arXiv NLP: In OpenCoder-1.5B, the set of neurons carrying any given Python construct continued changing through the final checkpoint even after loss on grammar-fixed positions had plateaued. Source: arxiv.org

Khmer PFGen-Bench: Building a Judge-Free Benchmark for Open-Ended Generation in a Low-Resource Language: arXiv NLP: Post-trained models supplied more valid but less varied references than base models for the first judge-free Khmer open-ended generation benchmark. Source: arxiv.org

KLEAR: Knowledge-Matched LLM Evaluation in Korean across Application Requirements via Controlled Benchmark Transformation: arXiv NLP: The framework generates knowledge-matched question pairs that vary application demand while holding required facts constant, exposing application failures even when knowledge is supplied. Source: arxiv.org

DUET: Dual-Sparse Inference for Block Diffusion Language Models: arXiv NLP: The training-free method applies prefix KV sparsity and early query sparsity to achieve 1.34×–2.83× speedups on LLaDA2.1-mini and 2.41×–4.72× on SDAR-8B-Chat-b32 at 32K context. Source: arxiv.org

Multilingual Artificial Datasets for AI-Driven Cardiology: the CliTe and ParaCliTe corpora: arXiv NLP: Seven European hospitals created 699 artificial cardiology reports across seven languages, with a 94-patient parallel subset providing sentence-aligned translations for model adaptation. Source: arxiv.org

Asymmetric Correspondence Learning for Lexical and Style Embeddings: Interpreting Latent Space via Acoustic Features: arXiv NLP: The split-latent method isolates acoustic word embeddings from style embeddings, revealing that vocal-tract filter features dominate lexical clustering while F0 dynamics dominate speaker clustering. Source: arxiv.org

Syn2DRE: Synergistic Dual Teacher-Student Framework for Document-level Relation Extraction: arXiv NLP: The lightweight teacher-student setup with cross-supervision and unified end-to-end training outperformed prior semi-supervised DocRE methods on multiple benchmarks. Source: arxiv.org

An Effective Spoken Language Understanding Approach Leveraging Query-Adaptive Intent Detection: arXiv NLP: The two-stage APEX retriever first selects candidate intents via prototypes then retrieves exemplars inside that intent space, lifting exact-match accuracy by 4.34 points on Qwen3-4B. Source: arxiv.org

On the Effectiveness and Limitations of Open-Weight LLMs for Low-Resource Offensive Language Detection in Danish: arXiv NLP: Open-weight LLMs without fine-tuning already surpassed three fine-tuned BERT baselines on Danish hate-speech detection, with further lightweight fine-tuning widening the gap. Source: arxiv.org

Benchmarking Speaker Embedders in the Sub-Second Regime for Real-Time Voice AI Agents: arXiv NLP: Truncating embeddings below the full voice-activity segment degraded every tested embedder, while denoising before embedding raised equal-error rates across architectures. Source: arxiv.org

Query-Guided RoPE Modulation for Lost-in-the-Middle Mitigation in Long-Context LLMs: arXiv NLP: The training-free QGPM method reshapes rotary positions using query relevance from the model’s own attention heads and improved retrieval on NoLiMa and RULER across multiple context lengths. Source: arxiv.org

RuACT: Recovering Inactive Rubrics as Adaptive Critics and Teachers: arXiv NLP: The method assigns unreachable rubrics the teacher role and saturated rubrics the critic role, raising recovery rates and reward-quality alignment on medical and scientific benchmarks. Source: arxiv.org

Steerable Multi-Objective Group-Relative Preference Optimization with Jointly Trained Low-Rank Adapters: arXiv NLP: LambdaMix trains one low-rank adapter per objective and mixes them at inference according to a preference vector, achieving higher hypervolume than specialist or merging baselines on helpfulness-harmlessness and math-instruction tasks. Source: arxiv.org

How Much Conversation History Does an AI Agent Need?: arXiv NLP: Increasing passage budget on 1,100 BEAM questions raised mean scores, but gains diminished at higher budgets while abstention scores sometimes rose as context shrank. Source: arxiv.org

Language as Trait, Politeness as Strategy: Controlled Tests of Linguistic Effects in LLM Social-Deduction Agents: arXiv NLP: Neither linguistic range nor politeness strategy altered trust, votes, or night actions in 30 LURK Werewolf games after multiple-comparison correction. Source: arxiv.org

ROSE: Risk-Aware Orthogonal Subspace Navigation for Lifelong Knowledge Editing in Multimodal Large Language Models: arXiv NLP: The framework restricts edits to orthogonal complements of prior subspaces while using risk masks to protect stability-critical parameters, maintaining performance across 1,000 sequential multimodal edits. Source: arxiv.org

Are These Programmers Ripe Enough to Eat? Evaluating How LLMs Handle Lexical Knowledge Conflicts in Translation: arXiv NLP: Prompt-supplied contextual meanings overrode prior lexical knowledge more often for low-frequency words and supportive sentence contexts, with larger models showing stronger contextual reliance. Source: arxiv.org

Compaction, decay and reinforcement in a two-tier conversational memory: a paired evaluation against retrieval-augmented generation: arXiv NLP: Atomic-fact compaction outperformed raw-transcript retrieval on LoCoMo by 6.8 points at 34% fewer tokens when answers depended on values rather than phrasing. Source: arxiv.org

CodeQuant2: Enhanced Outlier Mitigation and Adaptive Clustering for Low-Precision LRMs: arXiv NLP: The clustering-based LUT quantizer with rotation, scaling, and layer-adaptive codebooks delivered accuracy gains on reasoning benchmarks plus up to 2.85× speedup over BF16. Source: arxiv.org

Improving Contextual Retrieval for Long Documents using Semantic Chunking and Hybrid Retrieval Strategies: arXiv NLP: A hybrid knowledge-graph plus dense-vector pipeline with semantic chunking outperformed standalone vector and graph baselines on the MultiHop-RAG dataset. Source: arxiv.org

Structure is in the zoom: probing neural symmetry through dimensionality scaling: arXiv NLP: Participation-ratio slopes against subsample size separate into a sampling floor and an axis-aligned shift; only the latter tracks known symmetries in mouse V1 and language-model layers. Source: arxiv.org

Subliminal Learning in Vision-Language Models: arXiv NLP: Biased teachers transferred animal and facial preferences to students through numbers generated on an unrelated geometric task, with transfer strength increasing alongside training data volume. Source: arxiv.org

How Small Can We Go? Calibrating Activation-Cache Compression for SAE Training: arXiv NLP: Standard int4 and PCA compression lost more SAE features than a random reseed on GPT-2 and Pythia caches, while per-channel standardization recovered performance at the same file size. Source: arxiv.org

Turkish Multimodal Video Fact-Checking: A New Dataset and a Comparison of Zero-Shot Agentic and Fine-Tuned Systems: arXiv NLP: The first Turkish video fact-checking dataset was paired with a 3MFact-TR agent pipeline and a LoRA-fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B, both evaluated on accuracy and macro F1. Source: arxiv.org

ChainFlow: Few-Step Latent Flow Matching for Speculative Decoding: arXiv NLP: The flow-matching drafter conditioned on target hidden states achieved 39% and 11% higher speedups than EAGLE-3 and DFlash respectively on Qwen3.5 models inside vLLM. Source: arxiv.org

Greedy Byte-Pair Encoding Achieves at Least 0.361 of the Optimal Compression: arXiv NLP: The classic greedy BPE merge rule was proven to reach at least 0.361 of the optimal string-length reduction for any string, budget, and tie-breaking policy. Source: arxiv.org

Grok Bot’s usage limits: Why the fine print matters more than the price: CIO: The article examines how Grok’s rate limits and fine-print constraints affect practical usage more than headline pricing. Source: cio.com

Things to Try This Week

  • Obtain a free EXAONE API key and test K-EXAONE 2.5 on Korean-language agent or tool-calling tasks to compare against current open models.
  • Wrap lengthy AI-generated PR descriptions in HTML details/summary tags following Simon Willison’s pattern to keep GitHub pages readable.
  • Run the RL-ATTACK code on your own models to measure how quickly black-box attacks can degrade performance on your specific tasks.
  • Experiment with the BDE survey-history schema on your own respondent data to see whether adding an Evaluation block improves prediction accuracy.

On the Horizon

  • LG plans to release a frontier-level model in January following the K-EXAONE 2.5 validation run.
  • Additional open-weight models are expected to publish free APIs or weights in the coming weeks as competition in the agentic segment intensifies.
  • More multilingual and low-resource benchmarks are likely to appear as teams address gaps exposed by current English-centric evaluations.
  • Further work on black-box attack methods and corresponding defenses will continue as the community studies model robustness.
