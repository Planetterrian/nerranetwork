Space data centers could cost one hundred times Earth builds, Google's energy chief said.

By Patrick November 02, 2026 Ep 41 8 min read Listen to podcast
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top News Company Desk Also Today The Counterpoint The Thread: Fifty million agents, Meta's Muse on Microsoft's container On the Calendar The Tape

What You Need to Know: Google's head of data center energy said orbital data centers could cost up to one hundred times sites on Earth, even as Google tests AI server chips in orbit. Microsoft is opening Copilot to McGraw Hill's licensed curriculum, and Apple's unreleased HomeView software lists a HomeKit Secure Video camera mode ahead of a November 13 launch. Gemini can now rewrite a whole Google Slides deck from one prompt.

Top News

Google energy chief says space data centers could cost up to 100 times Earth sites: The Information

Data centers in space could cost up to 100 times as much as those on Earth, Amanda Peterson Corio, Google's head of data center energy, said at The Information's WTF summit. The remark is a cost check from a company that is itself testing AI server chips in orbit. Corio's line was to explore terrestrial options before going extraterrestrial. The Information tied the comment to SpaceX orbital data-center plans that underpin part of a roughly $1.8 trillion market capitalization. Yesterday's coverage had her planning sites for 2031 or later; today's figure is the multiple on leaving the ground. Source: theinformation.com

McGraw Hill will plug licensed course content into Microsoft Copilot: EdTech Innovation Hub

McGraw Hill plans a Microsoft 365 Copilot plugin that connects K–12, higher education, and professional learning content through Model Context Protocol, so educators and students can summarize, translate, or adapt licensed materials into lesson plans and study aids. A limited beta is set for early 2027, only for eligible McGraw Hill higher-education and school-district customers who already hold Microsoft 365 Copilot licenses. Dave Cortese, McGraw Hill's chief digital information officer, said the collaboration expands into new channels "while maintaining control over how our content is used and valued." Deirdre Quarnstrom, vice president of Microsoft Education Experiences, said bringing those strengths into Copilot can help educators create more relevant learning experiences "while giving institutions a secure, governed way to adopt AI responsibly." The plugin sits beside McGraw Hill's own tools, including AI Reader, Sharpen, Learning Studio, Writing Assistant, and Teacher Assistant. McGraw Hill has also selected Copilot as its enterprise AI productivity tool. Interested institutions can register through the publisher's AI connector beta program. Source: edtechinnovationhub.com

Gemini can edit an entire Google Slides deck from one prompt: MIXED News

Google is rolling out a Gemini update that can edit multiple slides, or a whole presentation, from a single prompt in Google Slides, changing content, visual style, or matching another deck used as a style reference. Edits land on editable slides, not a flattened image, and the side panel then summarizes what changed, so undo and version history still apply. Suggested jobs include consistent title styles, removing page numbers, recoloring a deck, expanding or simplifying text from Drive files, condensing slides, or adding slides. Gemini chooses which slides to touch, so the result still needs review. The update is in English, on an extended rollout that began October 29, and can take longer than 15 days. Eligible plans are Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, Google AI Pro for Education, and personal Google AI Pro and Ultra. Workspace customers get promotional access to higher multi-slide creation limits through at least December 1, 2026. This is separate from the editor inside the Gemini chat app. Source: mixed-news.com

A Gemini Gmail suit is refiled after a judge found no concrete harm: PPC Land

PPC Land reported that Google faces a refiled suit over Gemini and Gmail after a judge found no concrete harm. Source: ppc.land

Trump administration withholds AI testing framework records: The Information

The Trump administration declined to produce key documents on its voluntary AI testing framework, despite a September agreement with the nonprofit Protect Democracy Project after a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit. The Information reported that the refusal came in a letter sent to the nonprofit on Friday. Source: theinformation.com

Company Desk

HomeView software lists a HomeKit camera mode and ships on version 26.5: MacRumors

Code sleuth pdfu found settings strings in HomeView Software 27.2 for four camera modes — Off, Detect Activity, Stream, and "Stream & Allow Recording" — plus schedules for home, away, or asleep, and a HomeView-specific 1080p/30fps path with microphone input that the software selects for HomeKit Secure Video sessions. Other strings limit streams and recordings to home members, show a green light and play a sound when a stream is viewed or recording starts, and notify users when camera settings change. An away schedule is listed, but the strings suggest streaming and recording can be enabled from the HomeView itself only while people are home. HomeView launches Friday, November 13, starting at 299. Separately, AppleDB data cited by MacRumors say the hub will initially ship with HomeView software 26.5, described as six months old, and prompt an update to 27.2 during setup. Source: macrumors.com

Also Today

Apple's five new products go on sale Friday: MacRumors

The OLED iPad mini, M6 iMac, M6 14-inch MacBook Pro, and the first touchscreen MacBook Pros — 14-inch from $2,899 and 16-inch from $3,399, with the mini from $699, the iMac from $1,499, and the non-touch M6 Pro from $1,999 — are set for Friday, November 6, except the base 14-inch touchscreen in Space Black, which ships in three to four weeks, and Silber, in seven to ten business days. Source: macrumors.com

Three more iPads are penciled in for the first half of 2027: 9to5Mac

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman said Apple plans a new iPad Air, a new base iPad, and a new iPad Pro in the first half of 2027, with OLED on the Air and a vapor chamber on the Pro. Source: 9to5mac.com

Google's Computer Use leaves preview: CryptoRank

CryptoRank reported that Google's Computer Use is generally available, while client apps still execute the actions. Source: cryptorank.io

Google Cloud opens free AI courses for finance and legal teams: PYMNTS.com

PYMNTS reported that Google Cloud has launched free AI courses for financial-services and legal teams. Source: pymnts.com

Meta and Fourier move to a second humanoid version: marketscreener.com

Marketscreener reported that Meta Platforms and Fourier are working on a second version of the humanoid project. Source: marketscreener.com

AWS publishes a cloud blueprint for centralized crypto exchanges: bloomingbit

Bloomingbit reported that AWS unveiled a cloud-based blueprint for centralized crypto exchanges, aimed at speed and custody security. Source: en.bloomingbit.io

The Counterpoint

Finish the ground options before treating orbit as a plan: The Information

The 100-times line is not a build schedule. Corio's own instruction at the same summit was to explore all terrestrial options before going extraterrestrial, which leaves the orbital chip test as a probe, not a decision that the multiple is acceptable. The number that decides it is whether any terrestrial path Google is still willing to exhaust closes the gap that she put at up to 100 times. Source: theinformation.com

The Thread: Fifty million agents, Meta's Muse on Microsoft's container

Microsoft's quarter ended September 30, 2026, is the bill for the layer Satya Nadella is calling an operating system for work. Microsoft Cloud revenue was $63.9 billion, up 30 percent from a year earlier. Total revenue was $92.3 billion, up 19 percent. Operating income was $45.9 billion, up 21 percent. Azure revenue rose 48 percent. Reported net income was $36.6 billion, up 32 percent. Adjusted net income, removing the impact of investments in OpenAI, was $36.4 billion, up 18 percent. EdTech Innovation Hub carried those figures from the quarter and from Nadella's LinkedIn post on the same results.

Copilot daily users increased nearly 50 percent from the previous quarter and more than fivefold from a year earlier. Customers with more than 50,000 Copilot seats increased eightfold year over year. Nadella also reported a nearly 20 percent quarterly improvement in user satisfaction; the post did not say how that measure was calculated. The latest Copilot update brings Chat, Cowork, Autopilot, and Code into one experience. Dynamics 365 capabilities are being pulled into Copilot so employees can reach business processes through the assistant. On GitHub Copilot Fusion, which coordinates multiple models, he wrote that it delivers frontier-level quality at up to 77 percent lower cost. Foundry revenue more than tripled from a year earlier. Customers building with multiple AI models increased fivefold. Revenue from open-source models grew tenfold. Nearly 60,000 customers were accessing context through Microsoft IQ, up 25 percent from the previous quarter. More than 50 million agents were registered in Agent 365, up 30 percent quarter over quarter.

The shared machine is Microsoft Execution Containers, MXC, which links Windows and Agent 365 so an agent has a controlled environment on a device. Nadella named Meta Muse among the agents adopting it, next to OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude Code. Meta is not the landlord. Muse is a guest on Microsoft's runtime. Perception, Microsoft's security tools, sits outside the model and governs identity, permissions, and which actions an agent is authorized to take.

That split changes the spend and the product. Microsoft's quarterly capital expenditure was $45.2 billion. Cash from operations rose 29 percent to $58 billion. Free cash flow after property and equipment was $18.6 billion. For the following quarter Microsoft expects revenue of $96 billion to $97.1 billion, and Azure growth of 49 to 50 percent excluding currency, with demand still ahead of supply. Nadella's stated priority is maximizing intelligence per task while optimizing cost and performance. For Meta, Muse on MXC is a shopping assistant that can run inside another company's device container, not only inside Meta's own apps. For Microsoft, a named Meta agent on the adoption list is evidence the container is a platform, not only a Copilot feature. No source today dates the next MXC release.
