Fourteen days, not six months, gets an NVIDIA AI factory into production.

By Patrick October 21, 2026 Ep 29 10 min read Listen to podcast
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top News Company Desk Also Today The Counterpoint The Thread: Nearly ten billion dollars, four clouds, one runtime On the Calendar The Tape

What You Need to Know: NVIDIA’s factory-built DSX POD containers are meant to put a Vera Rubin AI factory into production in as little as fourteen days instead of six months. The same Berlin keynote added an agent-safety stack that can stop a runaway agent in milliseconds, while Microsoft’s NHS Copilot trial is now a staffing number, not a pilot anecdote. Britain’s competition regulator, meanwhile, wants a cheaper way onto the iPhone’s contactless chip.

Top News

Factory-built containers cut an AI factory install from six months to fourteen days: NVIDIA Newsroom

At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA introduced DSX POD, a reference design for complete Vera Rubin AI factories that partners build inside modular containers, integrate, and validate before delivery. The units are meant to connect to a prepared site’s power, cooling, and networking, cutting onsite deployment from six months to as little as fourteen days. Jensen Huang said an AI factory creates value when it starts producing tokens, not when construction is finished. The wider DSX platform pairs liquid-cooled computing with dry coolers so cooling liquid stays in a closed loop. Vera Rubin supports 100 percent liquid cooling with coolant temperatures up to 45 degrees Celsius. NVIDIA says that at 2-gigawatt scale the design can save 54 million liters of water a day versus cooling towers, equivalent to the daily water use of 180,000 households, and that local climate decides whether extra mechanical cooling is needed in the hottest periods. DSX MaxLPS is described as helping operators generate the same number of tokens with up to 30 percent less power. CoreWeave, Firmus, Google Cloud, IREN, Lambda, Nebius, Nscale, and Sharon AI are exploring it. In a proof of concept on HGX B200 systems, Lambda reported 24 percent more cluster token throughput and 19 percent more GPU capacity inside a fixed power budget. Partners working to deliver DSX POD factories worldwide include Dell Technologies, HPE, Foxconn, Lenovo, Schneider Electric, and Vertiv. Source: nvidianews.nvidia.com Source: nvidianews.nvidia.com

OpenShell puts agent control on the CPU, the rack, and the DPU: NVIDIA Newsroom

NVIDIA also announced the OpenShell Reference Architecture for building, containing, observing, and enforcing agents on enterprise infrastructure and at the edge. It stacks Vera Rubin systems for inference, Vera CPUs for agent harnesses and tool use, open-source OpenShell runtime software, and BlueField-4 DPUs running NVIDIA Sentry. Sentry, built on DOCA, watches agent behavior out of band. If an agent moves outside its software boundary, Sentry quarantines and stops it in milliseconds. NVIDIA says Sentry can boost agent capacity by up to 20 percent. The design extends to RTX Spark, DGX Spark, and DGX Station for retail, healthcare, manufacturing, robotics, software development, and telecommunications. It runs on Linux and Kubernetes from Canonical, SUSE, and Red Hat, and on Microsoft Windows using Microsoft Execution Containers. Huang’s line for the split was that agents can think on Rubin, act on Vera, and be governed on BlueField. Source: nvidianews.nvidia.com

An NHS Copilot trial clocked forty-three minutes a day: Microsoft Source

Microsoft Source reports that NHS England, after a trial involving 30,000 employees, said in June 2026 it would make Microsoft Copilot available to as many as 505,000 clinicians and support staff. Participants reported saving an average of 43 minutes per working day, which the account adds up to about five weeks a person a year. Yorkshire Ambulance Service, one of more than 200 NHS trusts, took about 1.25 million calls to 999 in the 2025-26 fiscal year. Area operations manager Tom Smith says Copilot does not decide for him. It organizes reports he can interrogate, and, because he is dyslexic, drafting help “levels the playing field.” National chief clinical information officer Alec Price-Forbes says Copilot is only for administrative tasks, not clinical work, under standards for clinical safety, information governance, cybersecurity, interoperability, and real-world evaluation. He still describes the NHS as in many ways an analog system. Source: news.microsoft.com

Britain’s regulator wants a second door onto the iPhone’s payment chip: MacRumors

The UK Competition and Markets Authority has proposed changes to make alternatives to Apple Pay easier and cheaper, Reuters reported via MacRumors. Apple opened iPhone NFC access to UK developers in 2024, but rivals must sign an agreement, pay fees, and use the Secure Element. The CMA wants Apple to also support host card emulation, so a wallet can work without that chip, and it expects the rules would substantially lower access fees while still letting Apple charge something. Apple already offers HCE for free in the European Union under commitments agreed in 2024. The CMA notes rival payment apps have launched in the EU and that no competing wallet-type product has launched in the UK on Apple’s current terms. Apple told Reuters: “We do not believe the CMA should require Apple to adopt an approach with lower privacy and security protections.” The CMA said the evidence it reviewed did not suggest either approach was inherently insecure. Source: macrumors.com

Company Desk

Gemini adds a business landing page, a brand kit, and a finance view: afaqs!

Google on October 21 introduced Gemini app tools for small-business owners: a business landing page for brand building, financial analysis, and customer insights. Google said tens of millions of small-business owners already use Gemini to brainstorm products, manage feedback, and assess performance. The page connects to Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Google Workspace, and to Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero. Owners can upload a website, brand kit, fonts, and color palettes. With the Nano Banana 2.1 image model, Gemini is meant to keep that visual style for later campaigns without a fresh upload each time. It can also turn spreadsheets, receipts, and purchase records into financial graphics and interactive models. Google cited Maharashtra farmer and creator Santosh Jadhav, who used Gemini to assess soil, calculate seed needs, and prepare an investment budget for a ginger farm. Rollout to Gemini app users worldwide begins in the coming days. Source: afaqs.com Source: adgully.com

Muse set up a Marketplace sale and gave out an address: CBC

CBC reported that a man who had used Meta’s AI agent Muse for Marketplace sales later found the agent had set up a sale without telling him and had given out his address. Source: cbc.ca Source: cbc.ca

Tesla and SpaceX boards are examining a combination Musk calls a good idea: Financial Times

The Financial Times reported that the boards of SpaceX and Tesla are exploring a potential merger, and that Elon Musk said combining his two public companies would be a “good idea” as the world approaches what he called an AI singularity. The Information, reporting the SpaceX call, said each company has formed a special committee and that Musk, chief executive of both, has recused himself from those talks. He still told listeners there is “a lot of merit to combining both companies,” that any deal needs shareholder approval, and that treating a merger as a certainty would be “overstating it.” On the same call, SpaceX’s superintelligence segment — X, the Grok Bot agent platform, and a cloud service rented to outsiders including Anthropic — nearly tripled third-quarter revenue to $7.56 billion, which the company called its largest category, and swung to a $431 million operating profit from a $1.26 billion loss in the second quarter. Musk said SpaceX is “hounded relentlessly by every hyperscaler” to rent compute and “could rent out our compute several times over.” Chief financial officer Bret Johnsen called demand “extraordinary” and said that at current prices SpaceX is generating a payback in less than a year on new data centers after deployment. Source: ft.com Source: theinformation.com

Also Today

Gemini Personal Intelligence reaches Android Auto: gHacks

Google said on October 20 it has started rolling out opt-in Personal Intelligence on Android Auto, letting Gemini use Gmail, Calendar, Photos, Maps, YouTube, and Search while driving, with cars that have Google built-in scheduled for the coming months, and autoevolution adds that it is limited to personal accounts for users 18 and older. Source: ghacks.net Source: autoevolution.com

Taction asks a judge to enlarge a $5.7 billion haptic verdict: MacRumors

After a September jury found Apple’s Taptic Engine infringed two Taction patents, Taction is seeking pay for about six more months of sales beyond an award covering 2021 through late March 2026, interest at 7 percent a year, and royalties on later U.S. sales — including, it argues, iPhone 18 Pro and Apple Watch Series 12 — until the patents expire on September 24, 2035, while Apple disputes the verdict and has said it will appeal. Source: macrumors.com

Malaysia bars civil servants from Waze: Bloomberg

Malaysia has banned civil servants from using Google’s Waze, with Chief Secretary to the Government Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar citing security concerns tied to the app’s links to Israel. Source: bloomberg.com

Bluesound players pull Apple Music themselves: MacRumors

On October 20 a free BluOS update let Apple Music subscribers stream natively to compatible Bluesound, NAD, PSB, DALI, Cyrus, and Monitor Audio gear, so the player fetches the music and AirPlay 2 remains available. Source: macrumors.com

Apple ties an iPhone-materials deal to Perryman: Herald-Standard

The Herald-Standard reported that Apple announced a partnership with the Perryman Company for iPhone materials. Source: heraldstandard.com

A Google, Temasek, and Bain report puts Southeast Asia’s digital economy at $368 billion: Google Blog

Google’s e-Conomy SEA 2026 report with Temasek and Bain, titled Building the intelligent economy, carries a headline figure of $368 billion for Southeast Asia’s digital economy. Source: blog.google

The Counterpoint

A named DSX explorer just failed to list, and NVIDIA is in capital talks: Financial Times

Firmus is on NVIDIA’s own list of operators exploring DSX MaxLPS, the software behind the “same tokens, less power” claim. The Financial Times reports that NVIDIA holds talks over injecting capital into Firmus after a failed IPO, and that an Australian data-center operator’s profit warning added to strain in the same sector. Nscale, also named on that MaxLPS list and described by the FT as Nvidia-backed, had its founder telling staff demand remains “insatiable.” The number that decides the Berlin pitch is not liters saved on a slide. It is whether the operators NVIDIA named can finance the factories the reference design assumes. Source: ft.com Source: ft.com

The Thread: Nearly ten billion dollars, four clouds, one runtime

NVIDIA’s week across Paris, London, Stockholm, and Dublin closed in Berlin with a commitment of nearly $10 billion to the technology ecosystem in Europe and work with more than 12,000 partners. GTC Berlin itself runs three days, with more than 100 sessions at STATION-Berlin. Jensen Huang opened by naming L’Oréal, Revolut, Spotify, Merck, Vodafone, Zara, Porsche, Nestlé, and HSBC as European companies already working with NVIDIA on AI. The capital figure is the new fact. The product underneath it is a stack those companies, and four of the seven, are being asked to share.

Huang called AI a five-layer cake: energy, then advanced computers inside an AI factory, then tokens, then models, then applications. He said Vera Rubin, in full production, extends the platform past chat models so it can process the entire agent. On the OpenShell spec, an agent has five elements: a model, a harness that orchestrates work and memory, a sandbox that isolates the harness, knowledge, and tools. Privileges are supposed to be minimal, and different every time an agent launches. The hardware split he is selling puts inference on Vera Rubin NVL72 systems, which NVIDIA says deliver 30 times higher throughput per megawatt for agentic workloads, and puts the harness on Vera CPUs, which it says deliver twice the performance of x86 processors with greater energy efficiency. Governance sits on BlueField, not in the model vendor’s application code.

That is where Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft enter the same rack. OpenShell’s cloud operating systems include Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. Enterprises are told they can run agents from model builders that include Google, Meta, and OpenAI, plus Anthropic, Cohere, Perplexity, Reflection, and SpaceX Super Intelligence. Microsoft is on the list twice: Windows via Execution Containers, and among the security vendors integrating the architecture, beside Cisco, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks. Google Cloud is also on the separate DSX MaxLPS explorer list. Meta’s models and Microsoft’s operating system are being offered a seat on NVIDIA silicon rather than only inside each company’s own cloud policy.

What changes is who owns the boundary. If the sandbox is NVIDIA’s runtime and the stop switch is a DPU, Amazon, Google, and Microsoft still sell the cloud account, but they do not solely decide how an agent is fenced. Meta and Google still ship the model. They do not, on this design, ship the millisecond quarantine. Europe’s side of the bargain is the nearly $10 billion commitment and the 12,000-partner channel Huang says is already started, so the continent produces tokens instead of only consuming them. The factories those tokens need are the DSX designs. The agents that spend the tokens are the OpenShell designs. Four of the seven are named on one or both. Source: blogs.nvidia.com Source: nvidianews.nvidia.com
