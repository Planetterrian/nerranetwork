Gemini 4 Hydrogen scores 51, one point ahead of Google's larger Argon.

By Patrick November 13, 2026 Ep 52 8 min read Listen to podcast
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top News Company Desk Also Today The Counterpoint The Thread: The mail server under the assistant On the Calendar The Tape

What You Need to Know: Google's Gemini 4 Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, one point ahead of the larger Argon model, and is rolling into Search and the Gemini app. Apple is selling HomeView and a new Apple TV 4K at 299 dollars each, with HomeView kept out of the European Union. Nvidia's board has Jensen Huang's resignation and finance chief Colette Kress as interim chief executive.

Top News

Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen: Mashable

Koray Kavukcuoglu, senior vice president at Google DeepMind, announced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon for speed, and said that at its default high thinking level it surpasses frontier model Gemini 4 Argon. Analytics India Magazine reported a score of 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at that default, against 50 for Argon, rising to 54 at a new extra-high setting. Google said Hydrogen finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at the default high level and scored 57 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.1, against 18.2 percent for Flash. The price is 1.50 dollars per million input tokens and 7.50 dollars per million output tokens across a context window of up to 1 million tokens, and the model is rolling out in Search, the Gemini app, the Gemini API, Google AI Studio and Gemini for business. Kavukcuoglu framed it for code, tests and everyday work, or as the fast engine beside Carbon and Argon; Orchestrator in Google Antigravity switches among the models and, on Terminal-Bench 4.0, landed near Carbon's success rate while using 24 percent less quota and finishing 19 percent faster. Google also said Hydrogen is two times less likely than 3.8 Flash to step past permission boundaries, and that it still hallucinates. Source: mashable.com Source: analyticsindiamag.com

Apple Launches Two Products Today: MacRumors

Apple is selling HomeView and a fourth-generation Apple TV 4K, each priced at 299 dollars, after pre-orders that opened in mid-October. HomeView is a smart-home display running Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, in tabletop and wall-mounted models, and it can recognize up to six users by face, each with a personalized Home Screen. The Apple TV 4K adds Siri AI, the A19 chip, an updated Siri Remote with Find My, 128GB of storage and four-speaker surround sound with HomePod mini 2. Both were announced on October 13 alongside HomePod mini 2, which launched on October 30. HomeView is not sold in the European Union because Siri AI is unavailable on iOS there under current regulatory constraints; Apple said it is "working constructively with the EU in an attempt to find a path forward." Source: macrumors.com

Nvidia announces CEO change as Jensen Huang resigns: Verdict

Nvidia's board said Jensen Huang resigned immediately as president, chief executive and director. The board that was already looking for a chief executive now has that resignation in hand, with Colette Kress — finance chief since September 2013, after nearly 25 years in finance roles at Microsoft and Cisco — as interim chief executive while she keeps the finance job, and a formal search of internal and external candidates underway. Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993; the exit followed reports of an alleged affair with a suspected Chinese spy, and The Information reported that general counsel Tim Teter asked him at home whether that report was true and that Huang said it was. The board named Harvey Jones, former chief executive and executive chairman of Synopsys, Nvidia's first chair; Jones said the board expects operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap to continue without interruption. Bloomberg, citing documents it reviewed, reported that a Chinese company founded by Alice Huang procured smuggled AI chips that were installed at a Shanghai data center with links to Megaspeed. The Financial Times reported that Huang had supported keeping sales of Nvidia chips open to China. Source: verdict.co.uk Source: info-reader-production.herokuapp.com Source: bloomberg.com Source: ft.com

Microsoft 365 Outage Hits 130,000 Users as Outlook, Teams and Copilot Services Fail Worldwide: Coinpaper

By Friday Microsoft said a configuration patch on upstream authentication and routing had improved server stability, and engineers were widening that fix toward regions entering peak business hours. The outage that began Thursday is still listed as service degradation, centered on Exchange Online, so mailbox connections, delayed mail, Teams calendar functions and Copilot's reach into email, calendars and files have been the failures. Copilot executive Charles Lamanna said there was no indication the incident was malicious or cyber activity. Source: coinpaper.com Source: itpro.com

Company Desk

Talks on SpaceX-Tesla Deal Said to Advance: Bloomberg

SpaceX and Tesla are further along in merger talks, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. Those people described a combination Bloomberg said could redefine the scale of global dealmaking. Source: bloomberg.com

Also Today

Malaysia Intensifies Investigation Into Megaspeed Chips: Bloomberg

Malaysia said it is examining whether AI chips a unit of Singapore-based Megaspeed brought into the country were later shipped overseas, tightening scrutiny of a firm tied to the scandal around Jensen Huang's resignation. Source: bloomberg.com

Companies Linked to Alice Huang Invested in DeepSeek: The Information

The Information reported that Century Litong and its Shanghai-listed parent, Jiangsu Lettall Electronics, companies tied to Alice Huang, recently invested in Chinese AI startup DeepSeek. Source: info-reader-production.herokuapp.com

Nscale warns it needs more cash ahead of $35bn IPO: Financial Times

Nvidia-backed neocloud Nscale has told investors it must obtain additional financing to fulfil Anthropic contracts, ahead of a 35-billion-dollar IPO, the Financial Times reported. Source: ft.com

Meta’s AI Glasses Arrive in Indonesia as Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs Issues Warning: Indonesia Expat

Meta's AI glasses have gone on sale in Indonesia, and the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs has issued a warning. Source: indonesiaexpat.id

Google Upgrades Robot “Brain,” Mercedes-Benz Plant Tests Achieve 96% Success Rate: Gasgoo

Gasgoo reported that tests of an upgraded Google robot brain at a Mercedes-Benz plant reached a 96 percent success rate. Source: autonews.gasgoo.com

The Counterpoint

Free accounts still land on Flash-Lite: Notebookcheck

The 51-point score does not mean a free user can choose Hydrogen. Notebookcheck, citing Google, says free users get it only through automatic model selection when Google judges the prompt suitable, that most prompts still go to Flash-Lite, and that turning auto selection off sends every reply to Flash-Lite. On Google's plans page, Google AI Plus at 4.99 dollars a month now lists Hydrogen as its headline model, with twice the free tier's usage limits, and the Pro model starts on Google AI Pro at 19.99 dollars, which also includes Argon. Google's help page on Gemini models still lists Pro under AI Plus, so the two pages disagree. Source: notebookcheck.net

The Thread: The mail server under the assistant

Nearly 130,000 Outlook problem reports showed up on Downdetector in the United States shortly after 1 p.m. Eastern on November 12. Microsoft 365 drew more than 38,000 additional reports and Teams about 4,500, Coinpaper said — user submissions, not a verified customer count. La Veu Lliure put the broader Downdetector tally near 200,000 reports in more than 70 countries. UC Today clocked the Exchange incident from 16:17 GMT Thursday to a 07:25 GMT Friday update that still had the suite in service degradation, more than 15 hours on. At 17:54 GMT Microsoft described an Americas impact; at 18:07 GMT it said users in any region could be hit; at 18:26 GMT it opened a suite-wide incident.

The load was not extra traffic. By 19:18 GMT Microsoft had concluded that many servers failed automated health checks and dropped out, so the machines still up took the work. A process that sets up secure connections became overwhelmed, and those servers stopped processing requests. At 19:06 GMT the company paused deployments and configuration changes, then started reverting a network change that correlated with the start of the impact. Machines brought back online failed again once traffic returned. At 04:10 GMT Friday, steps in some regions were not producing a sustained recovery. At 05:01 GMT the mitigations had driven a surge of calls from routing services onto backend servers, and some machines were stuck in a bad state, blocking a force-sync of the network change. At 06:56 GMT Microsoft reported only marginal success from patching and rebooting.

Teams split on that Exchange line. Joining calls and meetings, group chats, channel messages and standard channels largely kept working. Calendars, scheduling, invites, meeting edits, Live and Virtual events, contact sync, and creating or deleting private or shared channels did not. SharePoint sharing with people outside an organization broke, while files stored in SharePoint stayed available. A separate North America Teams incident, from 15:40 to 17:09 GMT Thursday, was fully resolved. The public Azure status page showed no Azure issues. A Dynamics 365 contact-center incident locked agents out for just under eight hours; Microsoft restored that access at 00:57 GMT Friday, while email, the usual overflow channel, was already impaired.

OpenAI said new Microsoft 365 connections may be broken and tools are not working as expected. Anthropic said mail, calendar and search in its Microsoft 365 connector were failing for most requests, and that Claude for Outlook was affected. Those assistants do not run the mail system; they call it, so a fault in Exchange reaches products Microsoft does not own. Verdict, in its account of the Nvidia leadership change, also noted that earlier this month Microsoft and Nvidia introduced Frontier Launchpad, a support programme for AI software companies in the UK and Ireland.

On the Calendar

Google told Google AI Plus subscribers they would lose the Pro models on November 13, Notebookcheck reported. Source: notebookcheck.net

Apple's HomeView and fourth-generation Apple TV 4K are on sale on November 13, MacRumors reported. Source: macrumors.com
