Meta's assistants are now 2.4 times less likely to forget critical details.

By Patrick November 07, 2026 Ep 46 10 min read Listen to podcast Watch on YouTube
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top News Company Desk Also Today The Counterpoint The Thread: The site Google would not rent On the Calendar The Tape

What You Need to Know: Meta is rolling specialised Muse and Meta AI models it says are 2.4 times less likely to forget critical information and 67 percent better at finishing everyday tasks when something goes wrong. Amazon has opened texting with Alexa for Shopping to an invited group of U.S. customers. Six pallets of Nvidia hardware worth 11.4 million dollars vanished between Oakland and Texas, with no arrests two months later.

Top News

Meta AI models roll out with Active Memory and Active Focus: India Today

Meta said specialised models for Muse and Meta AI, trained with capabilities from its frontier Muse Wonder model, would begin rolling out on Friday. Mark Zuckerberg said on X the models were 2.4 times less likely to forget critical information and 67 percent better at self-correcting and completing everyday tasks. India Today reported an internal evaluation in which the models used important context correctly in 90 percent of tasks, compared with 76 percent previously, and a 2.4-times reduction in forgetting critical information. Active Memory is meant to keep details that stay relevant across chats, rather than treating every conversation as a fresh start. Active Focus is meant to try another approach when a site fails or a booking sticks, and to judge when to work alone and when to ask the user. Subscribers can still use Muse Wonder for more demanding tasks. Meta AI runs across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Messenger. Meta is using Muse Wonder to train specialised models for everyday work instead of giving every user the frontier model. Source: indiatoday.in

Amazon opens texting with Alexa for Shopping: MarketScale

Amazon said on November 6 that texting with Alexa for Shopping is rolling out by invitation to a small group of U.S. customers, and will reach millions more in the coming weeks. Customers accept the terms, link a phone number, and the agent appears in contacts as Alexa Shopping. Amazon said more than 400 million customers used Alexa for Shopping in the last 12 months, and use of its agentic features has grown more than eight times since the start of the year. The agent can add items and complete a purchase with approval, reorder, set a weekly grocery order, watch for a price drop, show up to a year of price history, send new-release alerts, and check an order. Requests started over text carry over to the Amazon Shopping app, Amazon.com and Alexa-enabled devices, and the other way around. MarketScale, citing TechCrunch, also reported list management, web search, product comparisons, photo search for similar listings, and preferences learned over time. The launch follows Amazon blocking Meta's Muse agent from querying its site in September. Source: marketscale.com

Nvidia hardware worth 11.4 million dollars vanished en route to Texas: Ars Technica

Ars Technica reported that six pallets of Nvidia hardware worth 11.4 million dollars disappeared between Oakland and Texas, with no arrests two months later. Source: arstechnica.com

Hands-on weighs the OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro: MacRumors

MacRumors' hands-on of the PROLED MacBook Pro, now with customers, puts the 14-inch model about half a millimeter thinner and just over three pounds, about half a pound lighter than before, and the 16-inch model almost a millimeter thinner at four pounds, 0.7 pounds lighter. The 16-inch screen measures 16.3 inches rather than 16.2. The machines start at 2,899 dollars and use the same M5 Pro and M5 Max chips as the mini-LED models Apple is still selling, which MacRumors framed as about a 400-dollar premium for the OLED panel, the lighter body and touch. A Dynamic Island replaces the notch. The glowing lid logo is back. Touch can swipe, tap, drag and pinch; MacRumors said it does not replace the trackpad, and the sturdier hinge still jiggles depending on where you tap. Source: macrumors.com

Company Desk

Google Ventures takes nearly 3 million Qualcomm shares: The Information

Google Ventures has acquired nearly 3 million shares of Qualcomm because Qualcomm bought chip-software startup Modular, which Google partly owned. The Information cited a securities filing on Friday and a Google spokesperson. Source: theinformation.com

Systems Manager for SAP now covers HANA scale-out: Amazon Web Services

AWS Systems Manager for SAP now lets customers register a multi-node SAP HANA system as one application, with backup through AWS Backup, application-aware start and stop, and credential updates. Until this support, scale-out customers on AWS could not use the service to automate backups, start and stop, or credential rotation. Scale-out spreads one database across hosts that share a system ID and a file system, and the nodes can sit in more than one Availability Zone. Source: aws.amazon.com

Meta pauses commercial-terms changes that had been set for November 20: ad-hoc-news.de

Meta has paused proposed updates to its Commercial Terms, Product Catalog Terms, Self-Serve Ad Terms and Ad Creative Generative AI Terms, Yahoo Finance reported on Friday, November 6, so the changes will not take effect on November 20 and the existing terms stay in force while the company consults stakeholders. The proposed rules had drawn criticism because they could have allowed Instagram and Facebook to scrape websites linked to Meta Business accounts for AI training. The same report cited legal and regulatory pressure, including action involving media groups in Norway and Brazil and a UK competition investigation. Source: ad-hoc-news.de

US intelligence links Jensen Huang to an executive recruited by China: Financial Times

Separate from the request that he brief Congress, the Financial Times reported that American intelligence believes Nvidia chief Jensen Huang had a romantic relationship with an executive recruited by China to gather information on the U.S. company. The Verge, citing The New York Times, named that executive as Alice Huang of Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer, and said China's Ministry of State Security enlisted her to glean information about American AI chips and potentially influence Nvidia to push for its most advanced chips to be made available to China. The Times account, as relayed by The Verge, also said she has relationships with executives at the highest levels of the AI chip industry, including leaders at Gigabyte. Source: ft.com Source: theverge.com

SpaceX director Donald Harrison resigns as Musk argues for a Tesla merger: The Information

SpaceX disclosed in a filing Friday that longtime director Donald Harrison has resigned. Harrison, a president of corporate development at Google, is leaving the board while Elon Musk advocates that SpaceX merge with Tesla. Source: theinformation.com

Also Today

Dave Wehner leaves Meta to become Fireworks CFO: The Information

Meta chief strategy officer Dave Wehner, who joined Facebook in 2012 as vice president of corporate finance and business planning and became CFO in 2014, confirmed on X Friday that he is leaving to be chief financial officer of AI inference startup Fireworks. Source: theinformation.com

Prime Video sets a two-part Melania Trump series: WION

Brett Ratner directs MELANIA, The Series for Amazon MGM Studios, Muse Films, New Element Media and RatPac, with episodes titled Family & Trust and Pride & Honor, and with Melania Trump and Marc Beckman as executive producers, focused on her return to the White House rather than the 20 days before the inauguration. Source: wionews.com

Redshift SUPER columns take strings up to 16 million bytes: Amazon Web Services

With large-string support turned on, Amazon Redshift's SUPER type can hold individual strings up to 16 million bytes and run string functions in memory past the 65,535-byte VARCHAR limit, on patch 206 or later. Source: aws.amazon.com

DeepMind reports a 1,600-times faster path to older encryption flaws: Interesting Engineering

Interesting Engineering reported that Google DeepMind found a way 1,600 times faster to expose flaws in older encryption. Source: interestingengineering.com

DeepMind adds drug-discovery tools to Antigravity: MobiHealthNews

MobiHealthNews reported that Google DeepMind has added drug-discovery tools to Antigravity. Source: mobihealthnews.com

Google announces smartwatch hypertension detection: jetstream.blog

Google announced an AI technology it calls AI-PPG-ACC-HTN to detect signs of hypertension on smartwatches. Source: jetstream.blog

Apple TV renews Knife Edge for a third season: 9to5Mac

Apple TV renewed Knife Edge: Chasing Michelin Stars for a third season hours after season two ended. Source: 9to5mac.com

Epic, Samsung, Oura and Roblox push back on Apple and DOJ subpoenas: 9to5Mac

9to5Mac reported that Epic, Samsung, Oura, Roblox and others are pushing back on subpoenas issued by Apple and the Justice Department in their antitrust case. Source: 9to5mac.com

Apple is named in expected congressional inquiries: 9to5Mac

Reuters and The Washington Post, via 9to5Mac, reported that Apple is among tech companies that could face congressional investigations, including over ICE-tracking apps and Trump donations, as Democrats prepare to take both chambers in January. Source: 9to5mac.com

Forefront Dermatology cuts manual reporting 85 percent on Fabric: Microsoft

Microsoft said Forefront Dermatology cut manual reporting 85 percent with Microsoft Fabric and Formula5. Source: microsoft.com

The Counterpoint

The 90 percent figure is Meta's own test: India Today

The number that decides whether Active Memory is a product change or a lab score is that 90 percent. India Today reported it from Meta's internal evaluation, up from 76 percent, not from an outside benchmark, a regulator or a rival's trial. The same account says subscribers still turn to Muse Wonder for demanding tasks, because Meta is not putting the frontier model in front of every user. India Today left the open question as whether the upgrades make the assistants more useful in everyday life. Source: indiatoday.in

The Thread: The site Google would not rent

Crusoe, an eight-year-old Denver data-center developer recently valued at 30.9 billion dollars, confirmed Friday that it has sold an unfinished project in southeastern Wyoming to Google. A day after Google's Wyoming plan was public, that confirmation is the change: Crusoe had tried and failed to land Google as a customer, and Google is taking over development after reportedly raising concerns about cost and construction timeline under Crusoe. Google has said it plans to invest 17 billion dollars in the campus. Crusoe announced the project last year with Blackstone-backed Tallgrass and had planned to scale it to 10 gigawatts. Bloomberg first reported the sale in June. The setback did not stop a 3.9 billion dollar round in September, when Crusoe added three board members, including JB Straubel, the Redwood Materials founder and Tesla board member who invested in Crusoe in 2021. Crusoe later became the first customer of Straubel's energy-storage business.

The shared problem is delivery, not a logo on the fence. Independent developers have to secure equipment and power, and local opposition can slow a site, which is how a company valued in the tens of billions can still lose the project it pitched. Google did not leave the relationship. Crusoe is developing another data center for Google near Amarillo, Texas. Its Abilene campus is being built in phases for Oracle and OpenAI, and a separate 900-megawatt campus next door is for Microsoft, with the first building expected online in mid-2027. Meta is a Crusoe customer. Bloomberg reported that trading firm Jane Street signed a 13 billion dollar, five-year cloud deal with Crusoe. The business is three products at once: leasing space to customers who bring their own GPUs, renting Crusoe's own GPUs, and selling inference. Chase Lochmiller, now chief executive, and Cully Cavness started the firm as crypto mining powered by natural gas that would otherwise have been flared.

What shifts is who holds the construction risk on the Wyoming ground, and who does not. Google is now the developer on a campus it would not book as a tenant. Microsoft and Meta stay on Crusoe's customer list at other sites, so the same developer still sits in their power plans after losing this one. Tesla's connection is the board, not a vehicle: Straubel joined Crusoe's board in the September round while those campuses serve the build Google, Microsoft and Meta are all funding. The dated step a source actually names is that first Microsoft building in mid-2027. Source: techcrunch.com Source: bloomberg.com

On the Calendar

On November 20 at 10 a.m. local time, Apple reopens its store at The Forum in Carlsbad, California, in the original 2008 location after more than a year of renovations. Source: macrumors.com

On November 20, Amazon Prime Video premieres MELANIA, The Series, its two-part docuseries. Source: wionews.com

In December, Google's other Android XR launch partner has confirmed a release for the first batch of glasses, after a pre-order date earlier this week. Source: 9to5google.com
