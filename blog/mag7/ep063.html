Seven days to clear a Bedrock safety case, or Amazon may suspend the account.

By Patrick November 24, 2026 Ep 63 8 min read Listen to podcast
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top News Company Desk Also Today The Counterpoint The Thread: Who still has to approve the agent The Tape

What You Need to Know: Amazon’s Bedrock trusted-access API is generally available, and a customer has seven days to answer a safety case before a missed window can put the account at risk of suspension. Meta opened its Muse agent to adults in the UK the same day, and Nvidia’s board put Jensen Huang back as president and chief executive eleven days after he left. Australia raised the ceiling on penalties for the world’s biggest technology providers to 5% of global revenue.

Top News

Amazon Bedrock’s trusted-access API is generally available, with a seven-day safety case: Unite.AI

Amazon Web Services said on November 24, 2026 that the trusted-access program API on Amazon Bedrock is generally available, one interface to discover, request and enroll in model-provider programs. The first two offerings, Anthropic’s Enterprise Frontier Safeguards and Enterprise Data Controls, are in public preview, covering Claude Fable and Claude Mythos, plus Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Sonnet 5.5 when used through the Cyber Verification Program. Eligibility and enrollment are separate: the provider, or AWS with the provider’s authorization, approves eligibility, and the customer then accepts terms at account, organization or project scope. Under Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, a flagged case lists requests and points to transcripts in the customer’s bucket; the customer must answer within seven calendar days with violation or no_violation, the first valid response is final, and repeated failures to respond can lead to account suspension. AWS and Anthropic do not charge for either program and do not change model pricing, behavior or rate limits. Source: unite.ai · aws.amazon.com

Meta opens Muse to UK adults, and purchases still wait for a yes: Meta Newsroom

Muse, already offered in other markets, is available from today to adults in the UK, in the Muse app and in WhatsApp, powered by Muse Spark, the first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs. Each Muse gets its own virtual computer in the cloud; Meta says information and logins stay there, not in its other services, and no one else’s Muse can access them. Purchases need approval every time, with checkout through Link, built by Stripe, or Shop Pay from Shopify on supported stores. The Independent reported that regulators issued warnings after UK brands signed up to the assistant. Source: about.fb.com · about.fb.com · politico.eu · independent.co.uk

Nvidia’s board reinstates Jensen Huang, who is due before the Senate on December 3: Bloomberg

Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as president and chief executive eleven days after he left following links to a former Megaspeed executive and a suspected Chinese spy. Bloomberg reported that the return followed lobbying by President Donald Trump, and that investors say the return has done little to resolve the larger questions over the AI buildout. Former Reserve Bank of India governor Raghuram Rajan said the return puts a spotlight on corporate governance and on Nvidia’s pivotal role in the boom. Huang is set to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on December 3. Source: bloomberg.com · bloomberg.com · bloomberg.com

The US blacklists Megaspeed and an Alibaba subsidiary after the Nvidia scandal: Bloomberg

The Trump administration put Singapore-based Megaspeed International on a US trade blacklist that bars it from buying advanced AI chips, targeting the firm at the center of the scandal that briefly cost Jensen Huang his job. The same crackdown blacklisted a Singaporean subsidiary of Alibaba Group Holding, drawing one of China’s biggest technology companies into the Megaspeed action. Source: bloomberg.com · bloomberg.com

Australia’s Digital Duty of Care fine can reach 5% of global revenue: Bloomberg

The world’s biggest technology providers may face fines of as much as 5% of their global revenue under Australia’s new Digital Duty of Care law after the government toughened penalties to secure passage. Source: bloomberg.com

Company Desk

Search AI Mode adds Wallet, with Drive and Gemini chats next: PCMag

Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode, which since launch earlier this year could use Gmail, Calendar and Photos with permission, added Google Wallet support that began rolling out on November 23, and Google plans Drive and Gemini app support in the coming weeks. With Wallet connected, AI Mode can map financial milestones, look for ways to trim everyday spending, and help maximize benefits on cards linked in Wallet, including cash-back, travel perks and reward points; Google says it can only read account information and cannot move, manage or invest money. Drive support would let AI Mode reference Docs, Sheets and PDFs; the Gemini app connection would carry context from earlier Gemini conversations. The feature stays off by default, is limited to users over 18 who have turned on Personalized Recommendations, and a Personalization icon meant to show which context was used is still described as coming soon. Robby Stein, vice president of product for Google Search, announced the expansion. Source: pcmag.com · adgully.com · digitaltrends.com

Five executives are set to negotiate a SpaceX-Tesla combination: Financial Times

The Financial Times reports that five executives are charged with combining Elon Musk’s SpaceX and Tesla, which it describes as a potential $3.5 trillion colossus and the biggest merger ever. The story is about the directors negotiating that combination, a company-level deal rather than a vehicle or energy product. Source: ft.com

Also Today

Amazon announces a $7.5 billion Decart acquisition: JNS

Amazon has announced the acquisition of Israeli AI startup Decart for $7.5 billion, JNS reported, after earlier reporting that the company was closing in on that deal. Source: jns.org

DeepMind’s agent swarm speeds an arcsin subroutine 290-fold: BigGo Finance

Google DeepMind’s agent swarm optimized quantum algorithms, and an arcsin subroutine sped up 290-fold, BigGo Finance reported. Source: finance.biggo.com

AWS customers criticize a 93-minute delay in an outage alert: THE ELEC

AWS faced customer backlash over a 93-minute delay in an outage alert, THE ELEC reported. Source: thelec.net

AWS frames a $200 billion infrastructure push around agents: BigGo Finance

BigGo Finance reported that AWS is pushing a “cloud where agents work” framing alongside a $200 billion infrastructure investment. Source: finance.biggo.com

The Counterpoint

Joining the preview turns off zero retention, and a lost key stops the models: Amazon Web Services

The seven-day case is not the only condition that decides whether the new Bedrock access is usable. The public preview is limited to customers already enrolled in Enterprise Frontier Safeguards zero data retention, and joining disables zero retention for the accounts or projects entered, while other accounts keep it. If Bedrock loses access to the required key or bucket for 24 hours, covered-model serving stops for the affected scope and Region. Enterprise Data Controls keeps retained content in Amazon-managed storage for up to 30 days, and those cases are not delivered during the public preview. Review runs in US East (N. Virginia) and Europe (Ireland); records normally arrive within minutes, but retries can continue for up to 72 hours. The documentation warns against sending regulated data such as PII or PHI, and notes that APIs, IAM action names and endpoints may change before general availability of the programs. Source: aws.amazon.com · unite.ai

The Thread: Who still has to approve the agent

More than 100 customers, including a quarter of the Fortune 100, helped design Enterprise Frontier Safeguards on Amazon Bedrock, in industries AWS names as healthcare, financial services and law. Automated monitoring looks across many requests for offensive cyber operations and for agents that go beyond what a user asked, including using a software exploit to reach an internal system. On Bedrock, inputs and outputs under that program stay in the customer’s account, encrypted with the customer’s key and written to the customer’s bucket. Only automated safety monitoring inside AWS reads that retained text. Human reviewers at AWS and Anthropic do not, and the inputs and outputs are never used for training. A project enrollment overrides an account enrollment, which overrides an organization enrollment, and CloudTrail records the enrollment calls and the identity behind them. Each Region where covered models are called needs a key and a bucket in that same Region. Customers still pay standard storage and key charges, and data-transfer charges can apply when a bucket sits outside the Region where automated review runs.

Meta is shipping the acting version of the same idea. At launch it is bringing dozens of partners, including Spotify and OpenTable, and UK connector partners such as Marks & Spencer, Debenhams Group, Currys, Ocado Retail, Trainline, Argos, Sainsbury’s, ASOS, Jet2, LOOKFANTASTIC, MyProtein, JustPark, easyJet and JD Sports. With some, a person can connect an account so the agent can reorder a usual shop or pull up a booking; with others, the agent can move around the website from day one. It can keep working after the app is closed and come back when something changes or it needs approval, including before it sends an email. Hundreds of voice options include British accents. Usernames and passwords sit in a secure credentials store so the model can complete an authorized action without seeing the password. If email is connected, one-time codes and password-reset links are ignored. A separate security agent checks actions and outbound information against the permissions granted. If model-improvement stays on — it is on at the start, and it can be turned off under Data controls — Meta removes names, email addresses and phone numbers, and it does not use interactions that contain private messages, such as emails or Instagram direct messages, to develop and improve its models. Information about people who do not use Muse, encountered in a connected calendar or email, is not linked to a Meta account.

Plaid, linking accounts into the Gemini app through Google Wallet, cited its State of Intelligent Finance report: 55% of respondents had used AI for a money-related task in the past year, and 86% of those users said AI helped them understand money better. Plaid also said more than half of Americans believe managing money without AI will soon feel outdated. Linking is optional. Users can turn the Wallet connection on or off, review connections in Plaid Portal, and Gemini’s access on that path is limited to reading.

The shared dependency is an agent that can see a card, a file or a checkout page without the platform taking the action itself. Amazon leaves the violation call with the customer’s team and keeps its own reviewers off the retained text. Meta leaves the login in a store the model does not read, and leaves the chats out of advertising. Google’s money context arrives only if a person opts in, and that person can cut the link. What changes is who is on the hook when the agent is wrong: the cloud customer owns the safety case, the shopper owns the memory and the send, and the Search user owns whether a card benefit ever enters the answer.
