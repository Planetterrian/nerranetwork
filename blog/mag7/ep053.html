Nearly twenty billion dollars in Lambda leases rides an Nvidia guarantee executives now doubt.

By Patrick November 14, 2026 Ep 53 9 min read Listen to podcast
Listen to this episode
Contents
Top News Company Desk Also Today The Counterpoint The Thread: Cost per solved task On the Calendar The Tape

What You Need to Know: Nearly twenty billion dollars in Lambda leases rides an Nvidia guarantee executives now doubt. Microsoft says Microsoft 365 and Copilot are restored after an outage that reached users in 74 countries, and federal officials separated Friday's airspace ground stop from that fault. Amazon published an OpenFold3 endpoint on SageMaker, and unset UK Copilot tenants get Claude by default on December 9.

Top News

Nvidia's nearly $20 billion Lambda guarantee meets a fading neocloud assumption: The Information

Nvidia has guaranteed nearly $20 billion in Lambda leases and agreed to buy CoreWeave's unsold cloud capacity, The Information reported. Finance executives told The Information that, with Jensen Huang gone, the assumption Nvidia will step in to help neoclouds has largely evaporated. Source: theinformation.com

Microsoft says the 74-country Microsoft 365 outage is fixed, and officials separate it from the FAA ground stop: The Federal

Microsoft said it has restored Microsoft 365 and Copilot after users in 74 countries were unable to reach services for more than 24 hours. The company blamed an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade that surfaced after a recent network change, and described it as a technical fault not related to cyber or AI agents. The Federal reported that Teams chats and meetings returned before some Exchange Online calendar features, that OpenAI and Anthropic's Microsoft 365 connectors were affected, that IndiGo and Akasa Air reported email problems while Air India rerouted email and flights continued, and that NHS staff in the UK turned to paper records. GeekWire reported that Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin and Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said Friday's nationwide ground stop was aging FAA telecom gear, not Microsoft and not the FAA's SMART traffic-planning tool. Duffy said unstable radar and communications data began at 12:36 p.m. Pacific, the FAA reopened US airspace at 5:15 p.m. Pacific, new IP switches held while older hardware on 1980s copper lines did not, and he wants $50 billion after the administration asked Congress for an additional $30.1 billion. Microsoft said it had no indication the ground stop was related to its disruption and that a full post-incident report will follow the review. Microsoft said in January that it had more than 450 million paid commercial Microsoft 365 subscriptions. Source: thefederal.com Source: geekwire.com

OpenFold3-preview lands on SageMaker JumpStart for structure prediction: Amazon Web Services

Amazon Web Services published a way to deploy OpenFold3-preview from SageMaker JumpStart, an open-source all-atom model from the OpenFold Consortium and the AlQuraishi Lab at Columbia University, released under the Apache 2.0 license. The endpoint predicts joint 3D structures of proteins, DNA, RNA, small-molecule ligands and the complexes they form, and it runs with network isolation so sequences stay in the customer's account. A real-time SageMaker request is capped at 60 seconds, so this model uses asynchronous inference with an invocation timeout of up to one hour, and the endpoint can scale to zero instances when the queue is empty. Source: aws.amazon.com

Claude turns on by default for unset UK Copilot tenants on December 9: Mezha

From December 9, UK organizations that have not chosen a setting will have Claude enabled by default in Microsoft 365 Copilot, while EU organizations stay opted out unless an administrator turns it on. Message Center post MC1546435, as reported by Mezha and Notebookcheck, treats an unchanged setting as opting in. Microsoft says Claude models in Copilot, Researcher, Copilot Studio and the Power Platform are currently excluded from the EU Data Boundary, and The Register reports that Anthropic processes that data in the US. The UK is outside the boundary, so Microsoft is dropping the exception there and retiring the option to allow Claude only in Copilot inside the Microsoft 365 apps. UK administrators who want Claude disabled, or limited to specific users and groups, must select that before December 9; only AI administrators and global administrators can make the change. Models with data retention, including Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5, stay separate under Anthropic's own terms, which keep most inputs and outputs for up to 30 days, and administrators must enable those models themselves. The notices cover managed work accounts, not Microsoft 365 Personal or Family. Source: mezha.net Source: notebookcheck.net

Company Desk

Google Wallet connects to Gemini for US spending questions: Indian Television

Google is connecting Google Wallet to Gemini Personal Intelligence, which already draws on apps such as Gmail, so people can ask about spending, subscriptions and recurring payments. Indian Television reported the link is rolling out to all Gemini app users in the US on the web, Android and iOS, that connecting Wallet is optional, and that access is read-only: the assistant can analyse transactions but cannot transfer money, manage accounts or make investments. Users turn it on in Connected Apps, tag @Google Wallet, and can add bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid. Google is also adding Memory Summary, a view of the preferences, interests and goals Gemini uses, which users can review, change or delete. Shattered.io headlined the bank connection as US users 18 and older only. Source: indiantelevision.com Source: thurrott.com Source: shattered.io

Siri AI stays off the HomePod mini 2 after software 27.2.1: MacRumors

MacRumors reported that HomePod software 27.2.1, out today, still does not enable Siri AI on the HomePod mini 2. The speaker has 2GB of RAM, under the 8GB minimum for dedicated Siri AI, so personal-context requests need a paired Apple Intelligence iPhone or iPad with Siri AI turned on. The same day's tvOS 27.2.1 does enable Siri AI on the Apple TV 4K second generation and third generation, as an opt-in under Settings, General, Siri; leaving that switch alone keeps Siri Classic. Source: macrumors.com

Also Today

Amazon agrees to be Fermi's anchor tenant: Financial Times

Amazon agreed to be the anchor tenant of Fermi, the troubled data-centre venture backed by Rick Perry, after earlier talks failed and the boardroom turmoil that followed, the Financial Times reported. Source: ft.com

Amazon plans a World Product Graph of goods it does not sell: The Verge

Amazon plans a vault, called the World Product Graph, of data on every product sold anywhere online, including products Amazon does not sell, and intends to surface that information on its own site so shoppers are less likely to leave, The Verge reported from Reuters. Source: theverge.com

Android XR glasses will hide faces from Gemini: Android Police

Google's Android XR smart glasses will hide faces from Gemini and ban real-time facial recognition, Android Police reported. Source: androidpolice.com

A new AgentCore Runtime price charges 79% more for memory: Tech Times

Tech Times reported that a new AWS AgentCore Runtime pricing version charges 79% more for memory, with any savings depending on idle time. Source: techtimes.com

US shoppers avoided 4 billion store trips, Amazon says: Amazon News

Amazon said that last year US customers avoided more than 4 billion trips to the store and over $11 billion in fuel costs, and that Prime members each saved on average over $650 on delivery fees and fuel, 64 shopping trips and 55 hours. Source: x.com

Nvidia calls CEO-candidate talk premature: Bloomberg

Nvidia said speculation that it is already talking to chief-executive candidates is premature, and that it is still selecting a firm to find a successor to Jensen Huang, Bloomberg reported. Source: bloomberg.com

Amazon Threat Intelligence flags ARTEX agent campaigns: Amazon Web Services

Amazon Threat Intelligence said it has observed threat actors running cybercrime campaigns with ARTEX AI agents. Source: aws.amazon.com

The Counterpoint

Founders mark down the cheques that used to follow Huang: TechCrunch

The lease guarantee is a commitment already written; the funding behaviour around Nvidia is what founders are marking down. TechCrunch reported that Nvidia poured tens of billions of dollars into AI startups under Jensen Huang, and that founders and venture capitalists are bracing for smaller checks and lower valuations without him at the helm. Source: techcrunch.com

The Thread: Cost per solved task

NVIDIA server version 0.3.0 of NeMo Switchyard is the open-source library Amazon's sample uses to choose which Amazon Bedrock model answers a coding agent. The sample installs switchyard-server 0.3.0 from crates.io and runs LiteLLM image version 1.102.0, both in one container on Amazon Elastic Container Service on AWS Fargate in private subnets. Amazon says NVIDIA documents that server release as a demo and evaluation component, and Amazon calls the integration experimental sample code, not a production deployment.

A coding agent works in tasks. The developer asks for a change; the agent reads files, edits code, and runs tests until the work is done. One task can take a few model calls or dozens. Token price does not equal task price, because the bill depends on how many turns a model needs, how much history each turn replays, and how often a run is retried or never finishes. Every attempt is paid, including attempts that never finish. Amazon's score is cost per solved task: total cost of the runs divided by the number of runs whose tests pass.

The library supports two strategies through LiteLLM. Stage routing reads recent tool activity and results in the history, so the agent must send the full conversation, then picks between a capable model and a lower-cost efficient model. Random routing picks from the configured models, with optional weights, for splits and comparisons. Neither strategy calls a judge model. LiteLLM has also shipped a native Switchyard stage router as an auto-router. Deployment in the sample uses the AWS Cloud Development Kit, and Amazon tells customers to clear the design with security and legal teams and to test it in a non-production account first.

Amazon hosts the gateway and the models; NVIDIA supplies the chooser. The agent sends an OpenAI-compatible request with the model field set to switchyard and a shared gateway key. Only the IPv4 range the deployer allows can reach the hostname, which sits on a load balancer with an AWS Certificate Manager certificate. AWS WAF applies IP-reputation, known-bad-input, and per-IP rate limits. The load balancer forwards only three paths, chat completions, models, and a health check, and returns 404 for every other path. LiteLLM checks the key, the plugin picks the model, and the call goes out through the Bedrock Converse or ConverseStream API on the task's own identity role, via a runtime interface endpoint so the traffic stays on Amazon's network. The header x-litellm-model-name names the model that answered.

The split of work is the change. Amazon gets a harness that compares a routed group with single-model baselines on a customer's own workloads, which is how the post says to judge the router. NVIDIA gets its library in the request path of agents that already speak a common format, without operating the gateway or the capacity those agents consume. The benchmark runners in the sample are the step the post actually specifies for deciding whether that split is worth keeping. Source: aws.amazon.com

On the Calendar

UK Copilot tenants that have not picked a Claude setting move to all users on December 9, 2026, unless an administrator chooses otherwise before then. Source: mezha.net

Microsoft is merging consumer Copilot with Microsoft 365 Copilot and moving stored files to OneDrive from August 18, 2026. Source: mezha.net
