Amazon pledged more than one billion dollars to the towns hosting its data centers.

By Patrick October 02, 2026 Ep 10 6 min read Listen to podcast View summaries

MAG 7 Daily

Amazon pledged more than one billion dollars to the towns hosting its data centers.

What You Need to Know: Amazon pledged more than one billion dollars over five years to U.S. towns that host its data centers, funding schools, training, and energy and water work as local moratoriums pile up. Google put four of its AI chips on a prototype satellite. Prosecutors charged a California man over restricted Nvidia servers bound for China, and New Mexico is seeking as much as forty billion dollars from Meta after a privacy trial.

Top News

Amazon pledges more than $1 billion to data-center towns: GeekWire

Amazon Web Services said Friday it will spend more than $1 billion over five years in U.S. communities where it operates data centers, in a program GeekWire reports is called Built Together. The money targets local priorities including education, job training, energy affordability, and water and energy preservation; GeekWire adds free community college and energy upgrades for homes and schools, on top of more than $1 billion Amazon says it has already given those communities over the past three years. Amazon said it invested $276 billion in data centers between 2011 and 2025, with major projects and expansions in Indiana, Louisiana, North Carolina, Virginia, and Mississippi. AWS chief executive Matt Garman said more than 100 data-center moratoriums are being considered, and that “the countries that lead in AI will shape it and get the most from it.” Amazon also said it will stop using nondisclosure agreements with government agencies on data-center projects, install lower-emission backup generators at new sites, publish its energy and water use each year, and pay enough for power to keep local electricity bills from rising. AWS pledged to be water positive across its data centers by 2030, meaning it aims to return more water than it uses, and said it has already reached 75 percent of the progress toward that target. Source: geekwire.com https://www.arise.tv/amazon-aws-to-invest-over-1bn-in-us-communities-over-five-years/ https://finimize.com/content/aws-pledges-1-billion-to-ease-data-center-pushback

Google flies four TPUs on a Project Suncatcher prototype: Google

Google said it launched a prototype satellite carrying four TPUs into orbit with Planet, on SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare. The flight is the first step of Project Suncatcher, a research effort to see whether scalable machine-learning infrastructure can one day run in space. Over the coming weeks Google will collect in-orbit data on how the chips handle physical stress, radiation, and thermal extremes, and use whatever it learns to refine later designs. Source: x.com https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-project-suncatcher-facts/?utm_source=tw&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=og

Prosecutors charge a California man over Nvidia servers sent to China: Bloomberg

Federal prosecutors arrested a California man on Thursday on charges of smuggling computer servers containing $300 million worth of Nvidia artificial-intelligence chips to China, in violation of U.S. export controls. The Information identifies him as Greg Lui, owner of Earthmade Computer Inc., a privately held firm in the San Gabriel Valley, and says he allegedly worked with conspirators. Source: bloomberg.com https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/california-man-arrested-smuggling-nvidia-chips-china

New Mexico seeks up to $40 billion from Meta after a privacy trial: The Business Standard

New Mexico wants Meta to pay up to $40 billion in penalties after a data-privacy trial, The Business Standard reported. Marketscreener, citing MT Newswires, said Meta faces up to $40 billion in penalties following that trial. Source: tbsnews.net https://www.marketscreener.com/news/meta-platforms-faces-up-to-40-billion-in-penalties-following-data-privacy-trial-ce785ddadf81f727

AT&T warns some iPhone 18 Pro Max phones cannot reach 911: MacRumors

AT&T texted customers that some iPhone 18 Pro Max devices on its network may be unable to complete calls, texts, or data, including to 911, and told them to update to iOS 27.0.1 immediately. MacRumors reports at least thousands of users have described the failure on Apple’s support community, Reddit, and its own forums, with affected phones typically dropping into SOS mode. The U.S. iPhone 18 Pro Max is the only new iPhone unveiled last month with a Qualcomm cellular modem; the smaller iPhone 18 Pro and the iPhone Duo use Apple’s C2 modem worldwide, as does the iPhone 18 Pro Max in every other country. MacRumors says it is unclear whether that modem split is a contributing factor. Source: macrumors.com

Company Desk

Office and science chiefs join a run of Microsoft exits: The Information

Ryan Roslansky, executive vice president of Office apps, said Thursday he will leave at the end of this year. A day earlier it was Microsoft Science president Peter Lee’s turn, after Office and Windows chief Rajesh Jha and other senior departures over the past few months. The Information says the people leaving are mostly veterans of fifteen years or more, each with their own reasons, and reads the cluster as exits while Microsoft rethinks how it operates around AI. Source: theinformation.com

The Counterpoint

The community check is about a tenth of a percent of this year’s build: GeekWire

GeekWire’s arithmetic is the hard cut against the pledge. The additional $1 billion over five years works out to about $200 million a year, or about one-tenth of 1 percent of the roughly $220 billion in capital expenses Amazon is projecting this year, largely on data centers and chips — spending GeekWire says is more than the cash the business generates annually. The same report says Microsoft and Google are spending on a similar scale, and that local opposition has become a real limit on the AI buildout. A fund that small does not change the size of the build Garman says moratoriums could stall. Source: geekwire.com

The Thread: Campuses still rising, chips leaving the books

Across five operators, TrendForce’s October tracker finds that most of the capacity it can size is already under construction on the remaining path to 2030, and it reports AWS holding the most gigawatts under construction while Google’s build goes global. The tracker follows major hyperscalers in the United States and China and monitors nearly 2,000 facilities, checking satellite imagery, permit filings, and utility records against its own supply-chain research. Each bar is an operator’s remaining path to a 2030 pipeline target after capacity already online is stripped out: active construction, permitted or planned sites that already have a power figure, and pipeline that has reached neither stage. Permitted or planned sites that cannot yet be sized in megawatts are excluded, so the planned band understates the true total. The count includes self-built campuses, build-to-suit developments, and fully leased colocation, and it excludes shared colocation, cloud regions, and power offtake agreements. Booked delays and unstaged capacity are only a small share of that 2030 window, and where they appear they attach to a few named sites rather than the fleet. Off that construction map, Bloomberg says the Financial Times reported Amazon is exploring a deal to shift about $8 billion of top-end Nvidia chips off its balance sheet. On September 30, Synopsys used its 2026 Investor Day in New York to announce multi-year agreements with OpenAI and with Amazon Web Services, and it issued fiscal 2027 revenue and profit forecasts that came in above analysts’ expectations. Design Automation, the segment that holds its chip-design software and Ansys simulation products, made $5.3 billion, or 75 percent of revenue, in fiscal 2025, grew 25.6 percent that year, helped by the Ansys purchase, and earned a 41.8 percent operating margin. In August, management had raised its fiscal 2026 revenue guide to $9.69 billion to $9.74 billion and reported more than 30 active customer engagements for its agentic AI platform, including one agent that can run a whole chip verification cycle on its own. Yahoo Finance calculates that if those AI tools added a tenth to Design Automation revenue, the gain would be about $530 million a year, roughly 7.5 percent of fiscal 2025 revenue. Source: insights.trendforce.com https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/amazon-seeks-to-move-8-billion-of-chips-off-its-books-ft-says https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-aws-deals-reshape-synopsyss-075553483.html
