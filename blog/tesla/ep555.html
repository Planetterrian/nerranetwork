Tesla Shorts Time

REAL-TIME TSLA price: $304.86 ▼ $4.36 (1.4%)

Tesla locked in 90 percent of an Arizona solar-plus-storage project to power its growing AI inference clusters.

Top 12 News Items

  1. FSD v14 Lite wide release on HW3 vehicles: July 28, 2026, Evannex

    2. Tesla began pushing FSD v14 Lite version 2026.20.6.11 to a larger share of HW3 cars in the United States and Canada after a bug-fix point release. The update merges firmware with the HW4 branch at 2026.20.6.11, giving HW3 cars the latest distilled features available on newer hardware. HW3 vehicles will still trail HW4 capability due to compute limits on the larger model. Owners outside North America continue to wait. Roughly 18 percent of the HW3 fleet in the US and Canada had received the update by the latest tracking snapshot. The rollout began with a limited wave on July 21 and expanded steadily through July 23. Tesla’s head of AI had previously announced version 2026.20.6.10 before the quick bug-fix iteration. Source: evannex.com

  2. Tesla sues Cybertruck supplier over parts standoff: July 28, 2026, Electrek

    3. Tesla filed suit against a Cybertruck component supplier that is allegedly withholding tooling and holding production hostage. The dispute centers on owned dies and fixtures the supplier refuses to release without additional daily payments. Production of the vehicle remains constrained while the legal process plays out. The supplier is accused of demanding ongoing fees to hand over equipment that Tesla already owns. Source: Google News

  3. Tesla secures majority of Arizona solar-plus-storage project for AI compute: July 28, 2026, Traders Union

    4. Tesla has locked in 90 percent of the output from a KKR-backed solar and battery installation in Arizona to support its expanding inference workloads. The deal reflects the shift from GPU stockpiling to securing megawatts of dedicated renewable capacity. Construction and interconnection timelines will determine when the power reaches Cortex clusters. Danny Naz noted the 90 percent commitment in recent coverage. The project adds to earlier long-term power agreements Tesla has signed in the same state. Source: Google News

  4. Tesla signs long-term solar PPA with Zelestra in Texas: July 28, 2026, bastillepost.com

    5. Tesla and Zelestra finalized a multi-year power purchase agreement for solar generation in Texas. The contract adds dedicated renewable supply to support both vehicle manufacturing and energy storage operations at Giga Texas. Details on capacity and pricing remain undisclosed. A separate report from reNEWS confirmed the same Texas solar PPA signing. The agreement aligns with Tesla’s broader push to pair solar generation with on-site storage needs. Source: Google News

  5. BYD confirms August debut for its first humanoid robot: July 28, 2026, Benzinga

    6. BYD announced its initial humanoid robot will be unveiled next month, directly entering the space Tesla has targeted with Optimus. The Chinese automaker’s move adds another competitor to the race for low-cost general-purpose humanoids. No production timeline or target applications were disclosed. The debut is positioned as a direct response to Tesla’s ongoing Optimus development. Source: Google News

  6. Virtuix sells initial AI and robotics hardware to Tesla’s Optimus division: July 28, 2026, ACCESS Newswire

    7. Virtuix completed its first sale of specialized hardware to Tesla’s Optimus program as part of an expanding AI and robotics strategy. The transaction marks an early external supplier relationship for the humanoid effort. Further volume or integration details were not released. The sale advances Virtuix’s stated push into robotics hardware alongside its existing virtual reality products. Source: Google News

  7. Deutsche Bank cuts Tesla price target citing Robotaxi delays: July 28, 2026, TIKR.com

    8. Deutsche Bank lowered its Tesla price target after concluding that unsupervised Robotaxi progress has lagged internal timelines. The revision reflects slower regulatory and deployment milestones than previously modeled. Investors now await clearer production and approval dates. The bank specifically flagged lagging Robotaxi advancement as the primary reason for the adjustment. Source: Google News

  8. Lawsuit claims FSD team overworked to the point of safety risk: July 28, 2026, AOL.com

    9. Court filings quote a former Tesla manager stating that self-driving vehicle engineers were pushed so hard that test cars became rolling hazards on public roads. The complaint alleges excessive hours and inadequate oversight during rapid iteration cycles. Tesla has not yet responded publicly to the specific claims. The manager described vehicles operating without sufficient validation before being placed on public streets. Source: Google News

  9. Musk flags substantial challenges ahead for Robotaxi and Optimus timelines: July 28, 2026, The Motley Fool

    10. Elon Musk stated that both the Robotaxi and humanoid robot programs face meaningful remaining hurdles before commercial scale. The comments temper earlier expectations for near-term unsupervised deployment and volume production. Investors are watching for updated milestones in upcoming earnings commentary. The remarks came in response to questions about whether current development pace matches prior public targets. Source: Google News

  10. Federal probe opens into Tesla flush door handles and egress safety: July 28, 2026, MotorBiscuit

    11. U.S. regulators launched a broad investigation into flush door handle designs after multiple Tesla models raised concerns about emergency exit performance. The review covers vehicle egress under power-loss and crash conditions. Tesla has not disclosed any planned hardware changes. The probe examines whether the handles meet federal requirements for manual operation when primary power systems fail. Source: Google News

  11. Cybercab steering wheel retrofit images surface online: July 28, 2026, r/teslamotors

    12. Reddit users posted photos of a Cybercab prototype fitted with a conventional steering wheel, suggesting ongoing development of a manually operable version. The images indicate Tesla is exploring retrofit paths for regulatory or testing flexibility. No official confirmation has been issued. The modifications appear on vehicles originally shown without steering wheels at the We, Robot event. Source: reddit.com

  12. Model Y six-seater configuration now available for immediate delivery in Australia: July 28, 2026, The Driven IO

    13. Tesla opened immediate delivery slots for the six-seat Model Y variant in Australia after earlier allocation limits. The configuration adds a third-row bench to the existing five-seat platform. Local pricing and availability windows remain listed on the configurator. The move follows earlier production adjustments that had restricted the six-seat option in certain markets. Source: Google News

Tesla First Principles

Power constraints now shape AI development more directly than chip counts. Tesla’s decision to secure the bulk of an Arizona solar-plus-storage project shows the company treating megawatts as a scarce input that must be locked in years ahead of demand. The move mirrors how data-center operators once raced for substations rather than servers. Securing dedicated renewable output also reduces exposure to utility rate volatility that can swing inference costs unpredictably.

Traditional utilities plan generation around predictable load growth curves. Tesla’s inference clusters create bursty, high-density demand that existing grids were not sized to serve at short notice. By contracting directly for dedicated renewable capacity, the company bypasses interconnection queues that can stretch three to five years for new transmission. The Arizona project therefore functions as both a capacity hedge and a timing advantage.

The economics hinge on whether the secured power delivers lower effective cost per inference token than buying from the spot market or relying on grid upgrades paid for by all ratepayers. If the Arizona project reaches commercial operation on schedule, it gives Tesla a measurable head start on rivals still negotiating with utilities. If delays occur, the same contract becomes a stranded asset while competitors catch up on their own supply deals. The outcome will directly influence gross margins on any future autonomy or robotics services that rely on large-scale inference.
