Top 10 News Items

  1. Tesla sells Shanghai-made Model 3 in Canada at C$39,490 after Carney-Beijing deal cuts Chinese EV tariff to 6.1%: May 13, 2026, r/teslamotors

    2. Tesla is offering Shanghai-built Model 3 sedans in Canada for C$39,490, well below the C$79,990 price of the Fremont version. The move follows a January trade deal that lowered tariffs on Chinese EVs from 100 percent to 6.1 percent under a quota of 49,000 vehicles. Tesla becomes the first automaker to take advantage of the arrangement, which also opens the door for competitors like BYD. Source: reddit.com

  2. Tesla's (TSLA.US) Berlin Gigafactory secures an additional $250 million investment after rapid recruitment expansion! Musk's 'Physical AI Master Plan' still relies on the automotive business for funding.: May 13, 2026, 富途牛牛

    3. The Berlin plant received another $250 million in funding tied to its fast hiring push. The capital supports ongoing expansion even as Tesla's broader robotics and AI plans continue to draw from vehicle profits. This keeps the German site central to scaling both current production and future hardware ambitions. Source: Google News

  3. Tesla Kills Basic Autopilot in the Netherlands As It Wants Customers To Pay for FSD: May 13, 2026, autoevolution

    4. Tesla removed the basic Autopilot option for new buyers in the Netherlands, leaving only paid Full Self-Driving as the assisted-driving choice. The change pushes customers toward the higher-priced package and aligns with the company's strategy of monetizing software features more aggressively in Europe. It also highlights how regional regulations and pricing decisions now shape what drivers can access at purchase. Source: Google News

  4. Tesla Patented a Charging Adapter That Locks Itself Before NACS Adapters Become a Bigger Owner Problem: May 13, 2026, Torque News

    5. Tesla filed a patent for a self-locking NACS adapter designed to prevent theft or loss once attached to non-Tesla vehicles. The design addresses a growing practical issue as more third-party EVs adopt the connector and owners rely on adapters for road trips. It shows Tesla thinking ahead about hardware durability and user friction in an expanding charging ecosystem. Source: Google News

  5. Tesla lures budget-conscious Chinese buyers ahead of state visit by Trump: May 13, 2026, South China Morning Post

    6. Tesla introduced new financing options in China aimed at price-sensitive customers after falling out of the country's top ten EV sellers. The moves come just before a high-profile US-China visit and reflect efforts to regain ground against local rivals through lower monthly payments rather than new models. Success here would help stabilize domestic volume while the company pushes robotaxi and AI initiatives elsewhere. Source: Google News

  6. FSD Supervised is life-changing accessibility: May 13, 2026, @Tesla

    7. A driver born without arms described how upgrading to a Model Y with FSD Supervised reduced physical strain from arthritis and preserved daily independence after years of driving with his feet. The account details how the system handles steering and pedals that previously required awkward positioning. Tesla shared the story to illustrate practical benefits beyond typical performance claims. Source: x.com

Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the sudden availability of Shanghai-built Model 3s in Canada at sharply lower prices challenges the long-standing assumption that domestic assembly always protects local pricing power.

The 6.1 percent tariff now applied to a quota of 49,000 vehicles creates a direct cost gap of roughly C$40,000 between otherwise identical cars, showing how trade policy can override manufacturing location advantages in a single quarter.

Tesla's vertical integration in Shanghai already gives it the scale to absorb shipping and compliance costs while still undercutting Fremont-built inventory, which suggests the company will continue routing volume through whichever plant faces the lowest effective barriers rather than protecting legacy sites.
