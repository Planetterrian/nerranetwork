Offshore North Blog

The Vendée Globe is the Everest of the seas — solo, non-stop, around the world — and no Canadian has ever finished it. Offshore North follows the campaign trying to change that, and the extraordinary sport around it: flying boats, 20-minute sleep, photo finishes after 23 days at sea. No sailing knowledge required. One thing explained properly every week.
