In their own words

John Ossipinsky (LymphMan) is the host of The Lymph-Brain Revolution Podcast, author, and Lymphatic Health Coach. John started his career more than 30 years ago as a Certified Lymphedema Therapist, and a Diplomate in CranioSacral Therapy and completing advanced training in Visceral Manipulation. Throughout his career, he has helped thousands of clients improve their health and quality of life by maintaining the lymphatic system. John teaches practical ways to support the lymphatic system, improve natural detoxification, and enhance physical and mental well-being. His book, An Undetected Acid-Alkaline Imbalance, explores how acidity can affect lymphatic flow and lead to many diseases. In 2023, the NIH reported that changes in lymphatic function can impact multiple body systems and contribute to disease. His website HealthyLymphSystem.com explains how Disease Begins with Lymphatic Dysfunction.

What you need to know

John Ossipinsky described seeing iridescent lines of moving lymph fluid appear through a client's sheet during a hands-on session—an observation that led him to develop remote energetic lymphatic work clients report feeling in real time. After three decades as a Certified Lymphedema Therapist and former firefighter, he now teaches daily posture, breath, and shoulder-shuffle sequences that clients can repeat without ongoing therapist visits, arguing that lymphatic stagnation from acidity and poor movement underlies most chronic conditions. The conversation covers his own recovery from severe IBS, hair loss, and brain fog, plus the limits of conventional medicine's view of the system.

What we talked about
  • Ossipinsky recounted that after eating vegetables daily for 30 days following decades of avoidance, he lost weight, regained hair, and felt his brain "pop" back into place at age 47, prompting him to add diet and movement to manual work.
  • He explained that the main lymph vessels behind the clavicles form a narrow bottleneck; slouching at a desk compresses them and stops flow the same way an obstructed household sewer backs up.
  • Clients who completed three consecutive aggressive lymphatic sessions often experienced fatigue, anxiety, or sleeplessness hours later from released waste, leading him to space sessions every two or three days instead.
  • During remote energetic sessions he recreates the same sequence with a pillow and Zoom, and clients have reported sudden releases in the head or back while he works on the corresponding area.
  • He demonstrated the LymphMan shoulder shuffle—alternating one shoulder straight up to the ear while the other drops—followed by 10–20 deep breaths, noting that the motion opens lung and head flow within seconds.
  • A woman who had used a kitchen lounge chair for a decade of back pain stood longer after weekly sessions; another client stopped dragging her leg and arm and began caring for her appearance once lymphatic flow improved.
