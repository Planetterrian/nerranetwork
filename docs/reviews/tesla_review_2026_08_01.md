## P0 — Listener-facing bugs shipping today

### 1. Spoken closer mangles to “TSLA closed at down…” (6/10 eps)

**Evidence:** review snapshot repeated-phrase detector — `“tsla closed at down”` in **6/10** Whisper transcripts (Ep550–559 window). That contiguous bigram is not a plausible ASR substitution for a well-formed “TSLA closed at four hundred…, down …”; it is the failure mode where the direction word is glued where the price should be (“closed at down five dollars / one point two percent”).

**Source:** `shows/hooks/tesla.py` supplies `closing_block` via `_pick_closing(context)` (hook docstring + `pre_fetch` — closing is Tesla-owned because it carries the quote; `run_show.py` `setdefault` never rotates the shared pool). June-10 gated the zero-dollar path on `is_price_publishable` and added rotation, but the **price+direction template still emits “closed at {up\|down}…”** when price-to-words is empty, ordered wrong, or the change clause is substituted for the price clause.

**Proposed fix (code, A/B-listen):** harden `_pick_closing` so every variant is one of:
- publishable quote: `"TSLA closed at {price_words}, {up\|down} {change_words} on the day."` (price clause mandatory and first),
- or pre/post-market: `"TSLA is trading at {price_words} in {pre-market\|after-hours}, {up\|down} {change_words}."`,
- unpublishable: **omit the price sentence entirely** (already intended) and use a price-free closer.
Add a drift-guard that asserts no `_tts.txt` / closing string matches `(?i)closed at (up|down)\b`.
