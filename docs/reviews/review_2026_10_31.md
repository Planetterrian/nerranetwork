## YouTube

### Fixed

**1. The dead-Shorts tier read a channel-wide slump as six dead shows.**
- The tier's floor was an absolute 10 views at day 3. The EN channel's own
  hook-Short median was 11 (Oct 7–27, n=127), so half the shows sat under
  it by definition.
- FF, First Principles, Models & Agents and Omni View were cut to one Short
  a week. EN Shorts fell from 6–9 a day to 4–5.
- FF and First Principles carry the channel's breakouts (68–134 and
  54–73 views). FF, FPD, M&A and OV earned 33% of all EN hook views.

Now:
- The bar is half the channel's median, never above 10.
- A show with a breakout tail (p75 at or above the channel median) is
  never dead.
- A probing show leaves the tier as soon as its 21-day window no longer
  reads dead.

Recomputed on the committed reach file: FF, FPD, M&A and OV return to a
daily Short; MAB and MIT stay in the tier.

**2. A two-probe promotion turned daily RU/FR long-forms on.**
- SpaceX, Tesla and FF went from tier C to A on both dub channels between
  10-26 and 10-29.
- Their velocity was two young videos whose first-day burst was divided
  by one day.
- Dub long-forms since Oct 14 earned a median 6 (RU) and 19 (FR) views at
  day 3, against 60 and 58 for the hook Shorts.
- Each costs about 29 minutes of render. The translate jobs for those
  three shows went from 21–32 minutes to 68–93.

Now:
- The probe promotion is EN only.
- No long-form under two days old counts toward velocity.
- The five probe-promoted entries were reset to C. FR SpaceX keeps its
  long-form on a real four-video read (4.6 views a day).

**3. FF's dub Shorts shared a 2-scene pool.** The probe tier's portrait cap
now applies only when no dub or multi-platform channel uses the scenes.

**4. False "degraded" scenes on Shorts-only days.**
- A Shorts-only day pays for one 16:9 scene on purpose. That scene was
  thrown away and the run logged "degraded" (FPD, Planetterrian).
- It is now kept as the thumbnail base, and no warning is logged.

## The website

### Fixed

**5. Videos that went public after the post was built were never linked.**
- OV Ep222, M&A Ep220, MIT Ep217, FF Ep239 and Planetterrian Ep230 had no
  "Watch on YouTube" button. SpaceX and Tesla had one only because the
  translation sweep happened to rebuild their posts later.

  site script shows it once that time passes.

**6. The Russian and French front pages read the previous day until 21:25
UTC.** They are now rebuilt with every homepage build (about 8 s) and
committed by the same three workflows.

**7. Nine of the 26 homepage commits changed only "Updated <time>".**
- The stamp is now the newest story's time, so a rebuild with nothing new
  is byte-identical (verified across builds a minute apart), with no
  commit and no Pages deploy.

**8. The lead was yesterday's story for up to 80 minutes before dawn.**
- UC's scenes are portrait-only, so no story of the day qualified as a
  lead, and the fallback took the newest landscape story of any date.
- The newest day now comes first.

**9. A columnist's byline made a developing story** (two unrelated Vaughn
Palmer columns). The byline is stripped before matching.

## The episodes

### Fixed

**10. Bare letters spoken as units.**
- M&A Ep220 aired "h n t p plus equals **h meters t p**" (a tensor
  expression). The same shape aired at least six times before: "two a
  meters", "eleven forty seven p meters", "l meters underscore".
- A one-letter unit now expands only after a spoken quantity, and no unit
  expands after a lone letter.
- Swept over every committed digest, the change touches only "the m" and
  "g cm".

**11. The members-only desks asked members to follow and rate them** in
their podcast app (6 of their first 11 episodes). A show with no public
listing never asks.

**12. Reaction sentences.**
- Removed: "Elon Musk replied to the post with 'Cool' and a heart emoji";
  "Commenters speculated on whether…"; "The post received immediate
  engagement…".
- Swept: those five sentences and nothing else match.

**13. Planetterrian's astronomy filter missed plurals.** The "links
telescopes" story aired on both FF and Planetterrian. `telescopes`,
`rovers` and `orbiters` are now filtered.

  World and Omni View.

**17. The join page called the members-only desks "Hear it free".** The
plan table and the homepage band now say "every public show".

**18. `api/personal_front.json` was rewritten 27 times in 24 hours, 9 of
them for its timestamp alone.** An identical payload is now not written.
