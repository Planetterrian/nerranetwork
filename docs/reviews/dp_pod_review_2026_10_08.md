## P0 — Listener-facing bugs shipping today

### 1. Chapter titles are dialogue fragments with speaker labels

Verified in committed chapter JSON:

- `chapters_ep072.json`: `"PATRICK: You can have the hose"`, `"DAN: Ten"`, `"PATRICK: It copies if the recipe stays small"`
- `chapters_ep073.json`: `"DAN: Hang on"`, `"DAN: I'll take that"`
- `chapters_ep074.json`: `"PATRICK: I'll take the hit on that"`
- `chapters_ep077.json`: `"DAN: Minus eighty"`, `"DAN: Strongest version of that: a one-and-done metabolic…"`

Podcast apps show these as scrubber labels. Root cause is mid-episode chapter generation accepting raw dialogue lines (including `DAN:`/`PATRICK:` prefixes) rather than segment anchors or story headlines only.

**Proposal (code-only):** when finalising DP Pod (and preferably network-wide dialogue) chapters, (a) strip leading `DAN:`/`PATRICK:`/`HOST:` labels, (b) drop titles that are < 4 content words or match a short-reaction shape, (c) prefer section_markers + story headlines. Drift-guard: assert no chapter title in last N eps matches `^(DAN|PATRICK):`.

### 2. Cold Open chapter never latches

Spoken cold open (all of ep69–78 transcripts): **“This is the DP Pod, episode N. I'm Dan…”**

Configured marker (`shows/dp_pod.yaml` chapters.section_markers): `Welcome to The DP Pod|the Do Positive Podcast` with `where: start`.

Result: every chapters JSON in this window starts at “The Positive Papers” (~28–40s), so the cold open is invisible in chapter UI and Sign-Off/body timing still works only because other markers fire.

**Proposal (config):** extend the Cold Open pattern to also match `This is the DP Pod` (keep existing Welcome alternates). No TTS change; metadata only → `ab_listen_required: false`.
