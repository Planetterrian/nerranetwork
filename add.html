Add your edition to your podcast app

Tap the app you listen in. It opens with your feed ready to follow; your first edition, or today's, is the newest episode.

    Nothing opened? That app isn't on this device. Copy the link below and paste it into the app instead.

    On your phone

    Point your phone's camera at the code. It opens this page there, with one-tap buttons for the apps on your phone.

    The code carries your private link: anyone who scans it can follow your edition.

    Your feed link

    For any other app that can follow a show by its link (an "RSS feed" or "URL").

    Adding it by hand

    • Apple Podcasts: Library, then the ⋯ menu, then Follow a Show by URL, and paste.
    • Overcast: the + button, then Add URL, and paste.
    • Pocket Casts: Search, paste the link, then Subscribe.
    • Castro: Library, then the + button, then paste.
    • AntennaPod: Add podcast, then Add podcast by RSS address, and paste.
    • Podcast Addict: the + button, then RSS feed, and paste.
    • Spotify doesn't accept private feeds. Every free Nerra show is on Spotify.

    Prefer to listen right here? My Nerra plays your edition and your shows in the browser, and keeps your place.
