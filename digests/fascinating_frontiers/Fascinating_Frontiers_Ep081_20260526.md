### Top 15 Space & Astronomy Stories

   The rover identified rock formations indicating past water flows in Jezero Crater. These features include layered sediments and mineral deposits consistent with prolonged liquid activity. The observations add detail to the timeline of when Mars held surface water. Follow-up analysis of returned samples could confirm the duration of habitable conditions.

   The system combines tracking data with protective measures for orbital assets. New imagery shows the expanded monitoring network now covering more of the space environment. Officials released the images to demonstrate improved situational awareness. The project focuses on both collision avoidance and interference detection.

3. **Orbit Fab and Thales Alenia Study Satellite Refueling Interfaces** — SpaceNews
   The partnership will test how Orbit Fab’s docking hardware connects to electric propulsion systems. Thales Alenia’s thrusters use xenon or similar propellants that could be replenished in orbit. The study examines mechanical, fluid, and electrical compatibility. Results will guide future commercial servicing missions.

4. **NASA Tests Lunar Rovers on BLM Land in California** — Imperial Valley Press Online
   Autonomous rovers are undergoing trials in Imperial County desert terrain. The Bureau of Land Management provides the test site that mimics lunar surface conditions. Engineers evaluate navigation and obstacle avoidance without human intervention. Data from these runs will refine designs for future Artemis surface operations.

5. **Psyche Spacecraft Swings Past Mars for Speed Boost** — ScienceDaily
   The probe used the planet’s gravity to adjust its trajectory toward the metal asteroid. This maneuver shortens the journey and conserves onboard fuel. Psyche’s target is a body thought to be the exposed core of an early planetesimal. Arrival is still years away after the flyby.

6. **Exolaunch and SEOPS Book Dedicated Falcon 9 Rideshares** — SpaceNews
   Each company purchased a full Falcon 9 launch to serve multiple small satellite customers. The move responds to rising demand for dedicated small-payload missions. Both firms have previously arranged rides on shared flights. The new contracts allow tighter scheduling and orbit customization.

   The route uses gravitational interactions to reduce required propellant. Spacecraft following this path can reach the Moon with less delta-v than traditional transfers. The discovery could lower costs for both robotic and crewed lunar missions. Further modeling will map the most efficient insertion points.

9. **Landsat 9 Images the Moon for Calibration Checks** — NASA
   The satellite turns its instruments toward the lunar surface once a month. The full Moon provides a stable, well-known brightness reference. Engineers compare these readings against previous data to track sensor performance. The routine helps maintain consistent Earth-observation measurements.

    The observatory observed atmospheric changes on a world 700 light-years away. Data revealed temperature variations and cloud patterns across the planet’s surface.     Models show radiation from distant black holes can erode atmospheres on planets far from their stars. The effect extends habitability concerns beyond the traditional Goldilocks zone.     Studying the Red Planet’s thin atmosphere and tectonic history helps interpret distant rocky worlds. Many discovered exoplanets sit near the edge of habitability, much like Mars. ### Cosmic Spotlight
NASA’s Psyche spacecraft is using Mars as a gravitational slingshot to reach a metal-rich asteroid. The flyby adjusts the probe’s speed and direction without expending extra fuel, a technique refined over decades of outer-planet missions. Scientists expect the asteroid to reveal the interior structure of a planetesimal that never fully formed into a planet. Data collected after arrival could reshape models of how planetary cores differentiate. What remains unknown is whether Psyche’s surface still holds traces of its original molten state or has been heavily altered by later impacts.

### Cosmic Deep Dive: How Distant Black Holes Threaten Planetary Atmospheres
A supermassive black hole at the center of a galaxy can outshine its entire stellar population, flooding surrounding space with intense radiation. If that energy reaches an exoplanet hundreds of light-years away, it can heat the upper atmosphere enough for gas molecules to reach escape velocity and drift into space. The process unfolds over millions of years, gradually thinning a world that might otherwise sit in a comfortable orbit around its star. One surprising detail is that the danger zone can extend thousands of light-years from the black hole, far beyond the reach of its gravity. We still do not know how often this atmospheric stripping actually occurs or which galactic environments leave planets most vulnerable.
