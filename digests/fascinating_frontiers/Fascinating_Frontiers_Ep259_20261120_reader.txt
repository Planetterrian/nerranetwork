Lead author Doctor Hillary Davis, a Ph.D. student, reached her result, according to Phys.org, by comparing computer simulations of galaxies evolving over billions of years with observations of masers.

A maser is a natural microwave beacon tied to places where massive stars are being born.

The Milky Way looks unusual in where new stars form compared with nearby galaxies, and the result puts the Norma spiral arm in doubt, with at least part of that arm possibly just star formation along the bar's leading edge.

The comparison tightens limits on the bar's size, angle, and how fast it rotates, and researchers estimate that rotating structure reaches approximately four point three kiloparsecs along its long axis from the galactic center, about fourteen thousand light-years.

Nature Astronomy published the work.

BepiColombo's Mercury Imaging X-ray Spectrometer, or MIXS, has provided its first observations of the Crab Nebula.

The Crab Nebula is one of the brightest X-ray sources in the sky.

A University of Leicester team ran calibration measurements after the Mercury Transfer Module, the carrier that had flown joined to the science orbiters, split away in September.

The main sequence used fifty-five spacecraft slews and thirty-nine hours of observing on September eleventh and twelfth, and the telescope's ability to separate fine detail on the sky came out slightly better than the twenty fifteen lab test.

Principal investigator Emma Bunce said the detector shows no radiation damage after eight years in space, and stray light does not appear in the images.

MIXS is the first imaging X-ray telescope to fly on a planetary science mission and will make a planet-wide map of which atoms make up Mercury's surface.

NTT and SSC Space have put a memorandum of understanding in place, according to Unite.AI, to look at running optical ground stations in different countries as a single network.

An optical ground station is a ground terminal that talks to spacecraft with laser light.

The document takes in market needs, technical compatibility, geographic coverage, and connections to terrestrial networks.

NTT brings optical networking and network-control expertise, and SSC Space runs satellite ground infrastructure.

The opening phase studies interoperability and commercial needs; it does not launch a joint service.

Astronomers led by the Trottier Institute for Research on Exoplanets at the University of Montreal have, according to Notebookcheck, detected a triple-star system.

The system sits just twenty-seven point six light-years from Earth and contains a red dwarf and two white dwarfs, one orbiting close to the red dwarf and the other more distant.

A red dwarf is a small, cool star, and a white dwarf is the compact leftover of a star that has shed its outer layers.

The team was searching for planets around a red dwarf with the SPIRou instrument at the Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope when velocity variations of about ten miles per second prompted follow-up observations with the Gemini North telescope that directly detected the faint white-dwarf companion.

Professor Patrick Dufour said the system should not exist, and that gravitational interactions among the three stars remain poorly understood.
