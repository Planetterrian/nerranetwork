1. **Pakistani Astronaut Joins Shenzhou-24 Mission** — Ratopati
   A Pakistani astronaut will fly on China's Shenzhou-24 spacecraft as a payload specialist for a short-term visit to the Chinese space station. The astronaut will return to Earth aboard Shenzhou-23 after completing the brief in-orbit period. Two candidates, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud, trained at the China Astronaut Research and Training Center in Beijing alongside Chinese counterparts. Their preparation covered practical spacecraft operation skills and intensive Chinese language instruction. The mission stems from a space cooperation agreement signed between China and Pakistan in February 2025. It represents the first joint crewed spaceflight involving both Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's manned space program. The China Manned Space Agency has not released a launch date. The selected astronaut will serve as the first foreign national to reach the Chinese space station. Source: [english.ratopati.com](https://english.ratopati.com/story/85974/pakistani-astronaut-becomes-first-foreigner-to-reach-chinese-space-station)

2. **New Technique Reveals Hidden Exoplanet 51 Eridani b** — Space.com
   Astronomers applied an innovative image-processing method to James Webb Space Telescope observations of the star 51 Eridani. The technique subtracts the overwhelming starlight to isolate the faint signal from the giant exoplanet 51 Eridani b. Once the host star's glare was filtered out, the planet emerged clearly in the data. The approach opens a path to detecting and studying planets that would otherwise stay hidden within the bright stellar light. The planet is one of the few directly imaged worlds whose atmosphere can now be examined in greater detail with existing telescope resources. Source: [space.com](https://www.space.com/astronomy/exoplanets/giant-exoplanet-emerges-from-the-dark-when-the-james-webb-space-telescope-turns-out-the-starlight)

3. **Sunshade Ready for NEO Surveyor Mission** — NASA

NEO Surveyor is designed to discover near-Earth asteroids and comets that ground-based telescopes frequently miss because of their orbital geometry. The completed hardware moves the mission one step closer to launch and the start of its survey operations. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/NASA/status/2118779276547756448)

6. **Robot Builds Truss Structure in Orbit** — Phys.org Space
   A robotic manipulator conceived, designed, and built by Caltech researchers successfully assembled a perimeter truss in space without any astronaut assistance. The system repeatedly selected struts and joints from nearby dispensers to construct identical structural bays. The demonstration took place under DARPA's Novel Orbital and Moon Manufacturing, Materials, and Mass-efficient Design program. The achievement marks the first time an autonomous robotic system has performed construction tasks in the orbital environment. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-robot-successfully-space-autonomous-demo.html)

11. **Ice Giants' Magnetic Fields Linked to Interior Conductivity** — Phys.org Space
    Variations in electrical conductivity deep inside Uranus and Neptune may explain why their magnetic fields lack the orderly dipolar structure seen at Earth. Voyager 2 flyby data from the 1980s first revealed the highly disordered fields around both planets. Changes in how materials conduct electricity at extreme pressures and temperatures inside the ice giants could generate the observed complexity. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-wacky-magnetic-fields-ice-giants.html)

12. **1919 Eclipse Confirmed Einstein's Theory** — Astronomy
    British astronomers led by Arthur Eddington measured the deflection of starlight passing near the Sun during the total solar eclipse of May 29, 1919. The observed bending matched predictions from Albert Einstein's general theory of relativity, published four years earlier. The expedition provided the first empirical test of the new theory and helped establish its acceptance within the scientific community. Source: [astronomy.com](https://www.astronomy.com/today-in-the-history-of-astronomy/nov-6-1919-einstein-is-right/)

### Cosmic Deep Dive: How Tidal Heating Sustains Lunar Volcanism
Imagine a rocky body the size of a large city whose interior remained warm enough to generate lava flows two billion years after standard cooling models predicted it should have frozen solid. That body is Earth's Moon. Samples returned by China's Chang’e-5 mission formed only about two billion years ago yet display clear volcanic textures. Conventional thermal evolution calculations expected the Moon to have lost its internal heat long before that time, leaving its mantle cold and solid.

The resolution lies in repeated gravitational stretching and squeezing by Earth. As the Moon travels around our planet, Earth's gravity raises tides in the lunar rock that repeat on a monthly cycle. Friction generated inside the rock converts mechanical energy into heat, similar to the warmth produced when a paperclip is bent back and forth many times. Over billions of years this steady input can maintain pockets of the lunar mantle above the melting point even while the rest of the body cools.

Tidal heating reaches its peak when the Moon orbits closer to Earth. Early in lunar history the Moon circled at roughly one-third its present distance, so the gravitational forces were far stronger. That extra energy likely sustained widespread melting for hundreds of millions of years. As the Moon receded through tidal interactions, the heating rate declined but never vanished completely. The Chang’e-5 basalts indicate that at least some regions stayed warm enough for volcanism until relatively recently in lunar history.

Miao Chen of the Chengdu University of Technology and colleagues published the tidal-heating explanation in the journal Planet. Their work shows how the cumulative effect of monthly flexing can offset the expected cooling. The process operates most efficiently in regions where rock properties allow greater dissipation of tidal energy into heat.

We still do not know exactly how that heat was distributed through the Moon's interior. Current models disagree on whether tidal energy concentrated in a thin layer near the core-mantle boundary or spread more evenly through the mantle. Resolving the distribution requires new measurements of the Moon's present-day internal temperature profile and the precise rate at which heat continues to escape from the surface today. Without those data, scientists cannot determine whether tidal heating alone accounts for the observed volcanic activity or whether additional mechanisms also contributed. Source: [universetoday.com](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/earths-gravity-may-have-kept-the-early-moons-volcanic-heart-beating)
