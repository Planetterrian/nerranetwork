A single fast radio burst releases in milliseconds as much energy as the Sun produces in three days, yet the signal arrives from galaxies billions of light-years away.

Picture a brief flash so luminous it could be detected across the observable universe, then imagine riding that pulse as it crosses intergalactic space.
