Tianwen-2’s recent burns illustrate how spacecraft reach such tiny targets across hundreds of millions of kilometres.

A main engine firing first drops the probe onto an intercept trajectory, after which a series of much smaller thruster pulses—each lasting only seconds—correct for tiny errors in position and velocity.
