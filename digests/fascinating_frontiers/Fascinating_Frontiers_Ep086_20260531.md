5. **From Ep 84 (2026-05-29): Fascinating Frontiers**
   2. **Dusty Fingerprints Reveal Newborn Planet Masses — r/astronomy**
   Researchers at the University of Warwick developed a method to weigh forming planets by measuring gaps they carve in surrounding dust disks. The technique uses high-resolution images from ALMA to map how dust is cleared at specific orbital distances. Gap width and depth correlate directly with the planet’s mass through hydrodynamic models. The approach works on systems still embedded in their natal gas, offering a window into the first few million years of planet growth. It avoids the need for direct imaging of the planets themselves. Next steps involve applying the method to a larger sample of disks observed by ALMA and JWST.
