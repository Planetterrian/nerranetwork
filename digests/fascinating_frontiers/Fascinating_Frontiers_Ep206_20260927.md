### Top 15 Space & Astronomy Stories
1. **JWST Spots Density Shifts in Chariklo Rings** — Universe Today
   Researchers used stellar occultation observations from 2017 and 2022 to track changes in the rings of the Centaur Chariklo. The inner ring C1R increased in opaqueness by more than fifty percent while the outer ring C2R decreased by about sixty percent. Chariklo itself measures one hundred twenty five kilometers in radius with the rings orbiting at two hundred sixty five and two hundred eighty kilometers from its surface. The team of more than two dozen researchers published the findings in Science Advances. They interpret the shifts as material being gained by one ring and lost by the other. The observations also showed the inner ring denser overall than the outer ring in the later data set. Source: [universetoday.com](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/jwst-finds-dynamic-structural-evolution-in-chariklos-rings)

2. **Andromeda Galaxy Image Cleaned of Earth Artifacts** — NASA
   A stacked image of two hundred twenty three three hundred second exposures from Portugal in twenty nineteen shows the Andromeda galaxy before processing. Airplane trails satellite streaks cosmic ray hits and bad pixels appear in the raw version. Software packages including Astro Pixel Processor DeepSkyStacker and PixInsight reduced these imperfections without altering the galaxy itself. The processed result better matches the true appearance of M31 as seen from Earth. The before image came from a garden observatory during long exposure sessions. Source: [science.nasa.gov](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2026-september-27-andromeda-before-photoshop/)

### Cosmic Spotlight
JWST observations of Chariklo captured changes in ring density through stellar occultation measurements taken years apart. The inner ring brightened significantly in blocking starlight while the outer ring dimmed suggesting ongoing material exchange between the two structures. These shifts occurred over a five year baseline and point to dynamic processes even around a small body one hundred twenty five kilometers across. The findings open questions about how rings around Centaurs form and evolve without a large parent planet to supply debris. What mechanism drives the apparent transfer of material from one ring to the other?

### Cosmic Deep Dive: How Ablation Shields Spacecraft
A typical reentry vehicle traveling at orbital speed carries kinetic energy equivalent to the explosive yield of several tons of TNT concentrated into its heat shield surface. Picture a shield the size of a large dinner table absorbing that energy while the craft drops from space to sea level in roughly eight minutes. The outer layer reaches temperatures above three thousand degrees Fahrenheit and literally boils away molecule by molecule carrying heat outward instead of inward. Engineers embed sensors that record exactly how fast each millimeter disappears so the next design can shave off unnecessary mass. One surprising measurement showed ablation rates varying by more than twenty percent across a single panel because of tiny differences in material density. The open puzzle is whether we can ever predict those local variations well enough to trust an untested shield on a crewed return from Mars.
