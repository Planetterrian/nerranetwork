On a different note, a phased array concept would operate in the radio-quiet zone behind the Moon.

The design targets frequencies blocked or contaminated by Earth's ionosphere and human transmitters, including the still-unexplained 1977 "Wow!" signal band.

Picture a single large radio dish placed on the Moon's far side: every terrestrial transmitter, every satellite downlink, and even Earth's own ionosphere would sit behind 3,500 kilometres of solid rock, creating a silence deeper than any spot on our planet.

Radio waves from cosmic sources would arrive unimpeded while human-generated noise, which already swamps the "Wow!" signal's frequency band today, would be blocked.

The proposed array would scan at wavelengths blocked from Earth, listening for faint signals that might reveal the universe's first stars or unknown natural phenomena.
