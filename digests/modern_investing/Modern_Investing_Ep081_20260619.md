### Strategy Spotlight
Corporate share buybacks at a discount to market price transfer value directly to remaining shareholders by reducing float and supporting per-share metrics. Stingray’s $15.4 million repurchase from La Caisse at a 5.1 percent discount to the prior close demonstrates this in real time on the TSX. Investors can screen for similar private-agreement buybacks announced on SEDAR+ and compare the repurchase price to the 20-day VWAP before deciding on position size. This approach has historically worked best in low-liquidity names where the discount exceeds normal trading costs and the company has stable free cash flow. The main risk is that the buyback signals management sees limited internal growth rather than undervaluation. Screen TSX announcements daily and model the post-buyback share count change before adding exposure. ### Investor Education: How Internal Reorganizations Affect Regulated Utility Valuations
Imagine you bought AltaLink units last month ahead of the announced internal reorganization that moves all regulated transmission assets into BHE AltaLink Ltd. Your order filled at the pre-announcement price, but the actual economic exposure shifted because the new entity now directly holds the KainaiLink and PiikaniLink partnership interests. The mechanism is straightforward: the regulated rate base stays identical, yet the corporate wrapper changes, which can alter tax attributes and dividend flow without any change in operations. Most retail investors assume the ticker and yield remain static; in reality the reorganization can trigger a one-time distribution adjustment or a brief liquidity gap while the new entity lists. The pro tip is to check the press release for any mention of “special distribution” or “exchange ratio” before the vote—those lines tell you exactly how many new shares you will receive. The biggest mistake with reorganizations is treating them as neutral events. Instead, always model the post-reorg dividend stream and any tax-deferred rollover implications in your TFSA before deciding to hold through the effective date.
