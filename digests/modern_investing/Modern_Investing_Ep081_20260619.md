### Strategy Spotlight
Share buybacks at a discount to market price can transfer value directly to remaining shareholders when funded from cash reserves rather than debt. Stingray Group’s $15.4 million repurchase of one million shares at $15.40—a 5.1% discount to the prior close—illustrates the tactic in a Canadian small-cap context. Investors can screen for similar opportunities by monitoring TSX filings for private agreements with large holders and comparing the repurchase price to the 20-day volume-weighted average. This approach has historically worked best in low-volatility periods when management has high conviction in free-cash-flow stability, but it carries execution risk if the company later needs that cash for operations. Platforms such as TMX or SEDAR+ let retail investors pull the filings the same day they are released. ### Investor Education: Listener Guide: Investing Your First $10,000
Imagine you have just received a $10,000 windfall and want to put it to work in your TFSA this week. The first step is confirming your emergency fund already covers three to six months of expenses in a high-interest savings account; if it does not, that cash stays outside the TFSA. For most Canadians the TFSA comes before an RRSP because contribution room is flexible and withdrawals are tax-free. A simple three-ETF starter portfolio could be 60% a broad Canadian equity ETF such as XIU, 30% a U.S. total-market ETF such as VUN, and 10% a global ex-North-America ETF such as XEF, rebalanced once a year. Open the account directly in the brokerage app, fund it by electronic transfer, then place a single market order for each ETF during regular trading hours; the psychological barrier of pressing “buy” is reduced by dollar-cost averaging the same dollar amount every two weeks thereafter. Historically these allocations have delivered roughly 7% annualized returns over long periods, but single-year results have ranged from +25% to –20%. The biggest misconception is believing the first $10,000 must be perfectly allocated; instead, fund the account, buy the three ETFs, and set up automatic contributions so the process compounds without further decisions.
