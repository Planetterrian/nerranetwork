### Investor Education: TFSA Contribution Room Accuracy
Imagine you check your CRA account and see a contribution room figure that looks current. The actual number may lag because the agency updates TFSA data only once a year based on the previous calendar year's transactions. For two thousand twenty-six the base annual limit is seven thousand dollars but your personal room could be higher from unused amounts or withdrawals made in two thousand twenty-five. Using an outdated figure risks an over-contribution penalty of one percent per month on the excess. The pro tip is to keep your own spreadsheet of every contribution and withdrawal date so you always know the precise room available before you fund the account. Most retail investors assume the CRA portal is live when it is not, and the simple fix is to cross-reference your own records before every deposit. Waste Connections offers one example of a stock that could fit inside a correctly tracked TFSA after its recent nine percent pullback. The company reported six point four percent revenue growth and twenty-five percent free cash flow growth in its latest quarter while raising its full-year outlook. Keeping accurate records prevents the penalty that would otherwise erode those gains inside the tax-free wrapper.
