### Investor Education: Bid Ask Spread Mechanics
Imagine you bought shares of a mid cap TSX name last week after an earnings beat and your order filled at fifty four dollars and ten cents. The bid ask spread on that name went from two cents to fifteen cents in the first thirty minutes of trading as volume spiked. What most retail investors do not realize is that the spread widens most when retail order flow hits without institutional participation, turning a small commission into a hidden cost of one point five percent round trip. The professionals always check the level two quote and average daily volume before entering on news. The misconception to avoid is assuming your market order will always fill near the last trade price; instead set a limit order one tick inside the current spread when volume is below the twenty day average. This dynamic appears frequently in names with recent earnings surprises where retail attention surges quickly.
