### Investor Education: Crypto in a Traditional Portfolio: How Much and Why
Imagine you added a 3% Bitcoin allocation to your TFSA last month when regulatory clarity improved. Your order filled at the quoted price, but the actual mechanics involve low correlation to equities that breaks during risk-off events like the current inflation spike. Bitcoin’s 30-day correlation to the S&P 500 has averaged 0.35 over the past year yet spiked above 0.70 during the April sell-off, showing the diversification benefit is conditional on stable macro conditions. The efficient frontier math supports a 1-5% allocation for most Canadian portfolios because it improves Sharpe ratio up to that point before volatility drag dominates. Canadian investors gain a clear edge by using BTCX or ETHX inside a TFSA where gains are tax-free, avoiding the self-custody key-loss risk that has wiped out holdings for retail users. Professionals always check on-chain exchange reserves and funding rates before sizing up, data points most retail platforms hide behind simple price charts. The biggest mistake with crypto allocation is treating the 5% sleeve as a trading position instead of a permanent diversifier. Instead, set a hard 5% cap and rebalance only on 50% moves in either direction.
