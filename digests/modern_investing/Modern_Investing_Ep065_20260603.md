### Strategy Spotlight
Share repurchase expansions create a direct capital return mechanism that can support share prices even when broader markets stall. Shopify’s US$3-billion increase to its existing buyback program illustrates this approach in a high-growth software name trading at elevated multiples. Investors can screen for companies announcing or accelerating repurchases by monitoring TSX and SEC filings through platforms like TMX Money or EDGAR, then cross-reference free cash flow coverage ratios above 1.5x to confirm sustainability. This tactic has historically performed best in sideways or mildly declining markets where organic growth slows but cash generation remains strong; the main risk is that management overpays for shares or diverts capital from higher-ROI projects. A practical implementation step is to add a custom alert in your brokerage screener for “share repurchase authorization increase greater than 5% of market cap” and pair it with a 50-day moving average filter to avoid catching falling knives. ### Investor Education: Bid-Ask Spread Dynamics in Volatile Sessions
Imagine you bought an oil-services ETF yesterday morning when the latest Hormuz headlines hit; your market order filled at the ask but the quoted spread had already widened from 3 cents to 18 cents within the first 20 minutes of trading. That extra 15-cent gap represents immediate slippage that compounds on larger position sizes and is rarely visible in end-of-day charts. The mechanism works because market makers widen spreads to protect against adverse selection when news flow spikes volume without balanced two-way interest, turning what looks like a liquid name into a costly execution. What most retail investors don’t realize is that limit orders placed just inside the widened spread often get ignored until the next lull, so professionals pre-stage orders during lower-volatility periods or use midpoint peg orders when available. The biggest mistake with spread dynamics is assuming your broker’s “best execution” guarantee protects you from temporary widening. Instead, always check the Level 2 book and recent trade prints for the prior 15 minutes before entering size on any name showing headline-driven volume.
