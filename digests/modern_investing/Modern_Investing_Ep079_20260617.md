### Investor Education: Canadian Discount Brokerages Compared
Imagine you want to buy a U.S. tech stock in your TFSA right after the open. You click buy on Wealthsimple Trade and receive a fill, but the same order routed through Interactive Brokers might cost less in total and give better routing. Wealthsimple Trade offers commission-free trades yet limits advanced order types and international exchanges. Questrade charges low commissions with free ETF purchases and solid mobile execution for Canadians holding TSX and NYSE names. Interactive Brokers provides the lowest margin rates and broadest global market access but requires comfort with a more complex platform. Bank-owned brokerages like TD Direct Investing integrate easily with chequing accounts yet carry higher per-trade fees that erode smaller positions. The key edge comes from matching the brokerage to your style: frequent U.S. traders lean Interactive Brokers while simple ETF buyers stay with Questrade or Wealthsimple. Always compare total all-in cost including data fees and currency conversion before moving an account.
