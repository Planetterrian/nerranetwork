### Investor Education: Reading Volume Confirmation in Real Time
Imagine you bought shares of a chip stock yesterday after seeing the breakout headline and your order filled at the ask. The bid ask spread on that name widened from two cents to fifteen cents in the first thirty minutes because volume stayed below the twenty day average. What most retail investors do not realize is that a breakout without that volume filter often reverses within two sessions as the lack of follow through lets sellers step back in. The pro tip is to open the volume column in your platform before you click buy and compare today's bar to the twenty day line. The concrete misconception to avoid is treating any price jump as confirmation when the real edge comes from requiring volume to exceed the average on the same day.
