Canadian listed crypto exchange traded funds, such as BTCX and ETHX, trade on the TSX
with management fees around 1% and settle in Canadian dollars, so you never touch a wallet
or a private key.
The Ontario Securities Commission, or OSC, oversees those products and they come with custody
at qualified banks, unlike offshore exchanges that have faced insolvency, and OSC rules
require qualified third-party custodians.
Gains inside a TFA stay tax-free, but any transfer to self-custody triggers a deemed disposition
at fair market value, which is why most Canadian investors stay with the listed products and
skip private key risk.
Size the sleeve so that even a 50% drawdown leaves the overall portfolio inside your stated
risk tolerance.
