Calls give the right to buy at a set strike while puts give the right to sell.

Intrinsic value is the difference between strike and current price while time value reflects days until expiration and volatility.

A covered call on shares you already own collects premium income but caps upside.

Delta measures how much the option price moves with the stock while theta shows daily time decay.
