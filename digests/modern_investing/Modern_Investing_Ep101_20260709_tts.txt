Publicly traded REITs such as RioCan or Canadian Apartment REIT trade with daily liquidity but distribute most taxable income, creating immediate tax drag inside non-registered accounts.

Mortgage investment corporations offer higher yields through private lending yet carry liquidity risk and different tax treatment as interest income.
