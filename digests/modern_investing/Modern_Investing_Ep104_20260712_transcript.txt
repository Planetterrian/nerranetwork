than the NAV. The pro tip most retail investors miss is that limit orders placed at the midpoint
during the first 30 minutes of trading often get filled inside the spread once volume normalizes,
whereas market orders pay the full widened spread. The biggest mistake with dividend ETFs is assuming

distribution calendar and set a limit order at least one cent inside the current bid before the
ex-dividend date to capture the full compounding effect without paying unnecessary spread.
