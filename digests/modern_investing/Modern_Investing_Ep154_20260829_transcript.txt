Imagine you spot the new China AI Tigers LLMETFTGRZ on a launch headline and place an order at the open.
Your fill arrives, yet the position drifts lower because the first hour volume never cleared the 20-day average.
The mechanism is simple. Without that volume spike, institutional desks have not yet committed size, so retail flow alone cannot sustain the move and spreads stay wide for the first 30-60 minutes.
On a thin volume launch day, the ETF may print only a few thousand shares in the opening hour, leaving the bid asked spread at 20-30 cents instead of the tighter 5-cent level that appears once average daily volume is established.
Professionals always cross-check the same-day volume print against the 20-day line before treating any news-driven pop as confirmed.
The concrete misconception to avoid is assuming every headline equals immediate follow-through.
The one sentence fix is to require volume above the 20-day average on the entry day itself.
