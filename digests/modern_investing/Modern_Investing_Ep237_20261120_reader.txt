Covered-call writing, as The Motley Fool laid out, lets you collect premium on shares you already own or are willing to buy, capping upside in exchange for yield while you hold the position.

Think of it like taking a non-refundable deposit on a holiday cottage you still want to own — you keep the deposit if the week passes quietly, but if a buyer shows up at your posted price, you hand over the keys.
