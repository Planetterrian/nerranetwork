Once a single segment’s revenue exceeds half the total and grows faster than the legacy businesses, analysts re-weight the multiple toward that segment’s peer group, which often trades at a premium.

A practical check is to compare the data segment’s contribution margin against the trading segment’s margin in the same filing; when that gap widens, the stock tends to rerate upward even if headline earnings are flat.

The common misconception is that all revenue lines inside an exchange are interchangeable, as if a dollar of trading fees and a dollar of recurring data were the same quality of cash.
