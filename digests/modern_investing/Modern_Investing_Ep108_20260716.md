### Investor Education: Contrarian: The Canadian Home Bias Problem
Imagine you allocated 40% of your TFSA to Canadian equities last year because the dividend tax credit felt like free money and the names were familiar. Your order filled cleanly, yet the TSX’s 50% weighting in financials and energy left you exposed to exactly the sectors already tied to your job and mortgage. The mechanism is simple: Canada represents roughly 3% of global market cap, so a 30–50% home allocation means you are structurally underweight U.S. technology and healthcare that have driven most of the past decade’s returns. Professionals always check their total geographic exposure across all accounts before adding another Canadian bank or pipeline. The biggest mistake with home bias is treating familiarity as diversification. Instead, calculate your true Canada weight including employer stock and real estate, then cap the equity portion at 20–25% unless you have a specific currency-hedging thesis.
