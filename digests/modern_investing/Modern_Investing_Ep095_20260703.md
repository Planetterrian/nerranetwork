### Strategy Spotlight
Quality-compounding focuses on identifying durable businesses trading below intrinsic value using metrics such as return on invested capital and free-cash-flow yield rather than headline growth. Today's environment of selective AI-stock rebounds and broader market rotation makes this approach relevant because expensive momentum names can mask undervalued compounders that deliver steadier long-term results. Implement it by screening for companies with consistent earnings growth above 8% and enterprise-value-to-EBITDA below sector medians, then cross-check against 10-year average multiples on platforms such as TradingView or your broker's fundamental scanner. This strategy performed best during 2018-2019 and 2022 rate-hike periods when growth multiples compressed and value re-rated upward. The primary risk is opportunity cost during strong momentum regimes, so limit position size to 5% and maintain a 12-24 month horizon. ### Investor Education: Crypto as a Portfolio Allocation: A Risk-Managed Approach
Imagine you added a 3% crypto sleeve to your TFSA last month when Bitcoin broke above its 200-day moving average. Your order filled at the quoted price, but here's what actually happened between your click and settlement: the exchange spread widened from 0.2% to 0.8% during the first hour of volatility, and on-chain settlement added another 15-minute delay before your wallet reflected the position. What most retail investors don't realize is that Canadian-listed products such as BTCX and ETHX already embed custody and regulatory overhead, so the true cost difference versus self-custody is often only the 0.5-1.0% annual management fee versus exchange hacking or key-loss risk. The biggest mistake with crypto allocation is treating it like a momentum trade instead of a non-correlated diversifier. Instead, always size the position so a 50% drawdown still leaves total portfolio volatility under your target, and rebalance only once per quarter.
