by electronic transfer. Place a single market order for each ETF during regular trading hours.
Dollar cost averaging the same dollar amount every two weeks thereafter reduces the psychological
barrier of pressing buy. Historically these allocations have delivered roughly 7% annualized returns
over long periods, though single-year results have ranged from plus 25% to minus 20%.
