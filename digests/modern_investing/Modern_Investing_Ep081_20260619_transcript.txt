Now here is something most retail investors get wrong

Imagine you bought AltaLink units last month
ahead of the announced internal reorganization
that moved all regulated transmission assets
into BHE AltaLink limited.
Your order failed at the pre-announcement price
but the actual economic exposure shifted
because the new entity now directly holds
the Kainai link and Pee Kainai link partnership interests.
The regulated rate base stays identical
yet the corporate wrapper changes
which can alter tax attributes and dividend flow
without any change in operations.
Most retail investors assume the ticker
and yield remain static.
In reality, the reorganization can trigger
a one-time distribution adjustment
or a brief liquidity gap while the new entity lists.
The pro tip is to check the press release
for any mention of special distribution
or exchange ratio before the vote.
Those lines tell you exactly
how many new shares you will receive.
The biggest mistake with reorganizations
is treating them as neutral events.
Instead, always model the post-reor dividend stream
and any tax deferred roll over implications
in your TFSA before deciding to hold
through the effective date.
