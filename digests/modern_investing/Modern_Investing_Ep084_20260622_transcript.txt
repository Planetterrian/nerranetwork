minutes of trading, costing you an extra 0.4% before any price move. Calls give the right to buy
at a set strike while puts give the right to sell. Intrinsic value is the difference between strike and
current price while time value reflects days until expiration and volatility. A covered call on shares
you already own collects premium income but caps upside. A cash secured put lets you buy at a discount
if the stock drops. Delta measures how much the option price moves with the stock while theta
shows daily time decay. The biggest mistake is selling naked calls without defined risk. Instead
always size positions so maximum loss stays under 2% of portfolio and check open interest before
entering. Before we wrap pull up the 30-day options chain on CRM and calculate the annualized yield of
