I would treat it as a cut in translated earnings. The nominal dividend can sit still while the US cash, converted back, shrinks, and the effective yield on the position drops without a single distribution notice.

That mechanism shows up in the first thirty minutes of a session, when cross-border flows can widen bid-ask spreads on dual-listed names by three to five cents.
