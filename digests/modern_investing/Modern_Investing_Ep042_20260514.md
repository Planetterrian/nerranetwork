### Investor Education: Crypto in a Traditional Portfolio: How Much and Why
Imagine you added a 3% Bitcoin allocation to your TFSA last month through BTCX when cross-asset signals turned positive. Your order filled cleanly because the ETF trades on the TSX with tight spreads during market hours, yet the position still moved 12% while the rest of your equity sleeve rose only 4%. The mechanism at work is Bitcoin’s historically low correlation to equities (around 0.2–0.4 in normal regimes) that improves the efficient frontier by lifting expected return for a given level of risk when kept between 1% and 5%. Canadian investors gain an extra edge by holding BTCX or ETHX inside a TFSA, avoiding both capital-gains tax drag and the custody headaches of self-managed wallets. Professionals always check the 30-day rolling correlation before sizing up; if it spikes above 0.6 they reduce or exit because the diversification benefit has collapsed. The biggest mistake with crypto allocation is treating it like a growth stock and letting it exceed 5% of the portfolio. Instead, set a hard 3% target, rebalance annually, and treat anything above that threshold as pure speculation rather than portfolio construction.
