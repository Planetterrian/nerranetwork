Cash-secured puts let an investor collect premium income while committing to buy a stock only at a chosen strike price.

The approach turns a potential purchase into an income-generating position rather than a plain limit order that earns nothing if unfilled.

Today's options discussion highlights that a short put always returns less loss than an unexecuted limit order because the premium offsets part of any decline.

The main risk is opportunity cost if the stock rallies sharply and the premium is left on the table.
