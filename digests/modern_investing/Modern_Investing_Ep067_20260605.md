### Strategy Spotlight
Non-farm payrolls act as a high-frequency gauge of economic momentum that directly shapes sector rotation decisions. When actual hiring exceeds consensus by a wide margin, as it did today, markets typically reprice the path of interest rates higher, boosting financials and value stocks while pressuring growth names. To apply this, pull the latest NFP release into a simple spreadsheet, compare the surprise versus the prior three-month average, and tilt a TFSA allocation toward banks or pipelines only if the beat exceeds 50,000 jobs and volume on financial ETFs confirms the move. This approach worked well in 2023–2024 when repeated beats kept financials outperforming growth during the hiking cycle. The main risk is that revisions can flip the narrative within a month, so always size the tilt below 10% of the portfolio. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/NickTimiraos/status/2062875184944550336)

### Investor Education: Dividend Tax Efficiency in Registered Accounts
Imagine you added a Canadian bank stock to your TFSA last quarter expecting a 4% yield. Your brokerage shows the dividend credited, but the actual after-tax advantage only appears when you compare eligible Canadian dividends against foreign ones inside versus outside registered accounts. The mechanism works because eligible Canadian dividends receive a gross-up and dividend tax credit that eliminates tax inside a TFSA or RRSP, while U.S. dividends still face 15% withholding even in those accounts. Most retail investors overlook that the credit is wasted in registered accounts yet becomes valuable in non-registered accounts, leading them to hold high-yield U.S. names in TFSAs where the withholding permanently reduces returns. The pro tip is to run a quick comparison in your brokerage’s tax estimator: move any U.S. dividend payers into an RRSP (where withholding is reclaimable via treaty) and keep Canadian Dividend Aristocrats in the TFSA. The biggest mistake with dividend placement is treating all yields as equal. Instead, always map the tax character of each holding to the account type before the next contribution deadline.
