Most retail investors do not realize that holding the core in a registered account like

The pro tip is to set satellite positions at fixed dollar amounts rather than percentages,
so rebalancing happens only when the core drifts more than 5% from target.
The common misconception is that frequent satellite trading improves returns when the real
edge comes from keeping the core allocation stable and letting the satellites run until
they hit a predefined exit rule, such as a 20% gain or a sector rotation signal.
The creation redemption arbitrage keeps tracking error low enough that even a 5% satellite
allocation can be added or removed without moving the overall portfolio beta outside its
intended range.
Registered account placement of the core also shields the investor from the annual tax

years. Fixed dollar satellite sizing removes the need for constant percentage calculations,
