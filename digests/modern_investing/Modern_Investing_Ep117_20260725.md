### Strategy Spotlight
A concentrated rally occurs when a handful of large-cap names drive most of the index gains, leaving smaller stocks and other sectors behind. Europe's equity market has flipped from its traditional broad participation into exactly this pattern in 2026, mirroring the U.S. experience where a few mega-cap AI stocks have dominated. The Bloomberg Markets report notes that the European equity market had long been viewed as immune to shocks driven by only a handful of stocks, yet a narrow rally this year has completely flipped that script. For Canadian investors this matters because the same dynamic can appear on the TSX when resource or financial names lag behind a few dominant names. One practical way to monitor it is to compare the equal-weighted version of an index against the cap-weighted version using free charting tools on TradingView or your broker platform; a widening gap signals concentration risk. Historically this setup has produced sharp reversals when leadership rotates, but it can also persist for months, punishing diversified portfolios that hold the broader index. The risk is that a sudden policy or earnings shock in the leaders triggers a broad sell-off. Check the 20-day volume on the leaders versus the broader index before assuming the trend will continue. ### Investor Education: Understanding ETF Inflow and Outflow Mechanics
Imagine you added an Ethereum ETF to your TFSA last week after seeing five straight days of inflows. Your purchase executed at the prevailing market price, yet the very next day the fund posted net outflows and the price dropped. What actually happened is that authorized participants create or redeem ETF shares in large blocks only when arbitrage opportunities appear between the ETF's market price and the underlying crypto holdings; retail flows alone do not force immediate price movement. In the most recent week, Ethereum ETFs recorded a net outflow on the final day even while extending their three-week inflow streak, showing how daily noise can mask the longer trend. Bitcoin ETFs logged a second day of outflows yet also kept their weekly inflow streak alive at three weeks. The pro tip most retail investors miss is to look at the premium or discount to net asset value reported each morning on the issuer's site before trading; a persistent discount can signal that selling pressure is already priced in. The biggest mistake with ETF flow data is treating one day's print as a directional signal. Instead, always average the last five trading days and cross-check against the underlying asset's spot volume.
