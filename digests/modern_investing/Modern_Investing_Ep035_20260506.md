### Strategy Spotlight
The shift toward semiannual earnings reporting under the SEC's proposed 10-S form gives companies more room to prioritize multi-year capital allocation and product cycles instead of managing quarterly expectations. Today's market conditions amplify this relevance because high-growth sectors like AI and streaming have already shown they can deliver large single-day moves on results, yet frequent reporting can amplify short-term volatility that retail investors often chase. Implementation starts with reviewing a company's most recent 10-K or annual report for long-term guidance metrics such as multi-year revenue targets or capex plans, then cross-checking against current analyst models on platforms like your brokerage's research portal. Historically this approach has rewarded patient holders during periods when management could execute without constant headline pressure, as seen in past shifts away from quarterly guidance in certain industries. The main risk is reduced transparency for smaller investors who rely on frequent filings, so pair any position with strict position sizing and stop-loss rules. Source: [cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/05/sec-is-close-to-ending-mandatory-quarterly-earnings-reports-that-trump-called-for.html)

### Investor Education: Crypto as a Portfolio Allocation: A Risk-Managed Approach
Imagine you added a 3% Bitcoin position to your TFSA last month after seeing a cross-asset signal from equity futures and gold prices. Your order filled at the quoted price, but the actual execution cost you an extra 0.4% because the Canadian crypto ETF you used widened its bid-ask spread during the first 45 minutes of trading as volume spiked. That spread expansion happens because crypto markets operate 24/7 while traditional brokers batch orders, and liquidity providers pull quotes when volatility exceeds their risk limits. What most retail investors don't realize is that the three largest Canadian crypto ETFs (BTCX, ETHX, and their unhedged counterparts) carry annual management fees between 0.95% and 1.50% plus potential tracking error during high-volume periods, so the net drag on a 3% allocation can exceed 5 basis points per month even before price movement. The professionals always check the ETF's premium or discount to net asset value on the exchange before placing the trade and compare it against the underlying spot price on two separate data terminals. The biggest mistake with crypto allocation is treating it as a binary on/off decision rather than a calibrated sleeve. Instead, always size the position so that a 30% drawdown in the crypto sleeve produces no more than a 1% impact on total portfolio value, then rebalance only on a quarterly calendar or when the sleeve drifts beyond a 2-percentage-point band.
