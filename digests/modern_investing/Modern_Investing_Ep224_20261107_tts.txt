You hold ZRE inside a T F S A and the monthly distribution hits, and it is easy to treat every dollar like bond interest.

REIT distributions flow through as return of capital, income, or capital gains, and inside a T F S A none of those categories triggers immediate tax, unlike a non-registered account where return of capital reduces adjusted cost base and can create future capital-gains exposure.

The BMO Equal Weight REITs Index E T F pays monthly, and those payments retain their character even when sheltered, so the tax label does not vanish just because the account does.

Download the annual tax-information package each REIT or E T F publishes in February and confirm the split before filing, rather than assuming every dollar is pure income.

The misconception is that all REIT payouts behave like bond interest; the fix is to treat them as a three-part allocation and track the capital component separately even inside registered accounts, so you still know the true economic return if you ever move the holding outside the T F S A or need adjusted cost base for any reason.
