### Strategy Spotlight
Valuation investing identifies companies trading below their intrinsic worth based on metrics like return on equity and capital allocation strength, then holds until the gap closes or fundamentals deteriorate. Hannover Re reported a 21.1 percent return on equity in its latest quarter on strong profitability, showing how reinsurance operators can compound value when underwriting discipline holds. The firm achieved this RoE through disciplined risk selection that kept combined ratios in check even as premium volumes grew. Screen for names with RoE above 15 percent and stable combined ratios using your broker's fundamental scanner, then compare the current price-to-book ratio against the five-year average to quantify the discount. This approach worked well in prior rate-cycle peaks when excess capital was redeployed into share buybacks rather than new risk. It fails when catastrophe losses spike faster than reserves, as seen in earlier industry drawdowns where RoE dropped below 10 percent for multiple quarters. Apply the screen inside a TFSA to defer tax on any re-rating gains while monitoring quarterly reserve releases for early warning signs of deterioration. Canadian investors can run the same screen on TSX-listed insurers to capture similar capital-allocation advantages without currency conversion costs. ### Investor Education: How National-Team ETF Buying Affects Liquidity
You hold a broad-market ETF in your TFSA and notice the bid-ask spread tightening sharply on a day when Chinese state funds report record purchases. The mechanism works because large, coordinated ETF inflows from government-linked vehicles absorb sell-side supply in one block, narrowing spreads and reducing intraday volatility for retail orders that follow. In the most recent week those funds deployed the largest single-week total on record, lifting the underlying index without requiring individual stock-by-stock accumulation. The coordinated buying creates a temporary liquidity buffer that allows smaller orders to execute closer to the mid-price rather than at the wider ask. The pro habit is to check the previous session's ETF flow data before market open using your platform's news feed; if flows exceed the prior 20-day average, tighten limit orders by one cent to capture the improved liquidity. This check takes less than two minutes on most Canadian broker dashboards and prevents paying an extra spread when the national-team bid is active. The common misconception is that all volume spikes reflect organic investor demand; the fix is to cross-reference flow reports before assuming the move will persist once the coordinated buying pauses. When the national-team purchases slow, spreads can widen again within a single session, catching traders who entered on the artificial liquidity without checking the source of the volume. Source: [bloomberg.com](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/china-s-national-team-ramps-up-etf-buying-to-record-high)
