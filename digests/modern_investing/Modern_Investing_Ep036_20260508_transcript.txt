Modern portfolio theory shows that combining assets with low correlations can move your portfolio
closer to the efficient frontier where risk is minimized for any given return target.
Strategic allocation sets long-term weights based on your risk tolerance while tactical shifts
respond to short-term signals such as valuation spreads or sector momentum.
