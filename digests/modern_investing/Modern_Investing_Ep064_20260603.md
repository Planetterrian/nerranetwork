### Investor Education: How to Read an Earnings Report
Imagine you bought a healthcare name last week after it announced an overseas product launch. Your order filled at the open, but the real driver was whether management’s forward guidance exceeded consensus and whether free cash flow actually covered the expansion spend. Revenue growth shows top-line momentum while EPS reveals how efficiently costs are managed; a company can post higher revenue yet still miss on EPS if margins compress. Check the balance sheet for rising long-term debt relative to cash and compare operating cash flow to reported net income to spot aggressive accounting. The biggest mistake retail investors make is fixating only on the headline beat or miss. Instead, always read the management commentary section for explicit language on guidance changes and capital allocation before deciding to add or trim.
