### Investor Education: Crypto in a Traditional Portfolio: How Much and Why
Imagine you added a small Bitcoin allocation to your TFSA last year when ETF flows turned positive. Your purchase filled at the prevailing spot price, yet the actual cost basis also reflected the ETF's management fee and any premium or discount to net asset value that day. Bitcoin's correlation with equities has stayed low over multi-year periods, which improves the efficient frontier when the allocation stays between one and five percent, but the correlation spikes during liquidity shocks and can erase that diversification benefit quickly. Canadian investors gain an edge by holding BTCX or ETHX inside a TFSA because all gains remain tax-free and contribution room is preserved, unlike holding the coins directly where custody and reporting add friction. The pro tip is to set the position size once using portfolio-correlation software and then rebalance only on a fixed schedule rather than reacting to price swings. Most retail investors assume any crypto holding is automatically speculative, yet a one-to-five percent sleeve functions as a diversifier when the rest of the portfolio stays in broad equities and bonds; exceeding five percent shifts the sleeve into outright speculation.
