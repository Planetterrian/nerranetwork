### Investor Education: How Neutral Rate Revisions Shift Equity Valuations
You hold a utility or REIT position expecting lower rates to lift valuations, yet the stock stalls after a senior Fed official raises the neutral rate estimate. Waller revised his neutral rate higher, stating the policy stance is not as tight as thought 18 months ago, which directly raises the discount rate applied to long-duration cash flows in those sectors. The mechanism works because the neutral rate serves as the anchor for the entire forward curve; when it moves up 25 basis points, terminal value calculations on 10-year cash flows drop by roughly 3 to 5 percent depending on growth assumptions. Professional desks immediately rerun dividend-discount models with the new neutral rate before retail screens update. Waller added that if core CPI rises 0.3 percent for a third straight month, annualized inflation would run at 3.5 percent and that lack of progress would argue for frontloading anticipated hikes rather than sitting and staring at it. The common misconception is that any rate-cut signal is automatically bullish for yield names; the fix is to check the latest neutral-rate comments from voting FOMC members before sizing rate-sensitive positions.
