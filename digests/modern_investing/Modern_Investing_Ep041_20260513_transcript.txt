The mechanism that surprises most retail investors
is the tax treatment difference.
REIT distributions are not eligible for the dividend tax credit.
Inside a TFSA or RRSP, they grow tax-deferred.
Direct rental properties allow depreciation claims
and potential principal residence exemption on sale.
A pro tip is to check the REIT's AFFO payout ratio before buying.
Anything above 90% leaves little cushion for maintenance capex.
Mortgage investment corporations offer another route with higher yields
but illiquidity and credit risk that retail platforms rarely highlight.
The biggest mistake with real estate allocation
is treating your home and the REIT as interchangeable.

so that total real estate exposure,
