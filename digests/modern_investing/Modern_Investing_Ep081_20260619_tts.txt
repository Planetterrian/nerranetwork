Imagine you bought AltaLink units last month ahead of the announced internal reorganization that moves all regulated transmission assets into BHE AltaLink Limited

Your order filled at the pre-announcement price but the actual economic exposure shifted because the new entity now directly holds the KainaiLink and PiikaniLink partnership interests.

The regulated rate base stays identical yet the corporate wrapper changes which can alter tax attributes and dividend flow without any change in operations.

Most retail investors assume the ticker and yield remain static.

In reality the reorganization can trigger a one-time distribution adjustment or a brief liquidity gap while the new entity lists.

The pro tip is to check the press release for any mention of special distribution or exchange ratio before the vote.

Those lines tell you exactly how many new shares you will receive.

The biggest mistake with reorganizations is treating them as neutral events.

Instead always model the post-reorg dividend stream and any tax-deferred rollover implications in your T F S A before deciding to hold through the effective date.
