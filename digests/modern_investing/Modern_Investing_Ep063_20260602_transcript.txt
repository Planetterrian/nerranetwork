so the effective fill price moves against you
by the amount of that widening often 5 to 15 cents

is that the displayed bid-ass quote
is only the top of the book.

and a single large order can walk the book

The professionals always check level two depth
and average daily volume before sizing a momentum trade
then use limit orders or iceberg orders
