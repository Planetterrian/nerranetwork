Your shares were acquired at the prevailing market price

It was the float generated by premiums collected upfront

In 2025, the float exceeded $170 billion,
effectively providing Berkshire with interest-free leverage
that amplifies every percentage point of investment return.
Most retail investors overlook this
because they focus only on the stock holdings
rather than the liability side of the balance sheet.
The pro tip is to scan the footnotes
of any insurance heavy company's 10K for the float line item
and compare its growth rate to the investment yield.
When float expands faster than claims,
the compounding advantage widens.
The biggest misconception is treating float as free money
without duration risk.
Instead, always check the duration
of the insurance liabilities before assuming
the structure will work at smaller scales.
