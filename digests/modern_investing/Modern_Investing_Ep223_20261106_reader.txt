The Bank strengthened its analysis of how global supply shocks transmit into Canadian inflation and will apply those lessons while staying inside the existing inflation-targeting mandate, which is a change in homework, not a change in the reaction function.

A credible, well-understood framework cuts uncertainty for households and businesses and supports investment and job creation even after repeated shocks, which is why the Government of Canada and the Bank review and renew that framework every five years and then leave the Bank to run policy independently against the agreed goals.

Over the past five years the framework took supply-chain disruptions, rapid demand shifts, and geopolitical conflicts that pushed inflation higher worldwide, and the Bank responded forcefully, bringing inflation back to the two percent target and inside the control range, which is evidence the framework itself held when implementation was ugly.

The renewed statement underlines that high inflation erodes purchasing power and perceptions of fairness, which is the case for keeping inflation low, stable, and predictable rather than treating every scare as a signal to rewrite the rulebook.
