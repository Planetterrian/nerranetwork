Yesterday’s inflation reading is now a November paycheck. La Nación says older adults paid by Anses, Argentina’s social security agency, get that rise on pensions and benefits in the eleventh month.

The monthly rule uses the price increase to two decimal places. Indec’s September figure was 1.9 percent, and that is why the benefit adjustment is 1.93.

After the rise, the minimum pension itself is 444,158 pesos and 45 cents. The extraordinary bonus is still 70,000 pesos, and that is what brings the minimum payment to the total in the open.

The maximum pension is 2,988,765 pesos and 84 cents, and that line has no bonus.

The Universal Pension for Older Adults is 355,326 pesos and 76 cents, plus the same 70,000, for 425,326 pesos and 76 cents.

The non-contributory pension for disability or old age is 310,910 pesos and 91 cents, plus 70,000, for 380,910 pesos and 91 cents.

La Nación says the bonus has not moved since March 2024. It is paid with the benefit, mainly to people on the minimum, on non-contributory pensions, and on the universal pension.

Above the minimum, the bonus shrinks on a sliding scale until the payment hits a cap of 514,158 pesos and 45 cents.

The same update covers contributory benefits Anses pays, pensions for mothers of seven or more children, and other non-contributory and grace pensions.

For a new claim, the paper lists the agency’s steps: Mi Anses, a CUIL and a social-security key, then Work and Consult Labor History.

Missing contributions mean service certificates, pay slips, health-plan proof, or a sworn statement, plus form 6.18, an office appointment, and a national identity document that day.

Colombia’s main cities saw the left’s first marches against De la Espriella. El País says tens of thousands turned out on October 15, without major incidents, despite hail and despite the president’s threat of a hard line.

The account does not describe a new law, a vote, or a change in who holds office.

Jalisco has released more than 30 people police had held. El País says many of the cases against them are still open.

The paper reconstructs detentions after the October 11 march in Guadalajara, a demand for clean water, and says those held were kettled and beaten.

Release ends custody. Open cases leave the legal process running.

Greater São Paulo is the focus of a Thursday operation by the state public prosecutor’s office and the federal police. O Globo says the target is a group suspected of shipping drugs inside appliances bought online.

Investigators identified about 120 suspicious parcels addressed to three groups of people in the metro area.

They are serving 14 temporary arrest warrants and 15 search warrants in the capital, São Caetano do Sul, and Praia Grande, and also in Palhoça in Santa Catarina and in Foz do Iguaçu and Pinhais in Paraná.

Three people were arrested. O Globo calls them suspects, not people already convicted.

The inquiry opened in September 2025 after online orders with drugs hidden in appliances were identified. Since 2024, Greater São Paulo residents had been getting shipments of that kind, posted mainly in Foz do Iguaçu.

Investigators allege the group also moved proceeds through split transfers, cash withdrawals, and payments to third parties and to new companies with no real business.

Financial reports, the paper says, showed movements out of line with declared income. Possible charges are drug trafficking, criminal organization, and money laundering, and seized material is still to be examined as authorities look for others and trace assets.

Buenos Aires has crews inside the Centro Cultural General San Martín after several years of deterioration. La Nación says the city opened a full intervention at Sarmiento 1551 to repair the structure, modernize the systems, and keep the original design.

The complex is by architect Mario Roberto Álvarez. It opened on May 8, 1970, and it covers more than 42,000 square meters: an administrative tower, a main building of theater halls, and six basements with room for 900 people.

Jorge Macri, the city’s head of government, said the administration is recovering a symbol of Buenos Aires, part of the city’s cultural identity, and that the work should put the building back at the level of its history, modern, safe, and open to everyone.

The first stage is installations and structure. Crews are taking down old masonry, partitions, and ceilings and raising new internal structures, while specialists restore the heritage facades of Halls A and B.

Electrical, sanitary, thermomechanical, and fire-protection systems are to be replaced completely. Universal access and updated evacuation rules sit in the same stage, with changes to the elevator passageways.

Churba chairs and the foyer armchairs are to stay. New equipment is planned for workshops and offices. Later stages would add professional lighting and more efficient climate control to cut energy use, remove risks, and run the building on what the plan calls sustainable technology.

The address was the main computing center for the 1983 presidential election and hosted the 76th World Inter-Parliamentary Congress.

In 1990 it held the Latin American Writers’ Meeting, with talks by Mario Benedetti, Mario Vargas Llosa, and José Donoso.

The project material says that when every stage is finished, a building full of those memories is meant to recover its standing, as a renewed base for national and international culture.

Rio’s high-end offices are less empty than at any point since 2014. O Globo cites an unpublished Newmark survey: vacancy in classes AAA, AA, and A fell to 23.5 percent in the third quarter of 2026.

Net absorption was 17,000 square meters, up from 15,000 the quarter before. Average asking rent rose 4.2 percent to 83 reais and 43 cents per square meter a month, and 7.6 percent over 12 months.

The center accounted for 62 percent of absorption accumulated this year. Coren-RJ took about 5,200 square meters in the Bolsa do Rio building, and Cepel moved into AQWA Corporate in Porto Maravilha.

Vacancy is still high in the center, Porto Maravilha, and Cidade Nova: 26.9 percent, 27.4 percent, and 39.1 percent.

Newmark expects the recovery to continue, while warning that new buildings and caution over the macroeconomic and electoral outlook may make firms pickier about the space they take.
