**K-EXPO México 2026 is open NOW — free entry through Sunday, concert Saturday, here's what to know: Latin Times**
South Korean President Lee Jae-myung toured K-EXPO at the World Trade Center in Mexico City on Friday and attended a business forum there, a visit Latin Times says Seoul Economic Daily and Korea JoongAng Daily confirmed. The written results of the three-day state visit are the Korea-Mexico Joint Action Plan 2026-2030 and 17 memorandums of understanding signed Thursday at Palacio Nacional, including a commitment to build a new K-Culture Center in Mexico City. Public halls in Salones Maya 1 and 2, at the CIEC World Trade Center in Colonia Nápoles, Benito Juárez, are open with free entry through Sunday, September 27, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Visitors need a government-issued photo ID, and capacity controls may produce lines. Business sessions between Korean companies and Mexican buyers ran September 24 and 25. Lee is set to leave Mexico City on Saturday. The remaining paid event is the Korea Spotlight concert that same day at the Pepsi Center WTC, with WINNER, Alpha Drive One, Soyou, Dayoung and Touched. Source: [latintimes.com](https://www.latintimes.com/k-expo-mexico-2026-underway-whats-happening-now-what-comes-next-599596)

**O dilema de Fachin nos casos Master e INSS: O Globo**
Supreme Court president Edson Fachin has been receiving requests from defense lawyers for people investigated in the Banco Master cases and the INSS fraud cases after he concentrated those proceedings in his office without assuming the rapporteurships, O Globo reports. He is seeking a destination for the investigations that does not deepen divisions inside the court. The same account says presidential candidates are urging changes at the court, and that a fixed term for ministers is a point of consensus in that debate. It also says Newton Ramos, of the TRF-1, is identified in Daniel Vorcaro's phone as part of a "Turma KN," described as a reference to Justice Kássio Nunes Marques, and that Vorcaro's defense is preparing a new attempt to annul the inquiries. Those named are described as investigated, or as the subjects of defense requests, not as convicted. **La mora lleva a los bancos a restringir los préstamos: La Nación**
Rising delinquency has led banks and fintechs in Argentina to tighten the conditions on loans, La Nación reports. They are concentrating their offers on clients with stronger profiles. Those clients, the paper says, are precisely the ones least willing to take on new debt, so credit narrows for borrowers outside that group. Source: [lanacion.com.ar](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/economia/negocios/la-mora-lleva-a-los-bancos-a-restringir-los-prestamos-nid26092026/)

### Both Sides: How far schools should accelerate students with high abilities
How far public schools should move a student with high abilities is the question Brazil's new rules put in the classroom. Federal Law 15.436 of 2026, in force since June, created a national policy and a national registry and allows specialized support, an individualized plan, enrichment, and partial or full acceleration.

The case for moving faster is that a child who has already mastered the lesson gains nothing from repeating it. Bia Agra, who created the Coleção Horizontes in Guadalupe, built materials that compact what a child already knows so teaching can go deeper, and she says the materials do not replace the school. Priscila Melo says she started mothers' meetings in Maré because other families did not know where to begin; her son Álvaro, now 10, was measured at an IQ of 148.

The case against an IQ cutoff is just as direct. Cristina Delou, a psychologist who works in Méier, says there is no single IQ that defines giftedness, a test is a picture of one moment, and accelerating a child only because the score is above 130 is the biggest pedagogical error one can make. Her criterion is mastery of that stage's content plus a pedagogical assessment, and she says a student can stay in the class and advance in one subject. Bárbara Calmeto, of the Autonomia Instituto in Tijuca, says identification should ask what the child needs, not how intelligent the child is, and that a larger quantity of tasks is not enrichment.

Each side accepts that a good mark alone does not identify high ability, and that the federal law already includes enrichment beside acceleration. Next, Bill 2.799 of 2023, called Lei Alvinho, is scheduled for the Constitution and Justice Committee of Rio's state assembly on Tuesday, September 29.
