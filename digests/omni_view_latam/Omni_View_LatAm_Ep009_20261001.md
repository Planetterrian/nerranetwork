### Lead
**Bolivian attorney general blacklisted by U.S. is arrested over alleged drug cartel ties: CBS News**
A Ministry of Government source, who asked not to be named, told AFP that police arrested Attorney General Roger Mariaca on Wednesday. Santa Cruz prosecutor Alberto Zeballos, also sanctioned by Washington, was arrested as well, local media said. Both were among nearly 20 people from Bolivia, Colombia and Ecuador whom the U.S. State Department accused this week of corruption, links to drug trafficking, or conspiring against their governments. Police raided the prosecutor's offices in Sucre. Communications chief Claudia Pardo said on a live broadcast that the intervention was being carried out illegally, as hooded officers surrounded her and colleagues. A prosecutor's office official reported Mariaca's "abduction" on a live broadcast, Reuters reported, and local media said he was detained at Viru Viru International Airport in Santa Cruz. The State Department said Monday that Mariaca received bribes to facilitate drug trafficking and help violent criminals evade justice. Mariaca denied the accusations that day and called the visa decision interference and political pressure in response to his investigations. The United States revoked the visas of 12 Bolivian officials and made nine other Bolivian nationals ineligible to receive visas. The attorney general's office and the Santa Cruz prosecutor's office did not immediately respond to AFP. Source: [cbsnews.com](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bolivia-attorney-general-roger-mariaca-arrested-drug-cartel-ties/)

**Posso votar no mesmo senador duas vezes nas eleições 2026?: O Globo**
On Sunday, the 4th, Brazilian voters choose six representatives, including two senators, and the two Senate votes must go to different candidates. If the same name is used twice, the second vote is annulled. In 2022 each voter had only one Senate vote. Each state and the Federal District has three senators, on eight-year terms. This year two of the three seats are open, and senators elected in 2022 remain. Marco Antonio Teixeira, a political scientist and professor at FGV-SP, told Jornal Nacional that the point of staggered renewal is continuity of the agenda and of debates already underway. The electronic ballot shows which office is being chosen and how many votes remain. After federal and state deputies, the voter casts the two Senate votes, then votes for governor and president. The Senate reviews nominations for ministers of the Supreme Federal Court and for ambassadors. Teixeira said the eight-year term exists because the questions the Senate takes up shape and transform the Brazilian state. ### Both Sides: Foreign buyers and rural land
Argentina's Chamber of Deputies has been asked to decide whether article 154 of decree 70/23 stays in force. That article repealed the Rural Lands Law and removed the limits the law had set on foreigners acquiring rural land.

Elisa Carrió, founder of the Civic Coalition, asked the Chamber on Wednesday to reject the article. Her reason is the repeal itself: the decree left those statutory limits without effect, and she wants deputies to restore them. She made the request the day after the Supreme Court revoked a ruling that had declared article 154 unconstitutional.

The Court's ruling is the act that keeps the repeal in place. By revoking the ruling of unconstitutionality, the Court restored the article. Decree 70/23 was a measure the executive issued in the first days of President Javier Milei's government. The Senate rejected that mega-decree in March 2024. La Nación reports that the Milei government reacted to the Court's ruling with discomfort and concern, because the ruling removed the limits on sales of land to foreigners.

The request and the ruling share one legal fact: the Court put article 154 back into effect after a ruling had declared it unconstitutional. Carrió has asked the Chamber of Deputies to reject the article.
