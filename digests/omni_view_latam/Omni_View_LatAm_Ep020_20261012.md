### Lead
**Haiti’s elections face another delay, but Christians point to a more lasting hope: Mission Network News**
Haiti’s electoral governing body says it is preparing a revised electoral calendar, which likely means another delay. In July, officials scheduled the country’s first presidential election in ten years for December 13. Security worries tied to gang violence, plus low voter registration, have made that date unlikely. Greg Yoder of Christian World Outreach said security and safety are the biggest issue, especially in Port-au-Prince and the surrounding areas. In an October 2026 report, experts appointed by the United Nations Secretary-General said some gang alliances in Haiti have fractured and that this has exposed civilians to renewed violence. Yoder said any delay dashes hopes among the group’s local staff that things are going to change. Source: [mnnonline.org](https://www.mnnonline.org/news/haitis-elections-face-another-delay-but-christians-point-to-a-more-lasting-hope/)

**Encontraron diez kilos de oro en lingotes dentro del depósito de agua de un viejo inodoro en Santa Cruz: La Nación**
Argentina’s Prefectura Naval carried out two raids in Caleta Olivia, Santa Cruz, in an investigation into a man’s disappearance on December 7, 2025. In one house, officers found four gold bars, each weighing two and a half kilos, hidden in the water tank of an unused toilet. The force valued the seizure at more than 1,200 million pesos and also took a car, a DVR, two mobile phones, and 38 casings. Officers identified three adults at that address. At a second address they identified two adults and two minors and seized two phones. Instruction Court No. 1 in Caleta Olivia, under Judge Marcos Pérez Soruco, ordered the searches; Prefectura has led the inquiry since May 6, 2026. **Mais de 4 mil candidatos 'desperdiçaram' R$ 104 milhões em recursos próprios: O Globo**
Nearly 5,000 candidates in Brazil’s elections have so far given 160 million reais of their own money to their campaigns, O Globo reports. Of them, 4,080 lost in the first round, and 104 million reais went with those defeats. Gelson Merísio of the PSB led those self-donations with 1.15 million reais and finished second in the Santa Catarina governor’s race with 15.6 percent of valid votes. Another 905 candidates, who together put in 55 million reais, were rewarded at the ballot box. Eight of the self-funders reach the second round, with 880,000 reais of their own money in those campaigns. ### Progress Watch
**‘A big deal’: Jamaica Observer**
About 3,000 residents in Trelawny are due to gain from a National Water Commission investment of more than 100 million dollars to rehabilitate the Belfield well and upgrade the Bounty Hall supply, commissioned last Thursday. The work installed a new pump and motor, chlorination equipment, and about 300 metres of 12-inch ductile iron pipe, and it serves Bounty Hall, New Garden, Hampden Land, Cargen, Green Park, and Hermitage. Water Minister Matthew Samuda said households freed from trucking water would have more money for home improvements and children’s education. Trelawny Northern MP Tova Hamilton said the community’s water trouble pre-dated her birth. Source: [jamaicaobserver.com](https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2026/10/12/a-big-deal-20261012-1021-279603/)
