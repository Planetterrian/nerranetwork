> **Nine hundred fired Oi workers lose their severance schedule after Rio's bankruptcy ruling.**

**What You Need to Know:** About nine hundred people dismissed by Oi in August must wait for a new severance deal after the company's bankruptcy changed the rules on an installment agreement already approved in court. In Bahia, a jury cleared the man charged with the 2024 killing of former child actor João Rebello. In Buenos Aires province, former world champion Jorge "Locomotora" Castro died overnight at 59.

### Lead
**Falência da Oi muda acordo de rescisões e trabalhadores terão de negociar novo pagamento: O Globo**
Judicial administrator Bruno Rezende says Oi's bankruptcy decree changed the company's regime, so an installment agreement on severance has to be remade under the new rules. About 900 people dismissed in August, who had expected payment in six to ten installments, now wait for that new deal, and unions say they were not told beforehand. The court approved 98.5 million reais for Rezende while he made that argument. O Globo asked the Rio de Janeiro Court of Justice about breach of the agreement homologated by the 7th Business Court, the same court that authorized that pay, and had no reply by publication. The column also asked Rezende, who sent clarifications. He says the deal was signed in a judicial-recovery setting, and that after the bankruptcy decree payments must follow that regime's own rules, including how creditors are treated and how much cash the bankrupt estate has. Federations Livre, Fenattel and FITRATELP say the argument does not hold. They say the dismissals happened before the bankruptcy, so the earlier installment schedule should be kept. For the unions, a new agreement cannot simply repeat the old installments and will have to pay the amounts owed in full. At a meeting last Wednesday with representatives of those three federations, Judge Simone Gastesi Chevrand said the 18 August days worked that sat outside the agreement would be paid this week, between Wednesday and Thursday. Union representatives call that a partial victory only if the money actually arrives. The main demand remains a decision on the severance sums themselves. Workers describe financial strain and collections among colleagues to cover basic costs, including rent and food, for former employees in vulnerable situations. Another point put on the table is subsidiary Serede: the federations say those employees have gone about ten months without amounts tied to severance. The next stage is a meeting between the entities and Rezende to discuss how payment can proceed. Even after the judge pressed him to set that meeting, the administrator had not done so. ### Across the Region
**Acusado de matar ex-ator mirim por engano em Trancoso é absolvido, e irmã cobra respostas por 'atrocidade': O Globo**
On Tuesday a sentencing council in Bahia decided by majority that there was not proof against Felipe Souza Bruno, known as Zingue, on the charge of killing João Rebello. Prosecutor Igor Saulo Ferreira Rocha Assunção cited the lack of confirmation of authorship. The judge who presided over the session ordered a release warrant. Rebello, a former child actor and a DJ, was 45 when he was shot dead in October 2024 in Trancoso, a tourist district of Porto Seguro on the southern coast of Bahia. Days after the crime, Bahia's Civil Police focused on three suspects, including Felipe Souza Bruno. State police said at the time that evidence collected so far supported the institution's line of inquiry, which ruled out any involvement of the victim in criminal activity, and that operations were under way to serve the related warrants. His sister, Maria Carol Rebello, confirmed to followers that Zingue had been acquitted by the jury of the homicide charge. She wrote that there were indeed no concrete proofs and that she accepted that justice had decided that way. She also wrote that what the family always wanted was to understand what happened and what led to what she called a horrific mistake, that her brother's character was never in question, and that it had been clear from the start he was killed by mistake. She asked what happened, who ordered it, and who carried out what she called an atrocity. Witnesses told police he was alone in his own car in central Trancoso when two men on a motorcycle approached and fired at close range. He was a nephew of director Jorge Fernando, who died in 2010, lived there with his wife Karine, and worked as a DJ under the artistic name Vunje. Between the 1980s and 1990s he appeared in five TV Globo novelas: Cambalacho in 1986, Bebê a Bordo in 1989, Vamp in 1991, Deus nos Acuda in 1992, and Zazá in 1997. He also directed music videos and worked as a producer. **Murió el excampeón mundial de boxeo Jorge “Locomotora” Castro a los 59 años: La Nación**
Former world boxing champion Jorge "Locomotora" Castro died in the early hours of Wednesday at 59 after he collapsed at the Bingo de Morón, Security Ministry sources told La Nación. He was also known as "El Roña." After a 911 call, a Morón patrol command unit went to the venue on San Martín street. The alert was for a man in distress inside, in the machines area. The venue manager, identified by police sources as Matías Harfiel, said the former boxer was inside. Those sources said he apparently had diabetes and that investigators will try to determine whether the death was linked to that illness. When medical help arrived, they found he was already dead. The inquiry into the cause of death is with the UFI number 8 prosecutors' unit in Morón, those sources told the paper. Born in Caleta Olivia, Santa Cruz, in 1967, he was a middleweight world champion. In 1989 he won the Argentine middleweight title and the following year the South American title. His international title came on August 12, 1994, when he beat American Reggie Johnson in San Miguel de Tucumán and took the World Boxing Association middleweight belt. On December 10, 1994, in Monterrey, Mexico, he was behind on the cards against American John David Jackson, then landed a left hook that dropped his opponent and turned the fight. In later years he appeared on television, including Gran Hermano and MasterChef Celebrity. For years he kept soup kitchens and snack kitchens in the Buenos Aires suburbs. He told La Nación the food work began in the Covid-19 pandemic, after he bought a gym in Temperley in March and could not open it for a year and a half, then started a popular pot meant for 30 people that drew more than 60. A year ago, after leaving the Telefe cooking show, he said that on top of four kitchens he already had in Lomas de Zamora and ten others he supplied with groceries, he would add one in Morón. In mid-May 2021 he said on television that he wanted to run for national deputy. His partner, Noelia Vigliarolo, posted that they had planned to get engaged this weekend on a trip to the coast for Mother's Day, and wrote that she would love him all her life. ### The Closer Look
**Ministério do Esporte pode voltar a ser Secretaria? Com receio, COB procura candidaturas para defender a área: O Globo**
The Brazilian Olympic Committee will deliver a suggested sports-government plan this week to the campaign committees of both candidates in the presidential runoff. Marco La Porta, the committee's president, told O Globo the aim is to give the sports portfolio real weight, because sport is not a priority area in Brazil and keeping a ministry without protagonism, while constantly dealing with held-back funds, does not change that. He said the most important point is to value the Ministry of Sport and give it real relevance inside government, whether the next government is new or the current one. The fear he described is that the ministry returns to a special secretariat, the form it took under Jair Bolsonaro, father of Liberal Party candidate Flávio Bolsonaro, and that successful programs and public policies are discontinued. La Porta spoke to O Globo before the first-round result. Flávio Bolsonaro took 47.03 percent and Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva of the Workers' Party 45.16 percent. The two will face voters on October 25. Asked about the Olympic community's fear that a Flávio Bolsonaro win would turn the ministry back into a secretariat, La Porta said the fear is always present, because the tendency is to repeat what was done in the previous government, his father's. He noted that Flávio Bolsonaro had said, before the first round, that he intends to cut the number of ministries and commissioned posts to reduce public spending if elected, without naming which ones. In 2019 Jair Bolsonaro folded the Ministry of Sport into the Ministry of Citizenship. La Porta said that if Bolsonaro wins, a change of government still brings a ministerial reform, and the sector does not know what can happen. His message, he said, is clear: they do not want the end of the Ministry of Sport. He had already raised the point in his opening speech at COB Expo, the committee's sports fair, at the start of this month. That stance led allies of Flávio Bolsonaro, including Marcelo Reis Magalhães, special sports secretary at the Ministry of Citizenship under Jair Bolsonaro, to take the question to the candidate. On a live broadcast the same day, Flávio Bolsonaro said there is no such plan and that the Ministry of Sport will be one of the main ministries of his government, and that people can stay calm. On that same live, Magalhães said restoring the ministry had been Jair Bolsonaro's dream and that the secretariat had deserved to be a ministry. The campaign press office answered O Globo in a note that incentive to sport and a reduction in the number of ministries are both commitments in Flávio Bolsonaro's government plan, framed as the need to advance administrative reform against what the note called the disorder of spending under Lula's government. The note says, without naming the Ministry of Sport, that no essential ministry will be extinguished, and that the whole government structure will be analyzed during the transition and through the government, once technical teams are structured.

The paper itself, La Porta said, was written by entities, committees and other players in sport. It says Brazil still faces significant challenges in widening access to sport and physical activity. It uses Vigitel 2024, which found that only 42.3 percent of adults in Brazil's state capitals reach recommended levels of leisure-time physical activity. It suggests keeping the Ministry of Sport, implementing the National Sport Plan, strengthening the Bolsa Atleta program, and improving tax incentives for sports development, among other themes. La Porta said sport is not cited in the electoral campaigns, so the sector thought of presenting proposals to the candidates, because afterward it cannot complain that nothing was done. He spoke of turning Brazil into a sporting nation, and said that the day the Ministry of Sport is relevant — because it never has been — that nation can start to be built, and that without support it will not happen. The ministry is, in his word, new. It was created officially in 1995, under Fernando Henrique Cardoso, with Pelé as the first minister. It has passed through mergers, extinctions and recreations. In 1998 it merged with tourism as the Ministry of Sport and Tourism. In Lula's first government, in 2003, sport was separated from tourism and the Ministry of Sport was born again. The current government once more separated sport from citizenship, the configuration held from 2019 to 2022, and recreated the portfolio. La Porta said there should be a state plan, not a government plan, so that sport is included as a factor in health, education and security, and that this does not happen: with each government the ideas change. He added that a budget is not enough if projects are not put into practice, and that relevance, money, planning, and ministers who do not rotate constantly are required. Many resources are held back and never arrive, he said, which hits confederation projects that depend on them. He cited Revelar Talentos, launched in April 2024 to identify, select and support young athletes across Brazil: a project he called wonderful, covering five confederations, none of which has yet received this year's 2026 amount. Projects over time rather than one-off efforts, he said, lose their whole sequence. Among actions of the current government, the article notes approval of the New Sports Incentive Law and the General Sports Law, recent sanction of the Women's World Cup Law, which sets rules on commerce and security and also protects players and the development of the sport, and a rise in the value of Bolsa Atleta grants. The portfolio's budget for 2026 is 2.5 billion reais. The suggested plan is due with both campaign committees this week.
