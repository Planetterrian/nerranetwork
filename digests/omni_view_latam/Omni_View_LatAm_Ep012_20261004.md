**Tras la distención del clima en París, llegó el momento de transformar los gestos de los gobernadores en votos: La Nación**
After Argentina Week in Paris, Cabinet chief Diego Santilli is opening a new round of one-on-one contacts for agreements on decree 70, the 2027 budget, and a political reform that includes eliminating the PASO primaries, La Nación reports. President Javier Milei greeted each of the 13 governors who were there, thanked them for joining meetings with investors, and told them that appearing together was a good signal, in conversations of about 15 minutes, before they joined a photograph with Emmanuel Macron. Most of those 13 have decided to hold their provincial vote apart from the national election. What they are waiting to hear is how La Libertad Avanza will compete in their districts, with provincial elections in 2027 in view. **Elecciones Brasil 2026, en vivo: la población acude a las urnas para votar entre dos modelos antagónicos de país: Infobae**
Brazilians are at the polls today in the presidential first round. The Defense Ministry has mobilized 26,500 service members from the Navy, Army, and Air Force for security and logistics in 482 municipalities across 12 states, covering 8,827 polling places, in an operation requested by the electoral courts and authorized by the presidency, Infobae reports. Voting is compulsory for citizens aged 18 to 70 and optional at 16 and 17. More than 158 million people are eligible; El País puts the figure at almost 159 million. Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, 80, of the Workers' Party, is seeking a fourth term and has said it would be his last. Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, 45, of the Liberal Party, is the main rival. Infobae counts Lula, Flávio Bolsonaro, and 11 other candidates; France 24 reports 12 names on the presidential ballot. Datafolha's latest survey, cited by WWBL, puts Lula at 42 percent and Bolsonaro at 38 percent, with a two-point margin of error. El País headlined a last poll at 45 percent to 42 percent. Infobae says the surveys do not project either man at the 50 percent required to win today; a runoff, if needed, is set for October 25. On Friday the United States closed its embassy in Brasília and its consulates after an alleged bomb threat; the Federal Police investigated and found nothing that confirmed the risk, La Nación reports. Lula's campaign asked the electoral courts to examine the episode as possible external interference and attributed the order to Secretary of State Marco Rubio. Donald Trump called the election "very important" for his country on Thursday and said on Friday he would follow it closely. El País reports that Jair Bolsonaro was convicted of leading a coup attempt; Infobae reports a 27-year sentence over the attempt that followed his 2022 defeat. ### Both Sides: Whether governors should vote the 2027 budget and the political reform now
What is being decided is whether the governors who traveled to Paris should now supply votes for the 2027 budget, for decree 70, and for a political reform that includes eliminating the PASO primaries. Cabinet chief Diego Santilli is the official opening those talks.

The case for moving, as President Javier Milei put it to the governors before the meeting with Emmanuel Macron, is that they need to work together to avoid the return of kirchnerism, and that being seen together is a good signal after the investor meetings. Economy Minister Luis Caputo, at a shared dinner, described the provinces in the room as the ones the government wanted to reach agreements with because they had supported Milei's administration. According to La Nación, some sources said Karina Milei suggested that in those provinces the libertarians would not field heavyweight candidates — a remark the governors heard as a possible definition of how the national party would compete at home.

The case for waiting comes from the governors. Several told La Nación they have already given enough proofs of support, that the moment for agreements has arrived, and that there should be no more promises. Their calendar is the provincial elections of 2027, and what they say they still need is a clear account of how La Libertad Avanza will play in their districts. Most of the 13 have already decided to split that provincial vote from the national one.
