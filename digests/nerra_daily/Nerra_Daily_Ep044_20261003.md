## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Vienna and partners have shown that Asgard archaea, the closest microbial relatives to the cells that make up complex life, crawl across surfaces by extending and retracting long protrusions. The behavior, detailed in a new Nature paper, had been considered a eukaryotic trait. The team captured the motion in live imaging and confirmed it through genetic and structural analysis. Next they plan to test whether the same machinery appears in other archaeal groups.
