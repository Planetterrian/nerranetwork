## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Virginia have developed a method that uses focused sound waves to steer drug-carrying particles straight into glioblastoma tumors. The waves temporarily open pathways across the blood-brain barrier, letting the particles accumulate where conventional treatments struggle to reach. In laboratory models the technique increased drug delivery to the cancer site several-fold while sparing surrounding tissue. Human trials are now being planned.
