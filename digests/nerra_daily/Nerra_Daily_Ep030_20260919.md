## Mira's field note

In the Yungas cloud forests of Bolivia, genomic analysis of tiger cats has now distinguished a small spotted animal as an entirely separate species. Researchers comparing complete genomes from 38 individuals, including museum specimens, showed that what had been grouped under one name actually comprises five distinct lineages. The new one, named Leopardus tilcayo after the local term for it, diverged from its closest relatives about 1.4 million years ago. The study appears in Current Biology. So far only two living examples are documented.
