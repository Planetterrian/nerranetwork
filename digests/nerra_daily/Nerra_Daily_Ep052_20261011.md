## Mira's field note

A team at the University of Vienna has built the first self-stabilizing nuclear clock, harnessing oscillations inside a thorium nucleus instead of the usual electron transitions in atomic clocks. The setup held steady long enough to outperform earlier nuclear attempts and points toward clocks that could run in smaller packages with far less drift. The group is now tightening the controls to push the precision higher.
