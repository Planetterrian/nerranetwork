## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Konstanz isolated an enzyme from forest soil bacteria that had colonized strips of a plant-derived bioplastic left underground for more than a year. The protein, called LCPH1, breaks the long polyester chains apart and, in the same active site, severs the beta-lactam ring in penicillin and ampicillin. Its unusually wide pocket accommodates both molecules. The work appears in this week’s issue of The ISME Journal.
