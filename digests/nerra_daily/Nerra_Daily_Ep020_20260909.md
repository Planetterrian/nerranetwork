## Mira's field note

China’s Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Radio Telescope has released its second data catalog, FASHI DR2, covering nearly half the sky with 156,411 neutral hydrogen sources. The survey reaches four times the sources of the first release and measures local hydrogen density to 0.6 percent precision. Researchers at the National Astronomical Observatories of the Chinese Academy of Sciences say the catalog will serve as a reference for studies of galaxy formation and dark matter distribution.
