## Mira's field note

The Karolinska Institutet announced today that Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel share the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for their work on optogenetics. The method uses light-sensitive proteins to turn specific neurons on or off in living tissue, giving researchers a direct way to test which cells drive particular behaviors or disease processes. Labs have already applied it to map circuits involved in movement, reward and memory in mice and other animals.
