## Mira's field note

Paleontologists in North Dakota’s Hell Creek Formation have uncovered the first confirmed trackway left by an adult Tyrannosaurus rex. Four large three-toed prints, each from a full-grown animal, run roughly seven meters along 66.5-million-year-old rock. The find was reported September 9 by Sci.News. Researchers can now compare stride length and foot placement directly against skeletal estimates of the same species.
