## Mira's field note

Researchers at Harvard’s Wyss Institute have built a screening platform of DNA nanoswitches that hunts through enormous libraries for molecules able to change how two proteins interact with each other. Conventional tools usually look only for simple binding; this one finds modulators of the interactions themselves. In early runs the method already surfaced small molecules and nanobodies that promote interactions relevant to several disorders, including one affecting the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein. The work appears in Science.
