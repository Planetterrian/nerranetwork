## Mira's field note

NASA’s Perseverance rover has now driven farther across another world than any vehicle before it. On October 11 it logged 45.22 kilometers on the Martian surface, slipping past the 45.16 kilometers recorded by Opportunity during its long mission. The new mark came after five and a half years of steady travel from the landing site toward the ancient river delta in Jezero crater. Engineers are already planning the next traverses that will add still more distance while the rover continues sampling rock and soil for return to Earth.
