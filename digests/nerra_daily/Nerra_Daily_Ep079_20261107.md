## Mira's field note

UCLA neuroscientists have developed a way to watch astrocytes, the star-shaped support cells in the brain, make physical contact with synapses in living adult mice. The method relies on fluorescence resonance energy transfer microscopy, which resolves distances small enough to track those interactions as they form and shift over time. The approach works in intact tissue where earlier techniques could not, opening a window on how astrocytes shape nerve-cell communication. Researchers plan to apply it next to models of Alzheimer’s and Huntington’s disease.
