## Mira's field note

At the Paul Scherrer Institute and ETH Zurich, physicists have measured the optical Magnus effect for the first time by directing a tightly focused laser at a single trapped calcium ion. The point of strongest interaction sits a few hundred nanometers off the beam’s apparent center, a sideways shift that depends only on wavelength. The result appears in Physical Review Letters. Future runs will check how the displacement changes when the same beams address qubits.
