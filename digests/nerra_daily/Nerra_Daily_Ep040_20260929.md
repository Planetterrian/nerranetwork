## Mira's field note

Researchers working from an eighty-dollar motel room in Florida have described two new species of Paulinella, a rare single-celled organism that acquired its photosynthetic ability through an independent endosymbiotic event. The find, reported in the Journal of Phycology, raises the number of known photosynthetic Paulinella species to six. Only one other lineage in Earth’s history is known to have made that same leap to complex photosynthesis. The researchers collected the samples during a routine survey and are now sequencing their genomes to compare the two events.
