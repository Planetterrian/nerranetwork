## Mira's field note

At Northwestern University, physicists have trapped a single positron, the antimatter counterpart of an electron, and cooled it to temperatures near absolute zero inside a wall-free trap. The work, published today in Physical Review Letters, marks the first time researchers have exercised this level of quantum control over one particle of antimatter. The team is already planning the next measurements that could test how such particles behave in isolation.
