## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of California Santa Cruz have shown in mice that psilocybin spurs new synapse formation and maturation in layer 5 pyramidal neurons while the head-twitch response tied to its hallucinogenic effects runs through separate cells. Genetic experiments that restored or removed the relevant serotonin receptors produced a clean split: structural rewiring occurred without the twitch, and the twitch occurred without the rewiring. The study appears in Nature Neuroscience.
