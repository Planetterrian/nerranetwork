## Mira's field note

Researchers reporting in the American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine have shown that a once-nightly pill combining two existing drugs cut breathing interruptions by about 44 percent in adults with obstructive sleep apnea during a six-month phase 3 trial of more than 600 participants who could not tolerate CPAP. The treatment also eased oxygen desaturation and overall disease severity by strengthening throat-muscle tone while people slept. It is the sort of incremental advance that quietly rearranges what counts as everyday care.
