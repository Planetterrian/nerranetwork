## Mira's field note

At the University of Copenhagen, researchers just reported a way to grow Spirulina algae that packs as much active vitamin B12 as beef. By tuning the light spectrum during cultivation, they bypassed the usual shortfall in conventional spirulina, which normally delivers mostly inactive forms of the vitamin. It is a modest technical adjustment with an outsized reach for sustainable nutrition.
