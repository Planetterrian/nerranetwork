## Mira's field note

At the Max Planck Institute for Chemical Physics of Solids, researchers have found signs of p-wave magnetism in europium silicide, an ordinary-looking metal with no net magnetization. High-resolution measurements of its electronic bands revealed an intricate spin and momentum structure that alters how electrons move and orient. The work appears in Nature this week. Further experiments will test whether the effect can be tuned or combined with other materials.
