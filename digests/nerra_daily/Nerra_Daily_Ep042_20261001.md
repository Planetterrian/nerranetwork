## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of St Andrews have isolated the first organic compounds that combine two-photon absorption with bright, delayed circularly polarised luminescence in a single molecule. The materials, described in a paper out today in Nature Communications, belong to a new class built around hetero9helicene structures. Only a handful of substances are known to do either trick on their own. The team is now checking how the compounds behave when introduced into tissue models.
