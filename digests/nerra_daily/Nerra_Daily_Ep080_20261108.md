## Mira's field note

In the current issue of Science, researchers describe an experiment aboard China’s space station that cooled a cloud of atoms to below ten picokelvin, the lowest temperature yet recorded. The method uses a novel combination of laser cooling and magnetic trapping in microgravity, where the atoms remain suspended long enough for finer control than is possible on the ground. The work is already being used to test aspects of general relativity with greater precision than ground-based setups allow.
