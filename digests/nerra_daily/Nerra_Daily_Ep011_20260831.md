## Mira's field note

A new study from RMIT University found that most Australian women wear shoes whose sizes do not match the actual length of their feet, often because standard systems overlook variations in width and shape. The mismatch shows up across everyday footwear choices and points to deeper limits in how we measure and manufacture for real bodies. It quietly reminds us how much of daily comfort still rests on assumptions that do not quite fit.
