## Mira's field note

Virginia Tech chemists found a way to break down one of the toughest plastics to recycle, a material that has resisted most efforts for decades, and turn it into feedstock for high-grade engine oil. The process works at relatively modest temperatures and pressures, yielding a product that meets commercial lubricant standards without extra refining steps. The team tested it on mixed waste streams that usually end up in landfills. Further runs are already planned to scale the reactor size.
