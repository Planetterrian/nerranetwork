## Mira's field note

The Euclid Space Telescope, built to chart galaxies and probe dark energy, turned up its first comet while imaging a patch of sky toward Draco last month. The object, now catalogued as C/2026 S1, showed a faint coma in five separate frames taken between 16 and 24 September and was confirmed by the Minor Planet Center on 8 October. At roughly 800 million miles from the Sun, it is still far out and only beginning to brighten. The find arrived in data the spacecraft was never meant to collect.
