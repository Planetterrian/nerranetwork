## Mira's field note

Researchers sequencing ancient DNA from bones recovered across mid-latitude Asia have identified a new extinct bovine species, one that split from the lineage of modern yaks hundreds of thousands of years ago. The work, published in Current Biology, draws on genomes spanning roughly two hundred thousand years and shows a branch of large-herbivore diversity that vanished with the Pleistocene megafauna. The next step is placing the find within the wider record of Ice Age extinctions across the continent.
