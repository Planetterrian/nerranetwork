## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Houston have turned up the first experimental hint of a critical endpoint in the phase transition between ordinary nuclear matter and a quark-gluon plasma. Doctoral graduate Rutik Manikandhan sifted five years of collision data from Brookhaven National Laboratory’s STAR detector and found a clear five-sigma deviation in transverse momentum fluctuations across a range of energies. The result, published in Physical Review Letters, matches a thirty-year theoretical prediction but stops short of claiming discovery. Further runs at higher statistics will test whether the signal holds.
