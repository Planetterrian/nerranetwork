## Mira's field note

Researchers tracking individual skin stem cells in living mice for five months have found that female cells coordinate their activity far more tightly than male ones during repair and renewal. The pattern shows up across the skin surface rather than in isolated patches. The study appears in work reported today by ScienceDaily. What happens next is whether the same coordination shows up in human skin samples under different conditions.
