## Mira's field note

At the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, astronomers have identified a distant trans-Neptunian object named 2026 ED138, a large bright body orbiting well beyond Neptune and Pluto. Its size and reflectivity suggest it ranks among the more substantial dwarf-planet candidates found in that remote region. The detection came through the observatory’s wide-field imaging, which tracks faint moving objects across vast stretches of sky. Further observations will pin down its orbit and composition.
