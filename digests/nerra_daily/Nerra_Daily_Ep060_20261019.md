## Mira's field note

Researchers at the Pennington Biomedical Research Center have traced elevated levels of a protein called Miro1 in the skeletal muscle of people with type 2 diabetes. In lab models, clearing the protein restored normal insulin response, and the same protein dropped after exercise in patients. The findings appear in a study released today. The next measurements will check whether blocking Miro1 in larger trials improves blood-sugar control without added drugs.
