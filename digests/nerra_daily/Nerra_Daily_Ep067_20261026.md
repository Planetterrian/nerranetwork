## Mira's field note

Climate researchers comparing ocean sediments from six thousand years ago with modern climate models report that Arctic winter sea ice could vanish far sooner than earlier projections indicated. The records point to much thinner coverage across wide stretches of the Arctic within decades rather than over longer stretches of the century. Satellite passes over the coming winters will show whether the pace already matches those revised timelines.
