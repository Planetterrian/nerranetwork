## Mira's field note

Researchers at Kansai Medical University have turned Raman spectroscopy on the RNA strands of twenty different SARS-CoV-2 variants and subvariants, from the original Japanese isolate through a dozen Omicron lineages. Each produces a distinct vibrational fingerprint that can be encoded as a unique barcode, revealing differences in molecular architecture that sequencing alone does not capture. The approach distinguished every strain in the set. The group is now exploring whether the same barcodes can flag newly emerging variants in a single measurement.
