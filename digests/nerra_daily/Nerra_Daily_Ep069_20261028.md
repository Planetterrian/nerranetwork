## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Cambridge modeled how comets would have struck the young Earth and found that their speed and steep entry angles would almost certainly vaporize fragile organic molecules like hydrogen cyanide before they reached the surface. The simulations point to at most one viable delivery event across more than a billion years, and even then the molecules would dilute too quickly for sustained prebiotic chemistry. Mars fares only slightly better in the models.
