## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Göttingen pulled together measurements from thirteen forest sites in southwestern Germany, some running for twenty-four years. The soils there have been pulling methane from the air at a steadily higher rate, rising about three percent each year. The team traced the increase to warmer temperatures and lower rainfall, which let more of the gas reach the microbes that consume it. Their record is the longest and most detailed assembled for the region so far.
