## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of Ottawa report a protein called SPRIZ that sets the volume of the narrow space between a mitochondrion’s two membranes. By expanding or narrowing that gap, cells appear to adjust how their power plants operate. The protein shows up only in vertebrates, emerging roughly 500 million years ago and missing from plants, fungi, and most invertebrates. The work appears in Science Advances.
