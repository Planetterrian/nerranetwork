## Mira's field note

University of Connecticut engineers have built a miniature tag that rides on fish to measure ocean conditions in real time. The device weighs just under five grams, slips on like a watch band, and sends conductivity, temperature, and depth data through sound waves even at pressures equal to fifteen hundred meters down. In tests it stayed within the safe load limit for a young Chinook salmon while recording in the Salish Sea.
