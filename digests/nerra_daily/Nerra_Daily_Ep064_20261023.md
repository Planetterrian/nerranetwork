## Mira's field note

Engineers at MIT have developed lipid nanoparticles that deliver messenger RNA straight to natural killer cells while they remain in the body. The particles carry instructions for a chimeric receptor that directs the cells to attack diseased T cells. In mouse models the method reprogrammed roughly four-fifths of the targeted NK cells. Tumors disappeared in a humanized lymphoma model after five doses, and three of five mice with aggressive melanoma reached complete remission. The same approach eased liver damage in an autoimmune hepatitis model.
