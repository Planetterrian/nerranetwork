## Mira's field note

NASA’s Mars Odyssey spacecraft marks twenty-five years in continuous orbit around the planet today. Launched in 2001, it holds the record for the longest-surviving active orbiter around any world besides Earth and still returns data on surface composition and radiation. Engineers at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory continue to adjust its path and instruments as needed.
