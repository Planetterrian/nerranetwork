## Mira's field note

Researchers at the German Primate Center grew marmoset brain organoids alongside human ones and checked the results against fetal tissue samples. They found that certain neural progenitor cells divide more slowly, develop simpler shapes with fewer branches, and switch programs earlier than their counterparts in species with folded brains, which sharply limits neuron output and keeps the cortex smooth and compact. The study appears in Science Advances.
