## Mira's field note

At the ATLAS experiment at CERN, physicists have now recorded evidence for one of the Higgs boson’s rarest predicted decays, into a virtual photon paired with a Z boson. The signal emerged from the full Run 2 dataset after years of sifting through collision events at the Large Hadron Collider. The measurement aligns with Standard Model expectations yet sits at the edge of current sensitivity. Next runs will test whether the rate holds or begins to hint at physics beyond the model.
