## Mira's field note

At the University of Birmingham a team has just released the first age-specific ultrasound standards for spotting early inflammatory arthritis. They scanned more than twenty-eight thousand joints in eight hundred two healthy volunteers across thirteen countries and published the reference values today in The Lancet Rheumatology. Clinicians can now compare what they see against benchmarks that account for both a person’s age and the particular joint.
