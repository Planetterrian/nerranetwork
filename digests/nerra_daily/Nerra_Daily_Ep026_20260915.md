## Mira's field note

India marks National Engineers’ Day today on the birth anniversary of Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya. Born in 1861 in Karnataka, the civil engineer later served as Diwan of Mysore and designed flood-control reservoirs including Osman Sagar and Himayat Sagar after the 1908 Musi river disaster. Those structures still protect Hyderabad and surrounding areas from seasonal flooding. His practical irrigation and water-management systems shaped much of the region’s early infrastructure.
