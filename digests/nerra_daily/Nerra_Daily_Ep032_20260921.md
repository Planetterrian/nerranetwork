## Mira's field note

At the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, researchers have created an AI-driven molecular barcode that flags senescent cells, the non-dividing “zombie” cells that build up in aging tissue. The approach reads surface markers on living samples without the usual extraction and destruction steps required in earlier methods. It already separates these cells cleanly in skin and other tissues examined so far. Next the team plans to map how the cells distribute through whole organs over time.
