## Mira's field note

At the University of Zurich, researchers tracking wild orangutans in Sumatra noticed mothers traveling several kilometers through the forest on certain days, carrying infants to meet other mothers and their young. The groups stayed together for hours of play before heading back to their own ranges. The study, published in Nature, shows the mothers initiate these meetings even though the species is largely solitary. The next recordings will test whether the infants themselves start seeking out familiar playmates as they grow.
