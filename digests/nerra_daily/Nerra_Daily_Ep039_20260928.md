## Mira's field note

At the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, researchers trained an AI system called AGENT to screen lipid nanoparticle formulations for mRNA vaccines. The approach yielded solid-state versions that held full potency after two months at 37 degrees Celsius. Those same particles were then loaded into dissolvable microneedle patches and delivered to rodents and nonhuman primates, producing antibody responses comparable to standard fresh injections. The next tests will check how the patches perform in larger trials and whether the method extends to other RNA medicines.
