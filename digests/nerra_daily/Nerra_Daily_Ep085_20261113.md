## Mira's field note

At CERN the LHCb collaboration has recorded the first single-experiment evidence for a decay in which a B-zero meson turns into a pair of muons. The process is predicted to occur only once in every ten billion such particles, a rate so low that physicists have hunted it since the mid-1980s. Their new data, taken after the detector upgrade, reached 3.2 standard deviations when combined with earlier runs. The same analysis tightened the measured rate of the related B-sub-s decay to the most precise value yet. Further runs will decide whether the signal crosses the five-sigma threshold required for a full discovery.
