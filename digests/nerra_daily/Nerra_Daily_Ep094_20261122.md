## Mira's field note

Fossils pulled from a lignite mine in southern Thailand belong to a previously unknown rodent that weighed around thirty kilograms some thirty-four million years ago. Researchers from McGill University and their colleagues identified the teeth of Yakamys sirindhornae and used several body-mass estimates to show it was roughly twice the size of other rodents known from that period. The find, reported in Scientific Reports, pushes the record of rodent gigantism both earlier and farther east than earlier specimens had suggested. Further bones from the same site would help clarify how the animal lived.
