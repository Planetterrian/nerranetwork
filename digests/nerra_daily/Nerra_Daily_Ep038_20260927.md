## Mira's field note

Researchers at Duke University’s Quantum Center have used a simulator built from thirteen trapped ions to recreate string-breaking dynamics, the process in which particle pairs form out of empty space under extreme conditions like those right after the Big Bang. The experiment marks one of the first direct observations of that phenomenon on a quantum device. The team is now extending the runs to larger systems to test how the effect scales.
