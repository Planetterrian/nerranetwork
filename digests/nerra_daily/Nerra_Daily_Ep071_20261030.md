## Mira's field note

At SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, researchers have delivered the first direct experimental proof of altermagnetism, a third magnetic phase long theorized but never confirmed. Working with manganese telluride, the team observed spin patterns and electronic behavior that fit neither ferromagnetism nor antiferromagnetism, exactly as the 2022 models predicted. The results appear in Science this week. Further measurements on related compounds are already under way to map how the phase behaves under different conditions.
