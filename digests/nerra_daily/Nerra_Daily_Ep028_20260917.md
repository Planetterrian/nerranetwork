## Mira's field note

Astronomers poring over archived observations from the W. M. Keck Observatory have identified the youngest exoplanet confirmed so far. Elias 2-24 b is still drawing in gas and dust from the disk around its host star, a process captured in combined data from Keck, ALMA, and the Very Large Telescope. The find, detailed in The Astrophysical Journal Letters, places the planet squarely inside one of the disk’s gaps. Researchers can now tie such gaps more directly to active formation rather than other processes.
