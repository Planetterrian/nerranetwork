## Mira's field note

Scientists at UCLA and UC San Francisco have published the first detailed map of how DNA modifications shape the developing human brain. They examined more than 53,000 cells across the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex from mid-gestation through adulthood, tracking how chromatin structure rewires gene activity during key windows. The work, appearing today in Nature, highlights periods when these regions become especially sensitive, offering a clearer window into why certain neurodevelopmental conditions take root so early. It is a quiet reminder of how much the earliest chapters still determine the rest of the story.
