## Mira's field note

At Adelaide University, engineers have reworked the design of superconducting quantum interference devices. Instead of arrays with hundreds of differently sized loops to pinpoint absolute zero in a magnetic field, they kept the loops uniform and added a few bare ones without the usual junctions. The result creates a synthetic spread in effective area while keeping peak sensitivity intact. The work appears in Physical Review Applied today. Further tests are already planned to push the sensors closer to their quantum limits.
