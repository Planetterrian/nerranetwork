## Mira's field note

Physicists at the University of Innsbruck cooled cesium atoms to a few billionths of a degree above absolute zero and confined them in thousands of narrow one-dimensional tubes. There they created and directly observed Bethe strings, groups of atoms bound together purely by quantum interactions rather than chemical bonds, a state first predicted by Hans Bethe in 1931. The strings held together through collisions inside the tubes yet came apart the moment the one-dimensional confinement was lifted. The work appears in Nature Communications.
