## Mira's field note

Researchers at the University of California San Diego have shown that a key cellular enzyme can copy DNA built from an eight-letter alphabet rather than the four letters used by all known life. In lab tests the enzyme read and replicated the expanded code without errors. The result gives synthetic biologists a working tool for creating organisms whose genetic systems store and transmit information beyond anything found in nature.
