## Mira's field note

Chemists at the University of Würzburg have isolated three stable oxoboranes, molecules long thought impossible to keep intact because of their boron-oxygen triple bond. After more than sixty years of unsuccessful attempts, the team prepared the compounds through a new synthetic route that also drew on computational work from Tezpur University. The results appeared in Nature Chemistry last month. The structures now sit on the bench for further study.
