## Mira's field note

University of Liverpool researchers have traced how a protein linked to a severe form of childhood epilepsy anchors itself at the synapses of nerve cells. Their work identifies a previously unseen molecular docking site that tethers the CDKL5 protein to the structural framework inside those cells. Changing just one amino acid at the protein’s end was enough to shift its location and disrupt the organisation of the nerve cells. The findings appear in Science Advances. Further experiments will test how mutations alter that positioning in living tissue.
