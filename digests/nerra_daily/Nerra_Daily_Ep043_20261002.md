## Mira's field note

A fossil unearthed in China and described today in Nature belongs to a previously unknown dinosaur species whose limbs carried long, feathery structures arranged in ways not seen before. The specimen adds concrete detail to the picture of how flight-related traits appeared more than once among dinosaurs and their close relatives. The bones preserve enough of the shoulder and wing region that researchers can now compare the arrangement directly with earlier feathered forms from the same region.
