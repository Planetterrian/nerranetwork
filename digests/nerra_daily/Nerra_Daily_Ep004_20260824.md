## Mira's field note

A researcher at Monash University has finished the first complete digital map of every wind-blown sand dune on Earth. Dr. Andrew Gunn pulled together satellite imagery and field data to chart their distribution, shapes, and movement across deserts and coasts worldwide. The work reveals how these shifting landscapes quietly shape regional climates and sediment cycles in ways earlier partial surveys could only suggest. It is the kind of patient, planet-scale cataloguing that turns scattered observations into a single, usable picture.
