### The Positive Papers
1. **India Records Highest-Ever Solar Power Generation In October 2026 As Clean Energy Milestones Rise Globally - CREA**
   India generated more solar power in October 2026 than in any prior month on record. The milestone reflects continued additions of utility-scale and distributed solar capacity across multiple states. Clean-energy tracking group CREA documented the output alongside parallel gains in other regions. The result lowers the share of fossil generation on the Indian grid for that month. Grid operators still face the open question of how to balance evening peaks without new storage. Individual households and businesses in India gain from lower marginal electricity costs as solar share rises. The scale at which this pattern repeats elsewhere remains the point of disagreement. CREA’s tracking shows the October figure exceeded every previous monthly total, confirming the trajectory of capacity additions already underway. States contributing to the record include those with both large ground-mounted arrays and growing rooftop programs. The data release coincides with similar clean-energy milestone reports from other countries tracked by the same group. 2. **New Method Treats Severe Thumb Arthritis With Joint Replacement – Just like a New Hip or Shoulder**
   A new ball-and-socket implant replaces the trapezium bone at the base of the thumb. In trials patients regained functional range of motion comparable to a healthy thumb. The design avoids the traditional removal of the trapezium that leaves bone-on-bone contact. Surgeons insert the device through a standard approach already used for hip and shoulder replacements. Early results show improved grip strength and reduced pain scores. The approach is still limited to patients with advanced arthritis and requires longer-term follow-up data. The open question is how widely insurers will cover the implant versus established excision procedures. The implant functions as an insert that restores the joint surface without excising the entire bone. Patients in the initial trials reported gains in everyday hand function that had been limited by pain and instability. The surgical technique mirrors methods long established for larger joints, which may ease adoption among hand surgeons already familiar with similar hardware. Source: [goodnewsnetwork.org](https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/new-way-to-treat-severe-thumb-arthritis-with-joint-replacement/)

3. **‘Extraordinary’ Drug May Actually Cure High Blood Pressure Caused by Hormones, And it’s FDA-Approved**
   Baxdrostat blocks aldosterone production and reversed hormone-driven hypertension in study participants. Some patients maintained normal readings for years after stopping the drug. The medication is already FDA-approved for another indication, shortening the path to wider use. Researchers noted the effect size exceeded typical blood-pressure medications in the target subgroup. The finding applies only to the roughly 10-15 percent of hypertension cases tied to excess aldosterone. Larger trials are still needed to confirm durability across diverse populations. The practical question is which patients should be screened for the hormonal subtype first. The study documented sustained blood-pressure control even after the course of treatment ended. Because the drug already holds FDA approval, physicians can prescribe it off-label while additional hypertension-specific data accumulate. The mechanism targets the root hormonal driver rather than managing downstream pressure effects alone. ### Think Positive
Viktor Frankl taught that the space between stimulus and response contains the freedom to choose. That choice is the first act of agency even when external conditions remain difficult. In the face of a new diagnosis or a shifting energy bill, the pause before reacting turns a problem into a decision point. The mental rep is simple: name the stimulus out loud, then state one response you can control today. This practice builds resilience without denying the original difficulty. Frankl’s insight emerged from observing that individuals retained the ability to select their attitude even inside extreme constraints. Applying the same pause to smaller daily pressures—such as scheduling a medical conversation or reviewing an electricity statement—keeps the focus on the controllable next step. Over repeated practice the habit strengthens the sense that personal effort still moves outcomes.
