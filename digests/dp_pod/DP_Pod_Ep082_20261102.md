### Think Positive
Simon Sinek emphasizes starting with why before any action. The teams that completed the record US renewable project and advanced the Abbott trial both held a clear purpose through years of regulatory and technical setbacks. Listeners facing daily headlines about emissions or medical progress can apply the same principle by first naming their own reason for paying attention. That reason then guides the choice of one small follow-up step rather than passive scrolling. The practice is to write the personal why at the top of a note before deciding what to do next with any one story. This creates ownership over the response instead of letting external events dictate mood or inaction.

### The Lever
Contact your local utility this week and ask specifically about options to purchase renewable energy credits or join a community solar program if one operates in your area. The step requires one phone call or online form and typically takes under thirty minutes. It costs nothing to inquire and returns a verifiable shift in the share of your electricity matched to new renewables, usually at a modest premium of a few dollars per month. If one percent of listeners made the same call, the aggregated demand signal would equal the annual output of several hundred residential solar rooftops. A common barrier is uncertainty about exact pricing or eligibility; the way past it is to request the provider email the current rate sheets and any waiting-list details before ending the call. The action directly links household electricity choices to the scale of projects described in the Positive Papers. Utilities track participation numbers internally and can confirm the renewable matching percentage. Regional programs differ in availability, so the initial inquiry surfaces the precise local options. Follow-up confirmation arrives by email within days in most cases.
