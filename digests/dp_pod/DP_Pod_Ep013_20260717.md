### The Positive Papers
1. **Sugar-coated nanoparticles restore tumor-suppressing protein in glioblastoma** — ScienceDaily Top Science
   Researchers developed sugar-coated nanoparticles that ferry genetic instructions restoring a key tumor-suppressing protein directly into brain cancer cells. The coating lets the particles cross the blood-brain barrier, glioblastoma’s toughest defense that stops most drugs from reaching tumors. In mouse studies the therapy increased median survival by 50 percent while shrinking tumors. No noticeable damage appeared in other organs of the treated mice. The approach works by delivering the genetic payload straight to the cancer cells once the barrier is passed. The work stays at the mouse stage so human safety, dosing, and efficacy remain unproven. Glioblastoma ranks among the deadliest brain cancers with limited current options. The concrete survival gain in the model gives researchers a new route to test in larger animals next. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/07/260715083542.htm)

2. **Astronomers detect atmosphere on a habitable-zone rocky exoplanet** — Phys.org Breaking
   Researchers identified an atmosphere around an Earth-like rocky planet orbiting inside the habitable zone of its star. This marks the first such detection for any world that combines rocky composition with Earth-like temperature range. Ground- and space-based telescopes supplied the data that confirmed the atmosphere’s presence. The planet sits roughly 48 light-years away. The result supplies the strongest evidence yet that worlds with conditions similar to Earth in both composition and temperature could exist beyond our solar system. Confirmation of specific atmospheric gases and surface conditions will require additional observations. The milestone lets individuals track ongoing exoplanet research and join related citizen-science projects if they choose. Further data will clarify whether the atmosphere supports liquid water or other life-friendly traits. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-07-life-solar-atmosphere-habitable-zone.html)

3. **Governments approve transfer of 30 beluga whales from shuttered theme park** — Good News Network
   The US and Canadian governments approved a plan to move 30 beluga whales that have waited two years at a closed Ontario theme park. Marineland shut down years ago after captivity for intelligent marine mammals grew controversial, leaving the whales without new homes until now. The transfer moves the animals to more ethical facilities. The approval ends a prolonged period of uncertainty for the group. No specific timeline for the moves appears in the report, yet the joint government decision removes the main regulatory barrier. The outcome directly improves living conditions for these 30 individuals after years of delay. Source: [goodnewsnetwork.org](https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/30-beluga-whales-finally-rescued-after-2-years-waiting-in-shuttered-theme-park/)
