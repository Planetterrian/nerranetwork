To really understand how BRICS coordinates positions among members with differing external ties, consider the group’s consensus-based statements that deliberately avoid binding commitments on divisive issues.

Each member brings its own set of bilateral relationships, so formal declarations tend to emphasize broad goals such as expanded trade in local currencies rather than specific sanctions or military stances.

This structure emerged from the group’s original focus on economic coordination among large emerging markets rather than security alignment.

Knowing this helps readers see why a summit can produce a joint communiqué on development finance while remaining silent on the Ukraine war or the situation in the Middle East.
