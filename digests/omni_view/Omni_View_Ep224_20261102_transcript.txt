To really understand how an emergency alert can still reach a phone when ordinary networks fail,
you have to follow the path the message takes. A lot of daily coverage treats a breached
alert as simple vandalism. These systems are built as one of the few reliable channels left
when phone networks and broadcast towers are damaged or jammed. Governments pre-load the apps
on devices sold in the country. They push messages through dedicated servers that bypass normal cellular
congestion. That is why the same app can still function when voice calls fail.

sirens and television alerts often reach too few people too late. This design
