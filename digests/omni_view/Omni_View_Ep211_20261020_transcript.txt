In ordinary diplomatic practice, cutting staff as a routine, reversible signal that stops
short of ending relations. When a host country's actions create friction, an unplanned disclosure
of sensitive information is one classic trigger, the sending state can reduce personnel in
order to show displeasure. It can do that without closing the post. The standard remaining channel
