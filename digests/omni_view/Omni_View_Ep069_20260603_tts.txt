Now, to really understand the tariff proposal, there is an administrative sequence most headlines leave out.

The Office of the U S Trade Representative follows a structured review under statutes such as Section three hundred one.

Officials first identify practices deemed unfair, invite public comment, hold hearings, and then recommend duty rates that can later be modified or challenged in court.

This path explains why the current proposal distinguishes between economies with full versus partial forced-labor bans and why earlier tariffs were struck down by the Supreme Court.
