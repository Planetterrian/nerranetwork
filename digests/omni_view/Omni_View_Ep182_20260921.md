## Understanding the Issue: How index inclusion actually channels foreign money
Most coverage of a country joining a major stock index treats the event as an automatic flood of cash. In practice, the mechanism works through passive funds that must buy the new constituents in proportion to their weighting once the reclassification takes effect. These purchases are mechanical rather than discretionary, which is why analysts can estimate the size of inflows from the amount of assets tracking that particular index. The same rules also create selling pressure later if a country is removed. Knowing this, when you hear claims that index inclusion will “transform” an economy, you can check the actual size of the passive funds involved and whether active investors are following the same path. The reclassification decision itself rests on measurable thresholds for market size, liquidity and accessibility that the FTSE reviews on a fixed schedule. Next time an index announcement appears, look at the methodology document to see the precise rebalancing schedule and the minimum free-float requirements that determined eligibility. Vietnam’s addition follows sustained policy steps to open capital markets, including adjustments to foreign ownership limits that met the benchmark’s standards.
