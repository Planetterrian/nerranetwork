The war power's resolution of 1973 works as a sequence of deadlines.
The president is required to notify Congress within 48 hours of committing armed forces.
Engagements are then limited to 60 days without authorization, plus a 30-day period to withdraw.
Lawmakers can pass a resolution meant to force a pullout, though presidents have often
