## Understanding the Issue: How energy partnerships help countries navigate sanctions
Most reporting on trade between sanctioned nations treats rerouting as simple smuggling, yet the actual mechanism involves third-country refiners and established payment channels that predate current restrictions. India has increased purchases of Russian crude since 2022, processed it domestically, and exported petrol and diesel to buyers who previously sourced directly from Russia. This pattern mirrors earlier episodes when other countries used similar intermediaries during past sanction regimes, such as those involving different energy producers in the 2010s. The approach spreads volume across multiple legal entities and currencies, making full enforcement more difficult for regulators who must track refined products rather than raw inputs alone. Knowing this, when coverage highlights a single "oil deal," readers can check whether the transaction includes documented refining steps or merely lists crude volumes without downstream details. Next time a headline mentions a new energy supplier, note whether the reporting tracks the refined product destination or stops at the crude purchase stage.
