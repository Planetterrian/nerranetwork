Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company recorded September net revenue of five hundred eleven point eight six billion New Taiwan dollars.

That figure marks a fifty four point six percent increase from the same month a year earlier.

The company stands as the world's largest contract chipmaker and supplies advanced chips to clients that include Nvidia and Apple.

Third quarter revenue reached approximately one point four nine trillion New Taiwan dollars.

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company also announced it will adopt ASML's High NA extreme ultraviolet lithography machines.

Those machines enable production of even smaller and more powerful chips.

CNBC frames the results as evidence that demand for A I hardware remains strong even as foundation models improve and pricing pressure grows.

The same report notes that sales did not set a new monthly record.

Taiwan's government supported this industry through consistent policy, education investment, and infrastructure long before artificial intelligence became prominent.
