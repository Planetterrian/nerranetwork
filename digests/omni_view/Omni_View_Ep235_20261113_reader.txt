Microsoft reported early signs of stabilisation after Teams, Outlook and other services failed for millions of users worldwide, The Guardian said, with no indication of a cyber-attack.

The company extended the email timeout window to forty-eight hours while recovery efforts continued, and the disruption reached general practitioners, schools, and cancelled meetings.

Microsoft said a portion of its infrastructure experienced elevated load that likely contributed to connectivity errors, and some servers became unexpectedly unavailable during automated checks.

The company warned that restarted services could become unresponsive again once traffic returned.

The British government said it was working closely with Microsoft to assess the impact on services while maintaining contingency plans.

Indonesia's upper house of parliament will, the South China Morning Post reported, pick the country's new vice-president on December second after the resignation of Gibran Rakabuming Raka, the president's deputy and son of his predecessor.

Gibran's resignation followed a month of political pressure after courts ruled on his eligibility.

The move could widen the gulf between President Prabowo Subianto and former president Joko Widodo, and analysts have noted the timing places the selection just weeks after the resignation.

Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky and United States envoy Jared Kushner said Europe would join the next round of talks with Russia, France 24 reported following more than four hours of US discussions with Vladimir Putin in the Kremlin.

Putin indicated Moscow will not compromise on its hardline demands.

Everything turns on whether Europe sits in those talks and whether any deal starts with a ceasefire rather than a wider security design.

Kaja Kallas, the European Union's foreign policy chief, argued that excluding Europe would leave the continent in a worse position, The Guardian reported, because any outcome must reflect European interests directly and Russia must not be allowed to divert attention onto broader security-architecture questions.

She stressed that the agreed sequence of ceasefire first, then a peace agreement, must be maintained because Russia is presenting maximum demands, and that starting with European security-architecture discussions risks weakening the focus on the aggressor-victim dynamic and allows Russia to exploit divisions, which would leave Europe worse off.

Iran reported that a tanker carrying liquefied natural gas, or LNG, hit a mine and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz, The Hindu International said, and tankers continue to face attacks there despite a rebound in traffic and falling oil prices.

The incident comes amid expectations of de-escalation in the US-Israeli conflict with Iran.

Iraqi Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein told France 24 that the no war and no peace situation cannot continue, and confirmed Iraqi oil is now flowing through the strait under US protection.

European Union leaders met in Dublin for the first ever summit on artificial intelligence, Deutsche Welle reported, after Antonio Costa, president of the European Council, which brings national leaders together, had written calling for discussion on opportunities and the technology's transformative effect on economies and societies.

Arthur Mensch, chief executive of the French company Mistral, and researcher Geoffrey Hinton are expected to speak.

Estonia and Finland called for EU-level arrangements to respond to serious AI incidents, while Mistral urged Europe to push ahead rather than slow development.

BMW Brilliance, the joint venture in Shenyang, China, where BMW holds a seventy-five percent share, began cutting an estimated one thousand eight hundred to two thousand jobs from November second, the South China Morning Post reported, following rivalry from Chinese carmakers.

Brazil's Amazon offers progress worth knowing, with deforestation down forty-one percent between August twenty twenty-five and twenty twenty-six to the lowest rate since records began, Deutsche Welle reported, citing data linked to the Tropical Forest Forever Facility.
