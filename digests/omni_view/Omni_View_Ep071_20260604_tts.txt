Now, to really understand this story, there is something most coverage leaves out.

In the U K, parties must report donations above a certain threshold to the Electoral Commission within set timeframes, after which the information becomes public.

The system relies on self-reporting by parties rather than real-time independent verification, and cryptocurrency donations are treated under the same rules as other contributions once converted to pounds.

Knowing this, when you hear claims about record fundraising, you can check whether the totals reflect many small donors or a few large ones by reviewing the commission’s published registers.
