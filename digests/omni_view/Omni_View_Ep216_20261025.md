Vucic’s party argues that continuity in leadership has delivered stability and economic growth that Serbia needs after years of turbulence, pointing to infrastructure projects completed under its watch as evidence that experienced governance produces tangible results. The student-led movement contends that persistent corruption has eroded public trust and that fresh accountability mechanisms are required to prevent further tragedies like the 2024 collapse. Both sides accept that the canopy failure exposed serious oversight failures; they differ on whether the current leadership can credibly address those failures or whether new actors are necessary.

## Understanding the Issue: How border reopenings between rivals affect everyday supply chains
Most coverage of border closures treats them as simple diplomatic signals, yet the mechanics involve concrete logistics that directly shape prices inside each country. When Pakistan closed the Gabd-Rimdan crossing, trucks carrying LPG and food could not cross, creating measurable shortages that raised household energy and grocery costs within days. The same pattern appears in other regions where limited crossing points concentrate trade: a single route closure forces rerouting that adds time, fuel, and fees, which importers pass on to consumers. Historical cases, such as periodic India-Pakistan closures, show that even short suspensions produce price spikes that linger after reopening because restocking takes weeks. The Gabd-Rimdan crossing serves as a key conduit for goods that directly influence domestic energy supplies and food availability in Pakistan. Resumption of operations is expected to ease the LPG shortage and reduce pressure on food and energy prices that rose during the suspension. Knowing this mechanism, when reports mention a border reopening, readers can check whether the story includes data on which goods moved before the closure and whether those volumes have resumed. One concrete thing to verify is whether official trade statistics later show a rebound in the specific commodities that were delayed during the week-long halt.
