A redesign would start by collapsing color variety into a smaller set of blends that can be produced continuously rather than in separate campaigns.

Continuous production of a limited palette would eliminate repeated line changeovers and the carrying cost of dozens of color inventories.

The next move would shift from loose shingles to larger pre-sealed panels or rolls.
