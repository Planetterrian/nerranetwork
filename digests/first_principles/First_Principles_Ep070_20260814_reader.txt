A redesign would start by collapsing isolated unit operations inside the synthesis.

The regulatory path comes next.

Mutual recognition of well-characterized inputs and in-line analytical data could shorten the time from process change to commercial release.

Once the active ingredient costs less to make, distribution can shift toward direct verified supply from fewer high-volume facilities.

Several conditions must line up for any of these moves to reach the floor.

Regulators must accept real-time release testing instead of end-product testing on every batch.

Manufacturers must invest capital in dedicated continuous lines rather than multipurpose batch plants.

Payers and policymakers must create mechanisms that reward lower list prices instead of confidential rebates.

The hard parts are not the chemistry.
