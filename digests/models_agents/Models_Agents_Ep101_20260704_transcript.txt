Everyone talks about model differing
as if activation differences are the only reliable signal.

logits between base and fine tuned models
already surfaces verbatim training strings
with no weight access required.
The method works because narrow fine tuning
shifts probability mass on a small set of tokens.
Contrasting the two output distributions
amplifies exactly those shifts without needing
to inspect hidden states.
