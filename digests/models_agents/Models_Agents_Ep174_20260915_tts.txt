Pop the hood on early safety-signal extraction and the picture looks less like magic and more like careful signal processing.

Harmful intent already modulates the very first generated token's hidden state.

ForeSight trains a small per-layer projector that collapses the four-thousand-ninety-six-dimensional activation into a seven-dimensional summary.

The summary captures slope, curvature, and onset timing across the first few layers.

The projector stays frozen after a single forward pass on a modest safety corpus.

Inference cost therefore stays under three micro-FLOPs per token.

The resulting scalar risk score crosses a decision threshold in under sixty-one milliseconds on ninety-five percent of prompts.
