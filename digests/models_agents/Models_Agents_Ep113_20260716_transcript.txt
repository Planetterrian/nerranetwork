Okay, let's pop the hood on cost-aware speculative decoding for mixture of experts models.
Everyone talks about speculative decoding as a simple draft and verify speed up.
In practice, the technique collides with the non-uniform memory cost of mixture of experts inference.
When high probability draft tokens wrote to disjoint experts, the verification step triggers scattered
expert loads that erase most of the latency win. EcoSpec therefore adds a lightweight expert predictor
and a dynamic buffer that prefers draft pads whose experts are already resident in the current
verification set. The added predictor costs a few milliseconds, yet reclaims up to 1.62 times
end-to-end speed up on 671 billion scale mixture of experts models by cutting expert weight traffic.
The key engineering decision is to keep the target model's verification rule unchanged so the method
works as a drop-in draft selector. Use it when your mixture of experts workload is memory bandwidth
