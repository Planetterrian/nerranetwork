surface as 5070 TI owners add older cards. Idle time on a five device chain is the
reason to pop the hood on pipeline speculation for mixed kits. Multi-device
mixture of experts inference looks like simple layer sharding until the slowest
link leaves 4 or 5 gadgets waiting.
The practical fix is a small draft head on the first device that guesses the next few
tokens, after which the entire chain validates them in one pass.
That first device then starts the next speculation while earlier guesses are still traveling.
A verified step averages about 2.2 tokens, so the weight becomes decode.
On a 48-layer 512-expert model split across two PCs, an M4 Pro, Macbook, and two phones,
this yields 37-39 tokens a second at 60,000 contacts with every weight kept in VRAM or Unified
memory.
The first PC holds layers 0-9 plus the output layer and the draft head.
The Macbook holds layers 10-29.
The second PC holds layers 30-47.
Phone's handle expert selection for layers 10-18 over USB-C, while the Mac keeps the hottest
experts in its own memory.
That draft head has to beat the extra PCIe and USB-C latency as the window grows, and
once acceptance drops under about 55% the quality edge is gone.
The layout serves 69.6000 context natively before key value cash pressure forces a second

the Mac and phones.
Every change is exact by construction except the Mac's expert cache, which adds partial
