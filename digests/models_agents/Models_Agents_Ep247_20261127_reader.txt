Chariot Artificial Intelligence Pvt. Ltd. launched the Chariot-1 foundation model suite, Wocult reported, on November twenty-sixth at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, with MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan performing the launch under the IndiaAI Mission, and Chariot sits among the opening four groups picked to build sovereign foundation models.

The suite pairs an eleven-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts large language model, or LLM, that activates four billion parameters per token with a text-to-speech system, both trained from scratch in India, and it ships three sizes — ten point seven billion, one point seven billion, and seven hundred forty-five million parameters — for data-center, laptop, and smartphone deployment.

The speech model covers English plus Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, and Odia, and it chooses pronunciation from context for numbers, dates, currencies, URLs, and abbreviations, clearing over ninety-seven percent verbalisation accuracy against the Async TTS Pronunciation Benchmark.

IndicShift tokenization covers one thousand twelve characters across twelve Indian scripts, and nested training sped runs thirty-eight percent on the same GPUs while model loss barely moved; another reported method cut training time by sixty-two percent.

Co-founder Parth Sarthi stated that a nation of India's scale and diversity cannot depend on AI built elsewhere, and work is already under way on a seventy-billion-parameter model, with a trillion-parameter system as a longer-term aim.

Namazu Small, from Sakana AI, MarkTechPost details, is a thirty-B A-three-B reasoning model built on NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano and averages sixty point one across twenty-five benchmarks.

Nemotron 3 Super, at one hundred twenty billion parameters, sits at fifty-six point two, so the smaller active-parameter count beats a much larger dense counterpart on aggregate reasoning tasks.

Speculative decoding for sparse-attention LLMs gained an open-source drafter called DFly two from Tencent, and each draft layer reuses the target's key-value cache and top-k indexer picks, adding no draft key-value memory.

Serving throughput at sixty-four-K rises by as much as eighty-one percent, and because the drafter shares the target's cache, extra memory stays at zero while long-context generation still speeds up.

An unfinished Gogol novel now has an ending written by a Russian AI model, Reuters reported, and literary observers gave mixed reactions.

Those reactions cited both what current models can do when they continue classic literary works and the cultural sensitivities of AI stepping into national literary heritage.

Researchers introduced Stable Gated DeltaNet-2, an arXiv paper sets out, using symmetric erasure and decay-regulated residual writing to bound recurrent state growth at O of square-root T.

Asymmetric erase operators in the original Gated DeltaNet-2 design had caused exponential state growth, and on models of about one point six billion parameters the new method lifted the one hundred twenty-eight-K average RULER score by six point eight zero points in the recurrent setting and average accuracy across nine downstream tasks by zero point six two points in the hybrid setting.

A two-stage projected cross-model Chain-of-Thought vector transfer method lifted base student models by ten point three seven to twenty-one point nine eight accuracy points on GSM8K and MATH and by three point two three to nine point six five pass-at-one points on four code-generation benchmarks, with teacher and student both frozen.

Stage one learns a task-level Chain-of-Thought vector in the teacher; stage two trains only a lightweight linear projector that maps it into the student's residual space, and student adaptation needs no teacher outputs or intermediate traces.

LaunchDarkly demonstrated parallel AI agents running in cloud sandboxes, Howl Link reported, and those agents produced forty-four code changes.

Agent execution stays isolated while still posting measurable productivity gains on real engineering tasks.

Onchainbench, from Valory, HackerNoon details, tests how safely and reliably AI agents use crypto wallets.

Wallet operations run under realistic threat models, with standardized scoring for agentic financial actions.

Japan's Amada and Hitachi are building AI agents for autonomous control of factory equipment and robots, BigGo Finance reported.

Their work targets real-time decision loops on industrial machinery without constant human oversight.

Metano left stealth with a twelve million dollar seed, FF News reported, to unify AI agent security across the enterprise.

That round is meant to put consistent policy enforcement and runtime controls on agents that operate inside companies.

ORCASTRA gained upgrades at EACON, International Mining reported, plus a new mining AI agent that targets autonomous operation of heavy equipment and extends the platform's existing orchestration.

Developers are being advised to add explicit validation of tool responses, TechGig reported, to prevent unsafe agent behavior.

Skip that check and agents can act on malformed or malicious tool outputs, with security and correctness failures downstream.

Puzzling-Bench supplies twenty-three thousand seven hundred seventy-seven community-verified puzzles, an arXiv paper describes, including a rolling post-cutoff slice and a multimodal pilot, for contamination-resistant evaluation of frontier models.

Each item includes the author-accepted answer, a short reference, the author's hints, community tags, and the creation date.

MINE defends without training and without tying itself to one attack, reranking retrieved content by mutual information and embedding variance.

It sits at the front of accuracy-security trade-offs on Inj-SQuAD, Inj-TriviaQA, LC-Inj-SQuAD, and InjecAgent while adding the lowest latency on long contexts, evaluated on a new long-context benchmark averaging four thousand thirty-two words per context.

In brief, smaller on-premise models let banks keep sensitive data in-house rather than shipping it to outside providers, PYMNTS reported, cutting regulatory exposure while still running useful inference in controlled environments.

ChatGPT-generated stock selections, Chosun reported, delivered a twenty-two percent gain, beating the S&P five hundred.

OpenAI adjusted GPT six point two output limits, InfoWorld reported, shrinking tokens delivered at the existing price so customers now get fewer generated tokens per request with no change in subscription cost.

LEXI, described on arXiv, runs a large language model workflow that pulls in virtual data room files, pulls out due-diligence facts with provenance, and drafts report sections while lawyers keep the final legal judgment, classifying documents under a configurable taxonomy, assigning target entities, and surfacing supporting passages with page-level citations.

Self-evolution methods raise goal completion on AppWorld and SkillsBench, yet English still outruns Urdu and code-switched input, with the widest misses on multi-step tasks, and IRTIQA supplies aligned English, Urdu-translated, and code-switched versions of four existing datasets, checked by humans.
