That targeted approach cuts compute by roughly sixty to seventy percent compared with full regeneration from scratch.

A lightweight subject encoder extracts identity tokens once and feeds them at every denoising step, adding fifteen to twenty-five milliseconds per iteration.

The consistency gain holds only when reference images stay inside a narrow distribution.

Out-of-domain subjects lose fidelity unless the encoder is re-run or the attention mask is tightened, which removes the latency savings.

Builders therefore choose this path for repeated design iterations and switch to single-shot generation when the subject or style shifts dramatically between turns.
