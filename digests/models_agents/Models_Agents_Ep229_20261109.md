# Models & Agents
> **LG just opened free API access to its 750B K-EXAONE 2.5 model for agentic workloads.**

**What You Need to Know:** LG AI Research released free hosted access to K-EXAONE 2.5, a 750B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model focused on Korean and agent tasks. It improves on the prior version across software development, web browsing, SaaS automation, and agent problem-solving benchmarks. Builders working on Korean-language agents or tool-calling workflows can now test it without cost.
---
### Top Story
LG AI Research opened free API access to K-EXAONE 2.5, a 750B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model trained further from the August K-EXAONE 2.0 release. The model targets agentic workloads with strong Korean-language support and handles long context plus complex tool calls. Internal benchmarks show gains over the prior version on software development, web information retrieval, SaaS automation, and agent problem-solving tasks. Developers and startups can obtain an API key immediately through the EXAONE platform for service building and research. The release positions the model as a technology-validation step toward a frontier-level system planned for January. Source: [mk.co.kr](https://www.mk.co.kr/en/business/12202763)

**Compression of Emotion in Language Model Representations: arXiv NLP**
Seven LLMs tested on the GoEmotions dataset show consistent over-compression of 27 fine-grained emotion categories into broader positive-negative splits in their hidden representations. Linear probes still recover fine-grained information despite the unsupervised geometry favoring coarser groupings. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08683)

**Contextuality and Contradictions: Exploring Real-World Identifications of AI-Generated Writing: arXiv NLP**
Wikipedia editors and students identify AI text using diverse evidence types that prove contradictory and context-dependent rather than fixed textual markers. The study finds pragmatic infelicity, not specific features, often drives these judgments. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08704)

**RL-ATTACK: A Generalized Black-Box Adversarial Attack on Language Models via Reinforcement Learning: arXiv NLP**
A reinforcement-learning attacker trained against target models achieves lower post-attack accuracy and better query efficiency than baselines on natural language inference and generative QA tasks. The approach learns tailored transformations directly from the target without external knowledge. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08711)

**Guardrail Erosion in Domain Fine-Tuned Large Language Models: A Paired Audit of Knowledge-Centric Abstention in Biomedical LLMs: arXiv NLP**
A paired audit of 25 biomedical fine-tuned models against their base checkpoints found appropriate abstention rates on MedAbstain dropped from a median of 8.3% to 4.2% after fine-tuning. The change was heterogeneous across model pairs. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08780)
