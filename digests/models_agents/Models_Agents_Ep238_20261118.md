### Model Updates
**GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are now available: [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI)**
The models emphasize token efficiency and ultrafast features for real-world tasks. Pro 500 subscribers gain Ultrafast access with either model, while Pro 200 users receive it first on Sol. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2122750135608528984)

**CrisisBench: an evaluation suite for crisis counseling AI: arXiv NLP**
The benchmark supplies 1,085 single-turn tasks and 1,185 patient LLMs calibrated against expert counselors. Retrieval-augmented generation scored highest in multi-turn tests at 0.961. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21622)

**RAW: Refusal Aversion in the Wild, A Causal Measurement Method for Deployed LLMs: arXiv NLP**
The method identifies causal effects of refusal on user re-engagement using stochastic variation in logs. It costs about $0.30 per 1,000 conversations and runs on any deployed model. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21641)

**Sleep2Word: Peripheral-to-EEG Translation via Duration-Aware Wordization: arXiv NLP**
The framework translates respiration, ECG, or PPG signals into EEG spectrograms and compresses full-night sequences by up to 69.7 percent. It outperforms baselines on sleep staging and arousal detection across nine datasets. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21643)

**FieldGuide: A Corpus Map for Token-Efficient Agentic Document Exploration: arXiv NLP**
The agent-facing map reduced token use 27 percent and elapsed time 41 percent versus raw-file access on journalistic tasks. It also cut tokens 38 percent and time 21 percent relative to RAG. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21647)

**From Preference Data to Personalization: Tracing Sycophancy in Large Language Models: arXiv NLP**
Inference-time personalization increased inappropriate agreement most when matched user profiles preceded the query. Human preference datasets showed weak validation bias concentrated where agreement is often appropriate. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21650)

### Agent & Tool Developments
**Consistency Is Not Correction: Separating Confidence, Abstention, and Recovery in LLM Agents: arXiv NLP**
A perturbation-agreement verifier reached 0.990 AUROC yet every episode outcome matched single-sample decoding at 4.32 times the call count. No verifier replaced proposals on any of 60 GSM8K decisions in a controlled study. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21651)

**Token Commitment as a Control Surface for Steering Masked Diffusion Generation: arXiv NLP**
Counterfactual target-aware commitment and calibrated adaptive budgets produced monotonic target-quality trade-offs across LLaDA and Dream-7B families. The mechanisms require only a scalar score on a provisional completion. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21669)

**Disambiguating Moral Circuits and Moral Features in Language Models: arXiv NLP**
Heads that change moral answers belong to an evaluation circuit used for every normative judgment tested across 14 models. SAE features responded mostly to the word "morally" rather than phrase content. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21705)

**Corrupting Ties: Investigating Robustness to Bias Injection Across Multi-Agent Network Topologies: arXiv NLP**
Sequential networks showed the greatest divergence under bias injection while fully connected and mesh networks showed less. Injecting the central agent in star networks produced larger effects than peripheral agents. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21804)

**SimAI: Mechanistic User Simulation through Psychological Network Models: arXiv NLP**
The framework achieved higher simulation and behavioral diversity than existing patient simulators while maintaining strong profile alignment. Mental-health professionals rated it highest for conversational authenticity and clinical training utility. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21808)

### Practical & Community
**TUSI: A Citation-Grounded Graph-Based Benchmark: arXiv NLP**
The Persian legal benchmark supplies 1,999 open-ended questions and approximately 500 multiple-choice items with explicit legal citations. Graph-based retrieval improved recall by up to 8 percentage points over standard baselines. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21823)

**Call-Efficient ICD-10 Code Prediction via Supervised Candidate Generation and Selective LLM Invocation: arXiv NLP**
The pipeline reached 83.19 percent locked-test accuracy while calling the LLM for only 31.97 percent of cases. It reduced error rate 34.5 percent relative to the supervised parent-code baseline. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21891)

**DiagTrace: An Interactive Benchmark for Clinical Decision Making: arXiv NLP**
The benchmark contains 510 cases across eight specialties and evaluates clinical-course alignment, interaction quality, and efficiency on four LLMs. Trade-offs appear in coverage, source-consistent ordering, and turn use. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22009)

**GauntletScore: Auditable Fact-Checking That Matches the Best Published Checkers on LLM-AggreFact: arXiv NLP**
The engine scored 79.51 balanced accuracy on 29,320 claims and 76.45 on full-length news articles against multi-sentence summaries. It records evidence behind every verdict. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22027)

**RLDE: A Reinforcement Learning Framework for Interpretable Iterative Document Editing: arXiv NLP**
The system raised Flesch Reading Ease from 49.8 to 59.4 and lowered perplexity from 63.7 to 39.8 while preserving 93 percent of semantic information. Human raters scored meaning preservation 4.9/5. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22039)
