The technique re-parameterizes generation as learning of velocity field that transports noise to
data along straight paths in probability space. The model therefore predicts the instantaneous
direction of the probability flow rather than a score or noise estimate. Straight paths let the
Odesimal Disciver Solver take larger steps at inference time and cut the number of function
evaluations required. Training pays a cost because the velocity target must be estimated from paired
noise data points, which raises gradient variance compared with the simpler noise prediction loss.
