OK, let's pop the hood on cost-aware speculative decoding for mixture of experts models.

Everyone talks about speculative decoding as a simple draft-and-verify speed-up.

In practice the technique collides with the non-uniform memory cost of mixture of experts inference.

When high-probability draft tokens route to disjoint experts, the verification step triggers scattered expert loads that erase most of the latency win.

EcoSpec therefore adds a lightweight expert predictor and a dynamic buffer that prefers draft paths whose experts are already resident in the current verification set.

The added predictor costs a few milliseconds yet reclaims up to one point six two times end-to-end speedup on six hundred seventy one billion scale mixture of experts models by cutting expert-weight traffic.

The key engineering decision is to keep the target model's verification rule unchanged so the method works as a drop-in draft selector.

Use it when your mixture of experts workload is memory-bandwidth bound and you already have a fast expert predictor.

Skip it if expert activation is already dense or your draft model is tiny enough that scattering is rare.
