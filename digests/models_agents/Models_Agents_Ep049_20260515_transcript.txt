Everyone treats it as a simple speed boost you just enable.
In practice, it depends on a fragile agreement between the draft or model
and the target model distributions. When that agreement slips even slightly,
the average accepted length tau collapses and the promised two-time speed-up vanishes.
Mistletoe style attacks exploit exactly this gap. They nudge the drafter with null space projected
gradients that preserve output semantics while tanking acceptance rates.
The engineering trade-off is clear. You gain throughput only while the drafter stays close
to the target. Any defense that hardens acceptance also adds verification latency.
Most teams hit the wall once context scales past 8,000 tokens. Drift between drafter and target
