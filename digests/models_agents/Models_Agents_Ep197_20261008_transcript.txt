stream. Four emotions decode linearly from that stream. Three of them collapse onto one shared
direction. Sad stays orthogonal and therefore steerable on its own. The shared direction
cannot be subtracted equally from all four emotions without distorting the distinct one.
Steering therefore succeeds when the target emotion aligns with the shared vector and
fails when it needs an independent offset. The practical limit appears at the point where
three emotions share geometry while the fourth does not. Measure steerability per emotion
on the target model rather than assuming uniform results across the set.
