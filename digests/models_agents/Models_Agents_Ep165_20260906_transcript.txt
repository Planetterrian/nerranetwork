Pop the hood on reduced order models for dynamical system transfer learning.
And the approach projects the full state onto a low dimensional subspace learned from snapshot data.
Then learns the reduced dynamics before lifting actions back to the original coordinates.
This compression drops the effective state dimension from thousands to dozens and cuts sample complexity of policy search by roughly an order of magnitude on fluid flow and structural mechanics benchmarks.
The trade-off surfaces when discarded modes carry critical transient behavior, in which case the reduced policy can produce unstable closed loop trajectories even if open loop reconstruction error appears small.
Teens therefore retain a small set of residual modes or add an online correction term that re-injects energy from the neglected directions.
Reduced order transfer works when source and target systems share the same dominant physics but differ in geometry or forcing.
Full order fine-tuning or domain randomization remains preferable when important dynamics live in the tail of the spectrum.
