OK, let's pop the hood on the invisible reasoning tokens that drive the ten point six times cost spread. Published per token prices suggest predictable spend. In practice many models generate substantial hidden reasoning traces billed at output rates but never shown to the user.

The Reddit benchmark revealed one model consuming one hundred ninety seven internal tokens to emit a single classification word. These traces arise because current reasoning training encourages step by step computation even when the final answer is trivial. The effect compounds on agentic or multi turn tasks where failed attempts burn extra tokens at high rates.

This matches the reported negative zero point six two correlation between failed attempts and token burn. When choosing providers, run your actual workload rather than relying on list prices. The practical decision rule is to measure end to end token usage on representative tasks before committing to a cost optimized tier.
