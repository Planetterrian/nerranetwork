The engineering trade-off is maintenance overhead versus retrieval quality.
Sites that keep the file current see agents land on the right information faster,
and a stale file is just another outdated asset sitting at the root.
Publish one when you already maintain high-quality mark-down documentation
and you want coding agents or retrieval systems to consume it reliably.
