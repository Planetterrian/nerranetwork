Soft-prompt compression paid a quadratic Transformer tax on every extra token, so it is time to pop the hood on the Boa Compressor.

Boa replaces that Transformer backbone with a Mamba state-space model that encodes arbitrary-length context in a single linear-time pass.

That pass produces a representation which conditions a frozen target L L M.

The state-space model is trained by distillation so its compressed representation produces the same next-token logits as the target L L M ingesting the full context.

Because the compressor runs online on every query instead of once offline, it removes the latency overhead that previously confined soft-prompt methods to repeated-query settings.

At identical compression ratios the approach matches or exceeds prior offline Transformer-based baselines.

Compression time drops by roughly an order of magnitude.

It continues to scale to sixteen times compression and longer contexts without the quadratic cost that grows with Transformer-based compressors, and without adding per-query overhead.

The practical limit appears when the target L L M's own context window becomes the bottleneck rather than the compressor itself.

Teams facing repeated long-context queries should test Boa first; teams that compress once and reuse across many queries can still prefer the older offline methods.
