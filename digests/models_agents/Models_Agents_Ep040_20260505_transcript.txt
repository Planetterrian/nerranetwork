Okay, let's pop the hood on mixed precision KV caching for a moment. Everyone talks about quantizing
the KV cache as if it were a simple toggle. In practice, the decision splits weight precision
from attention state precision. The KV cache grows linearly with context length while weights
stay fixed. Keeping the cache in BF16 or FP16 while weights sit in FP8 or Q4KM prevents small
per-step rounding errors. Those errors compound across agentic loops and multi-turn tool use.

reduced repetition and more stable tool calling once context passes 80,000 tokens.
