Let me pop the hood on recurrent depth, where Hawk and Griffin match Llama-two after training on six times fewer tokens.

The design reuses the same transformer layers in a loop instead of stacking new ones, so a small model gets more sequential compute steps without extra parameters.

Each layer receives the current token representation and a recurrent state carried from its own previous pass, the mechanism laid out in the Block-Recurrent Transformer paper.

Complexity stays linear in sequence length, and the model can still refine its internal state over multiple iterations at inference.

Size or initialize that recurrent state carelessly and the model loses the ability to extrapolate beyond training lengths.

Relaxed Recursive Transformers add depth-wise Low-Rank Adaptation, or LoRA, modules that loosen strict parameter sharing and recover most of the original accuracy at roughly one-quarter the parameter count.

RecurrentGemma's two billion and nine billion variants reach downstream scores comparable to standard Gemma checkpoints despite fewer training tokens, and they run with fixed memory cost on long sequences.

Use recurrent depth when parameters or tokens are the bottleneck; keep a deeper feed-forward stack when you need the best single pass on short context.
