### Model Updates
**Ant Group Releases LingSante-3.1-flash: An Open-Weight MoE Model with 560B Total Parameters and 25B Activations per Token: MarkTechPost**
It leads open models on the InclusionMed benchmark and targets clinical reasoning, medical search, and tool-using agents. An MXFP4 quantized checkpoint is available, with reference SGLang setups using 8x H200 or 4x GB300 GPUs. The model mixes medical and general data through pretraining, mid-training, SFT, and RL post-training focused on reasoning and professional behavior. The architecture uses a hybrid-linear MoE design with 63 KDA linear attention layers and 9 gated MLA layers in a 7:1 ratio, 512 routed experts plus one shared expert, and eight routed experts active per token. Hidden size is 4096 with 64 attention heads and a vocabulary of 157184 tokens. The family also includes LingSante-3.1-air-VL at 124B total parameters with 5.5B active and 256K context. Three additional models remain closed: LingSante-Pulse for risk prediction across more than 150 conditions, LingSante-MedGuard for safety and bioethics screening, and LingSante-Live for real-time video consultation inside the Afu app that serves 150 million users and handles nearly 20 million daily health consultations. The model scores 65.72 on HealthBench Professional, the highest in the nine-model comparison on the model card. Source: [marktechpost.com](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/28/ant-group-lingsante-3-1-flash-open-source-medical-ai/)

**Switzerland Confirms First AI-Agent Cyberattacks; Four of Five Breaches Succeeded: BigGo Finance**
Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre recorded the country's first autonomous AI-agent cyberattacks, with four of five incidents fully compromising targets. Three targeted critical infrastructure operators and two succeeded; attackers used agentic frameworks that select tools and pivot autonomously. The number of freely available agentic offensive tools nearly doubled since October. Small and medium-sized businesses were among the organizations hit, and those that came forward did so voluntarily. The NCSC advisory issued November 24 stated the agents had fully compromised victims' systems in every known case at that point. The agency cites ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI as examples of reachable agentic offensive frameworks, though it does not confirm either appeared in the Swiss incidents. No major service disruption has been reported, and the agency has not named victims or attributed the campaign. Source: [finance.biggo.com](https://finance.biggo.com/news/ffe1da18-7a30-426e-b006-fa19a3b0eaa7)

### Under the Hood: Self-Hosting Open-Weight Models
Self-hosting open-weight models lets organizations keep data inside their perimeter and avoid per-token cloud fees once volume is high enough. The core tradeoff starts with hardware: consumer Blackwell GPUs such as the RTX 5090 deliver 3.5-4.6x higher throughput than the 5060 Ti on RAG workloads while cutting latency dramatically. NVFP4 quantization further improves this by giving 1.6x throughput over BF16 at 41 percent lower energy use and only 2-4 percent quality loss. At moderate sustained volume the electricity cost alone falls to $0.001-0.04 per million tokens, 40-200x below budget cloud APIs, with hardware payback in under four months. The practical limit appears when frontier capability is required; most teams therefore keep a hybrid pattern of local inference for routine or sensitive traffic and cloud calls for the hardest tasks. The gotcha that bites most teams is underestimating ongoing ops cost once context lengths and concurrency rise beyond the initial benchmark numbers. Systematic evaluations across 79 configurations spanning quantization formats, context lengths from 8k to 64k, and workloads including RAG, multi-LoRA agentic serving, and high-concurrency APIs show that budget GPUs achieve the highest throughput-per-dollar for API workloads with sub-second latency. The RTX 5090 stands out for RAG and multi-LoRA serving, but the energy and cost advantages of NVFP4 hold across model sizes from Qwen3-8B to GPT-OSS-20B. Source: [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09527)
