### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you're playing a team sport like soccer, each player has a position and they pass the ball so the whole team can score? Now imagine the players are AI programs instead of people, and the "ball" is pieces of information or code. One AI might look at the current state of a problem, another might suggest the next move, and a third might check if that move is allowed. They keep passing the information back and forth until the group reaches a goal, like solving a math puzzle or changing a game file.

Each AI in the swarm is specialized, so it only does its one job really well instead of trying to do everything. The group keeps going even if one member gets stuck because the others can keep working. Over time the swarm learns which passes work best, just like a real team gets better with practice. The P=NP swarm on pequalnp.com shows this in action as different agents test possible answers and share results instantly.

That's the idea behind these new agent swarms: instead of one AI doing all the thinking, lots of them work together like teammates who never need a break. The live site makes the handoffs visible so you can follow along without writing any code.

### Cool Stuff & Try This
**Bring your old ChatGPT chats into Gemini**
Google's Gemini can now pull in your conversation history from ChatGPT and other AI chats so everything stays in one place. This is useful if you have old homework help threads or creative writing ideas scattered across different apps and want to keep using them without starting over. Anyone with a Google account can try it on their phone or laptop. Go to the Gemini website or app, look for the import option in settings, and follow the steps to connect your ChatGPT account. You can then ask Gemini to continue an old conversation you started somewhere else. The feature works by letting you select specific chats or entire histories to move over, keeping the original context intact so the new model can pick up exactly where the old one left off. Source: [online-tech-tips.com](https://www.online-tech-tips.com/import-chatgpt-ai-chat-history-into-gemini/)

**Google's search now uses your own files for better answers**
Google's AI Mode in Search can now look at your Google Drive files and Wallet information to give more personal results. If you ask about a trip, it might check your saved documents or payment details to suggest options that actually fit you. This works inside the normal Google Search page or the Gemini app if you have those accounts set up. Try searching something like "plan a weekend trip under $200" and see if it pulls from your own data to make suggestions. The update brings Personal Intelligence features directly into AI Mode so responses can reference your saved Drive files or Wallet entries without extra steps. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/google-searchs-ai-mode-can-now-tap-into-drive-and-wallet-for-more-personalized-responses/)
