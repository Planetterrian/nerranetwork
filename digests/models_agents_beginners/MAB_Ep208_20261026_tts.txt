Step one is the goal, like you setting that reminder in the first place.

Step two is persistence, like the reminder surviving after you pocket the phone.

iPhone owners can now say Hey Grok out loud to start a voice chat without tapping anything.

Grok is a chatbot you can talk to, and this trick treats it like a voice assistant.

It uses a built-in iPhone shortcut, which is a tiny recipe that tells your phone to do a sequence of actions.

The shortcut basically fools the phone into waking Grok the way it might wake a regular voice helper.

That is handy when your hands are busy, like cooking or walking.

A tutorial shared by Texas Twins Dad shows how to set it up using only the Shortcuts app that already comes on every iPhone.

Open the Shortcuts app, search for a ready-made Hey Grok shortcut shared by users, and add it.

No extra apps and no payments. It is like teaching your phone a new nickname for a different helper.
