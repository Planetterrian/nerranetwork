# Models & Agents for Beginners
> **Imagine starring as the main character in your own AI-made videos without filming a single scene.**
Google just added a feature to its Vids tool that turns you into a digital star using AI avatars. This opens up fun creative projects for anyone with a phone. Today we break down how that works, explore music-making in iMessage, and share quick listens that show AI in unexpected places.
---
### The Big Story
Google Vids now lets users create videos that feature a digital version of themselves as the star. The tool uses Gemini Omni to generate and edit clips from simple text prompts plus reference photos of your face and body. Think of it like those face-swap filters in Snapchat or TikTok, except the AI builds an entire moving scene around your likeness instead of just swapping one static image. You upload a few photos, describe what you want to happen, and the system builds a short video where your avatar walks, talks, or acts out the prompt. This matters because making videos used to require cameras, editing software, and time—now a student could turn a history report into a short film where they appear as a historical figure, or a creator could test ideas without expensive gear. It lowers the barrier for people who want to experiment with storytelling or content creation but lack production skills. For teens especially, it means you can explore video ideas for school projects or social media without needing a film crew or budget. The feature is rolling out now inside Google Vids, so open the app or website, start a new project, and try uploading a selfie plus a prompt like “me explaining photosynthesis in a cartoon style” to see what it builds. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/google-vids-now-lets-you-star-in-your-own-ai-videos/)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you’re watching a short video on your phone, your brain fills in the tiny gaps between frames so the motion looks smooth and natural? AI video tools do something similar but at a much larger scale. First they break the prompt and your reference photos into small pieces of information the model can understand. Next they predict what the next tiny slice of the scene should look like, frame by frame, while keeping your face and movements consistent across every slice. Then they stitch those predictions together and adjust lighting or background details so nothing looks out of place. The whole process is basically a super-powered version of guessing what comes next, except the “next” is an entire moving picture instead of a single word. Because the model has seen millions of real videos during training, it has learned rules about how people walk, how shadows fall, and how expressions change. Once you see it that way, creating a video of yourself becomes less mysterious and more like asking a very well-read friend to draw the next page of a flipbook you started.

### Cool Stuff & Try This
**Make music right inside your texts with Suno**
Suno is an AI music generator that now works directly inside iMessage, so you can turn a voice note or typed idea into a short song without leaving your chat. It listens to a quick description or hummed melody and creates original music with lyrics in whatever style you request. This is exciting because making a custom song used to need instruments, recording equipment, or expensive apps—now it happens in seconds during a normal text conversation. Anyone who likes sharing memes or voice notes with friends should try it. Open iMessage, start a chat with a friend who also has the feature, and type or voice-note something like “a silly rap about forgetting homework” to hear what it creates. You may need a parent’s help if an account signup appears. Source: [engadget.com](https://www.engadget.com/2216558/suno-brings-gen-ai-music-gunk-to-imessage/)
