# Models & Agents for Beginners
> **An AI that keeps working for you even after you close the app just launched.**
Anthropic just released Claude Duet, a new kind of AI helper that runs on its own in the background and checks in only when it needs you. This matters because it moves AI from something you open on purpose to something that can quietly handle parts of your day. We'll also look at how ChatGPT is sliding into your browser, what Meta's Muse is doing with 10 million users, and why Walmart is suddenly friendly to AI shoppers.
---
### The Big Story
Anthropic released a research preview of Claude Duet, an app where an AI agent handles tasks for the user in the background even when the user is offline. The system runs on cloud computers and connects to outside services like Gmail, Slack, and Stripe. It can schedule meetings or prepare billing among other things. The agent always asks for the user's approval before actions like payments or sending messages, and bank transfers are blocked entirely. It is built on Claude Opus 5.5 and comes as a standalone app on iOS, Android, and the web at claude.ai. Pro and Max subscribers in the US can join a waitlist for access, while Team and Enterprise plans are not included yet. Every morning the agent sends a short summary of the most important items from connected apps, then only reaches out again when something needs attention. Users assign tasks by text or voice message in a single ongoing thread that covers scheduling, dashboards, financial forecasts, prototypes, and web apps. The agent works on its own virtual machine with a browser, so the user's laptop or phone does not need to stay powered on. It connects to services like Gmail, Slack, Stripe, Docusign, and Shopify through more than 6,000 connectors and plugins from the Claude Marketplace, letting it track unpaid invoices or get payroll ready. For purchases it uses Stripe Link with a virtual card so the user's real card number is never shared. This setup responds to similar products already released by xAI with Grok Bot in August, Meta with Muse in September, and OpenAI with Dots in September. For a student it could mean an AI that notices a project deadline in a connected calendar and sends a reminder without being asked. For someone running a small side project it could watch for unpaid invoices while the person focuses on the actual work. Right now access is limited to the waitlist for eligible US subscribers, and the agent still requires human approval on any risky step. You can join the waitlist at claude.ai if you have a qualifying plan, or open the regular Claude app today and test how it already handles multi-step tasks like planning a study schedule from a list of deadlines. Source: [the-decoder.com](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-takes-on-grok-bot-metas-muse-and-openais-dots-with-its-always-on-agent-claude-duet/)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you leave a video game running in the background on your console, the game world keeps moving even though you're not touching the controller? The game does not need you staring at the screen. It just keeps simulating the weather, the characters, and the clock on its own. An AI agent works the same way. Instead of waiting for you to open a chat and type a question, the agent gets its own virtual computer in the cloud with a browser and memory of everything you have told it before. It checks your connected apps every few hours, looks for things that need attention, and only sends you a message when it finds something worth your time. That background independence is what makes it different from a normal chatbot that only answers when you poke it. The agent still cannot do anything risky without asking first, because the companies building these tools know one wrong automatic payment could ruin trust fast. So the always-working part is real, but the always-safe part still depends on careful rules written by humans. ### Cool Stuff & Try This
**ChatGPT now lives inside Chrome**
OpenAI added ChatGPT directly into Google Chrome so you can highlight text on any webpage and ask the AI about it without switching tabs. The integration lets the AI see the current page and answer questions about it right where you are browsing. It is useful because you no longer have to copy and paste homework questions or research notes into a separate window. Anyone with Chrome on their laptop or phone can try it right now by installing the official ChatGPT extension from the Chrome Web Store, then clicking the new icon that appears in your toolbar. A fun first experiment is to open a news article, highlight a confusing paragraph, and ask ChatGPT to explain it like you're 14. You can also ask it to pull out the main facts from a long page or turn a product description into a simple comparison list. Source: [techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt-has-a-new-chrome-integration-i-put-it-to-the-test-to-see-if-it-really-makes-browsing-better)

**Meta's Muse hits 10 million users**
Meta's always-on AI app called Muse now has more than 10 million people using it, and shopping is one of the most popular things they do with it. The app can help find products, compare prices, and keep track of what you want to buy later. Users are also turning to it for personal productivity tasks like organizing daily plans. If you're curious about shopping assistants, you can download Muse from the App Store or Google Play and try asking it to find a budget-friendly pair of headphones. You can then ask it to compare two specific models side by side or set a reminder to check prices again in a week. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/meta-says-muse-has-over-10m-users-with-shopping-a-top-activity/)

### Quick Bits
**Walmart welcomes AI shoppers**
Walmart is rolling out tools that let AI agents shop on the site for you, complete with spending limits and approval steps you control. This stands out because Amazon has been blocking similar agents, so Walmart is betting that helpful AI shoppers will bring more customers. The system lets users set spending caps and review purchases before they go through. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1015715/walmart-ai-shopping-agents)
