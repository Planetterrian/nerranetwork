### The Big Story
Imagine typing a question into ChatGPT and realizing you just shared your home address or phone number without thinking. A new free browser extension called agentcloakai stops that from happening by catching those details before your message ever reaches the AI. It uses a tiny 14.7MB model called Rampart from National Design Studio that runs completely inside your browser to spot names, addresses, and other private information. Think of it like a helpful friend who reads your text, swaps in fake but realistic stand-ins, and then swaps the real details back in after the AI answers. The extension swaps the details it catches for realistic stand-ins before sending your prompt, then puts the originals back into the answer. This matters because most of us use AI for school projects, creative writing, or quick questions, and it's easy to accidentally include personal stuff that could be stored or misused later. The extension works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek, and it's completely free to install. Manually removing personal details from every prompt is a ridiculous thing to still be doing. For you personally, it means you can keep experimenting with AI without worrying about what data you're giving away. To try it right now, search for the agentcloakai extension in the Chrome Web Store, add it to your browser, and test it by typing a prompt that includes a made-up name or address — you'll see the swap happen automatically. I think this should be built into every AI chat box. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/LinusEkenstam/status/2101388607147880680)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how your phone can suggest the next word while you're texting without needing to send anything to a faraway computer? Now picture that same idea but for spotting private information. A small AI model can live right inside your browser and quickly scan what you're about to send. It looks for patterns like numbers that look like phone numbers or words that look like names and addresses. Because the model is tiny, it finishes the scan in a split second without slowing anything down. Once it finds something, it replaces the real details with stand-ins that still make sense to the AI on the other end. After the answer comes back, the real details pop back in so you get a useful response without ever sharing the original information. The whole process stays on your device the entire time. The 14.7MB size means the model fits easily in browser memory and runs locally without any internet connection for the scanning step itself.
