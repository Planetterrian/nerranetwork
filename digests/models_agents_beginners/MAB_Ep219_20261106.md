# Models & Agents for Beginners
> **Imagine an AI that plans your whole trip and books it for you like a super-organized friend.**
Airbnb just announced it is building its own AI travel agent that will launch in the first half of 2027. This matters because travel planning is something lots of teens and families do every year, and the new tool is designed to feel more like a helpful guide than a basic chatbot. Today we also look at how Meta is letting people video-chat with customizable AI avatars and explore what AI agents actually do under the hood.

### The Big Story
Airbnb plans to release its own AI travel agent in the first half of 2027. The agent will help users plan trips, compare homes, manage bookings, and get help during their stay. The company shared the details in its Q3 2026 earnings shareholder letter. Think of it like having a travel buddy who already knows your preferences and can pull up maps, photos, and itineraries without you typing long lists of questions. Instead of a regular chat that just answers in text, this agent builds a custom screen with the right visuals at the right moment, almost like a mini travel app that appears instantly. Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky explained that today’s AI agents handle simple tasks such as making a restaurant reservation or scheduling an appointment, yet planning a trip works differently because it is aspirational. The company noted that 80 percent of travelers say they enjoy spending time researching trips, and people want to look at photos, compare homes, and make decisions with the friends or family they are traveling with. The new agent draws from a library of hundreds of travel-specific interface components including maps, photos, comparisons, calendars, and itineraries, then composes them in real time to match what the user is looking for. That matters because general-purpose chatbots are text-heavy, while travel benefits from visual browsing and shared decision making. For a teen planning a school break or family vacation, it could mean fewer tabs open and more time actually looking at cool places. The company says it is making the agent because its experience matching hosts and guests, plus its role in existing reservations, gives it an edge over other AI tools. You will not be able to try it yet, but you can follow Airbnb’s updates on their site or app to see early previews when they appear. The announcement also described the agent as having a personalized interface built specifically for travel that responds to what guests are looking for by offering the right interface at the right time. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028412/airbnb-ai-travel-agent-q3-2026-earnings)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you are texting a friend about weekend plans, your phone sometimes guesses the next word or suggests a whole reply based on what you just said? Now picture that same idea but stretched across many steps: the AI does not just guess one word, it keeps track of your whole conversation and decides what task to do next, like pulling up a map or suggesting three places that match what you described. It can also remember earlier details you mentioned, such as “I like quiet spots,” and use that memory later without you repeating yourself. The system works by breaking big goals into smaller actions it can handle one at a time, checking what it already knows and what it still needs to ask or look up. Over time it gets better at choosing the right action because it has seen thousands of similar conversations before. In the case of travel planning, the agent might first show photos of homes, then switch to a calendar view for dates, then create an itinerary that includes both, all without the user having to switch between different apps. That is basically how an AI agent stays helpful instead of just giving one quick answer and stopping.

### Cool Stuff & Try This
**Video chat with your own AI avatar**
Meta now lets users have real-time video conversations with a customizable animated character called Muse. The avatar can show expressions and respond with voice, making the chat feel more like talking to a friend than typing to a screen. It is fun for anyone who likes creative role-play or just wants a more natural way to practice conversations. The feature rolled out first to the Verge writer on the iOS app, where the avatar appears at the top of the screen during the call. You can try it right now in the Meta AI app on iOS by starting a voice chat and choosing the avatar option. Ask it to act like a character from your favorite show or help you brainstorm a story idea while you watch its reactions on screen. The expressive avatar makes the interaction feel more alive because it reacts in real time to what you say. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028015/meta-muse-ai-voice-mode-expressive-avatar)

**Publish AI music straight to SoundCloud**
ElevenLabs and SoundCloud teamed up so you can create music with AI and post it directly to the platform without downloading files first. SoundCloud Artist Pro subscribers even get three months of free ElevenLabs access as part of the deal. This is exciting if you make beats or songs for fun and want to share them quickly with friends. The integration removes the extra step of exporting audio and uploading it separately. Go to ElevenLabs, generate a short track, and look for the new SoundCloud publish button to test it out. Once published, the track appears on your SoundCloud profile right away, ready for others to hear. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027427/ai-music-soundcloud-elevenlabs)
