You know that autocomplete on your phone, the gray text that tries to finish your sentence before you do.

A chatbot is that same trick, grown enormous. It guesses the next word, then the next, until a whole reply appears.

During training it swallowed a huge pile of text from the open internet, and it learned the patterns of how people usually talk.
