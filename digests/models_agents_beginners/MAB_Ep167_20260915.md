# Models & Agents for Beginners
> **Google just built an AI that notices your friend’s birthday and suggests a perfect gift from your own photos and calendar.**
Dreambeans is Google’s new opt-in AI that safely pulls together info from your Gmail, Calendar, Search, and Photos to create short personalized “stories” just for you. Instead of scrolling random feeds, you get one daily notification with useful, custom ideas like gift suggestions or movie recommendations. Today we’ll unpack how it works, explore what this kind of personal AI actually means for everyday life, and check out a few other beginner-friendly updates you can try right now.
---
### The Big Story
Google Labs just launched Dreambeans, a new AI experience that connects the different parts of your digital life in one place. The system gathers details from connected sources including Gmail, Calendar, Search, GeminiApp, and Google Photos through face grouping. It might notice your friend Beth’s birthday is coming up, generate a one-of-a-kind illustrated story of the two of you, and surface the perfect gift ideas. Think of it like having a really thoughtful friend who remembers small details about you and your people without you having to repeat yourself. Dreambeans looks across your Gmail, Calendar, Search history, Gemini chats, and Google Photos (including face grouping) so it can notice things like an upcoming birthday and then create a short illustrated story about that person plus gift ideas. The stories arrive as a single daily notification inside the app instead of an endless feed. When one catches your eye you tap it to see more details and direct links, whether that’s a movie trailer or a place to buy the suggested gift. Everything is opt-in, and you stay in control: a thumbs-down button lets you tell the system when a suggestion misses the mark so it learns what actually matters to you. This experience is protected by strict privacy filters that keep your data from being used without your permission. For students and teens this could mean less time hunting for ideas and more time getting helpful nudges that feel personal. For parents it offers a way to keep family moments organized without extra apps. The biggest shift is that your own data starts working for you in one spot instead of staying locked inside separate services. You can try the first version today by following the link in Google’s announcement on X and signing up through the Dreambeans waitlist or app when it opens to you. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/GoogleAI/status/2099489445846126676)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how your phone already knows your next dentist appointment because the calendar app and the reminder app talk to each other? Now picture that same idea but bigger: instead of just two apps, imagine a system that can safely peek into your email, your photos, your search history, and your chats all at once. It looks for patterns that actually matter to you, like noticing your friend’s birthday is coming up and remembering you took a fun photo together last year. The system then turns those connected details into a short illustrated story and a couple of gift suggestions. The key part is that it only does this when you’ve said yes, and it shows you exactly what it used so you can correct it if something feels off. That’s basically what Dreambeans is doing under the hood: it’s not magic, it’s just letting different pieces of your own information work together in one place while keeping you in charge of the whole thing. The process starts with you turning the feature on, then the AI quietly checks the connected apps for matching dates or names, builds the story tile, and waits for your tap before showing links or more options.

### Cool Stuff & Try This
**New Siri just got its own app in iOS 27**
Apple released iOS 27 today with a dedicated Siri app, fresh icons, and separate volume controls for system sounds and alarms. The update also brings big performance improvements that make the phone feel faster overall. The update works on phones all the way back to the iPhone 11, so most people can try it right away. If you have an iPhone, open Settings, go to General, then Software Update, and install iOS 27 when it appears. Once it’s on, open the new Siri app and ask it something simple like “remind me to text my friend about the weekend” to see how the refreshed experience feels. You can also test the separate alarm volume by setting a quiet alarm and adjusting the system sounds independently to see the difference in daily use. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/MKBHD/status/2099596577857831174)
