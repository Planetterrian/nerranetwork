> **# Models & Agents for Beginners**
A teen just built a full 100-page comic using only ChatGPT on a cheap Android phone.
One creator spent months figuring out how to make ChatGPT draw an entire comic with the same characters and style every time. The trick turns AI from a random doodler into a reliable storytelling partner. Today we break down how that works, explore what makes AI voices feel more natural, and share tools you can try right now on your phone.
---
### The Big Story
A Reddit user spent months testing whether ChatGPT could create a consistent 100-page comic called Tinky & Bocca, a post-apocalyptic road story. Instead of generating single panels that drift in style, the creator switched to one simple rule: each prompt produces an entire finished page at once, using the same character and environment references every time.

Think of it like giving an artist the same reference sheet and lighting notes before every new drawing. The AI now keeps faces, clothes, and backgrounds looking like they belong in the same world across dozens of pages. This matters because most people who try AI art get frustrated when characters suddenly change hair or backgrounds turn into different places. Solving that problem opens the door for anyone to tell longer visual stories without needing years of drawing practice.

For a student who loves comics or graphic novels but doesn’t have art classes at school, this changes what’s possible on a regular phone. You could create a short story for a class project or just for fun and share it with friends. The creator’s goal is a full 100-page book made entirely on a $99 Android phone, proving you don’t need expensive gear.

You can try the same approach yourself. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer, describe a character in detail once, then ask it to generate full comic pages while reminding it to keep that exact character and setting. Start small with a three-page scene and see how consistent it stays. Source: [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1v5tkcl/i_spent_months_testing_whether_chatgpt_can_create/)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you’re texting with the same group of friends every day, your phone starts remembering the way you usually talk and suggests words that sound like you?  

Now picture the same idea but with pictures instead of words. The AI doesn’t just guess the next sentence — it keeps a running list of every detail you’ve already shown it about a character’s face, clothes, and the world around them. Each new page prompt includes those details again, so the model treats them like fixed rules instead of starting fresh every time.  

That list of details works like a shared notebook the AI checks before drawing anything new. When the notebook stays the same across prompts, the characters stop changing randomly and the story feels like one connected world. The real magic is that the AI isn’t “remembering” like a person — it’s just getting the same instructions repeated so it follows the same pattern.  

Once you see it that way, keeping a comic consistent stops feeling like magic and starts feeling like careful note-taking.
