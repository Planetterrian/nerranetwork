Sesame just launched its conversational AI app on iOS, bringing the kind of natural back-and-forth chat that usually only happens in science-fiction movies straight to your phone. ### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you’re texting a friend, your phone sometimes guesses the next word before you even finish typing? Now imagine that same autocomplete could also remember everything you’ve said in the last few minutes and use that memory to guess not just one word, but a whole helpful reply. The AI listens to your voice or text, breaks it into small chunks it understands, checks what you talked about earlier in the same chat, and then builds an answer that feels like it’s actually paying attention. It keeps a short “conversation history” the same way you remember the last few messages in a group chat, so it doesn’t repeat itself or forget what you just asked. When the AI gets better at this, the replies stop feeling like random guesses and start feeling like talking with someone who knows the whole story so far. That’s basically what a conversational AI agent does when it holds a natural back-and-forth instead of resetting after every single message. So next time someone says “conversational AI,” you can tell them—it’s autocomplete that remembers the whole chat, not just the next word. Not so scary, right?
