> **AI can now turn your class notes into a 60-second TikTok-style video in seconds.**
Google just added a feature to NotebookLM that creates short vertical videos from whatever sources you upload. This matters because it makes turning research or ideas into shareable clips feel effortless. Today we’ll also look at an AI keyboard that works across your phone apps and a privacy-first chatbot getting new tools.

### The Big Story
Google added a new trick to its NotebookLM tool that turns your uploaded notes, documents, or research into 60-second vertical videos styled like TikTok clips. 

Think of it like having a friend who takes your messy stack of school notes or a long article and instantly edits them into a fast-paced, narrated mini-movie with visuals and text that pops on screen. The example Google shared shows how it handled a story about Australia’s war efforts by pulling out key moments and turning them into something you could watch in under a minute. 

This changes how students and creators can work because instead of spending hours making slides or editing clips for a project or social post, you can get a ready-to-share summary video almost immediately. It connects directly to things like homework presentations, YouTube ideas, or even quick recaps of books you’re reading for class. 

For you personally, it means your phone or laptop can help turn boring research into something fun and visual without needing video editing skills. The feature is rolling out now to people who already pay for Google’s AI Ultra or Pro plans. 

To try it, go to notebooklm.google.com, upload a few sources like a PDF or web article, and look for the new option to generate a vertical AI clip—start with something short like your favorite game’s backstory and see what it creates. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/959778/google-notebooklm-ai-clips)

### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you’re scrolling TikTok, the app already knows which short clips will keep you watching by picking out the most interesting parts from longer videos? 

Now picture that same idea but with your own notes instead of videos. First the system reads everything you upload and breaks it into small chunks, like sentences or facts. Then it decides which chunks are the most important or surprising, almost like choosing the best highlights for a sports recap. 

Next it matches those highlights with simple visuals and a voice that reads them out loud, lining everything up so it flows in 60 seconds. The whole process is like turning a long book report into a movie trailer that still tells the main story. 

That’s what NotebookLM is doing when it makes these clips—it’s not inventing new information, it’s just smartly picking and arranging what you already gave it.

### Cool Stuff & Try This
**AI that lives right on your phone’s keyboard**
Acti built a keyboard app for iOS and Android that lets you create custom AI shortcuts using normal words instead of code. It works inside any app you type in, so you could ask it to rewrite a message or pull up quick facts without switching screens. This is exciting because it puts helpful AI one tap away during everyday texting or note-taking. 

Anyone with a smartphone can try it. Download the Acti keyboard from the App Store or Google Play, then type a natural sentence like “make this sound more friendly” to build your first shortcut and test it in Messages or Notes. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/acti-puts-ai-agents-directly-into-your-smartphone-keyboard/)

**A chatbot that keeps your chats private**
Proton’s Lumo 2.0 is an updated version of their privacy-focused AI that adds more capabilities while promising not to store or sell your conversations. It’s useful if you want to ask questions about schoolwork or personal projects without worrying about data being saved elsewhere. 

You can try the upgrade this week by visiting Proton’s site and looking for Lumo—start by asking it to explain a topic you’re studying and notice how it handles the request without needing extra sign-ups beyond a basic account. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/lumo-protons-privacy-focused-ai-chatbot-gets-an-upgrade/)
