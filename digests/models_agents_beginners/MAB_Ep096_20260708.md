### Explain Like I'm 14
You know how when you're texting with different friends, each one replies in their own style? One friend might send short, quick answers and keep the conversation bouncing back and forth, while another friend takes a little longer but comes back with a whole thoughtful paragraph that moves the idea forward on its own. AI models are starting to work the same way. Some are built to stay right with you, reacting fast to every little change in what you say so you can figure things out together. Others are designed to take a clear goal you give them and then keep working steadily in the background without needing constant check-ins. The difference comes from how each model was trained on huge amounts of examples and how its instructions guide it to either stay collaborative or push ahead independently. When you switch between them, it's like choosing which friend to brainstorm with depending on whether you want fast back-and-forth energy or someone who will just handle the whole thing once you explain what you need. That choice is becoming part of using AI every day.

### Cool Stuff & Try This
**Claude Cowork now works on your phone too**
Anthropic has expanded its Claude Cowork AI agent so it runs on both mobile phones and web browsers instead of only the desktop app. This agent can keep working on tasks even after you close your laptop, then send you a notification on your phone when it needs you to make a decision or approve the next step. It's useful for anyone who wants an AI helper that doesn't stop just because you're switching devices or heading out. You can try it by going to the Claude app on your phone or visiting claude.ai in a browser and looking for the Cowork feature if it's available in your region. A fun first experiment is to give it a longer creative task like "help me plan a weekend story project with three characters and keep working until you have a full outline," then watch what happens when you step away and come back later. Source: [the-decoder.com](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-claude-cowork-ai-agent-is-now-available-on-mobile-and-web/)

### Quick Bits
**Meta's Muse Image model pulls from your Instagram**
Meta's new Muse Image model now lets people generate AI pictures using public Instagram accounts as part of the prompt, and the same tool is rolling out inside Instagram Stories and WhatsApp too. It means your photos could end up helping create new images for other users unless you choose to opt out in your settings. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/962485/meta-muse-image-ai-model-instagram)

**Solos makes even lighter AI glasses**
Solos released a new version of its AirGo smart glasses that weigh only about 19 grams and skip cameras completely, relying instead on voice for its built-in AI assistant. The lighter design makes them feel more like regular glasses while still giving you hands-free help with questions and tasks throughout the day. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/961711/solos-airgo-a6-smart-glasses-ai-assistant-privacy)
