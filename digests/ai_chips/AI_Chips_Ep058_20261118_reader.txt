Release film keeps hot epoxy off the compression mold, and if you take it apart as a process step, BigGo Finance’s account of Suregiant shows why the sheet, not the resin pump, decides whether the next package is clean. In IC packaging the mold presses hot resin around the die and the substrate. Resin that wets the steel contaminates the following shot and can tear the part on the way out.

Chairman Yang Yun-pin described the film as the isolator under high temperature and high pressure, the layer that lets the package leave the tool without residue.

Yang said Japanese rivals mostly extrude that film. Suregiant coats it, using more than twenty-five years of formulation work, from an optoelectronics business that entered semiconductors in twenty fourteen. Coating is how you cut a sample for one customer’s automated line.

The line scores three things: the sheet must not stick, it must leave no adhesive, and the release force has to match the tool. An extruded film can hit the same force number and still fail if the surface chemistry is not the one that mold and resin were qualified on. The custom sample is the product.

The mold face changed with the package. Suregiant’s film went into mass production for two-point-five-D advanced packaging in twenty twenty-four, into panel-level fan-out in twenty twenty-five, and toward three-D packaging in twenty twenty-six. Two-point-five-D is a wide, flatter compression. Fan-out on a panel moves more resin across a larger area.

A three-D stack adds height and more places for residue to hide. A peel test can pass and an automated camera can still reject the part. Suregiant says it is qualified at the world’s top five semiconductor manufacturers, with film already running in first-tier advanced packaging.

Orders are visible for two to three years, and customer forecasts run five to six years, so the coating line is booked to a package roadmap.

Plant one is full. Taoyuan Plant two finished trial production and is expected to start official output in December, at two and a half to three times Plant one, with a full year of contribution in twenty twenty-seven.
