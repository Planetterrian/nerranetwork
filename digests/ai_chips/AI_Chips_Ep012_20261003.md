**Semiconductor Quantum-Dot Resonator for Identical Photons: Semiconductor Digest**
Researchers at Paderborn University, the University of Basel and Ruhr University Bochum report in Physical Review Letters that semiconductor quantum dots in an optical resonator can emit individual photons and photon pairs that are almost perfectly identical. Lead author Timon Baltisberger, of Basel, describes a biexciton cascade: the dot is doubly excited, the excitation decays, and two photons come out one after the other. Stefan Schumacher, at Paderborn, says placing the dot in a laser-like cavity sped up and controlled that emission. Those indistinguishable photons are the particles the group ties to quantum entanglement and quantum interference. ### The Teardown: One Cache Die, or Two
A 64 MB SRAM die bonded on top of a compute chiplet is the mechanism in AMD’s 3D V-Cache. The stack puts extra last-level memory on the vertical path, so a core that keeps hitting the same working set can stay on the package instead of going out to DRAM. The bond is also a lid. The chiplet under the cache die has to shed heat through that extra silicon and live with the voltage the stack will accept, and that is what shows up as a lower boost clock when both chiplets are covered.

TechPowerUp’s write-up of a Chiphell post is the measurement set. An engineering sample of the never-released Ryzen 9 5900X3D, code 100-000000652, reports 128 MB of L3, split as 96 MB on one CCD and 32 MB on the other. Only one of the two six-core chiplets carries the 64 MB stack. CPU-Z screenshots from the owner show the part around 4.7 GHz with a 105 W TDP. The dual-stack 5900X3D prototypes AMD showed Gamers Nexus in 2023 had cache on both CCDs, 192 MB in total, and topped out at a 4.4 GHz boost. The sample would not boot on current BIOS versions and started only with older firmware carrying SMU 56.70.0. AMD has not confirmed the part.

Retail Zen 3 X3D began with the eight-core Ryzen 7 5800X3D in 2022 and never included a dual-CCD chip. The single-stack layout is the one AMD later used for Ryzen 9 X3D chips on AM5, before the Ryzen 9 9950X3D2 Dual Edition put cache on both chiplets this year. Cover both chiplets and the capacity figure is the one that moves. Leave one CCD bare and the clock figure is the one that moves.
