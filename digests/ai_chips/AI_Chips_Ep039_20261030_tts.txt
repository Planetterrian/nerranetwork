Night Owl is the worked case, from a stretch when TSMC was behind on process technology.

The internal theory was that a research team on twenty-four-hour days, against U S competitors on eight-hour days, would hold a time advantage and overtake them.

Twenty-four divided by eight is three, so three times the staffed hours was supposed to mean three times the learning rate.

Engineers were encouraged onto night development so the organization never closed.

The missing term was decision quality per hour. A tired team on a bad split converges on noise, and the morning shift spends the next day unwinding it.
