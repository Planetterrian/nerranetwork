A probe card is the last removable interconnect a wafer sees before dice are committed to a package. Probes land on pads or microbumps, feed power and patterns, and map which dice are worth stacking, and a larger logic die, a taller high-bandwidth memory stack, or a two-and-a-half-D or three-D assembly adds pads, force, and pattern length even when wafer counts do not.

Yahoo Finance, following the tooling layer rather than a single G P U vendor, puts FormFactor on that test chokepoint and ACM Research on the plating tools behind the package.
