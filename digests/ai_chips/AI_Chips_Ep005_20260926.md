### Top Story
Focus Taiwan reports that Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs has approved TSMC applications to invest US$44 billion in the United States since December 2020, while the chipmaker pursues a US$265 billion investment plan in Arizona. The ministry's Department of Investment Review has cleared seven applications: US$30.001 million in December 2020 to establish TSMC Arizona, then US$3.47 billion that same month, US$3.5 billion in March 2023, US$4.5 billion in September 2023, US$5.0 billion in June 2024, US$7.5 billion in September 2024, and US$20 billion in July, each to raise capital in the Arizona subsidiary. In March 2025 TSMC announced an additional US$100 billion on top of US$65 billion previously announced, to take advanced wafer fabs in Arizona from three to six and to build two advanced IC assembly plants and a research and development center. At a mid-July investor conference, chairman C.C. Wei said that additional US$100 billion would build four more wafer fabs and expand IC packaging capacity. In July the company raised its 2026 capital-expenditure forecast to US$60 billion–US$64 billion, from US$52 billion–US$56 billion set out in mid-April, and the ministry expects further approval filings at this pace. TSMC operates 19 advanced wafer fabs and IC assembly plants in Taiwan and is building 13 more fabs and packaging facilities in Hsinchu, Taichung, Chiayi, Tainan and Kaohsiung; the ministry's stated priorities are that the largest capacity, the most advanced technologies, and the most complete ecosystem stay in Taiwan. Source: [focustaiwan.tw](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202609260007)

### Silicon
**Intel Expects 14A Node to Be Within 5% of TSMC's A14:** TechPowerUp
TechPowerUp reports that Intel Foundry chief Naga Chandrasekaran told KeyBanc Capital Markets Intel expects its 14A node to land within 5 percent of TSMC's A14, without saying whether that gap is performance, power, density, an advantage, a disadvantage, or a mix. Intel 18A is the node that is shipping. 14A risk production is planned for the second half of 2027, with high-volume production expected in 2028, after tens of thousands of 14A pilot wafers and further yield work plus minor node adjustments before risk production next year. Against Intel 18A, 14A is described as a 15 to 20 percent performance gain at the same power, or a 25 to 35 percent power cut at the same performance, with up to a 30 percent density gain, and the report does not say whether that density figure is logic or SRAM. Source: [techpowerup.com](https://www.techpowerup.com/353094/intel-expects-14a-node-to-be-within-5-of-tsmcs-a14)

### The Teardown: The Row CDU Still Sets the Hall
An in-row coolant distribution unit takes heat off a secondary loop at the end of the aisle, so the cold plate on the package is not the building heat exchanger. StorageReview reports Supermicro NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 racks now shipping with 1.8-megawatt in-row CDUs and a 1,152-GPU scalable unit. That 1.8 megawatts is the thermal duty the row exchanger is built to move continuously. The 1,152-GPU count is the failure domain: pumps, valves, manifolds, and power for that many accelerators sit behind one scalable-unit boundary, so a flow fault is a row event. A CDU at that rating is plant equipment. Quick-disconnects, heat exchangers, and redundant pumps have to be qualified as a mechanical system before the hall accepts the IT load. AMD, in a separate validation note, describes a double-wide rack at 8,000 pounds lifted onto a vibration test while Helios is checked for real-world handling. Liquid manifolds and a chassis that wide add mass; the shaker is how joints and connectors are shown to survive lift and floor vibration before coolant is trusted in the row. Pluang reports, which is the demand pulling these row designs into shipping racks rather than lab slides. Chip-level efficiency does not retire the row plant by itself. The tradeoff flips only when the GPU count inside the scalable unit drops, or when heat per GPU falls far enough that a smaller CDU can cover the same row. Until one of those two moves, pipes, pumps, and floor loading are sized to the 1.8-megawatt row and the 8,000-pound lift.
