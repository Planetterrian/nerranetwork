A dead link, a power fault or a cooling fault takes that wafer out of the chain.

The scheduler then shrinks the job to the wafers still answering, or it waits.

Delivery asks whether the wafers are on the floor. Online use asks whether they are linked and scheduled together.

A spare counts only if it is already cabled, cooled and in the address map.
