### Top 15 Science & Health Discoveries
1. **Over 1000 genetic switches differ in female immune cells — r/science**
   Researchers mapped regulatory elements active in immune cells and found more than 1000 that operate differently between women and men. The differences align with the well-documented higher incidence of autoimmune conditions in women and point to sex-specific immune regulation as a core biological feature rather than a secondary effect. Source: [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/science/comments/1t9ngzn/study_uncovers_more_than_1000_genetic_switches/)

2. **Thymus function carries lasting consequences into adulthood — r/science**
   New analysis shows that thymic health continues to shape immune competence well beyond childhood. Reduced thymic output in adults correlates with weaker responses to new infections and vaccines, underscoring the organ's ongoing role in maintaining immune diversity. Source: [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/science/comments/1t9rlue/thymic_health_consequences_in_adults/)

3. **Jellyfish bycatch offers sustainable collagen source — Phys.org**
   Researchers at the Catholic University of Valencia are exploring collagen extracted from jellyfish caught as fishing bycatch. The material shows promise for cosmetics and biomedical applications, turning an underused marine resource into a lower-impact alternative to traditional collagen sources. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-qa-jellyfish-bycatch-valuable-collagen.html)

4. **Liver-targeted gene therapy clears brain amyloid in mice — Fierce Biotech**
   Delivery of a protective gene to the liver reduced amyloid plaques in the brains of Alzheimer's model mice without the therapy needing to cross the blood-brain barrier. The approach suggests a peripheral route to addressing central protein accumulation that has long challenged direct brain delivery. Source: [fiercebiotech.com](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/research/protective-gene-sent-liver-cleared-amyloid-mouse-brains-sparking-hope-alzheimers-gene)

5. **New antibodies block measles cell entry — Fierce Biotech**
   Scientists isolated antibodies from vaccinated individuals that prevent the measles virus from penetrating host cells. The findings open a path to therapeutic antibodies that could complement vaccination during outbreaks of the resurgent disease. Source: [fiercebiotech.com](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/research/antibodies-stop-measles-stabbing-cells-could-become-new-treatments-surging-virus-study)

6. **Single phonon couples to atomic spin in quantum experiment — Phys.org**
   Engineers at Harvard SEAS demonstrated the first interaction between a single quantum of vibrational energy and a single atomic spin. The result establishes a foundation for using sound waves, rather than light or electricity, to carry quantum information in future devices. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-good-vibrations-quantum-communications-couple.html)

### Science Deep Dive: Sex Differences in Immune Regulation
Most people assume the immune system operates the same way in everyone, with only minor variations from person to person. In reality, more than a thousand genetic switches already behave differently in female immune cells compared with male ones. Right now, as you sit reading this, those switches are shaping how your body recognizes and responds to threats in ways that differ by sex. One memorable figure is the count itself: over 1000 distinct regulatory elements show sex-biased activity, a scale large enough to influence disease risk across entire populations. The next time you hear about autoimmune conditions or vaccine responses, remember that these differences begin at the level of gene control rather than emerging only after symptoms appear. Tracking how these switches respond to nutrition, sleep, or environmental exposures offers a concrete way to watch for new findings that could refine prevention strategies for both women and men.
