3. **First Baseline Data Established for Pediatric Deep Fascia Thickness — Charles University**
   Researchers measured deep fascia thickness in healthy children's legs using ultrasound. These reference values provide the initial standard for comparison in clinical settings. The data support non-invasive diagnosis of musculoskeletal conditions. Doctors can now identify deviations earlier without invasive procedures. Validation across larger age groups is planned next.

   2. **Blue and fin whale sightings rise off southern Africa — Phys.org**
   More than 60 years of records from Namibia and South Africa’s west coast show a clear increase in confirmed sightings and strandings. Ninety-five percent of observations occurred after 2012. Both species remain at low absolute numbers decades after commercial whaling ended. The data come from the African Journal of Marine Science compilation. Continued monitoring will track whether the trend reflects population recovery or shifts in distribution.

3. **Imaging ellipsometry checks MXene films without damage — Phys.org**

The technique provides multi-scale data on uniformity and integrity during fabrication. It covers critical steps including lithography without contacting or altering the material.
