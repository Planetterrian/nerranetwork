From human development we turn to climate biology.

Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria and Deakin University researchers tested germination of Victorian alpine species under simulated future climates.

Seeds failed to sprout when winter chilling or spring warming cues were altered, even when moisture remained adequate.

The findings indicate that warming temperatures may desynchronize the seasonal signals these plants rely on.

Several species showed narrow thermal windows, raising extinction risk for high-elevation populations.

Seed-bank managers are now testing whether assisted migration or cue manipulation can improve recruitment.
