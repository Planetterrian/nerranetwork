### Top 15 Science & Health Discoveries
1. **Shared Genes Enable Limb Regrowth Across Species — Science Daily**
   Scientists studying axolotls, zebrafish, and mice pinpointed “SP genes” essential for proper bone regeneration. Disabling these genes halted regrowth, while a zebrafish-inspired gene therapy partially restored the process in mice. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260508003121.htm)

2. **Cycling Exergaming Improves Fitness in Adolescents — r/science**
   A randomised trial tested access to a cycling exergaming platform and measured changes in cardiorespiratory fitness and cardiometabolic markers. The setup delivered measurable gains in health indicators among teens. Source: [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/science/comments/1t7wuij/effects_of_providing_access_to_a_cycling/)

3. **Antibodies Block Measles Cell Entry, Suggesting New Therapies — Fierce Biotech**
   Researchers mined immune responses from vaccinated individuals to isolate antibodies that prevent the measles virus from penetrating cells. The findings open a path to treatments for a resurging disease beyond existing vaccines. Source: [fiercebiotech.com](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/research/antibodies-stop-measles-stabbing-cells-could-become-new-treatments-surging-virus-study)

4. **Liver-Targeted Gene Therapy Clears Amyloid in Mouse Brains — Fierce Biotech**
   A protective gene delivered to the liver reduced amyloid plaques in the brains of mice despite the blood-brain barrier. The one-time approach suggests gene therapy could address Alzheimer’s without direct brain delivery. Source: [fiercebiotech.com](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/research/protective-gene-sent-liver-cleared-amyloid-mouse-brains-sparking-hope-alzheimers-gene)

5. **Repurposed Medical Device Counts Microscopic Particles for Climate Research — Phys.org**
   Scientists adapted a clinical particle-analysis tool to sort and identify thousands of tiny grains in sediment samples. The method improves precision when reconstructing ancient climate shifts from microscopic records. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-repurposed-medical-device-ancient-climate.html)

### Science Deep Dive: Why Mammals Can Regenerate More Than We Assume
Most people picture limb regrowth as a salamander-only trick that mammals abandoned long ago. In reality, mice already possess fragments of the same genetic toolkit, and the recent work simply shows how to switch it back on. Right now, as you sit reading this, your own cells are running related repair programs after every minor cut or bone fracture. The key difference is that full limb regrowth requires coordinated activation of the SP genes across many cell types at once. A single nerve signal travels at roughly 120 metres per second during these repair cascades—fast enough to synchronise distant tissues. The practical takeaway is to watch for early human trials that test whether brief gene-therapy pulses can nudge existing repair pathways toward larger-scale regeneration rather than scar formation.
