1. **From Ep 60 (2026-05-11): Over 1000 genetic switches differ in female immune cells — r/science**
   2. **Thymus function carries lasting consequences into adulthood — r/science**
   New analysis shows that thymic health continues to shape immune competence well beyond childhood. Reduced thymic output in adults correlates with weaker responses to new infections and vaccines, underscoring the organ's ongoing role in maintaining immune diversity. Source: [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/science/comments/1t9rlue/thymic_health_consequences_in_adults/)

3. **Jellyfish bycatch offers sustainable collagen source — Phys.org**
   Researchers at the Catholic University of Valencia are exploring collagen extracted from jellyfish caught as fishing bycatch. The material shows promise for cosmetics and biomedical applications, turning an underused marine resource into a lower-impact alternative to traditional collagen sources. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-qa-jellyfish-bycatch-valuable-collagen.html)

2. **From Ep 61 (2026-05-12): Fossil reanalysis overturns early animal life assumptions — Science Daily**
   2. **Statistical patterns in molecules flag potential alien life — Science Daily**
   Researchers identified a consistent chemical fingerprint in the distribution of amino acids and fatty acids that living systems produce but nonliving chemistry does not. The approach offers a new detection method for future planetary missions without relying on specific known biomolecules. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260511213146.htm)

3. **Hubble captures lopsided planet-forming disk — Science Daily**
   The telescope imaged a massive, turbulent disk nicknamed “Dracula’s Chivito” with material concentrated on one side and towering filaments. The structure holds enough mass to form multiple giant planets and provides a new case study for disk dynamics. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260511213151.htm)

3. **From Ep 62 (2026-05-13): Plant Enzymes Decoded for Rare Anti-Cancer Compound — Science Daily**
   2. **Enteroviruses Share a Common Replication Weak Spot — Science Daily**
   University of Maryland researchers mapped how enteroviruses, including polio and common-cold strains, recruit both viral and host proteins to copy their RNA. The detailed mechanism functions like a molecular switch that decides between replication and protein production. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260512202320.htm)

3. **New Approach Targets Senescent Cells in Cancer and Aging — Science Daily**
   Researchers showed that senescent “zombie” cells surviving chemotherapy rely on the protein GPX4 to avoid iron-triggered death. Removing this protection caused the cells to self-destruct, shrinking tumors and extending survival in mouse models. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260509210646.htm)

4. **From Ep 63 (2026-05-14): Hidden geometric pattern found in money plant leaves — Science Daily**
   2. **One in four World Cup matches could face extreme heat — Phys.org**
   Climate change has raised the risk of dangerous temperatures for games across North America compared with the 1994 tournament. Researchers calculated that roughly 25 percent of matches in 2026 could occur under conditions that threaten player safety. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-world-cup-games-dangerous-north.html)

3. **Carbon dioxide cools the stratosphere through specific wavelengths — Science Daily**
   Columbia University researchers found that rising CO2 levels in the upper atmosphere radiate heat into space more effectively at certain infrared wavelengths. This mechanism explains why the stratosphere is cooling even as the surface warms. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260513221759.htm)
