Now, here's something most people get wrong.

Most people assume exercise is the main driver for dropping pounds in the first place.

In reality, its stronger documented role is keeping weight off once it is lost.

Right now, as you move through daily activities, muscle tissue you maintain through regular movement continues to burn calories at a higher rate than fat tissue would.

Studies tracking participants after weight reduction show that those who stay active regain less because preserved muscle supports resting metabolism and improves how the body handles incoming energy.
