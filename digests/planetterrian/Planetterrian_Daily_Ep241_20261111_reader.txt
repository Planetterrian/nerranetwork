A Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research model, covered by Phys.org, integrated the economy, energy systems, land use and climate to test pathways that hold warming to one point seven degrees Celsius.

Leaving land unused produced a three point five percent consumption loss from twenty twenty-five through twenty one hundred, while adding every feasible land measure brought the carbon price down to one hundred thirty dollars per ton of carbon dioxide and the consumption loss to one point three percent.

Biomass land demand ranged from sixty-six million hectares to more than three hundred million hectares depending on the policy mix, and that potential is concentrated in the Global South, where food, water and biodiversity pressures already exist.

A balanced package of residual biomass, capped methane and nitrous oxide pricing, and global forest conservation held the consumption loss to one point five percent.

Lead author Leon Merfort stated that a transformation in the land sector allows a slower energy transition to reach the same climate target.

Adults with overweight or obesity taking tirzepatide were randomized in a Nature-reported trial to resistance exercise or usual activity.

One hundred twenty participants followed the protocol for forty weeks, with the exercise arm training three times a week.

Both groups lost similar total weight and fat mass, yet the exercise group lost one point zero nine kilograms less total lean soft tissue mass and showed gains rather than losses in upper- and lower-body strength.

Adherence to the exercise protocol reached ninety-one percent, and the trial found resistance training can sit alongside incretin-based weight loss as a practical adjunct that preserves muscle function.

Polymetallic nodules in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone were dissected by MARUM and NIOZ teams after collection by box corers and diving robots.

Two hundred forty-four nodules yielded four thousand one hundred thirty-nine individual animals living inside cracks and pores, averaging seventeen per nodule and reaching two hundred forty-five in one case.

Genetic analyses showed the nodule communities were similar across distances up to one thousand kilometers yet differed markedly from surrounding sediment communities.

Roundworm abundance inside nodules was about ten percent of sediment levels, and copepod abundance about twenty-five percent.

The single-celled species Thurammina species one was especially common inside nodules and appears adapted to the sheltered spaces, while the nodules themselves grow only a few millimeters per million years at depths of four thousand to six thousand meters.

Sabine Gollner noted that commercial mining may result in irreversible loss of undescribed biodiversity associated with nodules.

Most people assume every mosquito species keys off the same human scent, so anyone who gets bitten often would draw them all in equal measure.

In reality, according to ScienceDaily, each species is tuned to its own narrow combination of skin odors, chemicals, and surface bacteria rather than a single universal human signal, and that tuning decides which mosquitoes find you on a given evening because the blend on your skin matches some species better than others.

The work documented those distinct profiles across multiple species and showed that the differences hold even when the same people are tested again and again, which undercuts the idea of one type of person who draws every mosquito equally.

Broad-spectrum repellents may therefore miss the specific signals used by the mosquitoes that carry particular diseases.

Refining repellents around the exact compounds each target species detects could improve protection without raising the overall chemical load, and the next step the team identifies is mapping which bacterial or chemical signals matter most for the species that transmit malaria or dengue so new formulations can be tested against those precise cues.

Germany's temporary value-added tax reduction from nineteen percent to sixteen percent during COVID, measured in the Review of Economic Studies, combined survey data, retail purchase records, and an economic model to track what households actually spent.

Private consumption rose, and the largest increases came from lower-wealth households and from younger and middle-aged households.

Those gains appeared regardless of financial literacy.

The first in-depth assessment of coyotes on North Carolina barrier islands found populations still present despite removal efforts.

That ongoing presence continues to threaten shore-nesting birds and protected sea turtles.

Northwestern University researchers tested the DRIVEN app, which pairs weekly self-guided mental health exercises with job-seeking tools, in a six-week clinical trial aimed at breaking the cycle of depression and unemployment.

Unemployed participants received both the mental-health exercises and the job strategies in the same program.

Cory's shearwaters follow migratory paths that, in a Molecular Ecology study, tracked an individual's genetic ancestry more strongly than conditions in its breeding colony.

Ancestry predicted the route more reliably than the social environment at the colony.

University of Liège researchers demonstrated that a herpesvirus spreads among European eels through biting during aggressive encounters.

Transmission ran efficiently via those direct bites rather than solely through water, a previously unrecognized route for fish virus spread.

Pet cats in observations of thirty-eight animals, reported via r/science, meowed four times more when owners were present after a problem was introduced.

Vocalization rates ran substantially higher with owners nearby than when the cats were left alone with the same problem.

Artificial intelligence tools show measurable value in some organic chemistry research steps, according to a review in Chemical Society Reviews, yet laboratory outcomes diverge from strong benchmark scores.

Reliability does not always match the benchmark performance the tools post on paper, even where they accelerate parts of a workflow.

Cornell researchers are using existing undersea fiber-optic cables to listen for whale vocalizations without deploying additional hydrophones.

The seafloor infrastructure already in place can monitor those calls for conservation.

Marine heat waves that bleach anemones lead clownfish to defend their hosts more vigorously and to behave more aggressively toward other clownfish.

Home defense and intra-group aggression both rose once the anemones had bleached.
