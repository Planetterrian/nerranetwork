### Top 15 Science & Health Discoveries
1. **Psilocybin Produces Lasting Brain Structural Changes — Nature**
   A crossover study of 28 healthy volunteers with no prior psychedelic experience found that one 25 mg dose created detectable brain structural shifts at the one-month mark, while a 1 mg dose did not. These results point to a concrete window for studying how brief pharmacological interventions might influence neural architecture in adults. Source: [nature.com](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71962-3)

2. **Exercise Review Highlights Broad Disease Prevention — Cell Metabolism**
   A new synthesis of evidence positions regular physical activity as one of the most effective tools for lowering risk across multiple disease categories. The review underscores that exercise acts through overlapping physiological pathways rather than any single mechanism. Source: [cell.com](https://www.cell.com/cell-metabolism/abstract/S1550-4131(26))00086-0

3. **Colonoscopy Benefits Require Population-Level Recalibration — Lancet**
   Thirteen-year follow-up data show that colonoscopy screening delivers measurable but narrower reductions in colorectal cancer incidence and mortality than earlier assumptions suggested. The findings help refine expectations for what population screening programs can realistically achieve. Source: [thelancet.com](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(26))00794-4/abstract

4. **Large DNA Assembly Advances Microbial Cell Factories — Phys.org**
   Researchers can now reliably construct and insert very large DNA segments, including complete metabolic pathways and additional chromosomes, into yeast and bacteria. This capability lets scientists redesign microbes to produce complex molecules such as medicines and industrial chemicals more efficiently. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-large-dna-fragment-scientists-microbes.html)

5. **Global Iron Deficiency Anemia Burdens Mapped in Women — Global Burden of Disease Study**
   An analysis spanning 1990 to 2021 quantifies how iron deficiency anemia affects women of childbearing age across regions and nations. The detailed geographic patterns supply public-health planners with clearer targets for intervention. Source: [reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/science/comments/1t563tk/global_regional_and_national_burdens_of_iron/)

6. **PEG400 Reveals Parasite-Specific Malaria Enzyme Pocket — Phys.org**
   Researchers used PEG400 to expose a binding site on the falcipain-2 enzyme that exists only in Plasmodium parasites. The discovery offers a route to compounds that disable the parasite while sparing the corresponding human enzyme. Source: [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-05-peg400-reveals-parasite-pocket-sharpen.html)

7. **Short Immunotherapy Course Keeps Colon Cancer Patients Clear — Science Daily**
   Patients with a specific form of colorectal cancer who received nine weeks of pembrolizumab before surgery remained cancer-free nearly three years later in a UK trial. The outcome suggests a condensed pre-operative regimen may reduce the need for prolonged post-operative chemotherapy in selected cases. Source: [sciencedaily.com](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260505234618.htm)

8. **Ottava Robot Completes First Gastric Bypass Study — Fierce Biotech**
   Johnson & Johnson’s Ottava soft-tissue surgical robot met its primary safety and performance endpoints in an initial clinical study of gastric bypass procedures. The results mark an early step toward broader clinical use of the system in minimally invasive abdominal surgery. Source: [fiercebiotech.com](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/jjs-ottava-surgical-robot-meets-primary-endpoints-gastric-bypass-study)

### Planetterrian Spotlight
Psilocybin produced clear structural shifts in the brains of healthy volunteers after just one dose, visible on imaging one month later. The changes occurred without prior psychedelic experience and stood out against the minimal effects of the 1 mg control dose. This finding opens a window into how brief interventions might influence neural connectivity in adults, with potential relevance for mood and cognitive disorders. Larger trials will need to link these structural observations to specific behavioral or clinical outcomes. What functional changes, if any, accompany the structural shifts seen on imaging?

### Science Deep Dive: The Adult Brain Keeps Building New Connections
Most people assume the brain’s basic wiring is finished by early adulthood and changes little afterward. In reality, the adult brain retains substantial capacity to alter its physical structure in response to experience or compounds. Right now, as you read this, synaptic connections throughout your cortex are being strengthened or pruned based on what you pay attention to and what you encounter. A recent study captured one striking example: a single 25 mg dose of psilocybin produced measurable shifts in brain architecture that were still detectable four weeks later. That number—four weeks of visible change after one exposure—illustrates how quickly and durably the adult brain can reorganize. The practical takeaway is that everyday choices such as learning new skills or managing stress continue to reshape neural tissue well into later life, and future therapies may deliberately harness this ongoing plasticity rather than assuming it has ended.
