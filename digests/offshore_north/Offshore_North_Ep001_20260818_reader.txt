time for Plain Sailing

The Canadian lineage in offshore racing runs through four distinct chapters that together explain why Shawyer's attempt carries weight beyond one person's goal.

Mike Birch, sailing the 12-metre trimaran Olympus Photo, won the first Route du Rhum in 1978 by 98 seconds after 23 days at sea when he passed a much larger monohull in sight of the Guadeloupe finish.

Gerry Roufs of Montreal lay second in the 1996–97 Vendée Globe when his last message described 15-metre seas and winds above 80 knots.

He was never heard from again and wreckage reached Chile months later.

Derek Hatfield campaigned Spirit of Canada through the round-the-world races of the 2000s but did not finish a Vendée.

Shawyer is the fourth chapter.
