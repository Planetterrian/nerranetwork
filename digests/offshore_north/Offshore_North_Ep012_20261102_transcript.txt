The 10 million euro project costs cited this week is the capital outlay for the boat itself.
The 4-5 million figure is what it then costs to keep that boat racing year after year.
Insurance alone can represent 9-10% of the build price, and every major component from
foils to mass requires dedicated spares and specialist labor.
