The boat is moving at highway speeds through the dark, and the only way to keep it safe
is to break rest into short fragments, usually around 20 minutes at a time.
The autopilot handles most of the steering, but the skipper still has to wake, check the
instruments, decide whether the route still makes sense, and then try to sleep again before
the next decision point arrives. That pattern repeats for weeks. It is why every system
on board, power, navigation, collision avoidance, has to work without constant human oversight,
and why a single computer fault or power glitch can force a hard choice about whether to
keep going. The 20-minute cycle is the real limit on how long one person can stay in command
