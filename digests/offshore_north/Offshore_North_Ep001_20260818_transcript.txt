Time for plain sailing. The Vendée Globe is the only round the world race that is solo, non-stop and unassisted.
A Skipper leaves Lesabeladolone, sails south through the Atlantic, rounds the three great capes of the southern ocean, then returns the same way without any outside help and without ever stopping the boat.
The 2024 to 25 edition was won by Charlie Dalen in 64 days 19 hours, 9 days inside the previous record.
Because the race is unassisted, every repair, every routing decision and every meal must be handled by one person on a 60 foot foiling monohole that can exceed 40 knots.
The boat carries roughly 5000 calories a day in freeze dried food, makes its own water from the sea and generates electricity from hydro generators and solar panels.
Sleep comes in fragments of 20 minutes while the autopilot steers. No Canadian has ever finished the race.
