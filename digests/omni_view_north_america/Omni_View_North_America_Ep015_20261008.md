### Lead
**‘Chaos and cruelty’: the kids trapped in Trump’s immigration enforcement campaign: The Guardian**
Through July this year, Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrested more than twice as many children as in the same period last year, and more than 8,500 so far in President Donald Trump’s second term, The Guardian reports. Many were held in hotels and in hold rooms in office buildings, where lawyers and family members could not find them. Elora Mukherjee, a Columbia Law School professor who has represented more than 100 children and parents in detention in the last year, said many of her clients were arrested while complying with immigration court instructions. The Marshall Project, using Deportation Data Project records supplied by ICE and held by the University of California, Berkeley and UCLA, reported that most immigration violations are civil and that the vast majority of children in the arrest data have no criminal conviction or pending charge beyond the immigration violation. After the January arrest of five-year-old Liam Conejo Ramos, held at the Dilley center in Texas, the government later sent fewer children there; lawyers had complained of contaminated food and poor healthcare, and activists had protested outside. Source: [theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/08/children-trump-immigration-enforcement-campaign)

### Across the Region
**Trump adviser Stephen Miller spoke to agents at recent FBI training on domestic terrorism, sources say: CBS News**
White House homeland security adviser Stephen Miller spoke to counterterrorism agents at a training conference at FBI headquarters just over a week ago, CBS News reports, days before prosecutors charged a man with making disturbing calls and texts to Miller’s wife, Katie. Former Justice Department officials called the visit unusual, citing the norm of insulating federal criminal investigations from political officials. The administration and the FBI said the visit was appropriate; a White House spokesperson said Miller regularly meets law-enforcement agencies on public safety and is tasked with carrying out President Trump’s executive order on domestic terrorism and political violence. Miller told the agents the initiative was personal, citing the 2025 assassination of Turning Point USA founder Charlie Kirk, and said agents should be more proactive and that no threat case is too small, according to a senior FBI official and a source briefed on the meeting. That official said the remarks fit a push to investigate threats and harassment sooner, on the view that those crimes can precede mass-casualty violence; the session included FBI counterterrorism supervisors and officials from IRS Criminal Investigation, Homeland Security Investigations and the Secret Service. Source: [cbsnews.com](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/stephen-miller-fbi-training-domestic-terrorism-trump-white-house/)

### Both Sides: Air Force One and the press
Whether reporters may travel on Air Force One is now a fight between the White House and three news organizations, with a court hearing later this week.

The administration’s strongest case is that a seat on the president’s aircraft is a privilege, not a right, and that an order about White House badges does not open the plane. Politico was barred from pool coverage on Air Force One on Monday, when President Donald Trump flew to Nebraska for a campaign stop. The White House said he will continue to grant an unprecedented level of access to the press. Officials have kept CNN off the aircraft repeatedly since a September court order, on the rationale that access to the White House is not access to the plane.

The outlets’ strongest case is that the exclusion is unconstitutional. In mid-September the White House moved to ban Politico, CNN and MS Now from the White House; the three sued. Trump-appointed Judge Timothy Kelly issued a temporary restraining order restoring the badges that had been taken. Ted Boutrous, the First Amendment lawyer representing the three organizations, said the law is absolutely clear and that the harm to his clients, the First Amendment and the public is profound. On Monday several outlets refused to fill Politico’s pool seat. The same week, Trump wrote on Truth Social that The New York Times and other outlets he called “Artificial Media Outlets” should be arrested for dishonesty and disservice to the country.

Neither side disputes that Politico did not board on Monday. Kelly will hear a request for a preliminary injunction later this week, a stopgap before any trial. Source: [theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/oct/08/trump-press-attacks-politico)
