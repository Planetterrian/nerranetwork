### Lead
**Kilmar Abrego Garcia leaves U.S. voluntarily for Spain: NPR**
Kilmar Armando Abrego Garcia, a Salvadoran immigrant, left the United States voluntarily on Tuesday for Spain. NPR reported the departure came after nearly two years of legal battles; The New York Times described it as the end of a 20-month fight. The Times reported that he was wrongfully deported last year. He left facing the prospect that the Trump administration might deport him to Liberia, the Times reported. NPR described him as a symbol of President Trump’s mass deportation campaign. The voluntary departure is the result the courts had not yet imposed: he chose Spain rather than wait on a further removal.
