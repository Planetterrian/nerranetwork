### The Closer Look
**Everybody hates Pat: is a cabal of college kids really trying to bring down ESPN’s star meathead?: The Guardian**
The Guardian reports that a handful of college students made jokes about Pat McAfee online ahead of his appearance with ESPN's College GameDay at the University of Utah. McAfee hosts The Pat McAfee Show. The Guardian says he landed a five-year, 85 million dollar deal in 2023 to take that show to ESPN, and that his persistently honking presence on College GameDay had helped push ratings to new heights. ESPN executives, the paper reports, were so impressed that they were reportedly in negotiations to award him a new contract worth between 60 million and 65 million dollars a year. One executive, The Guardian says, went so far as to describe him as a generational media talent. The outlet's own framing casts him as the star attraction of a bolder ESPN era of stunts, stupidity and what it calls memebrain swill. That is the paper's description, not a finding McAfee has adopted.

The Guardian says the weekend then went wrong, in the paper's telling. Students at Utah posted ahead of the GameDay appearance. The writer says "attacked" overstates it: they made a few mild jokes at his expense. McAfee's initial reaction, which The Guardian quotes, was mild. "The university's Instagram account posted something talking a little trash about me last night, which I certainly love," he said ahead of the Utes' clash with the Houston Cougars. The Guardian's argument, attributed to its writer, is that the reaction shows an ESPN personality happy to whip a crowd into a hostile frenzy but terrified of criticism. The piece does not report a cancelled appearance, a dropped contract or a ratings number for the Utah show. What it does report is the existing deal, the reported renewal range, the executive's "generational" description, the students' jokes, and McAfee's line about the university Instagram post before the Utes played Houston.

Who that touches is McAfee, the students who posted, ESPN's College GameDay set, and the Utah stop. The 85 million dollar, five-year deal dates to 2023. The reported talks, if the paper's account holds, would move him to between 60 million and 65 million dollars a year. The students' posts are what The Guardian says prompted the on-air remark. ESPN is the employer in both the 2023 deal and the reported negotiations. Source: [theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/oct/29/pat-mcafee-college-gameday-espn)
