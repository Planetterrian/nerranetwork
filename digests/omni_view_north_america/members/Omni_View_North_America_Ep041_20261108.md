### The Closer Look
**Daughter of WWII veteran selling ‘door poppies’ to support local Legion: Global News Canada**
As Remembrance Day approaches, Margot Rockett is working hard to keep up with demand after her craftsmanship went viral on TikTok, Global News reports. She makes what she calls door poppies: large, wreath-like poppies that can be hung on doors, walls and windows. She told Global News it is a bigger, bolder way to say she remembers. Each door poppy costs $10 and is officially licensed by the Legion. She said the policy is rigid and strict because the poppy is a trademarked image, and she said she respects that because the Legion has to protect it. All of the proceeds go to her Legion branch at Coxwell and Gerrard. She said all of the money stays in the community to support local veterans and their families.

The craft has a family history the article dates in stages. Rockett's father served in the Air Force in the Second World War. In the last few years of his life, she said, he lived at the Sunnybrook Veterans Centre. She said they took such good care of him that she wanted to give back to the Poppy Fund and to the Legion. During the pandemic, she said, the door poppy was born: she tweaked a flower she had made before and made the door poppy.

The branch treats the money as operating support for its own poppy campaign. Shelley Lyons, vice-president at the Coxwell and Gerrard Legion, told Global News that when Rockett approached them a couple of years ago, Lyons thought it was pretty amazing, and that proceeds coming to this branch specifically for the poppy campaign are a huge income for the branch. Lyons said it has been disheartening to see fewer people wearing poppies around Remembrance Day, and she hopes Rockett's initiative will encourage people to pay their respects. She said that as the First and Second World Wars fade further into history, a lack of education and memory knowledge could be driving factors. She said it always makes her smile when she sees someone wearing a poppy. Rockett is working all hours to keep up with demand and said she would not have it any other way. She said that if her dad were here, he would be really proud. Global News credited Sean O'Shea among the files on the story. Source: [globalnews.ca](https://globalnews.ca/news/12141371/door-poppy-remembrance-day/)
