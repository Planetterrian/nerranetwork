The Globe and Mail reported that more than five million women in Canada are between forty and sixty, and that most will have menopause symptoms, mild or overwhelming, from fatigue and headaches to anxiety, muscle aches and heart palpitations.

The paper listed too few family doctors, doctors without menopause training, long specialist waits, thin research funding, and a flood of misleading information online.
