> **The UN calls a planned US firing-squad livestream torture.**

**What You Need to Know:** The UN human rights office says a Pentagon plan to livestream the execution of Nidal Hasan would amount to torture. North Carolina's governor signed a temporary suspension of the state fuel tax, and a new Alberta poll finds most respondents want to stay in Canada. ### Lead
**UN says Pentagon plan to livestream execution of Fort Hood shooter would amount to torture: The Guardian**
The Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights told AFP that livestreaming the execution of Nidal Hasan would be tantamount to a public execution and would breach the absolute prohibition of torture. US defence officials said Hasan, a US Army psychiatrist who shot and killed 13 people at a military base in 2009, would face a firing squad, that the execution would be broadcast live, and that details would come later. The UN body said it had made similar statements about public executions in Afghanistan and Iran, which it said constitute a form of cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment. Robin M. Maher, executive director of the Death Penalty Information Center, told The Guardian her group is not aware of any regulation or precedent for a military execution to be livestreamed on the internet or otherwise made widely available to the public. She said public executions are typically held in authoritarian countries to instil fear in the population and to show the power and control of the government. The Guardian reported fierce backlash from US lawmakers, who branded the move grotesque and compared it to actions of some terror groups and oppressive governments. Source: [theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/live/2026/oct/09/pentagon-fort-hood-livestream-firing-squad-execution-reaction-backlash-pete-hegseth-donald-trump-mussolini-latest-news-updates)

### Across the Region
**NC gas tax suspended: Stein signs law aimed at lowering prices at the pump: WRAL**
North Carolina Governor Josh Stein on Friday signed a law suspending the state's tax on gasoline and diesel until November 30. The measure suspends the 41-cents-per-gallon tax and is expected to cost the state more than 350 million dollars that typically goes to the Department of Transportation for road projects. The law keeps that budget whole by transferring money from state savings of roughly 11 billion dollars. WRAL reported prices have remained elevated since President Donald Trump launched an attack on Iran earlier this year. The law goes into effect Saturday, though stations still holding fuel they already paid tax on may not sell cheaper fuel for days or weeks. Source: [wral.com](https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/north-carolina-gas-tax-suspend-lower-pump-stein-october-9/)

**Gunter: Alberta separatists, Premier Smith, take biggest hits in latest Leger poll: Edmonton Journal**
Leger surveyed 1,000 Albertans early this month and found 70 percent support for staying in Canada and 22 percent for beginning a separation process. The pollster found 77 percent were very likely to vote, compared with 50 to 60 percent who commit to ballots in provincial or federal elections, the Edmonton Journal column reported. In Edmonton the split was 80 percent to remain and 13 percent to leave; in Calgary, 66 percent to 22 percent; in the rest of the province, 62 percent to 30 percent. Voters for the stay option were 91 percent committed to that position, while separatist-option voters were 71 percent steadfast. Gunter wrote that the poll was taken before it was revealed that members of the Alberta Prosperity Project had met secretly with Trump administration officials and Republican operatives and had apparently offered Alberta's oil and freshwater for American help winning independence. Source: [edmontonjournal.com](https://edmontonjournal.com/opinion/gunter-leger-poll-alberta-separatists-danielle-smith)
