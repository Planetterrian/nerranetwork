SpaceX chose methalox, methane and liquid oxygen, over denser kerosene or toxic hypergolics,
because a clean burn lets a booster fly again with little inspection.
Kerosene cracks in hot cooling channels and leaves soot.
Methane is one carbon to four hydrogens, so there is far less carbon to deposit, and that
