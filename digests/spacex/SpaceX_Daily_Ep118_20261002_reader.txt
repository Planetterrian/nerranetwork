On the AI front the orbital TPU test continues to run through initial commissioning.

The Google TPU test satellite illustrates the core thermal and radiation constraints of orbital compute.

In vacuum heat can only leave through radiation or conduction along heat pipes so the team sized radiators to keep junction temperatures inside the Trillium operating window.

Each chip must survive fifty-to-one-hundred g launch loads and cumulative proton fluence that can flip bits in memory.
