From an engineering standpoint, Raptor 3’s shift to a full-flow staged combustion cycle routes every molecule of methane and oxygen through the respective turbopumps before they reach the injector.

This eliminates the separate gas-generator stage that previously dumped hot gas overboard, raising overall efficiency while removing several valves and manifolds.

The resulting part-count reduction directly attacks the Idiot Index, where earlier Raptors carried an estimated four to five× markup over raw materials and labor, and the new architecture targets closer to 3× by collapsing assembly steps.

That lower index matters because each Starship needs dozens of engines; even a twenty percent drop in engine cost multiplies across the booster and ship.

The design choice also buys margin on chamber pressure without increasing turbopump stress, because the pre-burners now operate at the same mixture ratio as the main chamber.
