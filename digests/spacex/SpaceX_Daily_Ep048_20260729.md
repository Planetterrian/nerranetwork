## The Counterpoint
NASA heat-shield specialists have stated that Starship’s current tiled thermal-protection approach may prove a developmental dead end for repeated lunar-return entries. The assessment centers on tile-attachment durability under higher-energy trajectories and the difficulty of inspecting or repairing the array between flights. Resolution would require either a redesigned attachment system or a shift to an ablative or metallic alternative. The critique draws on decades of Shuttle and Orion experience with reusable versus single-use thermal protection.
