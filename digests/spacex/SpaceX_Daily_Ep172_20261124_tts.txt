Typical Starlink altitudes sit near five hundred fifty kilometers, where leftover atmosphere still exerts measurable force, and a small density rise from solar activity or orbital position can drop a spacecraft several kilometers in days.

When the Sun heats the thermosphere, density jumps while that speed barely changes, so drag rises in proportion and altitude bleeds off.
