### Top News

1. **TakeMe2Space to launch AI computing satellite on SpaceX rocket on 1 October**
   TakeMe2Space will send its MOI-1A satellite to orbit on October 1 aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 on the Transporter-18 rideshare mission.
   The Hyderabad startup describes MOI-1A as India’s first orbital computing satellite because it processes data in space and returns only results rather than raw files.
   The satellite weighs less than 50 kilograms and carries Nvidia Orin NX chips built for edge computing.
   Twenty-three customers have already booked capacity, including mapping firms, schools, and businesses in farming, mining, supply chains, and insurance.
   One customer is the U.S. firm Little Place Labs.
   The satellite replaces an earlier unit lost in January when an ISRO PSLV third stage failed. Source: [thenextweb.com](https://thenextweb.com/news/takeme2space-moi-1a-orbital-computing-satellite-spacex)

2. **Open source datacenters and open source thinking will undo self-inflicted DC damage**
   The Register argues that datacenter operators must release trustworthy energy-consumption numbers before the public will accept further buildouts.
   The column cites xAI’s Colossus clusters in the Deep South as an example of projects that have drawn lawsuits over methane turbines installed without full regulatory permission.
   It notes that promises of future nuclear or fusion power do not address immediate hydrocarbon use during construction. Source: [theregister.com](https://www.theregister.com/columnists/2026/09/28/open-source-datacenters-and-open-source-thinking-will-undo-self-inflicted-dc-damage/5299068)
