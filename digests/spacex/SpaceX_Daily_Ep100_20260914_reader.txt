The engineering angle here starts with the orbit altitude choice itself.

Medium-Earth orbit for the O3b mPOWER satellites demands that the Falcon 9 second stage carry extra propellant margin compared with low-Earth orbit missions, which directly limits the instantaneous launch window and forces a longer burn sequence for precise insertion.

That same altitude choice trades manufacturing volume for performance per spacecraft, since fewer total satellites are required yet each one must carry more sophisticated phased-array antennas and larger solar arrays sized for the greater distance to ground users.

The completed network therefore shows how orbit selection sets the minimum vehicle performance floor for both the rocket and the satellite bus.
