### Engineering Deep Dive
Raptor 3’s shift to a full-flow staged combustion cycle routes every molecule of methane and oxygen through the respective turbopumps before they reach the injector. This eliminates the separate gas-generator stage that previously dumped hot gas overboard, raising overall efficiency while removing several valves and manifolds. The resulting part-count reduction directly attacks the Idiot Index: where earlier Raptors carried an estimated 4–5× markup over raw materials and labor, the new architecture targets closer to 3× by collapsing assembly steps.  

That lower index matters because each Starship needs dozens of engines; even a 20 % drop in engine cost multiplies across the booster and ship. The design choice also buys margin on chamber pressure without increasing turbopump stress, because the pre-burners now operate at the same mixture ratio as the main chamber.  

Next, watch for the first integrated test of a full Raptor 3 set on a Ship or Booster static-fire stand. If the simplified plumbing survives the thermal environment at 280 tf, the program gains a credible path to the production rate required for monthly flight attempts.
