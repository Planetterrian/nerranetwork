Radiation exposure in low Earth orbit adds another constraint that ground clusters never face.

Engineers must choose between heavy physical shielding and lighter error-correcting architectures that protect the silicon without exceeding mass budgets.

Each choice alters the power budget because active cooling and redundant controllers draw watts that must be generated and dissipated in vacuum.

The orbital setting therefore turns the familiar data-center power-density problem into a coupled mass-power-radiation optimization.
