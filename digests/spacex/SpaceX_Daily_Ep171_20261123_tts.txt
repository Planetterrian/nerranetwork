Space X sent the classified NROL-136 payload for the National Reconnaissance Office, or NRO, off Vandenberg Space Force Base Launch Complex 4E at four twenty-three in the morning local time on November twenty-second, technology dot org reports, three minutes and twenty-one seconds past the new pad mark.

Booster B1097 completed its fourteenth flight. It separated about two minutes after leaving the pad and landed at Landing Zone 4 roughly seven and a half minutes after liftoff, back at the launch base.

Space X vice president of launch Kiko Dontchev credited the West Coast team and posted that preparations finished several hours ahead of schedule, with the next target a twenty-four hour turnaround.

The flight was the NRO’s sixth of two thousand twenty-six and the fifteenth supporting its proliferated-architecture satellite network, and every one of those six NRO missions this year flew on Falcon 9 with classified payloads into low Earth orbit.

The proliferated design flies many small satellites in low Earth orbit so they can take turns watching a location and stand in if another fails, and the NRO has not disclosed how many satellites flew on this mission or their technical details.

The NRO began deploying the constellation in May two thousand twenty-four with NROL-146, NROL-179 launched on June nineteenth two thousand twenty-six, and the previous pad-turnaround mark had been set at Cape Canaveral Pad 40.

An uncrewed Dragon, SpaceQ reports, splashed down off California on November twenty-first with biology collected for cancer and aging research on the International Space Station.

It also carried a failed Canadarm2 wrist joint so engineers on the ground can inspect it, stowed by NASA astronaut Jessica Watkins after crews replaced it on a June thirtieth spacewalk.

Weather held the capsule three days, and it left the station on November twentieth.

ex A I stopped construction of a South Memphis wastewater recycling plant, Wisevoter reports, and now expects to restart in January.

That stoppage hits the cooling schedule the Colossus-class training cluster depends on.

Starlink service, Elon Musk posted, is unavailable in China because the system is illegal there.

Elon Musk tied Starlink’s next expansion to unsupervised Full Self-Driving, vehicles that operate without a human at the wheel, and said coverage should arrive toward the end of next year to feed the high-bandwidth those cars need so passengers can work and watch entertainment.

SciGuySpace posted on X that Tesla Full Self-Driving has become instantly intuitive and made driving measurably more pleasant, a personal observation rather than a company release.

China put out its clearest timeline yet for putting astronauts on the Moon before two thousand thirty, NASASpaceflight reports, starting with three uncrewed precursor flights.

Crewed flight out of Earth orbit is supposed to wait until those three robotic missions succeed.

SciGuySpace observed that earlier NASA decisions prevented Orion from gaining low-lunar-orbit capability despite two decades of funding, his reading of that history rather than an agency confirmation.

Rocket Lab’s chief financial officer, TradingView reports, noted stronger pricing power for Electron launches amid reduced availability of Space X rideshare opportunities.

Electron demand remains firm even as the stock just posted its worst week on record.

In the same window, a YouTube report not confirmed by the company says Space X is preparing Booster 24 and Ship 42 for Starship Flight 17 right after Flight 15, a test that could debut from Pad 39A in Florida.

Cathie Wood at ARK Invest, Dealroom notes, says orbital compute could undercut terrestrial data centers within two years, with Starship projected at three hundred fifty-eight dollars per kilogram to orbit in two thousand twenty-seven.

Worth keeping an eye on, lenders could block Space X from putting billions of dollars of advanced A I chips in orbit because their financing covenants, MLQ reports, were written before the orbital-compute strategy existed.

Those terms could stop the deployment even after technical and regulatory hurdles fall.

The way out is a debt renegotiation or a showing that orbital assets sit outside the current restrictions.

A seventy-four billion dollar A I data-center plan still has to clear votes in Texas and Arizona on December ninth.

On the A I front, Elon Musk said the price of RAM, the memory on each board, pushed the team to cut AI4.5 hardware from thirty-two gigabytes to twenty-four gigabytes per unit, because memory bandwidth already stops the larger configuration from being fully used.

That cut holds the design inside practical operating limits and trims what the parts cost.

Chamber pressure is the subject of today's engineering deep dive, starting with the V-2 engine that delivered two hundred forty-five kilonewtons of sea-level thrust at only fourteen to fifteen bar while burning liquid oxygen and seventy-five percent ethanol, its turbopump running on steam from hydrogen-peroxide decomposition, a cycle that limited efficiency and reusability.

Raptor 3 reaches two thousand four hundred fifty-two kilonewtons at sea level — roughly ten times two hundred forty-five — in a full-flow staged-combustion cycle that routes all propellant through the turbines before the main chamber, raising pressure to three hundred to three hundred fifty bar, about twenty times the V-2's fifteen bar when you divide three hundred by fifteen.

Specific impulse, the seconds of thrust from a unit of propellant, is approximately three hundred thirty seconds at sea level and three hundred fifty-five in vacuum, and three hundred thirty times nine point eight one meters per second squared is on the order of three thousand two hundred meters per second of exhaust velocity.

Mass stays at one thousand five hundred twenty-five kilograms, so two million four hundred fifty-two thousand newtons divided by one thousand five hundred twenty-five times nine point eight one yields a thrust-to-weight near one hundred sixty-four.

Higher impulse and pressure shrink the propellant fraction a rocket must carry, and full-flow eliminates gas-generator exhaust that dumps unburned turbine flow overboard on older cycles, converting nearly all of the chemical energy into useful thrust.

The V-2's spherical chamber and eighteen injector elements were state of the art in nineteen forty-four; Raptor uses pintle injectors and actively cooled chambers for mixing efficiency and thermal margin the V-2 could not reach.

Thirty-three of those engines on the Super Heavy booster lift a vehicle whose dry mass is only a few percent of its propellant load, then return that Super Heavy booster for reuse within hours, extending the physics to a vehicle an order of magnitude larger.
