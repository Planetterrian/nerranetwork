From an engineering standpoint, the recent Midnight Falcon 9 ride share from Vandenberg placed 81
spacecraft into Sun synchronous orbit through a sequence of two second stage burns separated by a
coast phase. The limiting factor is not thrust but the propellant mass that must be carried to perform
the plane change and circularization burns after the initial insertion. Each additional kilogram
