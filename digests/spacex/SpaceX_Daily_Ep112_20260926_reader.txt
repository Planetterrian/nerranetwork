The engineering angle on Cursor’s new bots centers on shifting verification from human review to automated comparison against live baselines.

Security Reviewer runs a full-codebase scan for each pull request and surfaces only the categories of flaw that survive ordinary peer review.

Rollouts extends the same principle into production by building an instrumentation map before merge and then comparing signals against pre-deployment baselines.

The approach works only when source control, deployment, and observability systems are already connected.
