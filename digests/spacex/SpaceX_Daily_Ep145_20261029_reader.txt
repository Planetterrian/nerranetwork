Fast rendezvous is the engineering reality of matching plane and phase with almost no spare propellant, and Grace’s hop only works if that match is already in the sky when the window opens.

Dragon flew phasing burns — small nudges that change catch-up rate — until the argument of perigee, the orbit’s low-point location, and the nodal alignment, where the orbit crosses the equator, matched the station at arrival.

The station travels at roughly seven point six six kilometers per second.

At an altitude on the order of four hundred kilometers, that speed gives a period of roughly ninety-three minutes.

Even a small miss in launch azimuth or upper-stage cutoff then slides relative phasing by hundreds of kilometers per orbit, so the hop needed an on-time liftoff and a second stage that left the right energy and plane.

Earlier US crewed flights averaged twelve to fourteen hours because windows followed daylight and lighting, not minimum-time geometry.

Soyuz docked in three hours in twenty twenty from a near-co-planar Baikonur launch, while Florida’s twenty-eight-degree latitude, a fifty-one point six degree station inclination, and the Atlantic range-safety corridor historically forced longer coasts.

If Starship refueling later lets crew leave from higher-energy orbits, the same rules decide whether transits fall below six hours as routine.

The limiter is insertion-burn precision and whether later phasing burns can fly with minimal propellant margin.

A back-of-envelope plane change at that speed puts a half-degree error on the order of sixty-seven meters per second, two-v times sine of half the angle.

The previous US crewed minimum still needed multiple orbits to close the phase angle, and compressing it meant a tighter window and a Falcon 9 second stage that delivered a cleaner velocity vector.

That trades operational flexibility for fewer hours in a small cabin, and future vehicles with more propellant could loosen the window and keep the short ride.

The magic-wand number is the cutoff vector, raw meters per second; get it perfect and the wait is just the phase angle already in the sky, and the Idiot Index on a long rendezvous is extra life-support mass, many times the propellant a precise insertion would burn.
