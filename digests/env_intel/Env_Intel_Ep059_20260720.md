### Lead Story
LNG Canada’s acid-gas incinerator at the Kitimat terminal has repeatedly malfunctioned, rendering the unit unusable for months at a time. The incinerator is a key control device for protecting local air quality and human health from acid-gas releases associated with the LNG export facility. Under the BC Environmental Management Act, major project permits require reliable operation of pollution-control equipment; extended downtime raises questions about whether the facility remains in compliance with its authorization conditions. Consultants managing air-quality monitoring or permit renewals at comparable coastal LNG sites should verify whether their clients’ systems have similar uptime shortfalls. Next steps include checking the most recent permit inspection reports and preparing for potential ministry requests for corrective-action plans. ### Practitioner Deep Dive: Glacial Lake Outburst Flood Risk Assessment in Mountain Watersheds
A Phase I desktop review for a proposed linear project near Pemberton flags Place Creek and Gates Lake as potential receptors, yet the client’s preliminary flood mapping stops at the 200-year event and omits glacial lake outburst scenarios. BC guidance under the Environmental Management Act requires practitioners to consider credible extreme events when receptors include fish-bearing streams or community water intakes; the Squamish-Lillooet Regional District has already issued public warnings for fast-moving water and debris from the Place Glacier lake. The experienced assessor recognizes that standard IDF curves and steady-state hydraulic models understate peak discharge and sediment load when a moraine-dammed lake drains rapidly. Field indicators such as trim lines on valley walls, lichen-free boulders, and recent channel avulsions provide stronger signals than modelled return periods alone. The most common mistake is treating the glacial lake as a static feature and simply extending the 200-year floodplain; the fix is to add a scenario-based outburst volume to the hydraulic model and re-evaluate receptor exposure before submitting the final report.
