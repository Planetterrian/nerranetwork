### Practitioner Deep Dive: Reconciling federal Gazette notices with provincial contaminated-sites timelines
A client submits a draft Phase II ESA report for a site near a federal port facility the same week the latest Canada Gazette Part II appears. The report references only provincial CSR standards even though the property triggers IAA screening because of potential Fisheries Act implications. The Gazette issue contains no new thresholds, yet it may list orders or exemptions that alter the applicable federal process timeline. Experienced assessors check the full Gazette text against the site’s federal permit history before finalizing the report, because an unnoticed order can reset notification windows or require additional baseline data. The practical distinction is that provincial CSR closure letters do not automatically satisfy federal reporting obligations when both regimes apply. The most common mistake is treating the Gazette as background reading rather than a live compliance calendar; the fix is to assign one team member to flag any instrument that intersects the site’s federal triggers within 48 hours of each bi-weekly release.
