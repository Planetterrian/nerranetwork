### What we talked about

Jason Fishman, CEO of a digital-marketing agency focused on equity crowdfunding, surprised listeners by stating that generative AI is mainly useful for ideation while human experts remain essential because “we constantly find errors with AI” and campaigns without them produce “very little to no results.” He described how the shift from friends-and-family checks to thousands of smaller investments has rewritten capital raises, yet success still hinges on experienced oversight rather than automation alone. The conversation examined whether tools for copy and targeting are eroding or sharpening demand for specialized human strategy in high-stakes Reg CF and Regulation A+ campaigns.

**Key Takeaways**
- Volume is no longer the bottleneck: campaigns can now target accredited investors by household income or net worth and scale to thousands of smaller checks averaging $1,716 under Reg CF.
- Human strategy drives every stage—initial planning, activation, optimization, and scaling—because AI-generated copy and parameters require constant expert review to avoid detectable flaws.
- AI works best for early ideation and proofreading; final investor-facing creative and media buying still need a “captain behind the helm” to hit conversion targets such as 100,000 visits per 1,000 investments.
- Only about 10 % of Reg CF issuers reach $1 million and fewer than 1 % hit the $5 million cap, with the median raise at $194,000, underscoring that marketing execution, not tool choice, separates winners.
- Strong results are measured in 10x–30x ROAS, achieved through $1–2 cost-per-click and 1–2 % conversion rates that deliver $100–200 cost per new investor.
- Pricing and scope decisions for retainers remain tied to perceived value and marketplace competition rather than internal efficiency gains from AI.
- Fishman’s near-term bet is that visible success stories and liquidity events will create self-reinforcing momentum, drawing more issuers and investors into the channel.

Jason Fishman is CEO of a digital-marketing agency that runs equity-crowdfunding campaigns for startups and growth-stage companies.
