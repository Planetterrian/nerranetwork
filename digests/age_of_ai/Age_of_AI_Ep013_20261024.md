# The Age of AI

> **A founder racing to embed agentic systems and fractional CAIO leadership across enterprises now tests whether those same tools can scale human judgment without eroding the critical thinking his own doctrine requires.**

*Episode 13 · October 24, 2026*

**What You Need to Know:** Jon Cheney said the AI of today is as bad as it will ever be while describing how he lets callers decide when to hand off from his voice agents and keeps sensitive decisions with humans. He built GenAIPI as a weekend Replit prototype that pivoted the same day into enterprise AI proficiency testing, live training, and fractional chief AI officer work. The company now ships custom agentic systems that replace repetitive tasks while preserving human judgment on anything that requires context, emotion, or liability.

### About Jon Cheney

**Founder and CEO, GenAIPI**

I’m Jon Cheney, Founder and CEO of GenAIPI. We help companies adopt AI in practical, measurable ways, often acting as a fractional Chief AI Officer to guide full-company transformation. Our work focuses less on one-off AI experiments and more on helping teams build the systems, habits, and agentic capabilities they need to pass real work to AI and change how the business operates.

### What we talked about

- The first live client hit Stripe test mode; once fixed, Jon closed deals by promising to measure how good 300 employees were at using AI and to run the training himself.
- Companies told him training alone failed because people forgot everything after he left the room, so he launched ongoing fractional CAIO engagements built on three pillars: strategy with leadership, hands-on workflow transformation, and repeated education.
- A staffing firm’s three-person incident team handled thousands of status calls; the custom voice agent plus dashboard reduced the headcount to one while routing only high-priority or emergent cases to the remaining person.
- Jon drew no hard line in the staffing build; the caller simply said they wanted a human and the agent stepped aside, with every call recorded for audit.
- Non-technical leaders only required proof the agent would not invent answers or drop calls; after a week of edge-case testing the system went live.
- He now runs most of his own work through agents that monitor other agents, but still demands they brief him on anything involving contracts, payments above routine bills, or relationships so he stays personally current.

Jon Cheney is Founder and CEO at GenAIPI.
