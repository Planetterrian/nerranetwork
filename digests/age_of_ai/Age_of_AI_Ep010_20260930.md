### About Roddy de la Garza

**Broker /Author / Area Leader, Roddy de la Garza Real Estate Group @ EPIQUE**

A Harlingen, Texas native and University of Texas at Austin graduate, Roddy grew up in a family brokerage spanning more than 50 years—measuring houses and hanging signs by age eight. His own sales journey began with $25,000 duplexes and eventually reached $4 million homes in Beverly Hills: a trajectory he never imagined, built by saying yes to every opportunity.

Those opportunities carried him through real estate FinTech, media, brokerage leadership, home staging, publishing, and more than $400 million in real estate sales. He has led and supported over 8,000 agents across California and Texas while remaining close to the work: clients, contracts, culture, and the messy middle where leadership actually happens.

---

### What we talked about

- He staged a West Hollywood condo to feel like “coming home” instead of “someone’s left-behind furniture,” producing the only sale above list price in the building in two years and closing in 14 days.
- After his father’s death, Roddy created the daily devotional *Above Asking* so agents could practice gratitude and boundaries without buying another coach or portal.
- He replicated the seasonal split he knew growing up in Texas’s Rio Grande Valley—year-round residents and winter Texans—by dividing his own practice between Hollywood and Palm Springs.
- When he experimented with an AI receptionist, callers hung up rather than leave messages; he dropped the tool because it created “cold distance” and lost direct human connection.
- On his 22-person team he assigns most of his own volume to others and adjusts splits for tenured agents, finding that “the more I’ve given away, the more I’ve gotten in return.”
- After solving a client’s request for non-gendered title language on a married couple’s deed, he required the solution to be taught to two other team members so the knowledge spread.

Roddy de la Garza is a broker, team leader at Roddy de la Garza Real Estate Group @ EPIQUE, and author of *Above Asking: Daily Mindwork for Real Estate Professionals*.
