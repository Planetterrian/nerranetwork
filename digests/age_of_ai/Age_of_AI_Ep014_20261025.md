### About Piper Martz

**Leads Table Stakes Media; formerly at Aampe**

Piper Martz is based in New York City and is deeply engrained in the Shopify ecosystem, especially the DTC community in NYC. Her most recent role was at Aampe, an AI decisioning tool pioneering agentic AI for lifecycle, CRM and product leaders, where she worked with enterprises such as ThreadUp, Chime and Deezer. She built agentic AI-native CRM stacks and moved enterprise brands from static automations to AI-driven lifecycle engagement across email, SMS, push, and in-app. She now leads Table Stakes Media, which brings more women to discuss AI and innovation in retail and ecommerce through community education and in-person gatherings, and is an emerging LinkedIn voice on operator-level MarTech.

### What we talked about

- At Aampe she saw agents run one-to-one micro-experiments that selected content components for each user instead of building segments such as “41-year-old cat lovers in Las Vegas.”
- A national sports association client launched over 100 agentic campaigns in six months; users receiving the personalized messages spent 13.5 more minutes per month in the app.
- She distinguishes true agents, which hold guardrails yet make independent decisions on timing, channel, and content, from workflow tools that simply hand off tasks for human review.
- After years building trigger-based journeys she now questions the value of classic A/B tests, noting that every nudge in an agentic system functions as a continuous micro-test.
- She argues that community dinners and in-person events alone cannot close the gender gap because the documented hesitation often stems from workplace psychological safety rather than access to tools.
- As a founder she is betting that better change-management support for leaders will reduce layoffs by letting teams redirect newly freed bandwidth into higher-value experiments instead of cutting roles.

Piper Martz is founder of Table Stakes Media, which produces media, executive dinners, and peer-mentorship programs for women driving AI adoption.
