Compounding means a pharmacy prepares a medicine instead of dispensing the manufacturer's approved product.

The survey ran from February third to March twentieth, 2025.

Two-thirds of the thirty-three products contained semaglutide and one-third contained tirzepatide.

Forty-eight percent combined one of those drugs with some mix of cyanocobalamin, glycine, niacinamide, docusate or ondansetron.

The chemistry shows a quality defect, not what the defect does in a person.

Human evidence in these papers is thinner than the marketing.

A retrospective study in Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism followed ninety-four people, eighty-one women and thirteen men, mean age forty-six point five seven years, at a commercial wellness studio.

That study is observational and uncontrolled, not a randomized comparison with F D A approved semaglutide, and it cannot show the compounded product matches the approved drug.

Harms appear in a case series in the Journal of the American Pharmacists Association.

Three adverse-event reports reached a regional poison control center after incorrect administration of semaglutide obtained for weight loss from compounding pharmacies and an aesthetic spa.

No paper here shows a compounded copy has the potency, impurity profile or clinical effect of Ozempic, Wegovy or approved tirzepatide.

Advantages of those sublingual or disintegrating compounded tablets over approved oral tablets are not determined.
