Users began optimizing for the visible score rather than the underlying activity.

accumulated progress. The causal mechanism was straightforward. The systems measured and rewarded
frequency and visibility so behavior migrated toward what was measured. Second order effects included
reduced willingness to attempt difficult material that risked temporary score drops and a growing
