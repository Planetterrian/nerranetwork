Newfoundland and Labrador would close indefinitely.
The decision aimed to give the depleted stocks time to recover after decades of heavy harvesting.

Federal fisheries managers faced a stark reality after the cod collapse.
Thousands of inshore and near-shore fishers suddenly had no legal harvest.
Officials believed that redirecting effort toward underutilized species such as Snowcrab
