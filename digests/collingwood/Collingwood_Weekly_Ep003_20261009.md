# Collingwood Weekly
> **Collingwood voters gained internet ballots at ten Thursday, ahead of October twenty-six.**

**What You Need to Know:** Collingwood voters gained internet ballots at ten Thursday, ahead of October twenty-six, and paper ballots are planned at more than one point through the rest of the month. Clearview’s window opens Monday, October 12, and runs to October 26, online, by phone, or at an Election Services Centre, using the voter information letters mailed in September. A Simcoe County Transit Service Delivery Review describes a split county-and-town network, Collingwood marked Probus Month for more than 2,300 residents in 10 local clubs, and the County of Simcoe says the Simcoe County Museum stays open Thanksgiving Monday while the archives close.

### The Week's Stories
**Voting starts today in Collingwood. Here's what you need to know: CollingwoodToday**
CollingwoodToday reports that internet voting in the town opened at 10 a.m. on October 8. The same account says multiple paper voting options are planned throughout the month, so a paper ballot is not limited to a single day. Election day is October 26. The opening of internet voting on October 8 is the dated start of the town’s voting period that runs to that election day. Paper options sit alongside the internet ballot for the remainder of the month. Residents who want a paper ballot have those planned options before October 26, and residents who want the internet ballot have had that route since 10 a.m. Thursday. **Councillors push for expanded transit links across Simcoe County: CollingwoodToday**
CollingwoodToday reports that councillors are pushing for expanded transit links across Simcoe County. The push follows a new Simcoe County Transit Service Delivery Review. That review says the region’s mix of county and municipal transit systems is fragmented. The review names three results of that split: gaps in coverage, separate fare structures, and inconsistent accessibility standards. Coverage gaps are the places the mixed systems do not reach as one network. Separate fare structures mean a rider can meet a different fare when the trip crosses from a county service to a municipal service, or the other way. Inconsistent accessibility standards mean the access rules are not the same from one of those systems to the next. The councillors’ push, as CollingwoodToday frames it, is for links that reach farther across the county than that fragmented mix now does. On October 2 this show was still on county roads and services as an open question; today’s new fact is the review’s finding of fragmentation, named with those three results, and the councillors’ push for wider links. Source: [collingwoodtoday.ca](https://www.collingwoodtoday.ca/local-news/councillors-push-for-expanded-transit-links-across-simcoe-county-12867312)

**Probus month marked with flag raising in Collingwood: CollingwoodToday**
Collingwood marked Probus Month with a flag raising, CollingwoodToday reports. More than 2,300 Collingwood-area residents are part of the 10 local clubs. The flag raising is the public mark of the month in town. The membership figure is the Collingwood-area total across those 10 clubs, not a count for a single club. The 10 clubs are the local set that holds that membership. The month being marked is Probus Month, and the place of the flag raising is Collingwood. Source: [collingwoodtoday.ca](https://www.collingwoodtoday.ca/local-news/probus-month-marked-with-flag-raising-in-collingwood-12872630)

### Council & County
**Clearview: Vote online, by phone or at an Election Services Centre Oct. 12 to 26: BarrieToday**
BarrieToday reports that Clearview electors can vote online, by phone, or at an Election Services Centre from October 12 to October 26. That is a 15-day window that starts Monday and ends on the same election day Collingwood uses, October 26. Voter information letters mailed in September have everything electors need to vote. Those letters include voting credentials. They include the voting website. They include the telephone number. They include the credentials needed to cast a vote. An elector using the internet uses the website and the credentials in that September letter. An elector using the phone uses the telephone number and the credentials in that letter. An elector who wants a staffed place uses an Election Services Centre inside the October 12 to 26 window. The three routes — online, phone, and an Election Services Centre — are all open across that same stretch of days. Source: [barrietoday.com](https://www.barrietoday.com/2026-municipal-election-news/clearview-vote-online-by-phone-or-at-an-election-services-centre-oct-12-to-26-12868041)
