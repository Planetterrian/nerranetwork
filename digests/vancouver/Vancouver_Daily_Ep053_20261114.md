### Top Stories
**B.C. arbitrator imposes 1st union contract for Amazon workers in North America: CBC British Columbia**
A B.C. arbitrator has imposed the first union contract for Amazon workers in North America, and the workforce it covers is in Delta. A union representing about 800 Amazon workers there says those workers are now covered by the first binding collective agreement for the retailing giant's employees anywhere in North America. The union is the party making that claim; the CBC report frames the deal as imposed, not negotiated to a voluntary signature. Castanet carries the same development as a contract imposed in B.C., the first of its kind for Amazon workers on this continent. For the Delta group, the change is that a collective agreement is now in force rather than still being sought. **B.C.'s Woodfibre LNG gets $200M federal cash injection: CBC British Columbia**
The federal government is investing $200 million in British Columbia's Woodfibre LNG project, spread over three years. Energy Minister Tim Hodgson says that funding will help diversify Canada's energy exports. The money is a federal cash injection into a B.C. liquefied natural gas project, attributed by CBC to the minister's export-diversification case. The figure and the three-year window are the concrete change; the stated purpose is Ottawa's, not an independent finding in the report. Source: [cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/woodfibre-lng-federal-investment-9.7421686?cmp=rss)

**Surrey Police Service doesn't have enough officers to fully take over policing in the city: Yahoo News Canada**
The Surrey Police Service will not be ready to fully take over policing in the city as scheduled, Global News has learned. The service does not have the staffing levels needed for that takeover. RCMP members will have to work voluntary overtime shifts to fill the gaps, which means the municipal force's shortfall is being covered by Mounties choosing extra shifts rather than by a fully staffed Surrey Police Service on the planned date. Catherine Urquhart is the reporter on the Global News account carried by Yahoo News Canada. The practical effect for Surrey is that the scheduled full handover does not happen on the existing timetable because the officer count is not there. Source: [ca.news.yahoo.com](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/surrey-police-doesnt-enough-officers-005734063.html)

**Following federal cuts, Immigrant Services Society of B.C. to close its Richmond branch: CBC British Columbia**
After more than 20 years, the Immigrant Services Society of B.C. says it will close its Richmond branch, and the society ties that closure to federal cuts. CBC describes the shutdown as a major blow in a city where well over half of residents were born abroad. The branch has been open for over two decades; the decision now is to end it. People in Richmond who have used that office for settlement help are the ones who lose the local door, in a municipality the report characterizes by how large its immigrant population is. Source: [cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/immigrant-services-society-bc-richmond-closes-9.7420991?cmp=rss)

### The Closer Look
**Rangers get four goals in second, top Canucks for fifth win in six games: NHL.com**
The New York Rangers beat the Vancouver Canucks 5-1 at Rogers Arena on Friday, and the game turned in a four-goal second period. Richmond's Glenn Gawdin scored his first two NHL goals in that frame. He is 29, he was playing his 15th NHL game since a debut in 2020-21, and it was his second game with New York after a call-up from Hartford of the American Hockey League on Wednesday, NHL.com reports. He had what he called 10 to 12 family and friends in the stands. "There's a lot that goes through your mind over the years and credit to the circle around me, credit to my wife, they push me when I need it," Gawdin said. "The success tonight, I think they have an equal part in that, so it was a special night." Pacific North Hockey notes he wore number 15, scored the two goals 3:10 apart, and that the second came at 16:10 of the middle period, after Alexis Lafreniere's 10th of the season sat between them. NHL.com times Gawdin's first at 13:00, on a rebound of Leevi Merilainen's clearing attempt that bounced off Filip Chytil. ESPN says the second made it 5-1 with 3:50 left in the period.
Elias Pettersson had opened the scoring at 12:13 of the first, tipping a Zeev Buium point shot, his fifth goal of the season and his first 5-on-5 goal since January 13, ending a 56-game drought at even strength. Gabe Perreault tied it at 18:47. Oliver Bjorkstrand put New York ahead 2-1 on the power play at 8:21 of the second, from J.T. Miller, and Lafreniere made it 4-1 at 15:14 before Gawdin's second. Brock Boeser assisted on Pettersson's goal for his 500th career point — 233 goals and 267 assists — in his 650th game, the eighth player to reach that mark for Vancouver. "Definitely a cool achievement," Boeser said. "It doesn't stop there. Got to keep going, continue to be a leader." Merilainen stopped 25 shots. Dylan Garand stopped 27 for the Rangers; he is from Victoria, played junior in Kamloops, and had an estimated 50 family and friends in the building. Miller had two assists. Pacific North Hockey and Canucks Army both report that the Rogers Arena crowd booed him when he touched the puck. Rangers coach Mike Sullivan credited Miller's corner battle on the tying goal with turning the night. Canucks coach Manny Malhotra said the second period was "a string of turnover after turnover after turnover." Pettersson said the team started well and did not follow it in the second.
The Canucks fell to 3-17-1. They have lost seven straight, they are 1-16-1 in their past 18 games, and Pacific North Hockey puts them at seven points, four behind the Chicago Blackhawks. The Rangers improved to 12-8-1, went 3-1-0 on a four-game road trip, and have won five of their past six. ESPN notes it was New York's first win this season after allowing the first goal; they had been 0-7-1 in that spot. The Canucks play at Vegas on Sunday night. The Rangers host Montreal on Monday night.
