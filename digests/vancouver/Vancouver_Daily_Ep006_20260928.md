**Concord Pacific's energy arm buys Saskatchewan's largest wind project: Daily Hive Vancouver**
Concord Green Energy, the renewable division of Vancouver-based Concord Pacific, has acquired the Golden South Wind Project near Assiniboia, Saskatchewan, Daily Hive reports. The site has 50 turbines, 210 megawatts of installed capacity and 200 megawatts of contracted capacity. It is Saskatchewan's largest operating wind project by both turbine count and installed capacity. Golden South began commercial operations in March 2022 under a 25-year power purchase agreement with SaskPower. Concord says the project generates enough electricity for the equivalent annual needs of up to 100,000 homes, and the purchase takes Concord Green Energy's operating portfolio to about 543 megawatts.

### Both Sides: ICBC's no-fault review after the election call
What is being decided is not a new ICBC rule today. It is whether a special committee of the B.C. legislature, already reviewing ICBC's no-fault policy, still has a path after the election call. The Vancouver Sun reports that victims, advocates and health-care providers made submissions and oral presentations to that committee this week.

Their strongest case for being heard is that the record is already in. They came to speak on the no-fault policy, and the paper reports they connect that work to their own ICBC claims. The reason attached to them in the report is that the presentations happened this week, the claims are theirs, and they want the committee to finish hearing them.

The Vancouver Sun says the election call crushed those hopes of being heard. The paper reports the presenters are now left wondering what will happen to the submissions and to their ICBC claims. That is the consequence the Sun attributes to the call.

The fact the report states outright, without either reading of motive, is that the submissions and oral presentations reached the special committee this week, and that the election call is what left those presenters asking what becomes of that work and of their claims. Source: [vancouversun.com](https://vancouversun.com/news/local-news/bc-election-call-crushed-victims-hopes-of-being-heard-by-committee-reviewing-icbcs-no-fault-policy)
