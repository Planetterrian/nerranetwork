**District of Squamish rejects $142M Woodfibre LNG deal:** CBC British Columbia. The District of Squamish council turned down a negotiated 10-year tax settlement that would have delivered $142 million from Woodfibre LNG to the Sea-to-Sky community. The decision ends the proposed agreement. The settlement would have seen Woodfibre LNG contribute the funds over the decade in exchange for tax certainty. Council voted against the package after negotiations concluded. **Vancouver company to acquire all eight Accent Inns and Hotel Zed hotels:** Daily Hive Vancouver. Synvest Group of Companies will buy the eight Accent Inns and Hotel Zed properties across British Columbia with the transfer expected at the end of November. The hotels total roughly 800 rooms. All existing bookings will be honoured and operations continue normally. The acquisition covers five Accent Inns properties in Richmond, Burnaby, Victoria, Kelowna and Kamloops plus Hotel Zed locations in Victoria, Kelowna and Tofino along with the ROAR restaurant at Hotel Zed Tofino. The largest property is Accent Inns Vancouver Airport in Richmond with over 200 rooms. Mandy Farmer CEO of the selling company said the day was emotional and that it mattered to find a new owner who understood the company culture. Azim Virani founder and principal of Synvest said the family has always viewed hospitality as being about people and that Synvest wants to work with existing teams to preserve the established approach. **VPD Seizes Record 328.55 kg Cocaine Worth $5.8M in Shipping Container:** VancouverPD. Vancouver police seized 328.55 kilograms of cocaine valued at an estimated $5.8 million from a shipping container in southeast Vancouver the largest such seizure in the city. Police were called to a Vancouver-based company on July 21 after staff located 14 duffle bags of drugs hidden amongst boxes of t-shirts that formed a legitimate order. Sergeant Travis Fraser of the VPD Drug Unit said the business was shocked and called police right away and is in no way associated with the seized cocaine. The amount of drugs as well as the stamps and packaging clearly indicates organized crime is behind the shipment he said. The investigation showed the shipping container originated in Honduras on May 18 2026 was loaded onto a vessel stopped in Jamaica on June 6 2026 and arrived in Montreal on July 5 2026. Fraser said police believe the cocaine was supposed to be removed prior to its arrival at the company. VPD continues to liaise with law enforcement and port authority counterparts in Canada Honduras Jamaica the United States Drug Enforcement Agency and the RCMP. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/VancouverPD/status/2102571228779475130)

### Both Sides: Woodfibre LNG tax settlement
Supporters of the deal argued it would provide stable long-term revenue for local services and infrastructure without raising resident taxes allowing the district to plan capital projects with certainty. They noted the funds would arrive over a decade giving the municipality predictable income that could support roads parks and emergency services in the Sea-to-Sky region. Opponents maintained that the project carries unacceptable environmental and safety risks to the Sea-to-Sky corridor and that accepting the funds would lock the community into supporting expanded LNG operations for a decade. They pointed to potential impacts on air quality marine traffic and emergency response capacity along the narrow corridor between Squamish and Whistler. Both sides accept that the project would generate substantial tax revenue if approved. The district will now seek other revenue options or revised negotiations; no further steps or timelines were stated in the coverage. Council members who voted against the package emphasized that the community retains the ability to pursue different fiscal arrangements that do not tie local finances to a single industrial project for ten years. Those who favored the settlement highlighted that rejecting it removes a concrete revenue stream that had already been negotiated between the district and the company.
