> **A one-million-barrel-a-day oil pipeline to the B.C. coast is now a national-interest project.**

**What You Need to Know:** Ottawa and Alberta have designated a proposed pipeline carrying one million barrels of oil a day from Alberta to the B.C. coast as the first project in the national interest. The Vancouver Sun describes the line as a $44-billion project that Prime Minister Carney tied to jobs, trade beyond the United States, and Canada's energy position. The designation is the decision that landed; it is not a pump-price change for drivers this morning.

### Both Sides: Gas tax cuts and price caps at the pumps
Premier David Eby and the B.C. NDP are offering a gas-tax cut and price caps at the pumps if the party is re-elected. Global News reports Eby said the measures would save the average two-car family up to $100 per month and would cost the provincial treasury about $250 million. My Powell River Now reports the same pledge: a gas-tax cut and price caps if the NDP is re-elected. This is a campaign promise. It is not a tax change already on the books.

The case for the pledge, as Eby makes it, starts with the household fuel bill. He said an average two-car family could save up to $100 a month. The Vancouver Sun reports that small business groups welcome the proposal. Their published reason lines up with his: people and businesses who buy fuel would pay less if the tax cut and the caps took effect. That welcome is the strongest non-government case for the pledge in today's coverage.

The case against, in the same Vancouver Sun report, is that at least one economist said the policy will have little impact. The objection does not deny that pump prices matter. It says this design is unlikely to move the price drivers pay by much. Eby is selling a monthly saving. The economist's reported view is that the saving may not arrive.

What both treatments accept is that the tax cut and the caps are a re-election offer, with a treasury cost Eby put at about $250 million, not a completed change at the station. What happens next is the election. Drivers do not get a new pump price from this pledge today.
