### Top Stories
**Cruise calls at Canada Place jump 20 percent in a record 2026 season: Vancouver Sun**
The Port of Vancouver’s Canada Place terminal recorded 1.4 million passenger visits and 358 cruise ship calls between February and October, the Vancouver Sun reports. Those figures are a 20 percent jump and a record season for 2026. Source: [vancouversun.com](https://vancouversun.com/news/vancouver-cruise-ship-record-season-2026)

**New Westminster fire leaves 24 households without a home: Vancouver Sun**
The Vancouver Sun reports that a New Westminster apartment blaze has left an uncertain future for 24 households. Seniors, people with mobility issues, and families with children lost everything and must find new housing. The paper describes lives as upended. Source: [vancouversun.com](https://vancouversun.com/news/new-westminster-apartment-fire-24-households-displaced)

**Eby warns of health-care privatization; Conservatives call it fearmongering: CBC**
On Monday, B.C. NDP Leader David Eby took his campaign on the offensive and warned that a B.C. Conservative government would move toward health-care privatization, CBC reports. The Conservatives say he is fearmongering. Canada’s National Observer reports the same exchange: Eby slammed U.S.-style health-care privatization, and the Conservatives called the warning fearmongering. Source: [cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/bc-ndp-health-care-privatization-9.7378216)

**About 50 people gather in downtown Vancouver over protests in India: CBC**
Roughly 50 people gathered in downtown Vancouver in solidarity with people protesting in India, and to demonstrate their right to protest peacefully, CBC reports. The gathering followed protests in India that CBC says resulted in thousands of arrests. **Fisheries asks for a sample from a swordfish on Haida Gwaii: Global BC**
A six-foot swordfish that washed up on Haida Gwaii is now the subject of a flesh sample. Licensed boat captain Quinn Barabash told Global News he contacted the Department of Fisheries and was asked to take the sample, and he hopes decomposition had not gone too far for it to be useful. Scientists presume only two earlier sightings in B.C. Isabella Morgante, a PhD candidate at the University of British Columbia’s Institute for the Oceans and Fisheries, told Global News the find is rare and, whatever the path of this fish, a warning and an indicator of warming oceans in B.C. Source: [globalnews.ca](https://globalnews.ca/news/12099335/swordfish-haida-gwaii/)

**A B.C. flute turns up in a Yukon canoe barrel, five years on: CBC**
Jun Takahashi, of British Columbia, has his flute back five years after it floated away down a Yukon river, CBC reports. Someone in the Yukon bush found a canoe barrel of his mildew-covered camping gear, and the flute was in it. Source: [cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/north/b-c-man-reunited-with-beloved-flute-5-years-after-it-floated-away-down-a-yukon-river-9.7376163?cmp=rss)
