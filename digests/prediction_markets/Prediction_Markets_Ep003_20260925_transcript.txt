Monthly Weather Review 2013 describes an improved version of Ensemble Kernel-Density
Model Output Statistics, EKD-MOS.
Its model output statistics equations are multiple linear regressions that relate observations
to predictors built from the Ensemble mean.
It's spread skill link is a one-term linear regression that predicts how accurate that
mean should be given the spread.
The method runs those equations on each member, fits a probability density with kernels,
then rescales the density spread to what the skill relationship predicts.
