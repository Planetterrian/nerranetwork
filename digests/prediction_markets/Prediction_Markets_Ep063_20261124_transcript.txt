Combining for adaptation means you do not know which single forecast will be best, so you

Combining for improvement means you think the blend can beat every candidate, including
the best one.
Use the wrong one of those two jobs, and you can manufacture the puzzle yourself.
The fix that paper offers is a multi-level after strategy.
After is sequential waiting.
The multi-level version treats the simple average as just another candidate, so the scheme
can drop back to equal weights when the estimated ones are too noisy, and it can still lean
on a strong forecast when the record supports the lean.
A 2023 paper, also on archive, says part of the puzzle is the test, not the forecast.
The way these combinations are usually produced leaves accuracy tests with low power and without proper size control.
