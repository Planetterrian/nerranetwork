> **European Union goods trade with Southeast Asia reached two hundred seventy five billion euros last year, strengthening supply chains for six ASEAN countries.**

**What You Need to Know:** The European Union closed in on bilateral free trade deals with six of eleven ASEAN members, with agreements already in force for Singapore and Vietnam and further signings expected by early 2027. Trade volume grew more than fifty percent from two hundred seventeen billion euros in 2016. No ASEAN-wide deal is in sight.

### Lead
**As the EU advances free trade with Southeast Asia, why is there no ASEAN-wide deal?:** DW Asia
The European Union reached substantial agreement on a free trade deal with the Philippines this week. Negotiations with Indonesia concluded and Jakarta targets signing in October for early 2027 implementation. Deals with Thailand and Malaysia are expected by the end of this year or early 2027. Free trade agreements with Singapore and Vietnam are already in force. EU-Southeast Asia goods trade reached two hundred seventy four point nine billion euros last year, up six percent from the prior year and more than fifty percent from one hundred seventy seven point nine billion euros in 2016. ASEAN is now the EU's third-largest goods trading partner outside Europe. Chris Humphrey, executive director of the EU-ASEAN Business Council, described EU engagement with ASEAN on trade and investment issues as at an all-time high. The Philippines is chair of ASEAN and hosted the ASEAN-EU Business Summit this week where both sides pledged cooperation against geopolitical tensions and unilateral trade actions. Brussels is expanding its network of trade agreements globally as it looks to expand to new markets and form more resilient supply chains. **(LEAD) Russia invites N. Korean leader to visit: TASS:** Yonhap
Russia officially invited North Korean leader Kim Jong-un to visit the country. The schedule will be coordinated through diplomatic channels. Russian President Vladimir Putin extended the invitation after summits in Pyongyang in June 2024 and at a military parade in China last September. Kim has visited Russia twice before but never Moscow. Pyongyang and Moscow have deepened their alignment since North Korea deployed troops to support Russia's war against Ukraine. Speculation that Kim could visit Moscow within this year grew after North Korean Foreign Minister Choe Son-hui made an unexpected visit to Russia in July at the invitation of Russia's foreign ministry. ### Progress Watch
**Japan-developed optical lattice clocks lead bid to redefine the second:** mainichi.jp
Optical lattice clocks developed in Japan lose or gain only about one second over thirty billion years, far surpassing cesium atomic clocks now used to define the second. The General Conference on Weights and Measures will discuss remaining issues next month with a new definition possibly proposed as early as twenty thirty. Optical lattice clocks trap thousands of atoms individually in an egg carton grid formed by intersecting laser beams. Hidetoshi Katori, a University of Tokyo professor who developed a strontium optical lattice clock, is widely regarded as a strong potential candidate for the Nobel Prize in Physics. A redefinition of a second could enable more precise experiments and observations in physics and astronomy, including research into dark matter. Source: [mainichi.jp](https://mainichi.jp/english/articles/20260924/p2g/00m/0sc/040000c)
