### Lead
**Japan’s 10-year bond coupon tops budgeted rate in ‘sci-fi’ spike: Asahi**
Japan’s Finance Ministry set the coupon on newly issued 10-year government bonds at 3.1 percent a year at an auction on October 6, the highest level in 30 years. The rate is above the 3 percent the ministry assumed when it calculated debt-servicing costs for the fiscal 2026 budget. The coupon is the interest the government promises investors, so the rise means higher payments on new debt and could narrow fiscal room. Long-term rates have risen by more than one percentage point since Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi took office last October, which the report says is due in part to market concerns over her fiscal stance. A senior Finance Ministry official said he had thought it was “something out of science fiction” that the coupon would catch the budgeted rate in the middle of a fiscal year. The ministry says the higher coupon will not immediately impede financing: in the first six months of fiscal 2026 it issued bonds below the 3 percent budgeted rate. The coupon had been 2.7 percent in July, after 2.4 percent in April and 2.1 percent from January to March. Source: [asahi.com](https://www.asahi.com/ajw/articles/16946592)

### Across the Region
**How did a Pakistan-linked spy network target Navy’s Eastern Command, and how was it cracked?: The Hindu**
Andhra Pradesh’s Counter Intelligence Cell, working with Naval Intelligence and anti-terrorist squads in 10 states, has broken what investigators call a Pakistan-sponsored espionage network aimed at the Indian Navy, with the Eastern Naval Command a particular target. The crackdown, code-named Operation Firewall-2026, identified 18 key suspects, including 13 naval personnel, in Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa, Delhi, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Bihar and Chhattisgarh. Two people are in custody: sailor Pradeep Mukherjee, arrested on October 3 at INS Agrani in Coimbatore, and civilian contract driver Annam Sai Vara Prasad, engaged for naval officers at the Visakhapatnam base. Investigators allege Pakistani operatives used fake women’s profiles on Facebook, including “Joshita Pall” and “Anvi Mehta,” then moved conversations to WhatsApp and sought ship and submarine movements. They allege the approach used relationship promises, explicit content and payments, including cryptocurrency. **Indonesia's VP likely didn't finish high school and it could cost him his job: ABC News Australia**
Indonesia’s Constitutional Court on Tuesday night dismissed a challenge to Vice-President Gibran Rakabuming Raka’s educational eligibility for the 2024 election. Election law requires presidential and vice-presidential candidates to have completed senior high school or an equivalent qualification. Petitioners, including the Independent Election Monitoring Committee, the Ummah Party, former deputy law minister Denny Indrayana and the Indonesian Military Retired Personnel Forum, argued that the paper he submitted was not a graduation certificate but a Ministry of Education supporting letter based on a Certificate of Attendance and a transcript from UTS College in Sydney. Their lawyer, Bambang Widjojanto, told the court there had been “a structured, systematic and deliberate process concealed through manipulation.” The court said the matter could no longer be resolved as a presidential election dispute because Mr Gibran was sworn in in October 2024, and that only presidential and vice-presidential candidates can challenge the result. ### The Region and the World
**Four Indonesian seamen return home after Somali pirate ordeal: The Straits Times**
Four Indonesian seamen arrived in Tangerang on October 6 after more than five months held hostage on the Palau-flagged tanker MT Honour 25, hijacked in April. They were among 17 crew rescued a week earlier when Puntland maritime police stormed the ship; a Puntland police statement said five crew — three Pakistanis, an Indian and a Myanmar national — had already been killed. Foreign Minister Arrmanatha Nasir met them at Soekarno-Hatta airport and thanked skipper Ashari Samadikun, who apologised in tears for not bringing every crew member home. Puntland police said the tanker carried 18,500 barrels of oil, that 16 pirates were detained, and that others fled inland. The report says Somali piracy has seen at least 15 attacks since April, the highest number since 2013, and that several pirates arrested days earlier on the tanker Sibu 1 escaped while being transferred to jail. ### Both Sides: Should South Australia ban good-character references at sentencing?
South Australia’s government is proposing to abolish good-character references at sentencing for every criminal offence in the state’s courts. Attorney-General Kyam Maher says consultation begins in the coming days and a bill should be introduced in the first half of next year. He says a review was a recommendation of the Royal Commission into Domestic, Family and Sexual Violence, and that the change would bring South Australia into line with New South Wales and Western Australia. He described child sex offences as having a profound and lasting impact on victim-survivors.

Survivor advocate Harrison James, who co-founded the Your Reference Ain’t Relevant campaign four years ago to ban these references in child sex cases, says the proposal would make a tangible difference. His reason is the courtroom itself. He says there is nothing more dehumanising than sitting there while the person who harmed you is described as kind, generous and a good person, after you have survived the worst thing that person did to you.

The case for leaving a path open is the law the state government describes as still in force. An offender may tender evidence of good character, or call friends, family or employers, to argue for leniency. Judges already may not consider that evidence when sentencing a child sex offender if the suggested good character assisted the offence. The same law still allows it if the offending happened without the aid of that reputation, or if the person had no prior convictions. That is the allowance the proposal would close for all offences, not only child sex cases.

Both accounts start from that present rule: outside the assisted-offending limit, good-character evidence can still reach a sentencing court. Next is the consultation Maher has set for the coming days, then the bill he expects in the first half of next year. Source: [abc.net.au](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-07/good-character-references-to-be-banned-sa-courts/107238760)

### Progress Watch
**(6th LD) Nuri rocket deploys 5 satellites for S. Korea's first microsatellite constellation: Yonhap**
South Korea’s 200-ton Nuri rocket lifted off from the Naro Space Center in Goheung at 12:25 p.m. on Wednesday and, about 20 minutes later, had deployed five NEONSAT microsatellites for the country’s first domestically launched microsatellite constellation, the Korea AeroSpace Administration said. Nine of 10 CubeSat secondary payloads separated as planned; PERSAT-02, from startup Quaternion, did not. NEONSAT No. 6 made initial contact with King Sejong Station in Antarctica. It was Nuri’s fifth launch and fourth success in a row after a failed maiden flight in 2021, taking the success rate from 75 percent to 80 percent, administrator Oh Tae-seok said.
