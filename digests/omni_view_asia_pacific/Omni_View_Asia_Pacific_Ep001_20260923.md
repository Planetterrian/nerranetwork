Japan is negotiating access for its startups to NATO’s defense technology innovation program, and could become the first non-member nation to take part. Nikkei Asia says the talks are aimed at boosting Japan’s defense capabilities through international collaboration. ### Both Sides: Shelling on the Pakistan-Afghanistan frontier
What is being contested is a reported attack on Pakistani border posts, and whether Afghan Taliban forces carried it out. The accounts come from the two sides of the frontier, as AP News reports them. No court, joint inquiry or third government is cited in that report as the body deciding between them.

Pakistani officials say Afghan Taliban forces shelled Pakistan’s border posts. That allegation, attributed by AP News to Pakistani authorities, is their case in its strongest available form: the shelling happened, and Taliban forces did it. The reason their account asks to be believed, on the face of the report, is that it is an official report of an attack on their own posts.

Kabul denies the incident. That denial, as AP News records it, is the other side’s case in its strongest available form: the shelling Pakistani officials describe did not take place. The denial asks to be weighed as a direct rejection of the allegation, from the authority accused of ordering or carrying out the fire.

The point the same report places around both positions is the condition of the frontier itself. AP News says the clash highlights ongoing tensions along the Afghanistan-Pakistan frontier. Listeners can keep the Pakistani officials’ allegation and Kabul’s denial side by side, each tied to the side that made it, without treating either statement as a finding.
