### Lead
**North Korea launches ballistic missile after Seoul demands apology over wounded soldiers: The Guardian**
South Korea's military said North Korea fired a ballistic missile from the Wonsan area at about 6:30 a.m. Saturday toward the East Sea, also known as the Sea of Japan, and that it flew more than 700 kilometres. South Korea and Japan both reported the launch. The Guardian tied the firing to the previous day, when Seoul demanded an apology over wounded soldiers, Pyongyang rejected the accusation that its mines had wounded South Korean soldiers, and the sister of Kim Jong-un denounced that claim about the DMZ. The South Korean military said Seoul and Washington were analysing the test for details. **Hardened narco-trafficking kingpin Navpreet Singh deported from Turkiye: The Hindu**

Shah called the return a historic milestone of the Modi government, said agencies had unmasked that identity and secured the deportation, and said Singh was put behind bars in connection with cases he is accused of. Shah said the Modi government is committed to destroying every narco-cartel before December 2029. ### The Region and the World
**Strategic partnership key to Taiwan-U.K. cooperation: Focus Taiwan**
Taiwan's Deputy Minister of Economic Affairs Cynthia Kiang told CNA in London on Friday that a strategic partnership is key to cooperation with the United Kingdom, after she co-hosted the 2026 economic and trade dialogue with U.K. Minister of State for Trade Lord Anas Sarwar. The vice-ministerial talks have alternated between Taipei and London since 1991, and her delegation of executives from nearly 20 Taiwanese companies was the largest in 10 years. Kiang said geopolitical tension and supply-chain shifts are pushing economies toward reliable strategic partnerships, called the U.K. one such partner, and said President Lai Ching-te's five priority industries — semiconductors, artificial intelligence, defense, cybersecurity, and next-generation communications — sit beside eight British high-growth sectors. She said the U.K. leads in research and innovation while Taiwan is strong in manufacturing and commercial use, and that two export-oriented economies can complement each other. Sarwar said the trading relationship is delivering growth, jobs, and investment, and that the talks identified openings in digital trade, clean energy, and advanced manufacturing. Source: [focustaiwan.tw](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202610030008)

### Both Sides: Should Congress have replaced its Punjab president before the 2027 election?
The All India Congress Committee ended months of uncertainty on October 2 by appointing MLA Pargat Singh president of the Punjab Pradesh Congress Committee, after Amarinder Singh Raja Warring formally resigned the post to Rahul Gandhi. Warring had held it since April 22, 2022. The Assembly election is due in early 2027. Singh is a former minister and a former captain of the Indian men's hockey team.

The case for the change is the one disgruntled leaders had pressed in public. The Hindu reports a power tussle, especially between Warring and former Chief Minister Charanjit Singh Channi, on display for several months. In a July reshuffle Warring stayed president while Channi was given the election campaign committee. Channi and some other leaders were reportedly unhappy with that restructuring, and they and their supporters demanded Warring's removal. Last month the central leadership replaced state in-charge Bhupesh Baghel with Sachin Pilot, which the paper describes as giving way to pressure from those leaders. Party sources told The Hindu that Singh was chosen largely for a clean, non-controversial image that made him acceptable across factions, and that the pick is a balancing act meant to bridge the two camps.

The case for the arrangement just undone is the July decision that kept Warring in the presidency he had held since 2022, while the campaign role went to Channi. That was the structure the later resignation reversed. The recorded outcome is Warring's resignation and Singh's appointment. The next date the reporting gives is the Assembly election due in early 2027. Source: [thehindu.com](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/punjab/punjab-pradesh-congress-committee-new-president-pargat-singh/article71539767.ece)

**(LEAD) S. Korea's Choi Ji-un wins gold in boys' dinghy sailing at Asian Games: Yonhap**
South Korea's Choi Ji-un, 15, won boys' dinghy gold on Saturday at Kaiyoh Yacht Harbor in Gamagori and became the youngest gold medalist in the country's Asian Games delegation, Yonhap reported. Fourth after 10 races, he placed first in the 11th and second in the 12th, finished on 25 points tied with Singapore's Goh Ian, and took the tiebreak on higher finishes. Cheng de Villemor Salgado Tiago of Hong Kong won bronze. It is South Korea's first boys' sailing gold at the Asian Games since Incheon in 2014. The country had won the men's dinghy gold the previous day. Source: [en.yna.co.kr](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261003002451320)
