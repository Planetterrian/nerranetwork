### Tesla First Principles

Battery cell faults rarely announce themselves until thermal runaway begins, yet the chemistry behind lithium plating has been understood for decades. When excess lithium deposits on the anode during fast charging or low-temperature operation, the metal can pierce the separator and create internal shorts. The UK safety report on Powerwall 2 simply applies that known mechanism to a consumer product that must remain unattended for years.

The regulatory response therefore centers on detection and removal rather than redesign. Tesla can remotely discharge suspect units because the packs already carry cellular connectivity and cloud monitoring. This capability turns a potential recall into a targeted intervention that limits both fire risk and customer disruption.

The larger implication is that stationary storage inherits the same cell-aging physics as vehicles but operates under different duty cycles. A Powerwall cycles daily and sits at partial state of charge for long periods, conditions that can accelerate plating if the battery management system does not actively counteract it. Future pack designs will need tighter control loops or different cell formulations if residential storage is to scale without repeated safety interventions.
