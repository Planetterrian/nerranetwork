### Tesla First Principles
Solar roof tiles must satisfy roofing codes, electrical codes, structural loads, fire ratings, and waterproofing simultaneously in custom sizes that vary from roof to roof. That multi-code requirement raises per-unit engineering and certification costs compared with separate panels mounted on a conventional roof. When volume stays low, those fixed costs never spread across enough units to reach the original price target. Tesla’s experience showed the gap between claimed economics and real output. The company aimed for 1,000 installs per week yet delivered roughly 3,000 total over seven years. At that scale the integrated product could not compete on cost with standard panels plus a separate roof, even before policy or installer-skill constraints entered the picture.

The remaining market for building-integrated photovoltaics now depends on whether other manufacturers can solve the same integration problem at higher volume or whether the category stays limited to niche historic-district and design-driven projects where appearance commands a premium. Two of the four hurdles of physics, trades, money, and policy have shifted since 2024, which changes the math for any new entrant. The category still requires a product that simultaneously meets multiple regulatory regimes while delivering acceptable energy yield and long-term durability. Manufacturers chasing the same goal now include solar facades, solar glazing, and flexible panels that adhere to any rooftop.

Tesla’s decade-long experiment ended without a press release or farewell blog post. The product page simply redirects to ordinary solar panels. Industry estimates put the total at roughly 3,000 US installations in seven years against a target that would have produced more than 350,000 units if the weekly pace had been met. The honest answer for why more roofs are not made of solar tiles has four parts and none of them is that the technology is bad. The remaining players must now decide whether the economics can close at any volume or whether the segment remains a premium niche.
