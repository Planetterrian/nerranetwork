### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today’s headlines, the decision by a major sovereign fund to exit its Tesla stake raises a narrower question than valuation debates usually allow: what concrete operating metric would have to improve for that capital to return.

The filing shows the sales occurred without any disclosed change in Tesla’s vehicle margins or energy-deployment volumes during the same quarter. Historical data from other large funds indicate that sustained quarterly growth above 15 percent in energy-storage deployments has previously coincided with re-entry by similar investors within two quarters.

Tesla’s vertical integration of battery and power electronics gives it a direct lever on that metric through cell production ramp and Megapack utilization rates. If those rates continue to rise while vehicle gross margins stabilize, the same balance-sheet logic that prompted the exit could support re-entry without any shift in narrative or macro conditions.
