### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the latest longevity study showing Tesla vehicles with a 4.6% chance of reaching 250,000 miles forces a closer look at how electric powertrains actually age compared with internal-combustion designs.

The data set of 174 million vehicles places Tesla near the industry average and ahead of several established premium brands. That outcome sits in tension with assumptions that battery degradation or electronic complexity would produce shorter service lives. The metric tracks real high-mileage survival rather than warranty claims or predicted failure rates.

Tesla's vertical integration of motors, inverters, and battery packs allows it to iterate on thermal management and software calibration in ways that traditional suppliers cannot match. Continued refinement of those systems could widen the longevity gap over successive model years, shifting the economics of fleet ownership and used-vehicle markets.
