### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today’s headlines, the decision to end Model S and Model X production to prioritize Optimus manufacturing challenges the usual view that Tesla should keep every legacy product alive as long as it sells.

The two sedans occupied factory floor space and engineering hours that now shift to a robot program whose output targets are measured in units per hour rather than vehicles per week. Production data from Giga Texas already shows how quickly line reconfiguration can occur when capacity is reallocated, and the same vertical-integration approach that cut battery costs on the 4680 cell line can be applied to actuator and sensor production for Optimus.

If the robot program reaches volume targets, the freed resources could generate higher long-term returns than continued low-volume sedan updates, but the move also removes two platforms that once anchored Tesla’s brand image in the luxury segment.
