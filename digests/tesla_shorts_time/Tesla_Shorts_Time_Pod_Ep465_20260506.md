### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the steady addition of real-world Semi operators alongside new regulatory clearances suggests that fleet data and policy support matter more for commercial EV progress than isolated vehicle specs.

The list of confirmed users now includes PepsiCo running roughly 50 units since late 2022 plus multiple smaller pilots that began in 2024 and 2025. When these deployments are paired with state-level incentives reaching $120,000–$175,000 per truck and California's recent approval of autonomous semis, the effective cost and operational barriers drop faster than hardware improvements alone would achieve.

Tesla's ability to combine its own battery production, software stack, and charging network with these external tailwinds gives it a practical route to scale in freight that pure-play truck makers without equivalent vertical integration will find harder to match.
