### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the decision to pilot neural assessment games for hiring at Giga Berlin challenges the assumption that traditional resumes reliably predict performance in advanced manufacturing and engineering roles.

The approach replaces document review with standardized tasks that measure problem-solving speed and pattern recognition under time pressure. Early internal data from the pilot will need to show measurable differences in later job performance or retention rates before wider adoption. One concrete benchmark will be whether candidates selected through games demonstrate higher success rates on first-year project milestones compared with prior cohorts hired through conventional screening.

If the method proves effective, Tesla's vertical integration of talent pipelines could shorten the time from hiring to contribution on programs such as Optimus actuator calibration or FSD edge-case labeling. That shift would matter most in locations where rapid scaling of specialized teams determines whether production targets for new hardware are met on schedule.
