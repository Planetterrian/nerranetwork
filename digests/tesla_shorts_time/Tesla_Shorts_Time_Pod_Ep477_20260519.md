### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the decision to abandon a planned factory in India challenges the assumption that Tesla can simply replicate its Shanghai success in every large emerging market.

The core constraint appears to be mismatched expectations around land allocation, power reliability, and local content rules that add months or years to timelines. In India these factors have historically stretched setup periods well beyond the two-year window Tesla achieved in China, where central coordination and existing supplier clusters compressed the process.

Tesla's vertical integration playbook offers one path forward by bringing more component production in-house and reducing dependence on local partners, yet that approach still requires stable grid capacity and predictable permitting. Without those foundations, even advanced manufacturing methods cannot fully offset the friction of entering a new market at scale.
