### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the largest single Semi order yet suggests that electric long-haul trucking is moving from demonstration projects to actual fleet economics faster than many assumed.

WattEV’s plan pairs 370 trucks with dedicated megawatt chargers that deliver roughly 300 miles of range in 30 minutes, while the operator has already accumulated millions of electric miles on earlier deployments in Southern California ports. That combination of vehicle range, charging speed, and integrated leasing removes the two biggest practical barriers—refueling time and capital risk—for carriers that log high annual miles.

If the model scales, it shows how vertical control over trucks, charging hardware, and financing can shift the cost equation in favor of battery-electric operation even before broader public charging networks mature, giving Tesla a practical path into a segment long viewed as too demanding for current battery technology.
