### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the decision to extend Dashcam and Sentry Mode buffers to a full 24 hours of rolling footage challenges the assumption that storage limits are mainly a hardware constraint.

The change relies on existing onboard memory management rather than new drives, showing that software scheduling alone can multiply usable recording time without added cost. In practice this means owners can review an entire day of activity instead of stitching together short clips, which matters for insurance claims or security reviews where context spans hours.

Tesla's vertical control over both the vehicle OS and camera hardware lets it push such increments through over-the-air updates, a path traditional automakers with fragmented supplier stacks rarely match at the same speed. The next indicator will be whether similar buffer extensions reach other data-logging features like the new blind-spot lighting system.
