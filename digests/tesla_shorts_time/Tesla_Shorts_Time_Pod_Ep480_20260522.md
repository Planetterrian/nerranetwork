### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the decision to move Full Self-Driving in Europe to a subscription-only model challenges the assumption that one-time hardware purchases best match customer willingness to pay.

Subscription pricing ties revenue directly to ongoing software performance and regulatory approval in each country. In markets where only two nations have cleared supervised operation so far, the model lets Tesla capture value without locking buyers into a large upfront sum that may not deliver immediate unsupervised capability. Data from early subscription uptake will reveal whether owners treat the feature as a recurring utility or a discretionary add-on.

If adoption stays strong, the approach could reshape how Tesla finances autonomy development across regions with staggered regulatory timelines. It also reduces the risk of stranded capital if future software versions require additional hardware upgrades. The shift tests whether recurring revenue can stabilize cash flow even as vehicle margins face pressure from price competition.
