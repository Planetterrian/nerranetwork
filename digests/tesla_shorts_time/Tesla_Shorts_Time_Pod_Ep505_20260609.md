### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the steady appearance of Cybercabs in service centers across more states challenges the assumption that regulatory approval is the only remaining bottleneck before wider deployment.

The pattern of sightings now spans 16 states plus D.C., showing Tesla has moved beyond a handful of controlled test routes. This distribution implies the company is already stress-testing logistics such as charging, maintenance, and transport between locations rather than waiting for final legal clearance.

If the goal is a scalable robotaxi network, these early physical movements reveal where the real constraints lie: parts availability, technician training, and regional permitting for even limited operations. Watching how quickly Tesla can expand the service-center footprint will indicate whether the vehicle itself or the supporting infrastructure becomes the tighter limit on rollout speed.
