### Tesla First Principles

The decision to locate a multi-trillion-dollar semiconductor campus in Grimes County rests on a simple physical constraint: orbital solar delivers roughly five times the energy density of ground-based arrays while space itself provides free convective cooling. Placing the majority of Terafab's output into low-Earth-orbit satellites therefore sidesteps both land-use limits and the massive water infrastructure that terrestrial AI clusters require. The remaining quarter of capacity reserved for terrestrial edge inference directly funds the scale of Optimus deployment Tesla has described, because one billion to ten billion humanoids will need onboard compute that cannot all live in orbit. This split also reflects the different duty cycles of the two workloads, with satellites running continuous inference and robots needing bursty, location-specific processing.

This allocation also changes the capital math. A conventional fab spreads its cost across consumer electronics and automotive chips with relatively predictable margins. Terafab's economics instead tie returns to two high-uncertainty bets: the regulatory path for unsupervised autonomy on Earth and the launch cadence of AI satellites. If either timeline slips, the facility's utilization drops faster than a diversified foundry would experience. Vertical integration buys some protection by guaranteeing internal demand, yet it does not remove the external dependency on launch capacity and spectrum rights. The $16.8 billion first phase already commits capital before either bet has cleared its primary regulatory or technical hurdles.

The 3,000 jobs projected for the first phase already exceed the staffing of most modern semiconductor plants, even before Optimus robots handle construction and maintenance at full scale. That labor intensity suggests the project will function as much as a regional economic anchor as a pure technology play. Observers will therefore watch not only chip yield curves but also the speed at which local workforce development and housing keep pace with the announced headcount growth. The scale comparison to Giga Texas makes clear that Terafab is not an incremental addition but an order-of-magnitude leap in both footprint and energy throughput.
