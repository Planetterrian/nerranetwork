### Tesla First Principles

Taking a step back from today's headlines, the confirmation that dry cathode electrode production could meaningfully lower cell costs challenges the assumption that incremental chemistry tweaks are the only remaining levers for battery economics.

The process removes solvents and associated drying ovens from the manufacturing line, which historically account for a sizable share of capital and energy expenditure per kilowatt-hour. Real-world pilot data from other manufacturers has shown solvent-free lines cutting those costs by roughly 20 to 30 percent once yields stabilize, though Tesla has not yet published its own internal figures.

If the approach scales inside Tesla’s vertically integrated cell production, it would reduce reliance on external suppliers for both equipment and materials while tightening the link between cell cost and pack price, potentially widening the gap between Tesla and competitors still dependent on conventional wet processes.
