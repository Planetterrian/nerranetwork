Today our word is магазин.

Ma-ga-ZEEN.

It sounds like magazine, but it means the place where you buy things.

Я иду в магазин.

Ya ee-DU v ma-ga-ZEEN means I am going to the store.

Can you say it with me? Ma-ga-ZEEN.

Now that you know магазин, let's learn more words for shopping.

The first is купить.

Ku-PEET.

Ku-PEET means to buy.

Я хочу купить хлеб.

Ya kho-CHU ku-PEET khleb means I want to buy bread.

Repeat after me. Ku-PEET.

Next comes деньги.

DYEN-ghee.

DYEN-ghee means money.

У меня есть деньги.

U me-NYA YEST DYEN-ghee means I have money.

Remember фрукты from a few lessons ago? You need деньги to buy them.

Tse-NA.

Ka-KA-ya tse-NA means what is the price?

The person who helps you is продавец.

Pro-da-VYETS.

Pro-da-VYETS po-mo-GA-yet mnye means the seller helps me.

Our last two words connect nicely.

Po-lo-ZHEE FRUK-ty v kar-ZEE-nu means put the fruit in the basket.

And скидка means discount.

SKEED-ka.

YEST SKEED-ka na E-tot to-VAR means there is a discount on this item.

Now for a quick grammar bite.

The verb купить changes depending on who is doing the action.

We say я куплю for I will buy.

Ya ku-PLYU.

Ты купишь for you will buy.

Ty ku-PEESH.

Он купит or она купит for he or she will buy.

On ku-PEET or o-NA ku-PEET.

The ending of the verb tells us who is acting, unlike English where we often keep the same form.

Now here's something really cool about one of today's words.

Want to know a secret about магазин?

Did you know that the Russian word магазин and the English word magazine are actually the same word?

They both come from the Arabic makhazin meaning storehouses or shops.

This word started in Arabic, travelled through Italian and French as magasin, and finally arrived in Russian in the eighteenth century.

But be careful.

The Russian word магазин sounds like magazine but actually means shop.

Next time you see a magazine on a shelf, remember it is cousins with магазин.

They both come from the same Arabic root for storing goods.

That connection is your secret shortcut to remembering it forever.
