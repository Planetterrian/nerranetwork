Want to know a secret about билет?

Did you know that билет and the English word ticket are actually cousins from the same French root?

They both come from the Old French billet, meaning a short note or document.

This word started in Latin as bulla, a seal or document.

It traveled through French and arrived in Russian in the seventeen hundreds when Peter the Great opened Russia to European travel.

A false friend lurks nearby.

Russian билет can also mean an exam ticket or test paper, not just travel.
