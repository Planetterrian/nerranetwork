Want to know a secret about one of today's words?

The Russian word kníga and the English word book share an ancient journey.

It started in ancient Greek as biblion.

It travelled through Old Church Slavonic and settled in Russian long ago.

Along the way it picked up the soft kn sound.

The two words look nothing alike yet both mean the same thing.

They share that hidden Greek root for papyrus scroll.

Next time you open a book remember its Russian cousin kníga.

That connection is your secret shortcut forever.
