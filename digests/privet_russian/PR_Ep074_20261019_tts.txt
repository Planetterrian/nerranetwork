Now that you know these animal names, we can put them to work in sentences.

Russian verbs change a little depending on who does the action.

Take Лиса бегает.

That means the fox runs.

Тигр спит means the tiger sleeps.

Слон ест means the elephant eats.

The verb ending shows the subject without extra words like he or she.

You can now say simple things about what each animal does.

There's something really cool about one of today's words that takes us on a quick journey through time.

Тигр started in ancient Persian as tigra meaning arrow because of the tiger's swift stripes.

It traveled through Greek and Latin before reaching Russian in the eighteenth century.

The word looks almost exactly like the English tiger yet watch out because the same sound can mean something else in other languages.

Picture a tiger shooting forward like an arrow and that Persian image helps the sound stick.
