Many space words in Russian are feminine. They often end in the letter a or ya. ракета, звезда, and планета are all feminine. We say большая ракета to mean big rocket. Paying attention to these endings helps your sentences sound natural, so listeners immediately understand what you’re describing without stopping to guess.
