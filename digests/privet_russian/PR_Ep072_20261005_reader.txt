In Russian the verb to buy changes its ending depending on who is shopping.

Я покупаю.

ya po-ku-PA-yu.

I buy.

Ты покупаешь.

ty po-ku-PA-yesh.

You buy.

Он or она покупает.

on or o-na po-ku-PA-yet.

He or she buys.

The endings tell everyone exactly who is doing the shopping.

There is something really cool about one of today's words.

магазин began as an Arabic word for a warehouse.

It travelled through Turkish and French before reaching Russian in the eighteen hundreds.

English kept only the printed meaning of magazine.

Russian kept the older sense of a place to store and sell goods.

Picture a giant magazine that turns into shelves of toys and snacks.

That picture will help you remember the Russian магазин.

In Russia many people still shop at small neighbourhood stores called продукты.

These little shops often stay open late and feel like part of the community.
