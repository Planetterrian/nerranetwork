Privyet, friends! Welcome to Привет, Русский!, episode fifty-four, for восьмое июля две тысячи двадцать шестого года. I'm Olya, and we've got some great Russian words to learn today. Poyekhali — let's go!

Our word of the day is семья.

сем...ья... сем...ья... семья!

It sounds like seminar, but it means the people you gather with every day.

This word helps us talk about the heart of a home.

Listen to it in a sentence. Это моя семья.

That means this is my family.

Now repeat after me. сем...ья... семья!

Let's meet the people inside a семья.

The first is мама.

MA-ma.

It means month over month.

Моя мама любит читать.

That means my month over month loves to read.

Repeat after me. мама. мама!

мама sounds exactly like the English word mama.

Both words come from the very first sounds babies make all over the world.

This word started as a natural baby sound and settled into Russian with almost no change.

Next time you hear mama, remember it is cousins with Russian мама.

Next comes папа.

PA-pa.

It means dad.

Папа работает.

That means dad is working.

Say it with me. папа. папа!

папа sounds exactly like English papa.

In some languages the same sound can mean something else entirely.

Now we add a sibling.

брат.

brat.

It means brother.

У меня есть брат.

That means I have a brother.

Repeat after me. брат. брат!

брат sounds like the English word brat.

It actually means the helpful sibling instead.

Here is another sibling.

сестра.

ses-TRA.

It means sister.

Моя сестра играет.

That means my sister is playing.

Try it. сестра. сестра!

сестра begins with ses like the English sister.

The ending tra completes the word.

We also have бабушка.

BAB-ush-ka.

It means grandmother.

Бабушка печёт пироги.

That means grandma bakes pies.

Repeat after me. бабушка. бабушка!

бабушка is the famous Russian word for grandmother.

Say it with a smile to remember it easily.

Meet дедушка.

DED-ush-ka.

It means grandfather.

Дедушка рассказывает истории.

That means grandpa tells stories.

Say it slowly. дедушка. дедушка!

дедушка starts with ded like dad.

The ending ushka turns it into grandfather.

One more family member is дочь.

doch’.

It means daughter.

Моя дочь любит кошку.

That means my daughter loves the cat.

Repeat after me. дочь. дочь!

дочь rhymes with dough.

It brings to mind the girl who helps the family rise with joy.

In Russian, family words often change their ending to show my or your.

We add а or я for my.

моя мама means my month over month.

мой папа means my dad.

моя сестра means my sister.

English uses my too, but Russian makes the ending match the person.

In Russia, бабушки often help raise grandchildren and cook big Sunday meals.

Families gather around the table for long talks and tea.

This makes семья feel like the center of daily life.

For kids, draw your семья and label each person with the new words, then say them out loud.

For adults, introduce your family using моя or мой.

Remember дом from a few lessons ago? That is the house where your семья lives.

Want to know a secret about one of today's words?

The Russian word мама and the English word mama are actually the same word.

This word traveled through ancient languages and stayed almost unchanged in both Russian and English.

That connection helps you remember it forever.
