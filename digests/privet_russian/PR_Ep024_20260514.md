  Memory hook: "Cosmos" sounds exactly like the English word "cosmos," so you already know it!

**Grammar Spotlight:**
Russian nouns have three genders: masculine, feminine, and neuter. This decides how words like "this" or "big" change slightly. For example: Эта звезда (feminine "this star"), этот спутник (masculine "this satellite"), это небо (neuter "this sky"). Unlike English, which uses "the" or "a" for everything, Russian shows gender through the ending sounds. Practice by pointing at pictures and saying "это [word]" for neuter things like the moon.

**Word Origins — Deep Dive: спутник**
Did you know that the Russian word спутник and the English word "sputnik" are actually the same word? They both come from the idea of a traveling companion! This word started in old Slavic languages meaning "fellow traveler" or "someone who goes along with you," then rocketed into global fame in 1957 when the Soviet Union launched the first artificial satellite and simply called it Sputnik. English speakers borrowed the word directly because nothing else captured that exciting new invention. But be careful — in everyday Russian, спутник can also just mean a human friend traveling with you, not only a space machine! Next time you see the word "satellite" or hear about old space news, remember it's cousins with спутник — they both come from the simple idea of keeping someone company on a journey. That connection is your secret shortcut to remembering it forever.

**Cultural Corner:**
Russians have a special love for space because their country sent the very first satellite and the first human, Yuri Gagarin, into orbit. Many families still watch old clips of these launches on Cosmonautics Day every April 12, celebrating with stories and drawings of rockets.

**Practice Challenge:**
For kids: Draw your own rocket and three stars, then point to each and say the Russian word out loud three times. For adults: Look up at the night sky (or a picture of it) and describe what you see using at least three new words in simple sentences like "Я вижу звезду."
