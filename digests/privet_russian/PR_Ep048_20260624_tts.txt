Now let's look at one small grammar idea.

In Russian some words change a little to match the word they describe.

We say сильный ветер for strong wind.

But we say сильная жара for strong heat.

It is like giving each word a tiny matching outfit.

English keeps the word strong the same every time.

In Russian the match makes the sentence feel smooth.

Russians love talking about the weather because it can change so fast.

From freezing winters to sudden summer heatwaves.

When friends meet, they often start with Как погода.

That means how is the weather.

It is a friendly way to begin any chat.

Want to know a secret about one of today's words?

The Russian word температура and the English word temperature are actually the same word.

They both come from an old Latin word that meant a proper mixture or balance.

The idea traveled through Europe in scientific writing and arrived in both languages in the seventeen hundreds when people started using thermometers.

Remember that connection the next time you check the weather.
