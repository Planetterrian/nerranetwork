Russian nouns have genders that change small words like my.

Кот is masculine, so we say мой кот.

Собака is feminine, so we say моя собака.

English keeps my the same for everything.

Russian gives every noun its own tiny label instead.

In Russia many families keep cats and dogs as pets.

Neighbors often leave food for stray cats near apartment buildings.

Russians also love folk tales about bears as wise but grumpy characters.
