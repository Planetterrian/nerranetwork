Russian nouns have gender.

They can be masculine, feminine, or neuter.

Food words show this clearly.

Хлеб is masculine because it ends in a consonant.

Молоко is neuter because it ends in o.

Вода is feminine because it ends in a.

In English we do not change words this way.

In Russian you match adjectives to the noun gender.

Vkusnyy khleb is masculine.

Vkusnoe moloko is neuter.

Vkusnaya voda is feminine.

The ending of the adjective changes to match.

Want to know a secret about chay?

Did you know that the Russian word чай and the English word chai are actually the same word?

They both come from the Chinese word for tea.

Cha.

This word started in China.

It travelled along the ancient Silk Road through Central Asia.

It was borrowed into Russian in the seventeenth century.

Later it reached English as chai through trade.

But be careful.

The Russian word магазин sounds like magazine but actually means shop.

Next time you see chai on a menu remember it is cousins with чай.

They both come from Chinese cha.

That connection is your secret shortcut to remembering it forever.
