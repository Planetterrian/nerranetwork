> **Let's warm up with Russian words for snow, ice, and winter weather that will help you chat about chilly days!**

# Привет, Русский! — Episode Plan

### Word of the Day
**снег** (snyeg) — snow
Example sentence: Снег падает на землю. (Snyeg padayet na zemlyu.) — The snow is falling on the ground.
Memory hook: It sounds a bit like “sneeze” but means the white fluffy stuff that makes everything quiet and magical outside.
Repeat after me: снег… снег.
- Russian (Cyrillic): снег
  Transliteration: snyeg
  English: snow
  Example sentence: Снег белый.
  Example translation: The snow is white.
  Memory hook: Think of “snyeg” as the soft sound snow makes when it lands.

- Russian (Cyrillic): лёд
  Transliteration: lyod
  English: ice
  Example sentence: Лёд холодный.
  Example translation: The ice is cold.
  Memory hook: “Lyod” rhymes with “load” — imagine a heavy load of slippery ice.

- Russian (Cyrillic): дождь
  Transliteration: dozhd'
  English: rain
  Example sentence: Дождь идёт.
  Example translation: It is raining.
  Memory hook: Sounds like “dosed” but brings water instead of medicine.

- Russian (Cyrillic): ветер
  Transliteration: vye-ter
  English: wind
  Example sentence: Ветер дует.
  Example translation: The wind is blowing.
  Memory hook: “Vye-ter” is like “better” weather when it cools you down.

- Russian (Cyrillic): солнце
  Transliteration: solntse
  English: sun
  Example sentence: Солнце светит.
  Example translation: The sun is shining.
  Memory hook: “Solntse” starts like “solar” — our big sunny star.

- Russian (Cyrillic): холодно
  Transliteration: kholodno
  English: cold (it is cold)
  Example sentence: Сегодня холодно.
  Example translation: It is cold today.
  Memory hook: “Kholodno” feels like “cold” with a Russian twist.

- Russian (Cyrillic): тепло
  Transliteration: teplo
  English: warm (it is warm)
  Example sentence: В доме тепло.
  Example translation: It is warm in the house.
  Memory hook: “Teplo” sounds like “tepid” but means nicely warm.

- Russian (Cyrillic): зима
  Transliteration: zeema
  English: winter
  Example sentence: Зима холодная.
  Example translation: Winter is cold.
  Memory hook: “Zeema” is like “zeal” for the season of snowmen.

**Grammar Spotlight:**
Adjectives in Russian change their ending to match the gender of the noun they describe. For weather words, “холодный” (kholodnyy — cold, masculine) becomes “холодная” (kholodnaya) with feminine nouns like зима. Examples: холодный ветер (cold wind), холодная зима (cold winter). Unlike English, where adjectives stay the same, Russian endings help the words “agree” and sound natural.

**Word Origins — Deep Dive: зима**
This word started in Proto-Slavic as *zima and shares an ancient root with the Sanskrit “hima” meaning snow or frost, which also gave us the name of the Himalayas — the “abode of snow.” It travelled through early Slavic languages and stayed in Russian with almost the same sound. A fun false friend appears in some other languages where similar-sounding words mean “earth” instead of a season. Remember it by picturing a Himalayan peak covered in зима snow that never melts.

**Cultural Corner:**
Russians often talk about the weather because winters are long and snowy, and people love comparing how cold it feels in different cities. Saying “Какой холод!” (What cold!) is a friendly way to start a conversation on the street.

**Practice Challenge:**
For kids: Draw a snowy scene and label the снег, лёд, and солнце in your picture, then say each word out loud. For adults: Look out the window and make one simple sentence in Russian about today’s weather using two new words, like “Сегодня холодно и снег.”
