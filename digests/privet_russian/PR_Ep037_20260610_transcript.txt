Russian nouns have gender.
Most animal names ending in art, feminine, like koska and sabaka.
We use maya for may with feminine words.
Майя koska means my cat.
Майя sabaka means my dog.
Compared to English, where may stays the same for everything.
Мой медведь means my bear, because bear is masculine.

Want to know a secret about кошка?
Did you know that the Russian word кошка and the English word cat
are actually cousins from the same ancient route?
They both come from a very old sound people made to call cats,
something like cut or cadoo thousands of years ago.
This word travelled through Latin cathos into many European languages.
While in Russian, it became кошка, keeping that soft care sound.
Be careful though, the Russian word mush sounds a bit like mouse
and means exactly the same thing.
Yet mush can also trick you because it ends with a soft sign
that changes the pronunciation.
Next time you see the English word cat,
remember it is cousins with кошка.
They both come from that ancient calling sound.
That connection is your secret shortcut to remembering it forever.
