Before we finish, here is something really cool about one of today's words.
Zima began long ago in an early Slavic form that shares an ancient route with the Sanskrit
word Hima, for snow or frost.
That same route gave the Himalayas their name, the abode of snow.
The word travelled through early Slavic languages and kept almost the same sound in Russian.
A playful false friend appears in some other languages, where a similar sound means
«Earth instead of a season».
