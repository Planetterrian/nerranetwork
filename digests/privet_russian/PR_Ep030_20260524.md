# Привет, Русский! — Episode Plan

  Transliteration: KOS-mos
  English: space
  Example sentence: Мы летим в космос.
  Example translation: We are flying to space.
  Memory hook: It sounds exactly like the English word "cosmos" — both mean the big starry universe!

- Russian (Cyrillic): звезда

**Grammar Spotlight:**
Russian nouns have genders (masculine, feminine, or neuter), which affect the endings of words that describe them. For example, "большая звезда" (big star — feminine) uses "большая," while "большой марс" (big Mars — masculine) uses "большой." This is different from English, where adjectives stay the same no matter the noun, but once you learn the three gender groups, it becomes a fun pattern like sorting toys by color.

**Word Origins — Deep Dive: планета**
Did you know that the Russian word "планета" and the English word "planet" are actually the same word? They both come from the ancient Greek "planētēs," meaning "wanderer," because the Greeks noticed planets moving across the sky unlike fixed stars. This word started in Greek, travelled through Latin into many European languages, and arrived in Russian centuries ago as space science grew. But be careful — while "планета" is a perfect match, some space words like "луна" can trick you because it sounds like "lunar" yet simply means "moon" on its own! Next time you see the English word "planet," remember it's cousins with the Russian "планета" — they both come from that ancient Greek wanderer idea. That connection is your secret shortcut to remembering it forever.

**Cultural Corner:**
Russians have a proud history of space exploration, with the first human in space being cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin in 1961. Many Russian children grow up dreaming of becoming космонавты and learning about the stars through school programs and planetariums.

**Practice Challenge:**
For kids: Draw a simple picture of the sun, moon, and a rocket, then label each with the Russian words while saying them out loud. For adults: Look up at the night sky tonight (or a picture of space) and practice saying three sentences like "Это звезда" or "Марс — планета" to describe what you see.
