Want to know a secret about one of today's words?
The Russian word, температура,
and the English word, temperature,
are actually the same word.
They both come from an old Latin word
that meant a proper mixture or balance.
The idea traveled through Europe in scientific writing
and arrived in both languages in the 1700s
when people started using thermometers.
Remember that connection the next time you check the weather.
