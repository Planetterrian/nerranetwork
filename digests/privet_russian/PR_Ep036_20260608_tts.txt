Now for a quick grammar bite.

Russian adjectives like вкусный change slightly to match the noun.

Вкусный суп works for the masculine word soup.

Вкусная вода works for the feminine word water.

Вкусное яблоко works for the neuter word apple.

English does not change adjectives this way.

Russian makes the sentence flow smoothly.
