Meta plans to put more Vera Rubin systems and Vera CPUs under Muse, and to add Spectrum-X Ethernet plus BlueField-4.

Nvidia’s newsroom says the two companies will push Muse and the wider Muse family for faster training and inference, and for a lower cost of running them.

They are also looking at OpenShell for safer agent deployment, and at Sentry to watch how agents behave.

Jensen Huang called agents a new kind of computing and said the point is infrastructure that lets Muse serve billions of people.

Mark Zuckerberg said Muse is how Meta brings personal superintelligence to everyone.

Nvidia says those models, on its hardware, have already posted frontier benchmark scores and a surge in consumer downloads since launch.

The three billion is a planned commitment into the share sale, not a cheque that has cleared.

Microsoft, in a memo from chief people officer Amy Coleman, opened a voluntary exit for people at level seventy and below on teams that opt in.

Instant Gaming News put the eligible pool at about sixty thousand, in Canada, India, the United Kingdom and the United States, roughly a quarter of the company.

Coleman framed it as support for people who already want to leave, and as a way for participating groups to resize.

Not every organization is offering it, and she tied it to a voluntary programme from earlier this year.

Eligible staff were due an email with the terms.

HDFC Sky, citing PTI, says Microsoft has about twenty-three thousand people in India, without a count of who qualifies, and that the India decision date is December first.

The package is understood to match ordinary severance: one month of base pay for each completed year, plus health cover and career-transition help.

A Microsoft spokesperson said India commitments total twenty point five billion dollars from twenty twenty-five through twenty twenty-nine, and that the company is on track to train twenty million people there in AI skills by twenty thirty.

Apple unveiled the iPad mini that had been talked about as an OLED rumor, and called this the biggest update the mini has had.

The Hindu says the body is five point four millimetres thin, with an eight point five inch OLED panel, twelve hundred nits at peak, six hundred nits full screen, a one-nit floor, and contrast of two million to one.

It runs the A20 Pro, with a seven-core GPU and a neural accelerator in each graphics core.

IOL put the CPU at up to sixty percent faster than the last mini, the GPU at about two point seven times, and memory bandwidth at double, with the five hundred twelve gigabyte model adding a CPU core and a second sixteen-core Neural Engine.

It is IP sixty-seven, good for a metre of water for thirty minutes, with twelve-megapixel cameras front and back, Smart HDR four, and 4K video at up to sixty frames a second.

Storage steps are one hundred twenty-eight, two hundred fifty-six and five hundred twelve gigabytes, in Midnight and Starlight.

The US price starts at six hundred ninety-nine dollars.

In India, Wi-Fi starts at eighty-two thousand nine hundred rupees, cellular at one lakh nine hundred, and education pricing at seventy-six thousand nine hundred.

Pre-orders open October twenty-eighth and the tablet goes on sale November sixth.

Battery life is listed at up to ten hours, and a thirty-five-watt adapter or larger is supposed to reach a fifty percent charge in about thirty minutes.

Dave Pakula, who runs hardware engineering, called it a full iPad that still fits in the hand.

The Times of India says the structure lets Mark Zuckerberg raise that money without giving up voting control, and that Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom want in as buyers.

Jay-Z will headline Apple Music’s Super Bowl sixty-one halftime show on February fourteenth, twenty twenty-seven, at SoFi Stadium, on ABC and ESPN in the US, and Apple Music has paid for that show since twenty twenty-two.

What cuts against Nvidia’s three billion is who else is buying. The Times of India calls the pattern circular capital.

Silicon suppliers are eyeing a five billion dollar stake in the same AI share sale, and Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom are on the interested list.

A supplier funding the customer that then orders that supplier’s machines is not an outside vote that the demand is real.

The Times also framed Zuckerberg’s goal as fresh capital that does not loosen his votes.

Almost a quarter of a trillion dollars is the pile The Information put on three Monday fundraisings, from Anthropic, SpaceX and Meta, and the shelf they are crowding also has Alphabet’s name on it.

Reuters has previously said Anthropic’s offering could seek as much as one hundred billion dollars.

SpaceX signed a forty billion dollar deal with an Apollo-managed entity to lease graphics processors, and called it the largest GPU financing on record.

Alex Eichenstein reported that the money is expected to sit in a special purpose vehicle off SpaceX’s balance sheet, which could soften any rating impact.

Meta’s board committee wrote last week that timing mattered, because the company wanted capital before rivals fill the same funding options, and it listed Alphabet, SpaceX, Anthropic and OpenAI in that queue.

Microsoft is the other of these seven in that piece, and the fresh figure is how many people actually leave.

The global headcount is two hundred twenty-three thousand.

An earlier exit offer went to nine thousand people, and about a third took it.

This round is expected to draw a smaller share, under twenty thousand.

The bind is the funding shelf, not a product the companies share.

Meta’s directors treated Alphabet’s deals as taking space on the same equity and debt menus.

Microsoft is offering exits while that window is still described as open.

The Apollo lease is the article’s picture of a Musk company choosing an off-balance-sheet GPU rental instead of a straight loan.

Musk, in a post The Information quotes, said debt markets have lately shut to firms whose only revenue is AI data centers, and that more than two hundred neoclouds face what he called guppy-level death and consolidation.

The Information calls that self-serving. Fewer neoclouds could leave more capital for the big names later.

For Meta, the filing makes the raise a race against other calendars, Alphabet’s included.

For Microsoft, expected take-up means the offer does not empty a quarter of a two hundred twenty-three thousand person company.
