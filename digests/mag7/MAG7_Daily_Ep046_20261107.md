### Top News
**Meta AI models roll out with Active Memory and Active Focus: India Today**
Meta said specialised models for Muse and Meta AI, trained with capabilities from its frontier Muse Wonder model, would begin rolling out on Friday. Mark Zuckerberg said on X the models were 2.4 times less likely to forget critical information and 67 percent better at self-correcting and completing everyday tasks. India Today reported an internal evaluation in which the models used important context correctly in 90 percent of tasks, compared with 76 percent previously, and a 2.4-times reduction in forgetting critical information. Active Memory is meant to keep details that stay relevant across chats, rather than treating every conversation as a fresh start. Active Focus is meant to try another approach when a site fails or a booking sticks, and to judge when to work alone and when to ask the user. Subscribers can still use Muse Wonder for more demanding tasks. Meta AI runs across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Messenger. Meta is using Muse Wonder to train specialised models for everyday work instead of giving every user the frontier model. Source: [indiatoday.in](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/meta-ai-will-forget-less-and-give-up-less-often-with-new-upgrade-3034380-2026-11-07)

**Amazon opens texting with Alexa for Shopping: MarketScale**
Amazon said on November 6 that texting with Alexa for Shopping is rolling out by invitation to a small group of U.S. customers, and will reach millions more in the coming weeks. Customers accept the terms, link a phone number, and the agent appears in contacts as Alexa Shopping. Amazon said more than 400 million customers used Alexa for Shopping in the last 12 months, and use of its agentic features has grown more than eight times since the start of the year. The agent can add items and complete a purchase with approval, reorder, set a weekly grocery order, watch for a price drop, show up to a year of price history, send new-release alerts, and check an order. Requests started over text carry over to the Amazon Shopping app, Amazon.com and Alexa-enabled devices, and the other way around. MarketScale, citing TechCrunch, also reported list management, web search, product comparisons, photo search for similar listings, and preferences learned over time. The launch follows Amazon blocking Meta's Muse agent from querying its site in September. Source: [marketscale.com](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/retail/amazon-alexa-for-shopping-text-orders-price-tracking)

### The Thread: The site Google would not rent
Crusoe, an eight-year-old Denver data-center developer recently valued at 30.9 billion dollars, confirmed Friday that it has sold an unfinished project in southeastern Wyoming to Google. A day after Google's Wyoming plan was public, that confirmation is the change: Crusoe had tried and failed to land Google as a customer, and Google is taking over development after reportedly raising concerns about cost and construction timeline under Crusoe. Google has said it plans to invest 17 billion dollars in the campus. Crusoe announced the project last year with Blackstone-backed Tallgrass and had planned to scale it to 10 gigawatts. Bloomberg first reported the sale in June. The setback did not stop a 3.9 billion dollar round in September, when Crusoe added three board members, including JB Straubel, the Redwood Materials founder and Tesla board member who invested in Crusoe in 2021. Crusoe later became the first customer of Straubel's energy-storage business.

The shared problem is delivery, not a logo on the fence. Independent developers have to secure equipment and power, and local opposition can slow a site, which is how a company valued in the tens of billions can still lose the project it pitched. Google did not leave the relationship. Crusoe is developing another data center for Google near Amarillo, Texas. Its Abilene campus is being built in phases for Oracle and OpenAI, and a separate 900-megawatt campus next door is for Microsoft, with the first building expected online in mid-2027. Meta is a Crusoe customer. Bloomberg reported that trading firm Jane Street signed a 13 billion dollar, five-year cloud deal with Crusoe. The business is three products at once: leasing space to customers who bring their own GPUs, renting Crusoe's own GPUs, and selling inference. Chase Lochmiller, now chief executive, and Cully Cavness started the firm as crypto mining powered by natural gas that would otherwise have been flared.

What shifts is who holds the construction risk on the Wyoming ground, and who does not. Google is now the developer on a campus it would not book as a tenant. Microsoft and Meta stay on Crusoe's customer list at other sites, so the same developer still sits in their power plans after losing this one. Tesla's connection is the board, not a vehicle: Straubel joined Crusoe's board in the September round while those campuses serve the build Google, Microsoft and Meta are all funding. The dated step a source actually names is that first Microsoft building in mid-2027. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/) Source: [bloomberg.com](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-06/google-takes-over-wyoming-data-center-that-crusoe-had-pitched)
