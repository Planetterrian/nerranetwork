### Company Desk
**Amazon has switched off Alexa voice control for AUX on older Echo speakers: Android Authority**
Amazon has disabled Alexa voice commands for the AUX input on the Echo Studio (1st Gen), Echo Plus (2nd Gen), and Echo (3rd and 4th Gen), and confirmed the change to Ars Technica. The 3.5mm port still plays audio, the Alexa app can still switch supported AUX modes, and Bluetooth remains an option. Amazon told Ars that very few customers use voice commands for AUX playback. Source: [androidauthority.com](https://www.androidauthority.com/amazon-echo-alexa-aux-input-voice-controls-3719987/)

**Musk’s empire will build the Texas Terafab, with no operational role for TSMC: Techmeme**
Elon Musk says his business empire will build and operate the Texas-based Terafab chipmaking project. He explicitly ruled out any operational role for TSMC, Debby Wu reported for Bloomberg. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/Techmeme/status/2107781956020883738)

### The Counterpoint

### The Thread: Two home calendars, four years apart
September is the month Bloomberg first put a year on Apple’s home security system and service: 2027. The LG-branded accessories Mark Gurman split off from Apple’s own camera are a video doorbell, a deadbolt lock, a thermostat, and indoor and outdoor cameras. That lineup is not expected for at least a few more months.

Amazon’s speakers in the same story are older. The second-generation Echo Plus is a 2018 product. The third-generation Echo and the first-generation Echo Studio are 2019 products. The fourth-generation Echo, from 2020, was the last mainstream Echo to ship a 3.5mm jack. Newer models have leaned on wireless links instead. The phrases that no longer land are “Alexa, play from line in” and “Alexa, stop aux input.” Some owners on Reddit are irritated that a control disappeared from hardware they already bought.
