### Top News
**Meta admits Muse’s likeness to OpenClaw isn’t a coincidence:** TechCrunch
Meta says Muse was built from scratch, but acknowledges the AI assistant was "heavily inspired" by OpenClaw — down to some of its workspace filenames and content. The company stated explicitly that the likeness to OpenClaw is not a coincidence. Workspace filenames and specific content elements inside Muse match those found in the earlier OpenClaw system. Meta positioned the overlap as the result of heavy inspiration rather than direct copying of code. Source: [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/meta-admits-muses-likeness-to-openclaw-isnt-a-coincidence/)

**Microsoft disrupts AI-assisted platform that compromised 12,000 accounts:** Ars Technica
EvilTokens provided an end-to-end platform that makes mass compromises faster and easier. Microsoft took action against the platform after it had already been used to compromise 12,000 accounts. The service offered automated tools that accelerated the process of gaining unauthorized access at scale. Microsoft’s disruption removed the infrastructure that supported these AI-assisted attacks. Source: [arstechnica.com](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/microsoft-disrupts-ai-assisted-platform-that-compromised-12000/)

**Tim Cook meets EU chief Ursula von der Leyen as bloc pushes new child safety rules:** 9to5Mac
Keeping with Apple’s pledge that Tim Cook would remain involved in Apple’s government relations around the world after stepping down as CEO, he met this week with Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission. The meeting addressed the European Union’s push for new child safety rules. Cook’s continued role covers regulatory engagement outside the United States. The discussion occurred as the bloc advances its updated child-safety framework. Source: [9to5mac.com](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/22/tim-cook-meets-eu-chief-ursula-von-der-leyen-as-bloc-pushes-new-child-safety-rules/)
