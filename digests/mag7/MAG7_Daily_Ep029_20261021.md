# MAG 7 Daily
> **Fourteen days, not six months, gets an NVIDIA AI factory into production.**

**What You Need to Know:** NVIDIA’s factory-built DSX POD containers are meant to put a Vera Rubin AI factory into production in as little as fourteen days instead of six months. The same Berlin keynote added an agent-safety stack that can stop a runaway agent in milliseconds, while Microsoft’s NHS Copilot trial is now a staffing number, not a pilot anecdote. Britain’s competition regulator, meanwhile, wants a cheaper way onto the iPhone’s contactless chip.

### Top News
**Factory-built containers cut an AI factory install from six months to fourteen days: NVIDIA Newsroom**
At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA introduced DSX POD, a reference design for complete Vera Rubin AI factories that partners build inside modular containers, integrate, and validate before delivery. The units are meant to connect to a prepared site’s power, cooling, and networking, cutting onsite deployment from six months to as little as fourteen days. Jensen Huang said an AI factory creates value when it starts producing tokens, not when construction is finished. The wider DSX platform pairs liquid-cooled computing with dry coolers so cooling liquid stays in a closed loop. Vera Rubin supports 100 percent liquid cooling with coolant temperatures up to 45 degrees Celsius. NVIDIA says that at 2-gigawatt scale the design can save 54 million liters of water a day versus cooling towers, equivalent to the daily water use of 180,000 households, and that local climate decides whether extra mechanical cooling is needed in the hottest periods. DSX MaxLPS is described as helping operators generate the same number of tokens with up to 30 percent less power. CoreWeave, Firmus, Google Cloud, IREN, Lambda, Nebius, Nscale, and Sharon AI are exploring it. In a proof of concept on HGX B200 systems, Lambda reported 24 percent more cluster token throughput and 19 percent more GPU capacity inside a fixed power budget. Partners working to deliver DSX POD factories worldwide include Dell Technologies, HPE, Foxconn, Lenovo, Schneider Electric, and Vertiv. **OpenShell puts agent control on the CPU, the rack, and the DPU: NVIDIA Newsroom**
NVIDIA also announced the OpenShell Reference Architecture for building, containing, observing, and enforcing agents on enterprise infrastructure and at the edge. It stacks Vera Rubin systems for inference, Vera CPUs for agent harnesses and tool use, open-source OpenShell runtime software, and BlueField-4 DPUs running NVIDIA Sentry. Sentry, built on DOCA, watches agent behavior out of band. If an agent moves outside its software boundary, Sentry quarantines and stops it in milliseconds. NVIDIA says Sentry can boost agent capacity by up to 20 percent. The design extends to RTX Spark, DGX Spark, and DGX Station for retail, healthcare, manufacturing, robotics, software development, and telecommunications. It runs on Linux and Kubernetes from Canonical, SUSE, and Red Hat, and on Microsoft Windows using Microsoft Execution Containers. Huang’s line for the split was that agents can think on Rubin, act on Vera, and be governed on BlueField. Source: [nvidianews.nvidia.com](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openshell-reference-architecture)

**An NHS Copilot trial clocked forty-three minutes a day: Microsoft Source**
Microsoft Source reports that NHS England, after a trial involving 30,000 employees, said in June 2026 it would make Microsoft Copilot available to as many as 505,000 clinicians and support staff. Participants reported saving an average of 43 minutes per working day, which the account adds up to about five weeks a person a year. Yorkshire Ambulance Service, one of more than 200 NHS trusts, took about 1.25 million calls to 999 in the 2025-26 fiscal year. Area operations manager Tom Smith says Copilot does not decide for him. It organizes reports he can interrogate, and, because he is dyslexic, drafting help “levels the playing field.” National chief clinical information officer Alec Price-Forbes says Copilot is only for administrative tasks, not clinical work, under standards for clinical safety, information governance, cybersecurity, interoperability, and real-world evaluation. He still describes the NHS as in many ways an analog system. Source: [news.microsoft.com](https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/features/before-the-ambulance-arrives-how-ai-is-helping-the-nhs-rethink-patient-care/)

**Britain’s regulator wants a second door onto the iPhone’s payment chip: MacRumors**
The UK Competition and Markets Authority has proposed changes to make alternatives to Apple Pay easier and cheaper, Reuters reported via MacRumors. Apple opened iPhone NFC access to UK developers in 2024, but rivals must sign an agreement, pay fees, and use the Secure Element. The CMA wants Apple to also support host card emulation, so a wallet can work without that chip, and it expects the rules would substantially lower access fees while still letting Apple charge something. Apple already offers HCE for free in the European Union under commitments agreed in 2024. The CMA notes rival payment apps have launched in the EU and that no competing wallet-type product has launched in the UK on Apple’s current terms. Apple told Reuters: “We do not believe the CMA should require Apple to adopt an approach with lower privacy and security protections.” The CMA said the evidence it reviewed did not suggest either approach was inherently insecure. ### The Thread: Nearly ten billion dollars, four clouds, one runtime
NVIDIA’s week across Paris, London, Stockholm, and Dublin closed in Berlin with a commitment of nearly $10 billion to the technology ecosystem in Europe and work with more than 12,000 partners. GTC Berlin itself runs three days, with more than 100 sessions at STATION-Berlin. Jensen Huang opened by naming L’Oréal, Revolut, Spotify, Merck, Vodafone, Zara, Porsche, Nestlé, and HSBC as European companies already working with NVIDIA on AI. The capital figure is the new fact. The product underneath it is a stack those companies, and four of the seven, are being asked to share.

Huang called AI a five-layer cake: energy, then advanced computers inside an AI factory, then tokens, then models, then applications. He said Vera Rubin, in full production, extends the platform past chat models so it can process the entire agent. On the OpenShell spec, an agent has five elements: a model, a harness that orchestrates work and memory, a sandbox that isolates the harness, knowledge, and tools. Privileges are supposed to be minimal, and different every time an agent launches. The hardware split he is selling puts inference on Vera Rubin NVL72 systems, which NVIDIA says deliver 30 times higher throughput per megawatt for agentic workloads, and puts the harness on Vera CPUs, which it says deliver twice the performance of x86 processors with greater energy efficiency. Governance sits on BlueField, not in the model vendor’s application code.

That is where Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft enter the same rack. OpenShell’s cloud operating systems include Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. Enterprises are told they can run agents from model builders that include Google, Meta, and OpenAI, plus Anthropic, Cohere, Perplexity, Reflection, and SpaceX Super Intelligence. Microsoft is on the list twice: Windows via Execution Containers, and among the security vendors integrating the architecture, beside Cisco, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks. Google Cloud is also on the separate DSX MaxLPS explorer list. Meta’s models and Microsoft’s operating system are being offered a seat on NVIDIA silicon rather than only inside each company’s own cloud policy.

What changes is who owns the boundary. If the sandbox is NVIDIA’s runtime and the stop switch is a DPU, Amazon, Google, and Microsoft still sell the cloud account, but they do not solely decide how an agent is fenced. Meta and Google still ship the model. They do not, on this design, ship the millisecond quarantine. Europe’s side of the bargain is the nearly $10 billion commitment and the 12,000-partner channel Huang says is already started, so the continent produces tokens instead of only consuming them. The factories those tokens need are the DSX designs. The agents that spend the tokens are the OpenShell designs. Four of the seven are named on one or both.
