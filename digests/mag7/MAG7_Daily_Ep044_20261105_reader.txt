Amazon has shipped a managed path on S3 Tables that moves Apache Hive tables onto Apache Iceberg. The AWS post limits it to Parquet tables already in Amazon S3 and registered in the AWS Glue Data Catalog, and says you do not build pipelines or provision compute while production workloads keep running.

Before a copy starts, the service lists every source object in scope and writes a compatibility report, then does the validation, the type conversions, the recompression, and the compaction. You can sync as often as you need without freezing writes on the source, except for a few minutes at cutover. The new table is read-only while that runs and becomes read-write when you complete it.

You pay only for the data copied, not for the Hive-to-Iceberg conversion, and objects already in S3 Intelligent-Tiering stay in the tier they are in.
