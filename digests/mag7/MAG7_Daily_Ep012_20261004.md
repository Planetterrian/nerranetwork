> **Google will pay about one hundred publishers for what their pages add to AI answers.**

**What You Need to Know:** Google is paying roughly one hundred digital publishers based on how much their content contributes to AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini, The Information reports. ### Top News
**Google Pays 100 Publishers Based on AI Overviews Contributions: The Information**
Google is paying roughly 100 digital publishers based on how much their content contributes to AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini, The Information reports. The payments are tied to that contribution, not to a flat license described in the report. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-paying-100-digital-publishers-ai-overviews?utm_campaign=Editorial&utm_content=Article&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_source=bluesky,threads,twitter)

**Google prepares to defend a £1.2B UK class action over Play charges: Financial Times**
Google is preparing to defend a £1.2 billion UK class action, the Financial Times reported via Techmeme. The claim says Google levied excessive charges on Android apps downloaded from Google Play since 2015. Source: [x.com](https://x.com/Techmeme/status/2106621798720967031)

**Microsoft Backs Effort to Standardize Business Metrics for AI Tools: The Information**
Microsoft is backing an effort with Snowflake and others to standardize business metrics so AI tools can more easily understand company data, The Information reports. The move is an about-face from Microsoft’s earlier effort to protect Fabric from rivals. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/applied-ai/microsoft-tears-ai-data-wall?utm_campaign=Editorial&utm_content=Article&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_source=bluesky,threads,twitter)

### Company Desk
**Meta AI 292.1.0 adds email and calendar for glasses owners: MIXED Reality News**
Meta AI reached version 292.1.0 on October 2, on iPhone at 19:25 UTC and on Android the same day, and the build connects the assistant to a user’s email and calendar. Meta’s store copy calls the app the required companion for managing AI glasses, including Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta. Release notes add daily briefings with scheduled tasks and reminders, in-depth research reports, and tools to create presentations, quizzes and workout planners. The description says users can link an Instagram professional account, Meta Ads Manager and Google Workspace so Meta AI can analyze content and ad performance. Voice conversation is quoted at 13 languages, against 39 locales listed for the app; the iOS build is 344 MB and needs iOS 17.2 or newer. The notes describe an assistant on the phone and do not say those features reach the frames. Source: [mixed-news.com](https://mixed-news.com/en/meta-ai-292-1-0-required-companion-app-glasses-email-calendar/)

### The Counterpoint
**Publisher payouts under a tenth of a percent of ad revenue: The Information**
Some participants in Google’s publisher program say the payouts amount to less than 0.1% of their advertising revenue. That is the figure that decides whether contribution-based pay for AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini is a new market or a rounding error against ads. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-paying-100-digital-publishers-ai-overviews?utm_campaign=Editorial&utm_content=Article&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_source=bluesky,threads,twitter)

### The Thread: Disks Beside a Two-Hundred-Billion Plan
Toshiba plans to double HDD production capacity for AI data centers within FY2027 from its 2025 level, targeting a 30% share by storage capacity, up from about 10%, Nikkei Asia reported. That capacity plan sits next to cash Amazon has already assigned to the buildings those drives are meant to fill. Leverage Shares reported on September 11, 2026, that Amazon’s second-quarter revenue rose 20% year over year for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The same coverage says Amazon posted $200.6 billion in revenue for that quarter. Operating income increased 43% to $27.5 billion, and the operating margin moved from 11.4% to 13.7%. AWS revenue was $42.2 billion, a 36.7% year-over-year increase, while AWS operating income reached $16.6 billion and the segment margin reached 39.4%, according to Fortune. On the second-quarter call, Amazon raised its 2026 cash capital-expenditure plan to $220 billion from $200 billion, Fortune’s transcript reported, aimed largely at AWS and artificial intelligence capacity. A doubled disk line aimed at a 30% capacity share, and a cash plan lifted from $200 billion to $220 billion, are the two scale numbers in front of that build. AWS at $42.2 billion of quarterly revenue, with $16.6 billion of operating income, is the segment those dollars are supposed to keep growing.
