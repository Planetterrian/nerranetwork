# MAG 7 Daily
> **An 8.1-severity bug let denied AWS callers run privileged cloud calls.**

**What You Need to Know:** An 8.1-severity bug in sample AWS code let a caller who was explicitly denied still run privileged cloud operations. Microsoft, the same weekend, told the industry to assume advanced models are compromised, and it also put out a small decision-scoring model. Nvidia's talks with Reflection AI are being described in two different ways.

### Top News
**An 8.1 bug in AWS sample code let denied callers run privileged calls: shattered.io**
AWS patched a privilege-escalation flaw, tracked as CVE-2026-94384 and scored 8.1, in the sfExecuteAWSService function inside the AmazonConnectSalesforceLambda application. That app is the glue between Amazon Connect contact centers and Salesforce Service Cloud Voice. The function accepted caller-supplied parameters for which AWS operation to run, then executed that operation with its own privileged role without checking whether the invoking identity was allowed to do it. The only permission required was lambda:InvokeFunction. From there the function's role could run API calls the caller's own policy explicitly denied, which the write-up calls an IAM identity bypass and a confused-deputy problem. Source: [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/aws-lambda-iam-bypass-cvss-8-1-2026/)

**Nadella says assume models are compromised and fit an emergency brake: The Verge**
In a Saturday post, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said companies should treat powerful AI models as potential insider threats, assume those models could be compromised, and create an emergency brake so agentic models cannot go rogue. TechCrunch quoted him saying it is time to step back and assess the trust architecture of AI. The Verge reported that he rejected a world in which AI is a set of nested black boxes whose advice and actions people simply accept. **Outlook loses Themes by Copilot from mid-October: BetaNews**
Microsoft told administrators that Themes by Copilot in Outlook will be retired worldwide from mid-October 2026, with completion expected by late October, on the new Outlook for Windows, Outlook for Android, Outlook for iOS, Outlook for Mac, and Outlook on the web. Copilot-license users lose the Settings control and cannot return to a Copilot theme after they switch. A theme already applied stays until the user changes it, and themes set to refresh automatically stop refreshing while the current imagery remains. XDA reported that the feature arrived in November 2024 and described the cut as a shift toward the Copilot app and Taskbar Search. ### The Counterpoint
**The 8.1 hole was patched in June, inside one sample app: shattered.io**
AWS quietly patched the sample in June 2026 and published the advisory on September 22. Security trackers and trade press picked the story up on October 9 and 10. The flaw sat in a pre-built app in the Serverless Application Repository, not in the Lambda service as a whole, and the write-up says the two CVSS scores on the bug disagree. Whether a deployed copy of AmazonConnectSalesforceLambda was updated after that June patch is the number that decides who is still exposed. Source: [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/aws-lambda-iam-bypass-cvss-8-1-2026/)

### The Thread: A Tuesday invitation and a 2023 Google deal
October 13 is the date on Apple's media invitation for a New York event it is calling Welcome home, and MacRumors treats that date as the possible moment a home-security relationship moves from Google to Apple. The outlet says the partnership is still a rumor. Since around July, ADT has posted Low Voltage Technician listings in various U.S. cities. Hires told MacRumors they have mostly shadowed other technicians while waiting for a job description, and that they signed a nondisclosure agreement. ADT issued those hires iPhones; the company's other technicians have received Android phones. Some managers told new hires the secretive role relates to home automation and in-home installation. ADT first planned to explain the role in September, then delayed that briefing until October, which MacRumors lines up with Apple's repeated postponements of the home hub while a revamped Siri was not ready. That Siri version launched last month. The hub described in those reports would come as a tabletop version with a speaker base and a wall-mounted version, and Apple is also expected to show third-party accessories developed with brands including LG. Reported hub functions include accessory management, home security monitoring, photo faces and galleries, a calendar overview, music playback, and FaceTime. The Google fact in the same piece is older: ADT reached a home-security partnership with Google in 2023, and MacRumors says ADT dropped some Nest features earlier this year. A few people familiar with the matter told the outlet that ADT's Google agreement either already expired this year or will expire next year, and is not set to be renewed. If that account holds, 2027 is the year the Google contract would be clear, which is the opening the Apple rumor needs. MacRumors also says the clues could be a coincidence. No term sheet is in the piece. The date that confirms or kills it is the 13th. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/10/rumored-apple-adt-partnership/)
