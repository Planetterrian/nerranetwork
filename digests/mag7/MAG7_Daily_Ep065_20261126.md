> **Four of nine advertised iPhone AI features missed schedule, and Korea's examiners want a fine.**

### Top News
**Korea's examiners recommend a fine over iPhone 16 Apple Intelligence ads: DigitBin**
South Korea's Fair Trade Commission said on November 26, 2026 that it received a complaint in March 2025 and investigated Apple Inc. and Apple Korea under the Fair Labeling and Advertising Act. Examiners filed a report on November 24 stating that Apple advertised nine Apple Intelligence features as if all were coming on schedule, when four did not. The four named are Genmoji, Image Playground, a Siri that understands personal context, and a Siri that gives step-by-step guidance. The release uses personal-context Siri as its example: advertised for US English that year and for Korean by early April 2025, it reached US English in September 2026 and Korean in October 2026. Examiners classify the conduct as a serious violation of the deceptive-advertising clause, Article 3, Paragraph 1, Item 2, and recommend a corrective order, an order to publicize the violation, and a fine. The report does not bind the commission. ### The Counterpoint
**The examiners do not bind the commission, and Apple's US pages described a phased launch: DigitBin**
The case against the fine recommendation is in the same record. The examiners' report does not bind the commission, Apple has six weeks from the November 24 delivery to file opinions and seek evidence, and a final judgment comes only after independent deliberation. Apple's own US announcements, which DigitBin notes are not the Korean homepage ads, described a phased launch: on September 9, 2024, first features the following month with iOS 18.1 in US English; on October 28, 2024, the first set that day, more in December, and personal-context Siri "in the months to come." No fine amount is stated. What decides the outcome is whether the commission treats the Korean ads' dates as a promise the US phased language does not excuse. Source: [digitbin.com](https://www.digitbin.com/korea-ftc-apple-intelligence-ads-fine/)

### The Thread: Dates that became the case
The Korean homepage is a sequence of dates, and those dates are now the evidence. In September 2024 the iPhone 16 page said the Apple Intelligence features were coming in US English within the year. In October 2024 it said they were running in US English. In February 2025 it said Korean support would start in early April. Examiners allege Apple hid, downplayed or left out which of the nine features could not ship on that schedule. Personal-context Siri is the worked example: promised for US English that year and for Korean by early April 2025, it arrived in US English in September 2026 and in Korean in October 2026. The commission's release puts related sales at about 3.35 trillion won. Apple may file written opinions within six weeks of receiving the report sent on November 24, 2026.

Meta's calendar is a different promise about the same kind of material, a creator's images. Proposed terms that would let automated software scrape external websites linked to business accounts were first set for October 30, then postponed to November 20. On November 5 the company paused them indefinitely. Some users stopped posting from October 30 through November 6. Megan Wimberley said she started a Meta blackout campaign on October 22. Tim O'Brien, a board member at the Society of Illustrators, told CNN artists deserve meaningful consent, fair compensation and a way to challenge unauthorized use. One impersonator's Instagram post of Andrew Scott's work, rebuilt as AI video. CNN spoke with more than a dozen artists. Nicole McKay said many US artists had begun removing accounts, and that leaving is a hard choice for people who sell through those apps.

What the two calendars share is not a joint product. It is the gap between a date a platform published and what a person could still control after that date. For Apple, the examiners say the ads' dates overstated when four features would exist, and the sales figure attached to that claim is the 3.35 trillion won already in the release. For Meta, the dates were about when new scraping terms would start, and the indefinite pause on November 5 left the old terms in place while artists deleted pages anyway. Neither filing sets the other's remedy. Korea's commission still has to deliberate; Meta's spokesperson said the company is still talking to stakeholders.

The next dated steps the sources actually name sit on those same clocks: Apple's six-week window from November 24, and the December 10, 5 p.m. opening of Apple Kukatpally, a store decision running on a retail calendar while the advertising case runs on a legal one.
