> **Five months is the best case for EU iPhone users waiting on Siri AI.**

**What You Need to Know:** Apple's upgraded Siri may stay off European iPhones and iPads for months, and Bloomberg's Mark Gurman calls five months the best case while Apple negotiates the bloc's digital rules. John Ternus is in the design studio several times a week, and a touch-screen OLED MacBook Pro is coming out significantly lighter. ### Top News
**EU iPhone and iPad users face a months-long wait for Siri AI: MacRumors**
Bloomberg's Mark Gurman writes in his Power On newsletter that Apple's upgraded Siri AI could remain unavailable in the European Union for several months, and that a delay of around five months would be the absolute best case. He said users there could wait even longer while Apple negotiates compliance with the bloc's digital rules. The upgrade is not available in the EU on iPhone or iPad. Gurman compared the hold with Apple Intelligence's delayed EU rollout. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/05/extended-eu-wait-predicted-for-siri-ai/)

**Ternus takes the design job himself as the OLED MacBook Pro sheds weight: Bloomberg**
Gurman reports that after years without a design chief over both hardware and software design, new chief executive John Ternus has taken that role himself. Since becoming CEO he has worked hands-on in the industrial design and human interface studio several times a week, sometimes more than once a day. Tim Cook typically visited for about an hour once a month at most. The same newsletter says the touch-screen OLED MacBook Pro is significantly lighter after Apple reengineered the interior, miniaturizing parts and rearranging components. ### The Counterpoint
**The EU hold is leverage, and the Mac already has the model: MacRumors**
Gurman reckons Apple is withholding the Siri upgrade to pressure the European Commission, hoping frustrated customers will push for a change in the Commission's approach. EU users already have Siri AI on Mac, so the software is on one Apple platform while iPhone and iPad stay blocked. The open question is whether those talks end inside the five-month best case or run past it. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/05/extended-eu-wait-predicted-for-siri-ai/)

### The Thread: A hundred pauses and a billion-dollar ask
More than 100 data-center moratoriums are being considered across the United States, and that count is now a cloud-build problem, not only a zoning story. AWS chief executive Matt Garman, in a blog post reported by IT Pro, said the build-out is so important geopolitically that there are widespread reports of various countries intentionally seeding misinformation in the US about data centers to trick the country into slowing down. He wrote that with any change there will be important questions, but also misinformation and outright lies, and that myths take hold faster in social media and round-the-clock news. Making the wrong choices, he said, could put the country behind, potentially irreparably. The post then names how Amazon wants communities to say yes. Under a program called Built Together, Garman said the company plans to allocate $1 billion over the next five years. How it is spent would depend on the communities, but the money would likely go toward education, job training, energy affordability, and water and energy conservation. He set Amazon next to Microsoft on process: like Microsoft, he said, the company has stopped using nondisclosure agreements when seeking approval from government agencies, is providing information as early as possible, and is listening to concerns. On the resource claims, he said US golf courses alone use around 200 times as much water as all of Amazon's data centers put together. He also said the sites are not driving up energy prices and are not emitting huge amounts of pollution via large backup diesel generators. The local case he offered is tax revenue, positive economic impact, large numbers of good jobs, education programs, and other benefits, plus thousands of construction jobs that he said benefit families and local small businesses. Source: [itpro.com](https://www.itpro.com/security/aws-ceo-matt-garman-says-hostile-nations-are-intentionally-seeding-misinformation-in-the-us-about-data-centers-to-trick-us-into-slowing-down) Source: [timesofindia.indiatimes.com](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/aws-ceo-matt-garman-says-data-centers-arent-using-as-much-water-as-claimed-debunks-4-popular-myths-says-there-are-clearly-/articleshow/134689386.cms)
