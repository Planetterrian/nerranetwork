A Commerce license rule, a new Nvidia board committee, and Apple’s memory contract follow, then Blackstone’s Amazon campuses, the company desk, the counterpoint, a thread on where the machines sit, and the calendar.

A I Weekly says Nvidia used Supercomputing 2026 to tee up Rubin HPC for scientists, engineers, and analysts working large data sets, and the current Rubin tops out at twenty-eight thousand six hundred seventy-two CUDA cores. Nvidia is calling the new part a balanced design, with NVLink 7 keeping the three point six terabytes per second of per-G P U bandwidth already shipping on NVLink 6.

Tom’s Hardware reports the layout likely reuses four dies, in the manner of Rubin Ultra, linked by NVLink-C2C. Ian Buck, Nvidia’s vice president of hyperscale and HPC, said faster simulation means faster discovery.

The Trump administration moved to restrict foreign countries from giving Chinese companies remote access to advanced A I chips and servers, Bloomberg reports, amid scrutiny of export controls after an alleged Nvidia espionage scandal.

An interim Commerce Department rule says several Southeast Asian nations, along with Brazil, will need a U S government license to buy advanced A I chips from Nvidia and other American manufacturers.

Nvidia’s own board answered the control problem with a standing committee. The National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee will watch national security policy, export-control compliance, and global trade risk, chaired by director Suzanne Nora Johnson, with Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri beside her.

It meets at least quarterly, with management and in executive session, and it will keep independent outside experts in law, national security, and trade compliance. The National Security Team and the Global Trade Compliance Team report to that committee and to interim chief executive Colette Kress on day-to-day work.

Chairman Harvey Jones said Nvidia has a responsibility to help safeguard national security while innovation continues. The Financial Times says the step comes as Washington criticizes Nvidia’s ambition to grow revenue in China, after the U S moved to cut off China’s access to the company’s most powerful A I chips.

Apple is close to a long DRAM contract that breaks its usual habit of short deals and hard price pressure. Maeil Business Newspaper, via MacRumors, says the Samsung agreement would run four to five years from the first half of twenty twenty-seven, set a minimum supply volume, and leave prices to be set later from market conditions.

Apple recently signed a similar, supplier-favorable deal with SK hynix as the memory shortage worsens and the A I data-center buildout lifts component costs.

Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust agreed to buy a seventy-five percent majority in two joint ventures whose data centers are leased to Amazon Web Services, its first deal since the New York Stock Exchange listing. Telecompaper puts the portfolio at about five billion dollars: thirteen newly built powered-shell buildings on two U S campuses, six hundred forty-seven megawatts of IT capacity.

Google Cloud opened applications for a Sovereign A I startup accelerator in Europe and Israel, equity-free and fifteen weeks, for late-seed to post-Series A companies building under European rules. The blog calls artificial intelligence an estimated one point two trillion euro economic opportunity for Europe, and it names the E U A I Act, NIS2, DORA, and national frameworks such as SecNumCloud.

Defense work is out. The cohort is aimed at government services, healthcare and life sciences, regulated finance, and civilian cybersecurity.

Community opposition to Amazon data centers is not new. The Financial Times now ties it to a seventeen-billion-dollar Ohio A I data-centre bet under voter backlash, and says revolts across the U S threaten billions in A I investment as communities turn against Big Tech’s expansion.

Meta says it has strengthened the default protections on what Instagram recommends in India, to cut unwanted content, guided by what users said they want. Bloomberg reports Meta removed more explicit Instagram posts there after a series of lapses, under tougher scrutiny in a country of over half a billion users.

The Times of India reports the government has flagged risks from recommendation systems.

Also today, Amazon said it will invest seven billion dollars in renewable energy in India by twenty thirty and announced four new wind and solar projects, including its largest wind farm contracted in Asia. Amazon also set a science-aligned interim target to cut electricity carbon intensity sixty-five percent by twenty thirty, calling itself a leading corporate buyer of carbon-free energy.

Android Central reports Gemini users can now keep chat history without feeding Google’s training pipeline. Google is adding file attachments and sharing to Gemini Skills as Gems retire starting today. Gemini is getting sixty-six new regional voices on Android and iOS. TradingView reports Google DeepMind strategy chief Jagjit Sekhon is joining Anthropic.

Deepu Talla, Nvidia’s robotics chief, told The Information that Open A I’s Astra model, out since September, has made some robotic training data a hundred times faster to create and could help automate putting robots to work in factories.

Rubin’s core count is the claim that still has to survive procurement math. A I Weekly, citing the HPCG benchmark, says top supercomputers reach only three percent of theoretical peak on the sparse workloads most relevant to Rubin HPC, with two procurement cycles between this tease and silicon.

The Vera CPU rack — one hundred ninety-two CPUs, about three hundred terabytes of memory, and one petabyte of storage — arrives in early twenty twenty-seven, a full year before that G P U.

Nvidia, Alphabet, and Meta are each trying to place advanced compute where a shared queue is not the default. Nvidia’s DGX Spark HPC Developer Kit is already on sale at the same supercomputing conference, four Spark units totaling five hundred twelve gigabytes of memory and twenty terabytes of NVMe.

Each node is a GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip: one hundred twenty-eight gigabytes of LPDDR5X, six thousand one hundred forty-four CUDA cores, up to one petaflop of FP4, a one-hundred-forty-watt thermal design power, and two hundred seventy-three gigabytes per second of bandwidth.

Twenty Arm cores split into ten Cortex-X925 cores at up to four gigahertz and ten Cortex-A725 cores at up to two point eight gigahertz. ConnectX-7 joins each box to two neighbors in a ring, so there is no separate switch. Nvidia says NVFP4 lets those four nodes run models of up to seven hundred billion parameters, a class it illustrates with DeepSeek V4.1.

Nvidia says the Rubin Lab at the University of California, Berkeley, got genomic results fifteen times sooner than an eleven-hour cluster queue, and Berkeley’s Hartwig Group fine-tuned a one-hundred-twenty-billion-parameter-class chemistry model on a single Spark. The shared problem is who holds the machine. cuPhoton.xBeam cut Event Horizon Telescope analysis from one month to eight minutes.

KTH Royal Institute used Nemotron for Science to repair eighty-one percent of faulty molecular-dynamics simulations overnight; that toolkit includes Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and the LitmusBench v0.2 dataset. TCBP-JOBS cut a design step from twelve weeks to forty-five minutes.

In Brussels, Google said select Gemini models are coming to Google Cloud Dedicated, run by an independent local partner on isolated infrastructure. Those models already carry an in-region processing commitment in the E U, and Gemini is already on Google Distributed Cloud.

In France, S3NS has made managed model serving generally available on PREMI3NS, including Mistral and Google open models or a customer’s own. Confidential External Key Management is in preview, so a customer or local partner can hold root keys outside Google while operations run in hardware-attested confidential computing.

A Telefónica partnership in Spain, also in preview, widens who can hold keys. A Data Boundary framework for Switzerland is available today, and a DORA framework is planned for financial services. A lab can develop on that ring with Nvidia’s HPC S D K — G P U accelerated C, C++, and Fortran, plus MPI — then scale to larger Nvidia supercomputers.

LAMMPS and NAMD are confirmed on the four-unit kit; the community reports GROMACS, OpenFOAM, and WRF run too. A European hospital or finance team can aim Gemini at a partner-operated cloud while key control is still partly a preview. Meta’s path is custom memory: a Korean outlet reports Samsung is developing HBM4E for Meta’s A I chips on a two-nanometer base die.

Cassava Technologies is seeking as much as one billion dollars to buy Nvidia chips for African A I data centers, people familiar with the matter told Bloomberg — a raise being sought, not a cluster switched on. The kit itself is the piece already for sale, through Acer, Asus, Dell, Gigabyte, H3C, HP, Lenovo, MSI, and xFusion, and through the Nvidia Marketplace.
