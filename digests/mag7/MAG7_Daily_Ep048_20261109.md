# MAG 7 Daily
> **Nvidia will link Korea's R200 chips into its racks for sovereign AI.**

**What You Need to Know:** Nvidia, Rebellions and SK Telecom announced a plan to connect Korean-designed R200 processors to Nvidia's rack platform for sovereign AI. The same Seoul day brought startup cheques that are not all closed, and Apple pulled a retail subscription target after consent complaints. Google's cheapest paid Gemini tier loses Pro model selection on November 13.

### Top News
**NVIDIA, Rebellions and SK Telecom Announce Collaboration to Advance Sovereign AI Infrastructure With NVIDIA NVLink Fusion: NVIDIA Newsroom**
Nvidia, Rebellions and SK Telecom, with support from Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT, announced a collaboration that uses NVLink Fusion to join Rebellions' Korean-designed processors to Nvidia's open infrastructure platform. Rebellions plans to adopt NVLink Fusion so its next-generation R200 neural processing units can connect for rack-scale inference, and to use the MGX rack architecture for that system work. SK Telecom plans to deploy the systems in its AI data center, tying the racks to its models and to services under Korea's "AI for All" project. Jensen Huang said sovereign AI lets nations turn language, culture and expertise into intelligence, and that Korean chipmakers can connect custom ASICs to Nvidia's AI factory platform. Rebellions chief executive Sunghyun Park said the aim is low-latency, energy-efficient inference for frontier models, including multitrillion-parameter mixture-of-experts models. SK Telecom chief executive Jung Jai-hun said the company will link its data centers, its A.X K2 model and public services under "AI for All." Source: [nvidianews.nvidia.com](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-rebellions-and-sk-telecom-announce-collaboration-to-advance-sovereign-ai-infrastructure-with-nvidia-nvlink-fusion)

**NVIDIA Announces New Planned Investments in Korean Startups: NVIDIA Blog**
At the MSIT-NVIDIA AI Startup Partnership Forum at COEX in Seoul, Nvidia announced an investment in Point2 Technology and planned investments in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage, made directly rather than only through NVentures. Point2 builds radio-frequency interconnects that move data between chips and racks; Seoul Economic Daily reported that NVentures had already funded it in the first half of this year, and that Nvidia has now invested directly. Motif develops agent models, including Motif-3, a large language model released with open weights. RLWRLD builds robotics foundation models for tasks that need five-finger dexterity, including inserting components, threading nuts onto bolts and kitting parts from moving conveyors. Upstage develops Solar language models and document AI tools. Vice minister Ryu Je-myung joined Nvidia leaders with more than 20 Korean startups. Nvidia Inception already has more than 1,000 Korea-based members, who get preferred GPU pricing and investor introductions. Seoul Economic Daily reported that the three planned investments are not yet completed, and it named the robotics firm Realworld. Source: [blogs.nvidia.com](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/korean-startup-investments-msit/)

**Apple Store Subscription Sign-Up Controversy Exposed: MacRumors**
MacRumors reported that technician Louis Rossmann's videos over the past two weeks describe Apple Store employees signing customers up for auto-renewing trials of Apple One and other services without informed consent, including by tapping Agree themselves or calling trials gifts. After a customer scans a "Connect and Personalize" QR code, staff can see devices owned, services already subscribed and offers tied to recent purchases, and the default Apple Account payment method is available if it has not expired. Until early last week Apple tracked a "services attach rate" with a 40 percent target for each retail employee at at least some U.S. stores, meaning four of every 10 customers helped with a device purchase also taking a subscription. MacRumors confirmed Apple removed that metric early last week; the explanation given was that it is less relevant in the holiday season, when many purchases are gifts. Later in the week Apple sent retail staff a memo on privacy and consent for trials, Connect and Personalize, Apple Upgrade sign-ups and Genius Bar authorizations, ahead of holiday shopping that started Friday. Apple emails customers when a trial starts. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/08/apple-store-subscription-controversy/)

**Nvidia's board opens an independent probe after Huang's admission: The Information**
Nvidia's board is initiating an independent investigation, overseen by independent directors and outside counsel, into reporting that chief executive Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, after he admitted an extramarital affair. He remains chief executive for now. The company said the board has no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. The inquiry coincides with a congressional inquiry into the same subject. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/nvidia-board-launching-investigation-reporting-ceo-targeted-chinese-informant)

### The Thread: The five-dollar gate
Google AI Plus is four dollars and ninety-nine cents a month in the United States and four euros and ninety-nine cents in Germany. Google AI Pro, the next tier, is nineteen dollars and ninety-nine cents in the United States and twenty-one euros and ninety-nine cents in Germany. Notebookcheck, working from an email to subscribers that 9to5Google published in mid-October, puts the U.S. step-up at fifteen dollars more a month. On a page Notebookcheck checked on November 9, AI Plus already lists the Flash model only. Gemini 4 Argon and the Pro model start with AI Pro. Plus keeps usage limits two times standard and a context window of one hundred twenty-eight thousand tokens. Pro gets limits four times standard and a one-million-token window. Mezha reported that Google had cut AI Plus to that four-dollar-and-ninety-nine-cent price and raised storage on the plan to four hundred gigabytes.

Microsoft and Meta do not get prices in these pieces. Notebookcheck tells a reader weighing that fifteen-dollar step to compare current plans from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot before paying it. Copilot is Microsoft's name on that list. A second Notebookcheck article the same day asks what parents can control in ChatGPT, Gemini and Meta AI. The text supplied here does not spell those controls out, so Meta enters as the company whose assistant is now in the same parental-settings question, not as a priced rival tier.

The shared piece is the paid picker, not a joint product. Google's email says that from the change date, selecting Pro models in the Gemini app and in AI Mode in Search will be available only to Google AI Pro subscribers and above. Plus subscribers keep the latest Flash-Lite model and Flash. Older Pro chats are not deleted and can continue on a different model. The help page makes the practical split about file length: the token gap matters most when someone uploads long PDFs or large sets of files. Flash is described as quick for short questions, emails and summaries. Pro is described as more thorough for longer research, coding questions and complex math. Subscribers can manage or cancel at any time.

What changes is who can call the heavier model on the cheap plan. Alphabet keeps a low monthly price and a larger storage allotment on that plan, then reserves Argon and Pro, the larger context window and the higher usage multiple for the dearer tier. Microsoft is the named Copilot alternative in the comparison Notebookcheck points to, without a figure of its own in this reporting. Meta is the named assistant in the same day's parent-control guide, again without a control list or a price in the text we have. The gate is a setting in an app and in Search, not a new model launch. The date the email names for the picker change is November 13, 2026. Source: [notebookcheck.net](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Google-AI-Plus-loses-Gemini-Pro-models-on-November-13.1445681.0.html)
