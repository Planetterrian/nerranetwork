> **IP68 failed: dust killed the iPhone Duo's inner display.**

**What You Need to Know:** Dust entered the iPhone Duo's hinge in JerryRigEverything's test and left the inner display unresponsive, despite the phone's IP68 rating. Teardowns the same day put the two battery cells at a combined 4,883 mAh. Sweden's Government Offices will block ChatGPT and Claude on staff computers from October 26 and leave Microsoft Copilot as the only approved assistant.

### Top News
**iPhone Duo fails a dusty durability test: MacRumors**
JerryRigEverything's test got dust into the iPhone Duo hinge through several ingress points, and the dust overwhelmed the magnets that hold the phone shut. The bend test then left a permanent green line across the inner display, and the screen became unresponsive. The outer screen, with Ceramic Shield 2, scratched at level 7 on the Mohs scale. The inner screen managed level 2, though its matte nano-texture coating made marks disappear. Nelson, who said he has been testing folding phones since the first generation seven years ago and that most survive, said the iPhone Duo did not. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/24/iphone-duo-fails-jerryrigeverything-test/)

**iPhone Duo teardowns show two batteries and a repair path: MacRumors**
REWA Technology and Phone Repair Guru posted the first teardown videos. The phone has two battery cells that come out with electrically activated adhesive: a larger cell rated 2,962 mAh (11.56 Wh) and a smaller one rated 1,921 mAh (7.5 Wh), for 4,883 mAh in total. REWA's labeled logic board shows Apple's A20 Pro chip and C2 cellular modem. Phone Repair Guru found the Touch ID button can be removed for repair, but called the process difficult, and said the USB-C port is fully modular. The Duo is eSIM-only worldwide. Apple has published its repair manual, and iFixit is expected to post a fuller teardown in the coming days. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/24/iphone-duo-teardown-videos/)

**Sweden blocks ChatGPT and Claude, keeps Microsoft Copilot: Decrypt**
Sweden's Government Offices will block ChatGPT and Claude on staff computers from Monday, October 26, and direct civil servants to Microsoft Copilot as the only approved assistant, Sveriges Radio reported. Mikael Wettercrantz, the offices' director of digitalisation, wrote on the staff intranet that using AI services without a formal contract can have consequences for information security, citing both the sensitivity of information entered and how AI-generated responses were handled. Consumer tools without a formal agreement send queries to external company servers, and U.S. law can compel those companies to hand data over. Copilot cleared a review in which the offices raised its security settings; ChatGPT and Claude did not get that treatment. Some employees can still reach both blocked tools through an internal platform the offices built. The Tax Agency and the National Board of Institutional Care have already banned consumer AI tools. The Enforcement Authority is preparing its own decision. A Transport Administration audit found roughly 10 AI services in use in a widespread and risky way. In February the European Parliament disabled ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot on parliamentary devices. Microsoft added Claude models to Copilot in early 2026; in the EU those Anthropic models default to off unless an administrator enables them, and the Government Offices have not confirmed whether Claude is switched on in their Copilot. ### The Thread: Version 66.0 lands on Apple TV
Version 66.0 is the build number Apple's TV-specific release metadata assigns to Instagram, and that metadata dates the build to October 23. That is the first day Instagram is on Apple TV. An archived App Store listing from October 19 still showed compatibility with only the iPhone and the iPad. The app plays Reels, longer videos, and live shows. Users can watch content from creators they follow, discover videos, and resume watching across devices. Instagram describes the app as a way to watch together with friends and family, which puts a feed built for a phone onto a screen a room can share.

Meta did not start that television job on Apple's hardware. Instagram first began testing its TV app on Amazon Fire TV devices in the United States in December 2025, before expanding to Google TV in February 2026 and to Samsung smart TVs in June. The initial experience focused on Reels organized into channels based on viewers' interests, with videos playing automatically. In June, Meta outlined plans to expand beyond short videos with longer creator content, episodic series, and live broadcasts. The Apple TV app's description lists longer videos and live shows alongside Reels, which means the June plan is the list that reached Apple's store. The release follows Instagram's dedicated iPad app, which arrived in September 2025 after years of requests for an interface designed for Apple's tablets. The Apple TV app requires tvOS 26.0 or later, and it is on the Apple TV App Store.

The dependency is the store and the operating system, not a shared balance sheet. Meta can reach Apple's streaming device only if Apple lists the app and only on tvOS 26.0 or later. Apple's box, in turn, now carries a Meta catalog — Reels, longer videos, and live shows — next to the shows Apple sells under its own TV brand. Resume-across-devices is the practical link: a video started on an iPhone can continue on Apple TV, so Meta's account and Apple's screen are in the same session. The earlier dates show a platform tour, not a one-company launch. December 2025 was a test on Amazon's Fire TV, in the United States. February 2026 was Google TV. June was Samsung, and June was also when Meta said the product would leave short video behind. October 19 is the last listing in today's record that still stopped at iPhone and iPad. October 23 is version 66.0 on Apple TV. September 2025 is the tablet step that came before the television step on Apple's own hardware. No article here states a price for the app, a user count, or a date for a following version. What changed, with dates attached, is availability: from a test on Amazon's living-room device, through Google TV and Samsung, to a shipping app on Apple's. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/instagram-launches-apple-tv-app/)
