### Top News
**NVIDIA unveils Vera CPU rack-scale platform for HPC: Engineering.com**
NVIDIA announced the Vera CPU rack-scale platform, pairing its first fully custom data-center CPU with Quantum-X800 InfiniBand for climate modeling, molecular dynamics and other scientific workloads that are not accelerated on GPUs. The liquid-cooled rack brings together 192 Vera CPUs and delivers more than 200 TB/s of aggregate peak memory bandwidth. Each Vera CPU has 88 custom Olympus cores and 176 threads, with serviceable SOCAMM2 LPDDR5X memory delivering up to 1.2 TB/s; the rack holds about 300 TB of memory and 1 petabyte of storage in an MGX design that reuses NVL72 chassis, power and cooling, with eight CPUs, NVMe storage and management in each 1U tray. Quantum-X800 connects the system, and SHARP in-network computing is meant to cut the time processors spend coordinating. HPC SDK 26.11 is available now, including a public beta of a next-generation Fortran compiler so existing Fortran programs can run faster on NVIDIA platforms without a rewrite, and Vera racks are expected from Dell Technologies, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro beginning in early 2027. Source: [engineering.com](https://www.engineering.com/nvidia-unveils-vera-cpu-rack-scale-platform-for-hpc/)

At Ignite 2026, Microsoft made Word, Excel and PowerPoint generally available inside Copilot, and chief executive Satya Nadella described Copilot as the new operating system for work. Estimates of employee time saved are calculated with a model rather than measured directly, Digital Trends reported. ### The Counterpoint
**NVIDIA's own tests, and a forecast labeled projected: Engineering.com**
The speed claim that has to survive a customer's benchmark is still NVIDIA's. In NVIDIA's tests, Vera delivered 1.9 times the performance and 2.4 times the performance per watt of x86 systems on one OpenFOAM case, the motorBike Large airflow simulation. On the open-source Weather Research and Forecasting model, the gains NVIDIA cites are projected: 1.6 times the performance and 2 times the energy efficiency versus x86. Those four multiples, not the core count, are the numbers that decide whether the rack changes a climate or airflow run before a buyer has it. Source: [engineering.com](https://www.engineering.com/nvidia-unveils-vera-cpu-rack-scale-platform-for-hpc/)

### The Thread: The revenue under an open chair
Nvidia's third-quarter revenue was $115.8 billion, up 103 percent from a year earlier, including $107.6 billion from the data-center business, interim chief executive Colette Kress said on Tuesday's earnings call. The company raised its forecast for next fiscal year's revenue growth to about 75 percent, from 70 percent. Yesterday the open question on this spending cycle was whether guidance would move; that raise is the move. Kress spent 13 years at Microsoft earlier in her career. She said customers, suppliers and employees remain behind the company, called the transition business as usual, and ended the call with "We've got this."

Amazon's founder answered the vacancy from outside the building the same day. Jeff Bezos, now co-chief executive of the AI engineering startup Prometheus, said at Semafor's Silicon Valley and the World event that if Nvidia does not bring Jensen back he would not hire an outsider. Nvidia is too complicated, he said. Outside chief executives at complex companies, he added, have a vanishingly small chance of succeeding, and he would treat that hire as a last resort. He said Blue Origin is using Prometheus tools on two projects. When Semafor raised his 2021 handoff of Amazon to Andy Jassy, the longtime insider who built AWS, Bezos called the comparison a poor one. There has never been a more carefully planned transition, he said, and he did not know enough about Nvidia's board meetings to say whether Huang should return. He called Huang a remarkable founder who deserves admiration and respect no matter what. Asked about his own calendar, he described running Prometheus as nirvana.

Huang had already pointed inward. In a Bloomberg interview recorded October 14 he said he hoped his successor was already inside the company. He was chief executive for 33 years. Harvey Jones, the longtime director named Nvidia's first board chair last week, said the search will include internal and external candidates, that the board wants a permanent chief executive quickly, and that it will not rush the pick. Altimeter Capital's Brad Gerstner and Atreides Management's Gavin Baker asked the board to leave open a return. Dan Ives of Yorkville Ives wrote in a Sunday note that talk of a comeback is ridiculous.

What changes is who is allowed to carry the book those three companies already touch. Amazon's own handoff, the one Bezos refused as a template, put the person who built its cloud in the chair, and he is now telling Nvidia's directors that an outsider is a last resort even while their search still lists external names. Microsoft is not bidding for the job; it is the company that trained the interim who just signed the higher growth forecast and told customers the quarter was ordinary. Nvidia's candidates, inside or out, inherit a data-center franchise whose interim steward came from that Microsoft career and whose most famous outside adviser built Amazon by promoting from within. Investors who want the former chief executive kept in play are asking the chair to hold a door he has framed as a search. Customers heard the interim say the company still has it.
