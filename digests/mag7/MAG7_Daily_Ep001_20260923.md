### Top News
**U.S. government urges Supreme Court to reverse Apple contempt ruling over App Store commission:** 9to5Mac
The U.S. government and several legal and business groups filed briefs with the Supreme Court urging reversal of at least part of the civil contempt ruling against Apple in the Epic Games lawsuit. The district court held Apple in contempt after the company allowed external purchase links yet imposed a 27 percent commission on those transactions. Apple has since removed the commission entirely. The company maintains that the original injunction text did not prohibit charging a commission and that lower courts improperly relied on the spirit of the order rather than its wording. The injunction states that Apple is permanently restrained from prohibiting developers from including buttons, external links or other calls to action that direct customers to purchasing mechanisms outside In-App Purchasing. The Supreme Court agreed in late June to hear Apple’s argument on this point. Source: [9to5mac.com](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/22/u-s-government-urges-supreme-court-to-reverse-apple-contempt-ruling-over-app-store-commission/)

**Google and the Gates Foundation to bring AI resources to 200 million farmers across the Global South.:** Google Blog
Google partnered with the Gates Foundation to direct $100 million toward organizations that scale AI-powered climate and crop insights. The program targets 200 million smallholder farmers in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, an increase from the current 50 million reached. Smallholder farmers produce nearly 35 percent of the world’s food across more than 500 million farms yet often lack access to satellite data, financial services and public support needed for unpredictable weather. The combined funding includes dedicated technical support from Google researchers to deploy real-time climate, agricultural and language AI tools. Source: [blog.google](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/google-org/partnering-with-the-gates-foundation-to-bring-ai-resources-to-200-million-farmers-across-the-global-south/)

**Microsoft to Boost Copilot Discounts as It Launches AI ‘Super App’:** The Information
Microsoft leaders told sales staff this week that the company is authorizing 30-to-50 percent discounts on Copilot AI software subscriptions for corporate customers who commit to large numbers of seats and agree to additional payments based on usage of certain features. The steeper discounts apply when customers commit to specific feature usage levels. The company is simultaneously preparing an AI super app for launch. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/exclusive-microsoft-boost-copilot-discounts-launches-ai-super-app)

The feature lets merchants accept contactless payments directly on iPhone without extra hardware.
