The piece treats the missing vote as the reason antitrust authorities would not have to worry about influence over Anthropic's management. That is The Information's reading, not a court ruling. A convertible note is a promise to become stock, not stock today, so Amazon can stay at or under twelve percent of the shares that count while the notes wait.

An economic stake does not, by itself, put either company's executives in the room for a model, a price, or a customer. The eighteen billion six hundred million is spent capital. The two hundred fifty-eight billion is midpoint math on a proposed range, and it moves if the final print moves.
