> **Unilever cut order handling from twenty minutes to ninety seconds for retailers.**

**What You Need to Know:** Unilever cut order handling from twenty minutes to ninety seconds for retailers, using a Microsoft-built agent system now running in production. NVIDIA published a full reference architecture for containing enterprise agents, Mazda put Gemini in a Canadian CX-5, and a New Mexico order plus a California tax arrangement landed on Meta the same week Apple blocked a set of weak web certificates.

### Top News
**Unilever’s multi-agent creating customer value at global scale: Microsoft UK Stories**
Unilever has launched NOVA, five AI agents built with Microsoft on Foundry, to read, check and enter customer orders that still arrive as emails, PDFs, spreadsheets or photos. Microsoft UK Stories reports that about 300,000 direct customers are served, and that 3.7 billion people use a Unilever product every day. Four agents read the message and extract the order; a fifth checks the work. Francis Castro, head of technology for customer operations, says orders in the pilots are eight times faster than before, refund claims are processed twice as fast, and new-customer onboarding has been reduced by 90 percent. Microsoft engineers joined Unilever to build and test claims validation in less than eight weeks. NOVA has already processed thousands of orders in production. Source: [ukstories.microsoft.com](https://ukstories.microsoft.com/features/unilevers-multi-agent-creating-customer-value-at-global-scale/)

**NVIDIA unveils architecture for secure enterprise AI agents: Engineering.com**
NVIDIA today announced the OpenShell Reference Architecture for building, containing and enforcing safe agents, extending the OpenShell secure runtime it had already set out. Engineering.com reports that Vera Rubin NVL72 systems are said to deliver 30 times higher throughput per megawatt on agentic workloads, while Vera CPUs are said to deliver twice the performance of x86 processors and to run the OpenShell boundary. BlueField-4 runs NVIDIA Sentry, which quarantines an agent that leaves its software boundary in milliseconds and boosts agent capacity by up to 20 percent. ServiceNow said Arc, a personal long-running agent, is generally available, with policies carried to OpenShell and Sentry so access can be revoked across software and hardware within seconds. The stack is described as able to run on Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, including Microsoft Windows via Microsoft Execution Containers, and to host agents from builders including Google, Meta and OpenAI. Source: [engineering.com](https://www.engineering.com/nvidia-unveils-architecture-for-secure-enterprise-ai-agents/)

**Apple Addresses Web Security Threat, Working on macOS Update With Additional Fix: MacRumors**
Apple has been blocking eMudhra’s compromised TLS certificates since October 19 in Safari, Mail, and apps that use Apple’s networking and security frameworks, MacRumors reports from Apple’s support document. A researcher found that private keys on eight of the Indian firm’s intermediate certificate-authority certificates were weak; those keys could have allowed impersonation of legitimate sites, and Apple says there is currently no evidence of malicious exploitation. The block is automatic and does not require a software update for most devices. macOS command-line tools can still accept the certificates until a macOS update changes the default /etc/ssl/cert.pem file; until then, administrators can remove the root CA certificates from that file. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/apple-blocks-compromised-emudhra-certificates/)

**Meta loses $124bn in value as AI spending plans hit US tech shares: Business Matters**

**Springer Nature and Google DeepMind partner on AI peer review assistant: Research Information**
Springer Nature and Google DeepMind have announced a research partnership to build an AI peer-review assistant that helps reviewers navigate manuscripts, charts and figures without replacing human judgement. Research Information reports that a prototype is in test, built with Google’s open-source Agent Development Kit and run inside Springer Nature’s private Gemini Enterprise environment so manuscripts used in development stay on Springer Nature’s infrastructure. The project builds on Google tools including ScholarPeer and Deep Review, and on Springer Nature’s in-house platform Snapp. Saskia Steinacker, chief product officer at Springer Nature, said reviewer expertise remains fundamental and that innovation and trust have to move together. Seshu Ajjarapu, senior director of applied AI at Google DeepMind, said the aim is a review experience that keeps pace with submission volume while holding journal standards. Source: [researchinformation.info](https://www.researchinformation.info/news/springer-nature-and-google-deepmind-partner-on-ai-peer-review-assistant/)

### The Counterpoint
**The ninety-second cut is still a pilot: Microsoft UK Stories**
The time cut is Castro’s figure for 11 pilot markets, not for the order book as a whole. Unilever still takes about two million orders a year in non-standard formats, out of about 20 million, and those are the ones NOVA is built to catch. The plan is to expand past 50 markets. In the pilots, orders are 95 percent touchless, with people still stepping in on exceptions, and Deepalok Roy Chowdhury said there was apprehension when the work started. Whether ninety seconds survives contact with the other markets is the number that decides it: the touchless rate once those 50 are in production, not the rate inside the 11. Source: [ukstories.microsoft.com](https://ukstories.microsoft.com/features/unilevers-multi-agent-creating-customer-value-at-global-scale/)

### The Thread: Two clouds inside one Korean bank
More than 2,000 servers and more than 100 services already run in Amazon Web Services for KB Financial Group. In 2020 the group became the first South Korean financial group to sign a corporate contract for AWS financial cloud, Seoul Economic Daily reports, and AWS has now invited AI executives from the group and from KB Kookmin Bank to its Executive Briefing Center.

Alphabet is already inside the same group. In July, KB worked with Google Cloud on KB Davis, a Gemini-based data analytics AI, and on KB Star Byeoli, a customer consultation AI. As internal AI services multiply, KB Kookmin Bank is trying to link AWS, Google Cloud and its own AI systems so the data those tools need can move between them.

The 2020 contract was a diversification, not a swap. KB wanted more than one cloud so a single provider could not become a single point of failure, and it wanted AWS technologies in financial services faster. The practical result was a parallel estate: AWS was added to the group platform Cleon, and the group now runs that estate of more than 2,000 servers and more than 100 services beside whatever it already had.

The newer layer is models, not racks. Amazon Bedrock lets a financial firm call an outside model, including Anthropic’s Claude, inside AWS security and management controls, without training the model itself. KB is tying internal systems to Bedrock at individual affiliates. The named ones are an AI investment-product information service at KB Asset Management, an AI development and operations platform for employees at KB Securities, and a customized real-estate information service at KB Kookmin Bank.

The sharpest build is the KB AI Dev Center, a dedicated AI software unit the bank opened in July this year. Developers describe the function they want in ordinary language. AI then supports design, coding and testing. AWS provided technical support for that development environment and for its monitoring framework, according to the paper’s sources. That is Amazon inside the bank’s software factory, not only inside its data center.

Google’s July work sits on the other side of the same factory. Davis is analytics. Star Byeoli is the customer conversation. Neither replaces Bedrock, and Bedrock does not replace them. The bank’s stated problem is the join: an environment in which AWS, Google Cloud and internal systems can share the data a given service needs. A deeper AWS briefing, which the paper says AWS proposed after taking an interest in how KB built its AI development framework, is a contest for the next workload between two suppliers the group already pays.

What changes for Amazon is a flagship financial account moving from a 2020 infrastructure contract into agent-style development, on an invitation AWS itself extended. What changes for Alphabet is that two named Gemini tools are already in the group, so any new AWS scope is won against an incumbent, not into an empty seat. What changes for KB is operational: more of the design-to-test loop sits with a vendor runtime, and the data path between the two clouds becomes the constraint on how far either assistant can go.

A KB Financial official said being invited to speak directly with senior AWS officials is itself meaningful, and that the group will look at further technical support and at how overseas financial firms use AI. The Seoul Economic Daily account is by Do Hye-won and was AI-translated from Korean; the paper warns that quotes from foreign sources may not match the original wording. The cooperation the official described is the scope still open: global practice, and more technical support for a framework that already spans both clouds. Source: [en.sedaily.com](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/26/kb-financial-group-deepens-aws-tie-up-to-speed-ai-banking)
