# MAG 7 Daily
> **Four Copilot pillars turn Microsoft's work software into an agent layer.**

**What You Need to Know:** Microsoft's largest Copilot update, announced on 25 September, is the same shift: Satya Nadella is pitching Copilot as a new operating system for work, built from Autopilot, Code, Home and Office. Home and Code start through the Frontier programme in the coming weeks. A later private-preview clock covers Autopilot and a new Today view.

### Top News
**Microsoft's largest Copilot update adds Home, Code and Autopilot: Dealroom**
Satya Nadella announced the largest Microsoft Copilot update to date on 25 September 2026 and called Copilot "a new OS for work" spanning every model, form factor and task. Autopilot is a proactive, long-running agent for the enterprise. Code lets people build apps with Copilot hosted inside the company tenant, on the same underlying technology as GitHub Copilot. Home merges Chat and Cowork into one starting point, and Office puts Word, Excel and PowerPoint fully inside Copilot. Copilot can be invoked in Teams. Today is a proactive view that surfaces the most important information across Microsoft 365. Microsoft says Home and Code begin rolling out through its Frontier programme in the coming weeks, Autopilot expands to private preview at the end of September, and Today enters private preview in October, later coming to Outlook and Teams. Source: [app.dealroom.co](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/microsoft-unveils-its-biggest-copilot-update-home-code-and-autopilot)

### Company Desk
**DeepMind's chief says Gemini 4 should land well before year-end: Trending Topics**
Post-training on Gemini 4 was already in view; what changed is the clock. Koray Kavukcuoglu, head of Google DeepMind, said at The Information's AI Agenda Live Summit that the model should launch "much earlier" than the end of the year, and that Google wants an early version "as soon as possible," then fast improvements, while it is fine-tuned for safety and reliability. Sundar Pichai describes Gemini 4 as a "significantly larger" frontier model focused on coding and autonomous AI agents. In July, DeepMind started what it called its "most ambitious pre-training run yet." Gemini 3.5 Pro, originally announced for I/O in May, never shipped, reportedly because its coding performance fell short. Google instead released cheaper Flash models, Gemini 3.7 Flash in August and Gemini 3.8 Flash in early September. The last true flagship, Gemini 3 Pro, dates to November 2025. TestingCatalog reports Google has been testing Gemini 4 Pro checkpoints under the code name Argon since mid-September; those leaks say a response can run to 256,000 tokens, four times the previous limit, and that the highest reasoning mode took 2.4 to 20 minutes an answer. Source: [trendingtopics.eu](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/gemini-4-rumors-google/)

### The Counterpoint
**The new work OS is being stripped from Windows: Windows Latest**
The lead treats Copilot as a new operating system for work. Windows Latest reports the opposite move on the actual PC operating system: Nadella says "We're building Copilot as a new OS" even as Microsoft strips Copilot from Windows 11. The open question is which surface the agent layer actually lives on once it is no longer a Windows 11 feature. Source: [windowslatest.com](https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/09/26/were-building-copilot-as-a-new-os-says-satya-nadella-even-as-microsoft-strips-it-from-windows-11/)

### The Thread: A twenty-trillion claim and an empty stage
Ars Technica is the dispatch that puts two of the seven in one frame. On Thursday, Tesla's team was in Sparks, Nevada, for a livestreamed, invite-only factory event that officially launched high-volume production of the Semi. The truck in that account is the 500-mile Semi. The concept first rolled onto a Tesla stage in late 2017, and this production start comes nearly a decade later. The room was aimed at a niche: cost-conscious people who manage large trucking fleets. A handful of them, all men, crossed the stage to be thanked for their orders, under a pulsing techno beat. Musk was not there. A pre-taped video played his line: "It's going to be, really, a driver's truck. It's like a sports car in truck form." Ars notes he skipped his second vehicle debut in a row. He was at a White House China State Dinner featuring President Xi Jinping. The company claim that names another of the seven sits beside that absence. Musk has insisted investors think of Tesla as a robotics and autonomous-vehicle company, and he has targeted a $20 trillion valuation, nearly four times the value Ars assigns to Nvidia as market leader. The piece sets a physical limit next to that figure: charging gaps still limit electric trucking as the 500-mile truck moves into volume production. The cross-company fact is not a shared chip order or a joint filing. It is a valuation premise measured against Nvidia, spoken by a chief executive who did not attend his own production launch. Source: [arstechnica.com](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/09/teslas-big-electric-truck-faces-an-even-bigger-infrastructure-challenge/)
