**Muse for Small Business connects the agent to shops' existing tools: Meta Newsroom**
Earlier this month Meta introduced Muse, a personal AI agent in the United States and Canada. Today it is adding skills and connectors so the agent can help people run small businesses. Muse can connect Instagram professional-account analytics, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts, and it can also connect to dozens of tools a business already uses, including custom connectors. Interested partners can apply at muse.ai/platform. Tom Mulholland, owner of Mulholland Grocery in Malvern, Iowa, said he works about 65 hours a week and that Muse is taking over a few roles so he can get back to cutting the steaks and making the sausages. Canva co-founder and chief product officer Cameron Adams said that by connecting Muse with Canva, templates and brand context become part of the conversation. Source: [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/)

**Gemini Gems give way to Skills on November 17: Android Central**
Google has posted an alert in Gemini's Gems view that Gems will be replaced by Skills on November 17, 2026, and that migration of existing Gems will begin automatically that day. People can keep using Gems until those Gems migrate. Skills are reusable custom instructions that can be stacked in one chat, called by typing the skill's name, and applied when Gemini decides a skill fits the prompt. Google is also working on typing @ and then the skill name. Skills are available now in Gemini chats for signed-in users over 18 on personal accounts, with work and school accounts planned later. Google introduced Gems in August 2024 and later brought them to Workspace, including pre-made Gems for Gmail, Drive, Sheets, and Slides. Source: [androidcentral.com](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/ai/gemini-gems-are-heading-out-google-to-replace-them-with-skills-in-november)

**Google has started removing Assistant from phones for Gemini: Android Authority**
Android Authority reports that Google has started killing Assistant on phones in favor of Gemini. Autoevolution reports that Android Auto users must now use Gemini. Source: [androidauthority.com](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-now-killing-assistant-gemini-3716545/)

**One Step Ahead has reached more than 303 million people: Meta Newsroom**
Meta said its One Step Ahead anti-scam education campaign, launched in May 2026 with more than 25 local safety organizations, law-enforcement bodies, and government agencies across Asia-Pacific, has reached more than 303 million people across 18 countries. The campaign delivered over 1.3 billion impressions and more than 1.2 million link clicks. A Know Your Local Resources element alone reached more than 27.4 million people across 14 markets. It points people to two-factor authentication, passkeys, Security Checkup, Privacy Checkup, WhatsApp's Linked Device Warning, and WhatsApp group settings that control who can add someone to a group. Meta said the campaign will continue throughout 2026. Source: [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/meta-one-step-ahead-campaign/)

### The Thread: An authorization larger than Apple's 2024 program
Nvidia's board approved an additional 150 billion dollars of share repurchases, leaving 235 billion dollars of authorization still available, and the company expects to execute that remaining program through fiscal 2028. An authorization is not cash already spent. It is permission to buy shares, with the actual purchases still subject to Nvidia's own decisions and to market conditions. The company said this increase is the highest ever. Its latest SEC filing showed repurchases already under way: 94 million shares for 19.7 billion dollars in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, and 203 million shares for 39.8 billion dollars in the first half. Apple is the comparison sitting inside the same announcement. Apple's board approved an additional 110 billion dollar repurchase program in May 2024, and Apple later used the final 19.8 billion dollars under that program during fiscal 2025. Nvidia's new 150 billion dollar addition is larger than Apple's entire 2024 authorization. The two figures measure different things: one is room Nvidia has just created, the other is a program Apple has already drawn down to its last 19.8 billion dollars. Source: [kucoin.com](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/nvidia-announces-150b-share-buyback-stock-rises-0-84)
