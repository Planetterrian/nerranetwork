**What You Need to Know:** Google's uprates and restarts have enabled over 1.5 gigawatts of new U.S. nuclear capacity, and a new Constellation Energy agreement is what carries that total over the line. ### Top News
**Google's Constellation deal pushes enabled U.S. nuclear capacity over 1.5 gigawatts: Google Blog**
Google says a new agreement with Constellation Energy brings the new nuclear capacity it has enabled for the U.S. energy system to over 1.5 gigawatts. The project named with the agreement is an uprate at the Braidwood Clean Energy Center in Illinois. The 1.5 gigawatt figure is the running total from uprates and restarts, and this agreement is what carries that total over the line. ### The Counterpoint
**The 1.5 gigawatts are grid enablement, not a Google-owned fleet: Google Blog**
The cut against reading the total as Google power plants is the post's own wording: capacity enabled for the U.S. energy system. Braidwood is an uprate project at an existing center, and the new Constellation agreement is the increment that moves a combined uprate-and-restart total over 1.5 gigawatts. The number that decides how much of that total is today's deal is the split between this agreement and the earlier ones, and the post gives the combined figure. Source: [blog.google](https://blog.google/company-news/why-were-backing-americas-existing-nuclear-plants/)
