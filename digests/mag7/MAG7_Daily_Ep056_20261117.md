> **More than 50,000 CUDA cores define Nvidia’s Rubin HPC for science, shipping in 2028.**

**What You Need to Know:** Nvidia used Supercomputing 2026 to unveil Rubin HPC, a GPU it says carries more than 50,000 CUDA cores and will ship in 2028 for scientific simulation. The current Rubin tops out at 28,672 CUDA cores. A Commerce Department license rule on remote chip access, a new Nvidia board committee, and an Apple memory contract sit beside that roadmap.

### Top News
**Nvidia unveils Rubin HPC with more than 50,000 CUDA cores: AI Weekly**
Nvidia used Supercomputing 2026 to tee up Rubin HPC, a GPU with more than 50,000 CUDA cores that it says will accelerate agentic workflows and simulation for scientists, engineers, and analysts working with large data sets. The current Rubin tops out at 28,672 CUDA cores. Nvidia is framing the part as a balanced design, pairing more compute with faster node-to-node movement over NVLink 7, which retains the 3.6 TB/s of per-GPU bandwidth already shipping on NVLink 6. Tom’s Hardware reports the design likely reuses a four-die layout similar to Rubin Ultra, linked by NVLink-C2C. Nvidia says Rubin HPC will arrive sometime in 2028. Ian Buck, vice president of hyperscale and HPC, said faster simulation means faster discovery. Source: [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/nvidia-unveils-rubin-hpc-gpu-with-over-50000-cuda-cores-at-sc26-shipping-2028)

**US requires licenses for some Nvidia chip sales, and curbs remote access: Bloomberg Technology**

**Nvidia’s board adds an export-control committee: NVIDIA Newsroom**
Nvidia’s board established a standing National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee for board-level oversight of national security policies, export-control compliance, and global trade risk management. Director Suzanne Nora Johnson will chair it, with directors Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri as members. The committee will meet at least quarterly, including with management and in executive session, and will retain independent outside experts in law, national security, and trade compliance. The existing National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team will report to the committee and to interim CEO Colette Kress on day-to-day operations. Chairman Harvey Jones said Nvidia has a responsibility to help safeguard national security while supporting continued innovation. The Financial Times reports the move comes as the company faces criticism in Washington over its ambition to increase revenues in China, after the US moved to cut off China’s access to Nvidia’s most powerful AI chips. ### The Thread: A desk, a partner cloud, a chip raise
Nvidia, Alphabet, and Meta are each trying to place advanced compute where a shared queue is not the default. Nvidia’s DGX Spark HPC Developer Kit, already on sale at SC26, links four Spark units into 512 GB of memory and 20 TB of NVMe. Each node is a GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip with 128 GB of LPDDR5X, 6,144 CUDA cores, up to 1 PFLOP of FP4, a 140-watt thermal design power, and 273 GB/s of bandwidth. Twenty Arm cores split into 10 Cortex-X925 cores at up to 4 GHz and 10 Cortex-A725 cores at up to 2.8 GHz. ConnectX-7 joins each box to two neighbors in a ring, so the kit needs no separate switch. Nvidia says NVFP4 lets those four nodes run models of up to 700 billion parameters, a class it illustrates with DeepSeek V4.1. The Rubin Lab at the University of California, Berkeley, Nvidia says, received genomic results 15 times sooner than a workflow stuck in an 11-hour cluster queue. Berkeley’s Hartwig Group fine-tuned a 120-billion-parameter-class chemistry model on a single Spark using NVFP4.

The shared problem is who holds the machine, not who posts the largest core count. At the same conference, cuPhoton.xBeam cut Event Horizon Telescope analysis from one month to 8 minutes. KTH Royal Institute used Nemotron for Science to repair 81 percent of faulty molecular-dynamics simulations overnight; the toolkit includes Nvidia’s Nemotron 3.5 Super VL open model and the LitmusBench v0.2 dataset. TCBP-JOBS cut a design step from 12 weeks to 45 minutes. In Brussels, Google said select Gemini models are coming to Google Cloud Dedicated, operated by an independent local partner on isolated infrastructure. Select Gemini models already carry a commitment to process customer data in-region in the EU, and Gemini is already available with Google Distributed Cloud. In France, S3NS has made managed model serving generally available on PREMI3NS, so teams can deploy Mistral and Google open models, or their own, inside an environment S3NS operates. Confidential External Key Management is in preview, designed so customers or a local partner hold root keys outside Google while key operations run in a hardware-attested confidential-computing environment. A Telefónica partnership in Spain, also in preview, expands who can hold those keys. A Data Boundary framework for Switzerland is available today. A framework for the EU’s DORA act is planned for financial services.

What changes is the path, not a single delivered campus. A lab can develop on the four-node ring with Nvidia’s HPC SDK — GPU-accelerated C, C++, and Fortran plus MPI — then scale to larger Nvidia supercomputers; LAMMPS and NAMD are confirmed on the four-unit kit, and the community reports that GROMACS, OpenFOAM, and WRF also run. A European hospital or finance team can aim Gemini at a partner-operated cloud while key control is still partly a preview and Switzerland already has a mapped framework. Meta’s route is a custom memory program: a Korean outlet reports Samsung is developing HBM4E for Meta’s AI chips on a 2nm base die. Cassava Technologies is seeking to raise as much as $1 billion to buy Nvidia chips for African AI data centers, people familiar with the matter told Bloomberg — a raise being sought, not a cluster switched on. The kit is the piece already on sale, through partners including Acer, Asus, Dell, Gigabyte, H3C, HP, Lenovo, MSI, and xFusion, and through the Nvidia Marketplace.
