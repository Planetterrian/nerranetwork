> **Moonshot scrapped a $150 million Nvidia chip rental after a new U.S. rule.**

**What You Need to Know:** Moonshot AI scrapped a $150 million rental of Nvidia Blackwell chips in an Indonesian data center after the U.S. restricted remote access to advanced chips. ### Top News
**China’s Moonshot AI Scraps $150 Million Deal to Access Nvidia Chips in Indonesia: The Information**
Moonshot AI, the Beijing developer of the Kimi models, has scrapped a $150 million deal to reach Nvidia Blackwell chips through a data center in Indonesia, after the U.S. this week announced a rule restricting Chinese companies from accessing advanced chips remotely, two people with direct knowledge told The Information. Moonshot had signed with a Southeast Asian neocloud to rent a sizable cluster of Blackwell Ultra B300 chips, with access starting as early as next year. The B300 is barred from sale to China. Remote rental from data centers outside China had been the workaround; the Commerce Department rule released this week closed it. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/chinas-moonshot-ai-scraps-150-million-deal-access-nvidia-chips-indonesia)

**Gemini 4 Neon: Google's new $3 AI model for coding: DNE Africa**
After shipping Gemini 4 Hydrogen for speed, Google on Wednesday released Gemini 4 Neon, placed between Hydrogen and Carbon and aimed at coding, analysis, science and long agent tasks. Google priced it at $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 per million cached inputs and $15 per million output tokens, with a context window of up to 1 million tokens across text, PDFs, charts, audio and video, and four thinking levels. Google said Neon scored 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, third overall, and ranked first on that firm’s coding-agent index; InfoWorld reported a coding-agent score of 58.6, plus 65.2 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.1, and dev.ua reported SWE-sweep rising from 12.8 percent in a base shell to 39.0 percent in Antigravity without a model change. Google said a task at the default high setting costs 32 percent less than on Gemini 4 Carbon, and quoted Glean saying Neon matched Carbon’s quality at three times the speed. It replaces Argon for Google AI Pro, $19.99 a month in the U.S.; free users and AI Plus, at $4.99 a month, do not get it, and DNE Africa said Google’s benchmarks have not been independently replicated. **Announcing distributed KV caching for self-hosted LLMs with Amazon ElastiCache: Amazon Web Services**
Amazon said distributed KV caching is generally available on ElastiCache for Valkey, a shared cache beside a GPU fleet that stores key-value state computed during prefill. Amazon said it cuts self-hosted inference cost by up to 40 percent and time to first token by up to 80 percent. Existing engines, including vLLM, SGLang and TensorRT-LLM, connect through the open-source LMCache connector with a configuration change and no code changes. In Amazon’s account of tests on B300 GPUs, prefill takes seconds for a 32K-token prompt, tens of seconds for 100K tokens and minutes past 250K; coding-agent traces from SemiAnalysis and TraceLab show more than 95 percent of prompt tokens sit in prefixes already processed. Source: [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/database/announcing-distributed-kv-caching-for-self-hosted-llms-with-amazon-elasticache/)

### The Thread: Scripted aliens, two robot suppliers
In 1963 Disney opened the Enchanted Tiki Room on audio-animatronics locked to one script. On Wednesday Disney and Amazon described three Anzellan robots — Vilo Vele, Kado Bre’a and Jibu Bre’a — that react to guests, to the workshop and to each other, with the cloud side on AWS.

Kyle Laughlin, senior vice president of Walt Disney Imagineering research and development, technology and engineering, told The Hollywood Reporter the Amazon work goes back more than a decade, from Disney+ into daily Imagineering. The species was in The Rise of Skywalker and in the 2026 film The Mandalorian and Grogu. BigGo Finance said these three were created with Lucasfilm for this project, and that the build took just a few months. Vilo leads. Kado is a droid mechanic who wants attention. Jibu, his twin, is shy. They mix English — Galactic Basic, in the story — with Anzellan, and they pick from a large bank of lines recorded in advance.

Concealed cameras and microphones read the scene. Samira Panah Bakhtiar, AWS general manager for telecommunications, media, entertainment, games, advertising and sports, said each figure tracks three streams at once: the guests in front of it, the workshop, and the other two characters. A fast model handles an instant move, such as eye contact. Larger models shape a longer exchange. Imagineers wrote every action and every line. The model chooses inside that script and does not write new words on the spot. AWS engineers used the coding tool Kiro to turn the ideas into software, and people approved the code. Imagineers also used Amazon Bedrock, which can call foundation models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta by task. Bakhtiar said the cloud tools shortened the path from concept to a working system by orders of magnitude compared with not using them.

Nobody drives the figures from backstage. What they do changes with how many people are present and with whether those people are children or adults. A birthday badge or a particular Star Wars shirt can become the topic. Laughlin said guests want meetings that feel genuine, and that today’s audio-animatronics are scripted in how that meeting happens. BigGo said the three are expected at a Disney park after the conference showing.

Disney is not doing the robot work with one supplier. BigGo reported a robotics partnership with Nvidia, including Olaf from Frozen as a walking robot at Disneyland Paris and at Hong Kong Disneyland, and a guide robot under consideration for a Disneyland parking lot. Laughlin described characters from a single franchise moving and talking together, and he indicated the pattern is meant to spread past Star Wars.

Amazon is supplying the cloud, Kiro and the model switcher for lines a person still wrote. Nvidia already has walking characters in two Disney parks, and a parking-lot guide still on the table. Meta is not building the bodies, but its models are on the Bedrock menu, so the reasoning step need not be an Amazon model. The guest hears a recorded line. The new piece is which company’s software picks it, in the room, with no one behind the curtain.
