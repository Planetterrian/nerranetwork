**iOS 27.3 meters Siri AI and Apple Intelligence, then sells a higher cap: Mashable**
Apple is putting daily limits on server-side Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features with iOS 27.3, while basic Siri requests keep working after the advanced allowance is used up. Mashable, citing Apple, said the capped categories are separate: image tools including Clean Up, Extend, Spatial Reframing and Image Playground; AFM Cloud and AFM Cloud Pro models in Shortcuts; and third-party apps that use Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute. Limits sit on the Apple Account and apply across iPhone, Mac and Apple TV. A limit prompt reads “Daily Request Limit Reached” and “You can continue with basic requests,” with a button to raise the cap through iCloud+. Apple’s published multipliers, as Mashable laid them out: the 50GB plan at $0.99 a month adds nothing; 200GB at $2.99 is up to 2x; 2TB at $9.99 is up to 5x; 6TB at $29.99 is up to 10x; 12TB at $59.99 is up to 20x. People already on an eligible plan get the increase inside that subscription. Limits reset at about midnight in the user’s time zone; travelers reset around midnight in the central time zone of the Apple Account’s country or region; a Family Sharing group resets around midnight in the plan purchaser’s time zone; someone not signed in resets about 24 hours after first use of that category that day. Unused allowance does not roll over. Source: [mashable.com](https://mashable.com/tech/ios-27-3-apple-intelligence-siri-ai-usage-limits-what-to-know)

**Google drops the desktop Search button and pins Gemini to the homepage: Startup Fortune**
Google began rolling out a redesigned desktop homepage on Thursday, November 19, 2026, Robby Stein, VP of Search, said on X. The Google Search button beside I’m Feeling Lucky is gone. Under the box sit Create images, Ask about files and Brainstorm; shattered.io identified the image tool as Nano Banana. A collapsible sidebar holds Gemini chat history and tasks, a New chat control, and, per Engadget’s account in Startup Fortune, AI and ordinary search history, with each entry reopening in AI Mode. Pressing Enter still runs an ordinary search. Stein said: “People are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done, and in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress and continuing to explore the web.” The rollout is global, though 9to5Google noted availability may vary by account and device. Stein’s announcement covers the desktop homepage, not phone users or the Google app. Earlier server-side tests, including one for signed-out users, let file analysis and brainstorming work without an account while image generation needed a sign-in. Source: [startupfortune.com](https://startupfortune.com/google-drops-the-search-button-from-its-desktop-homepage-and-adds-a-gemini-chat-sidebar/)

**Google and Penn State describe a memory log so one model’s harness can teach the next: eu.36kr.com**
A paper from Google and Pennsylvania State University, reported by 36kr, proposes Continual Recursive Self-Improvement built on Self-Improvement Memory. The memory is a structured log of harness changes — prompts, tools and interception rules — not a rewrite of model weights. Each change stores a rationale and the code touched, predicted outcomes, observed outcomes, and the evaluation context of model and dataset. The account says a harness evolved on Claude Opus scored lower on Gemini than Gemini’s own starting harness, because the new model kept the result and lost the reason. In the cross-model test described, a harness evolved by Claude Opus 5.5, packaged with that memory, was handed to Gemini 3.8 Flash to keep optimizing. Source: [eu.36kr.com](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/4078984519750789)

### The Thread: The cart, and the door that shuts
Google’s November 20, 2026 post put grocery ordering inside Search and the Gemini app for users in the United States, naming Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s and Target. Albertsons, Safeway and Shaw’s are banners of Albertsons Companies. In the published Target example, a request for a quick brunch returns product cards, and the first bakery card is Favorite Day Petite Butter Croissants at $6.49, sold by Target, rated 4.8 stars from 32 reviews. A follow-up that begins with [@Target](https://x.com/Target) asks for two bagels and cream cheese, strawberries, croissants, cold brew and juice, for pickup. The confirmation adds two Thomas Everything Bagels in an 18oz six-count pack at $3.99 each, quantity two, $7.98, and two Philadelphia Original cream cheese tubs at $4.19 each, quantity two, $8.38, plus Fresh Strawberries, Butter Croissants, Good & Gather Cold Brew Coffee and Tropicana Pure Premium pulp-free orange juice, for pickup at a store named Nashville West in Nashville, Tennessee. The cart panel shows a subtotal of $37.49 for 8 items. Google says the apps help build an order and check out, powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol, the open standard it linked to a January announcement. The same post says Experian, already live on the Gemini app, is rolling into Search so U.S. users can check a credit rating and shop for a personal loan; that H&R Block can calculate taxes and estimate refunds from Search; and that links to Angi and Thumbtack accounts in Search arrive in the coming weeks, for jobs from a leaky faucet to last-minute decorations.

Amazon is closing the other door on its own store. Mezha and The Register reported that Amazon is blocking shopping bots on its main site while AWS Marketplace opens to agent purchases. Google is letting a conversation assemble a retailer’s cart inside Search. Amazon is keeping outside agents off the flagship site and allowing agent buying on the cloud marketplace instead.

The shared dependency is the last step of a purchase: who is allowed to turn a suggestion into an order, and on whose system the cart lives. Google’s protocol is the pipe it wants agents and merchants to share, dropped onto the page people already open, next to a chat sidebar that keeps the thread. Amazon’s block keeps third-party agents away from the catalogue, the reviews and the fulfillment on the main site, and points permitted agent spending at infrastructure the company already bills as cloud. One assistant is being invited into the grocer’s cart. The other company’s store is being reserved for shoppers Amazon is willing to serve itself.

For Alphabet, a holiday list, a credit check, a tax estimate and, within weeks, a home-services booking can start in Search, so the retailer’s app is no longer the only front door to its own inventory. For Amazon, an outside shopping agent that cannot enter the main site has to stop, hand the task to a person, or buy through the marketplace path AWS is opening. The next marker Google actually dated in prose, not on a calendar, is those Angi and Thumbtack links, described as arriving in the coming weeks.
