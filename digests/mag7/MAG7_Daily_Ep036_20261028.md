**Google DeepMind Releases Genie 4 World Model With Real-Time Editing and Audio: AI Insider**
Google DeepMind has released Genie 4 in research preview for trusted testers. Users steer with keyboard controls and plain-language prompts, including mid-simulation changes to weather, camera, people and objects, and the model generates ambient sound, music and dialogue with the picture. MarkTechPost reports scene consistency of up to two minutes, double Genie 3, which it says ran at 720p and 24 frames per second with visual memory of about one minute after an August 2025 announcement. Gemini Omni anchors look and behavior; Gemini 3.8 Flash breaks a goal into steps, checks frames and replans if the user intervenes, and in one demo Gemini 4 Argon split a checkers task into steps that Genie 4 simulated with Apptronik's Apollo humanoid. Research director Tejas Kulkarni said future world models will be hybrids, with Gemini writing code and text while Genie simulates reality. All Genie 4 video and audio will carry SynthID watermarks; a waitlist is open, Google AI Ultra subscribers are due to get it in Project Genie, and early-access Gemini API and Google AI Studio partners follow, with The Hindu listing uses from creative direction and historical scenes to simulation after internal safety evaluations. **FIA Partners with AWS to Drive Innovation Across Global Motor Sport: Federation Internationale de l'Automobile**
The FIA has named Amazon Web Services its official partner for artificial intelligence, cloud computing, machine learning and cloud infrastructure, from club racing through Formula 1. AWS will help shape technical rules for the next generation of Formula 1 cars; the FIA already runs aerodynamic simulations on AWS high-performance computing so engineers can test airflow without building parts, and AWS will give technical teams AI tools for rules on safety, performance and sustainability. The deal extends the Digital Licensing Platform AWS developed and hosts, which The Next Web says launched in 2025, now reaching more national sporting authorities with more languages, FIA University links and licence renewals, with event management for volunteers and officials planned next. More business applications are set to move to AWS, work the FIA ties to doubling global motor sport participation, and AWS becomes an official partner of the FIA Awards and the FIA Sustainable Innovation Series. The agreement is separate from AWS's deal with Formula 1's commercial rights holder, cloud provider since 2018. FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem said the partnership can make it simpler for drivers, officials and volunteers to take part. **Meta AI director Rohit Patel quits after eight years to take on Microsoft Office and Google Workspace: Indian Television Dot Com**
Rohit Patel, a director at Meta Superintelligence Labs, is leaving after eight years to found Ndoc, aimed at replacing Microsoft Office and Google Workspace with one AI-native workspace. Three tabs split the work: Stage for decks, dashboards and mini apps, Narrative for analysis and citations, and Model for datasets and Python scripts, and users can ask the assistant to explain any script or formula. Ndoc is built on fine-tuned open-source models, keeps context across people, documents, teams and companies, and Patel plans to open-source a .ndoc.html format, which he tied to leaving rather than seeking consensus from hundreds of people at Meta. The startup has a team of three, has not raised outside funding, is in an enterprise pilot and limited alpha, and has connectors to import data from existing apps, with a beta expected in the coming weeks and a full launch in 2027. The exit follows Joel Kaplan, Andrew Tulloch and Emily Dalton Smith; OpenAI launched Space, a shared workspace inside ChatGPT, in September. ### The Counterpoint
**A few minutes, and no ground truth: Trending Topics**
DeepMind's own limits, as MarkTechPost lists them, cut against reading Genie 4 as a place to train agents for long: interaction lasts a few minutes, not an extended session, and visual detail and object permanence drift in longer runs, after complex prompt edits, or during navigation, with real-time audio accuracy still unsolved. Trending Topics cites AI writer Ben Dickson, who argues a generated world has no ground truth to check against, so errors in the model could compound when robots are trained in it. The two-minute consistency mark is double Genie 3's visual memory, not a solved session. Source: [trendingtopics.eu](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/google-genie-4-world-model/)

### The Thread: A prospectus, a reseller, and next year's TPU revenue
The Information reports that Anthropic's IPO prospectus records a plan to spend $114.5 billion with Google Cloud, and the filing does not split out how much of that sum is tensor processing unit compute. The same prospectus estimates a further $125 billion to $150 billion on TPUs with Google and other providers, and $161.2 billion for TPU compute with Broadcom, which co-designs the chips with Google. TPUs began as chips Google used only internally, starting more than a decade ago. Outside customers first reached them through Google Cloud in 2018.

Oracle is the new reseller in that stack. The Information says Google has said Oracle will be the first major cloud provider besides Google itself to offer TPUs to customers from Oracle's own data centers. Google began recognizing revenue from selling TPUs into outside data centers last quarter. Chief financial officer Anat Ashkenazi said on that earnings call that only a small portion of revenue from those agreements showed up in the quarter, and that the vast majority kicks in next year.

John Blackledge of TD Cowen, in a report The Information cites from earlier this month, estimates TPU sales of $9.4 billion in the second half of this year, about 13 percent of the roughly $70 billion he forecasts for Google Cloud in that half. By 2028 he thinks TPUs could be a $138 billion business, 34 percent of his Google Cloud revenue estimate for that year. Analysts, on average, expect Nvidia to report $692 billion of revenue in the fiscal year ending January 2028. The Information notes that Blackledge's 2028 TPU figure would still be a fraction of that Nvidia total. Those are his estimate and the analysts' average, not figures Google or Nvidia have booked.

Meta is already a tenant on the same chip. The Information says it reported earlier this year that Meta struck a deal to rent TPUs from Google. Alphabet therefore has three commercial paths in one piece of reporting: a cloud commitment from Anthropic at prospectus scale, a sale channel through other companies' data centers with most of that revenue still ahead, and an existing rental with Meta. Nvidia's side of the comparison is the analyst revenue number, not a new chip announcement in this article. Oracle's role is to put Google silicon in a second cloud's buildings. The next dated marker the source names is next year, when Ashkenazi said the vast majority of outside-data-center TPU agreement revenue is due to be recognized. Source: [theinformation.com](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-tpu-moment)
