Moonshot A I, the Beijing lab behind the Kimi models, had signed a Southeast Asian neocloud for a sizable cluster of Blackwell Ultra B300 chips in an Indonesian data center, with rental access meant to start as early as next year, The Information reported from two people with direct knowledge. Google on Wednesday put Gemini 4 Neon into the family, between the fast Hydrogen model and the larger Carbon model, for coding, analysis, science and long agent jobs.

The price is three dollars per million input tokens, fifteen cents per million if the input is cached, and fifteen dollars per million output tokens, on a window of up to one million tokens that can take text, PDFs, charts, audio and video, with four thinking levels.

Google’s own score is sixty-one on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, third overall, and first on that firm’s coding-agent index. InfoWorld carried a coding-agent score of fifty-eight point six and sixty-five point two percent on Terminal-Bench four point one.

In a base shell Neon solved twelve point eight percent of SWE-sweep problems; inside Antigravity the same model reached thirty-nine percent. At the default high setting Google said a task costs thirty-two percent less than on Carbon, and it quoted Glean saying quality matched Carbon at three times the speed.
