# MAG 7 Daily
> **5.9 million Macs shipped, down 11.3 percent, and Apple still gained share.**

**What You Need to Know:** Apple shipped an estimated 5.9 million Macs in the third quarter, 11.3 percent fewer than a year earlier, and its share of the PC market still rose. Meta has cut ByteDance ads off its platforms in the US and several other countries, and Ars Technica reports the US has barred Microsoft from sponsoring foreign workers for residency. Separately, Nikkei says Apple has trimmed iPhone 18 Pro component orders after a price increase.

### Top News
**Mac shipments fall while Apple's share rises: MacRumors**
IDC's preliminary estimates put Apple's third-quarter Mac shipments at 5.9 million units, down 11.3 percent from 6.7 million a year earlier. Share rose to 9.5 percent from 8.5 percent, and Apple stayed fourth. Across all brands, shipments fell to 62.7 million units from 78.5 million, a decline of 20.1 percent. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/09/mac-shipments-fall-but-apple-gains-ground/)

**Meta bans ByteDance ads on its platforms: The Verge**
Meta has banned ads from TikTok's parent, ByteDance, across the US and several other countries, as Bloomberg reported and The Verge wrote up. The ban took effect Thursday. It restricts ads and paid marketing messages from ByteDance, along with third-party ads that link out. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1008658/meta-tiktok-bytedance-ads-ban)

**US bars Microsoft from residency sponsorship: Ars Technica**
Ars Technica reports Microsoft is barred from sponsoring foreign workers for US residency. The US says Microsoft abused immigration rules. The company disputes the claim. Source: [arstechnica.com](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/trump-administration-targets-microsoft-in-new-immigration-crackdown/)

### Company Desk
**Googlebook software is the weak point: The Verge**
Google has newly launched five Googlebook laptops. The Verge says the hardware ranges from great to excellent and the software, in its current state, is the weak point. Four staffers are testing the machines. The promise is a fresh alternative to Windows or Mac for Android phone owners, and the early read is a rocky start. Source: [theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1008563/googlebook-software-impressions-thoughts-roundtable)

**Amazon launches Android tablets with Alexa+: Mezha**
Amazon has launched a new range of Android tablets. The machines feature the built-in Alexa+ assistant. Source: [mezha.ua](https://mezha.ua/en/news/amazon-alexa-tablets-316004/)

**Suppliers told to cut iPhone 18 Pro parts: MacRumors**
Nikkei Asia, cited by MacRumors, says Apple asked some suppliers to cut production of iPhone 18 Pro components because demand is below what it expected. Component orders for October were cut by at least 15 percent compared with the original request. The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max start at 1,199 and 1,299, which is 100 more than the models they replace, after Apple grew more cautious from early September as memory-chip costs rose. Nikkei links softer demand from late August through October to a split launch: the regular iPhone 18 is expected in the spring of 2027, alongside the iPhone 18e and the iPhone Air 2. The Pro models have 12GB of RAM, the same as last year's iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max. One rumor says Apple had planned 16GB and decided against it on cost. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/09/apple-cuts-iphone-18-pro-orders-price-demand/)

**GitHub is about 60 percent onto Azure: GeekWire**
GitHub chief operating officer Kyle Daigle, who also leads developer marketing for all of Microsoft, told GeekWire that GitHub is roughly 60 percent through its migration to Azure as it works through capacity problems and outages. In August 2025 Microsoft moved GitHub under its CoreAI division after running it as a quasi-independent company since the 2018 acquisition. Chief executive Thomas Dohmke said he would leave at the end of 2025. Daigle, a GitHub veteran of more than 13 years, added the title of chief marketing officer of developer for Microsoft in January 2026. He said GitHub now has influence beyond the GitHub product set, and that bringing engineering and marketing teams together has been a large part of the work. Source: [geekwire.com](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-2-5-github-coo-kyle-daigle-on-stitching-the-developer-universe-together/)

### The Counterpoint
**Share rose because the rest of the market fell faster: MacRumors**
IDC's vendor table cuts against reading Apple's higher share as a stronger Mac business. Shipments still fell, from 6.7 million to 5.9 million. ASUS, in fifth place with 5.5 million shipments, recorded an 8.6 percent decrease, and IDC says it was the only top-five vendor with a smaller percentage decline than Apple. The firm does not expect prices to return to where they were a year ago, and it warned a weakening economy could further depress demand through the end of 2026 and into 2027. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/09/mac-shipments-fall-but-apple-gains-ground/)
