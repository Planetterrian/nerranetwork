### Top News
**Microsoft starts disabling the protocol Apple Mail still needs: MacRumors**
Microsoft today began disabling Exchange Web Services by default in Exchange Online, MacRumors reports, which cuts the protocol Apple's Mail and Calendar apps on Mac still use to sync Microsoft 365 work accounts. Microsoft stopped adding features to EWS in 2018, and in 2023 said it would start disabling the protocol in Exchange Online from October 2026. A post updated in June describes the change as a phased rollout, so some Mac users could lose access sooner than others. Apple's Platform Deployment support document says the company is actively working with Microsoft to move Mail, Calendar, Contacts, Notes and Reminders on Mac to the Microsoft Graph API in a future macOS 27 update. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/01/apple-mail-mac-stop-sync-microsoft-365/)

**Gemini 4 Argon reaches trusted cyber researchers first: Yahoo Finance**
Gemini 4, already set to replace the Gemini 3.5 Pro line, now has a shipping model: Google launched Argon, focused on coding and cybersecurity, and said it will go first to a group of trusted cybersecurity researchers. Paid API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers are expected to receive access next, followed by developers, businesses and consumers. Google said Argon scored 77.9% on the DeepSWE v1.1 coding benchmark and described that as a record, while Jefferies noted it still trails leading competitors on FrontierSWE v2. Google said the model can autonomously identify, verify and address software vulnerabilities, and for this first cyber rollout it is providing Argon without some standard safeguards to trusted defenders and internal teams. Wiz is using the model through its Scan for Good programme, Google said. The output limit is now one million tokens, up from 64,000, and Google said it is already using Argon internally for software development and data-centre optimisation. Source: [finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/alphabet-shares-rise-google-launches-102136709.html)

Source: [datacenterdynamics.com](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/aws-granted-planning-permission-for-data-center-in-iver-uk/)

### Company Desk
**AWS signs a Synopsys chip-design deal worth over one billion dollars: Simply Wall St**
Amazon Web Services has signed a multi-year agreement with Synopsys worth over one billion dollars, focused on custom chip design. The deal positions AWS as a leading customer for Synopsys silicon IP tailored for AI data-centre workloads. It includes both licensing and royalty components, which the account reads as ongoing commercial use of Synopsys technology in AWS in-house silicon. Source: [simplywall.st](https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/retail/nasdaq-amzn/amazoncom/news/amazoncom-amzn-signs-1-billion-ai-chip-design-deal-as-aws-go)

### The Counterpoint
The Mac Mail cutoff is not a same-day loss for every work account. MacRumors reports that organizations which have chosen to keep Exchange Web Services running are not affected for now, and IT administrators can still restore access. Affected organizations can also move Mac users to a Graph-compatible client such as Outlook. Companies that run their own Exchange servers are outside the change. iPhone and iPad are not impacted, because they use Exchange ActiveSync rather than EWS. Source: [macrumors.com](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/01/apple-mail-mac-stop-sync-microsoft-365/)

### The Thread: Token Price, Tailored Chip
Two dollars per million input tokens and ten dollars per million output tokens is the price Jefferies puts on Gemini 4 Argon. The brokerage prices Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 at ten dollars and fifty dollars on that same input-and-output split, and Claude Opus 5.5 at four dollars and twenty dollars. Google is also offering a ninety-five percent discount on cached input tokens, Jefferies said. Jefferies said Argon ranked first for coding on LLM Arena's Text Arena and eighth on Code Arena: Web Dev, twenty-one positions higher than Gemini 3.8 Flash. On CWE-bench v1, a benchmark designed to test vulnerability fixes, the model tied Grok 4.7 at sixty-eight percent, the brokerage said. Figures Jefferies cited put the success rate for prompt-injection attacks at 0.7 percent for Argon, compared with 1 percent for Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1. Anthropic's competing models have an output limit of 128,000 tokens, Jefferies said. Google said Argon also produced results across tests covering automation, long-form video and professional tasks in finance, legal services and tax.
