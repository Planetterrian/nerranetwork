Across the region, the European Commission wants labels on how data centres use energy and water, Cyprus Mail reports, for sites of five hundred kilowatts or more.

Operators would report those efficiency figures, how their water use sits against local water stress, and whether they can help the local grid, including by reusing waste heat.

The plan sets no ceiling on electricity or water, and it does not make operators publish total power used.

European Union figures put these sites at about two and a half percent of E U electricity. Combined capacity is expected to rise from twelve gigawatts last year to twenty-eight by twenty thirty, and the Union wants capacity tripled within seven years.

Governments and lawmakers have two months to object. If they do not, the rules take effect, and the labels could later be required before any binding minimum standards.
