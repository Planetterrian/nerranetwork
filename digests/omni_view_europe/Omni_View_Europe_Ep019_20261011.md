### Lead
**‘Stab to the heart’: arrest of German ex-spymaster sparks calls for reform: The Guardian**
Federal prosecutors have charged August Hanning, who led Germany's federal foreign intelligence service, the BND, from 1998 to 2005, with treasonous espionage and with handing state secrets to a foreign power. They believe he paid his former chief of staff, identified only as Manfred D, to remove nearly 2,000 documents, at least some classified, over 12 years, and then used that intelligence for clients of a private consulting firm he started after retirement. On Tuesday, masked commandos led the 80-year-old from his estate in Nordwalde to a police van without handcuffs; experts told The Guardian the detention of someone of his former rank is a first in a western democracy. Konstantin von Notz, a Bundestag member on the parliamentary intelligence supervisory committee, said the case could become a national scandal and warned of a devastating loss of trust among partner countries. Hanning is in pretrial detention; his lawyers deny he passed confidential documents to foreign powers, and a conviction could mean 10 years in prison. Many of the files are believed to have been briefing papers for top officials, including the chancellor. Hanning once called Germany's own services "vegetarians" among carnivores and demanded broader powers. Source: [theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/11/stab-to-the-heart-arrest-of-german-ex-spymaster-sparks-calls-for-reform)

**Google Maps gains UK fuel prices fed by 30-minute station reporting rule: PPC Land**
Google on October 8 began showing petrol and diesel prices at UK filling stations in Google Maps and Waze, using Fuel Finder, a government scheme that requires every station in the United Kingdom to report a price change within 30 minutes. The government estimates the scheme will save car-owning households an average of £40 a year. Sarah-Jayne Williams, Global Product Partnerships Director at Google Maps and Waze, said a search for petrol or fuel returns the latest prices, rates appear on the map, and a station listing shows every fuel it sells. Google is the newest and largest outlet for data that a dozen comparison apps and websites already use. The government scheme covers the whole United Kingdom. Source: [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/google-maps-gains-uk-fuel-prices-fed-by-30-minute-station-reporting-rule/)

### Both Sides: Italy's wider deficit for defense and energy
Italy's government is changing its budget path, and markets are arguing over whether that path is manageable. On October 2, Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's center-right government approved an extra 28 billion euros in borrowing over two years for defense and energy. The cabinet will present the budget next week. The new targets put the 2027 deficit at 3.4 percent of GDP and the 2028 deficit at 3.2 percent, up from April projections of 2.8 percent and 2.5 percent.

The case for going ahead is the government's own allocation. The money is split evenly between defense and energy, each worth about 0.3 percent of GDP a year in 2027 and 2028. CNBC reports the plans were scaled back, yet they remain the spending Meloni's cabinet chose to finance by borrowing, in a week when French debt strains have already drawn investors toward eurozone public finances. PIMCO's stated reason for staying with Italian bonds is domestic ownership and stronger fundamentals, which it says offer support even if an election adds uncertainty.

The case against a calm reading comes from Filippo Taddei, senior European economist at Goldman Sachs. He says wider deficits and higher yields could push Italy's debt ratio to Europe's highest by 2028, and that fiscal risk premia could rise before a general election due no later than December 22, 2027. On Friday, CNBC recorded France's 10-year yield at 4.85 percent and Italy's at 4.55 percent, with the spread to German Bunds about 108 basis points. Taddei's point is that the new targets sit above earlier projections and above Goldman's forecasts, so the bond market may charge Italy more, not less, as the vote approaches.

Both sides are working from the same approved sum and the same new deficit targets. The next dated step in the reporting is next week's budget presentation; the election deadline Goldman cites is December 22, 2027.
