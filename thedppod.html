How the club works

Briefing. Mindset. Invitation. Proof.

1 The Positive Papers

You get the briefing

The day's most consequential developments in science and tech — the result, the mechanism, the honest caveat, the builders behind it. No fluff, no doom.

2 Think Positive

You get the mindset

The first show to take mental health as seriously as the science: one principle a day — action, creativity, accountability — from thinkers like Tony Robbins and Simon Sinek, with a mental rep you can try today.

3 The Lever

You get one invitation

One concrete thing worth trying this week — with honest numbers on cost, time, and impact. Not homework. A challenge the hosts take themselves.

4 The Do Positive Dispatch

You send the proof

Tried it? Write in. Dispatches get read on the show — real ones only, that's the whole point. The club runs on receipts, not intentions.

The Do Positive Dispatch

Did something? Get read on the show.

Dispatches follow the club format — three parts, honest numbers, real actions only. We will never invent listener mail, which means every dispatch we read is a real member doing a real thing.

Two honest sentences count. You don't need all three parts, a big result, or a success story — “I booked the assessment, still waiting on the report” is a dispatch. Small is the whole point: every little bit moves the number.

1 · What you did

The action, plainly. “Sealed the three worst drafts in my house.”

2 · The honest numbers

Cost, hours, savings, impact — rough is fine, honest is required.

3 · A shoutout

Who helped, or who should try it next. Gratitude fuels the club.

Send your dispatch

Or just hit reply to any daily briefing email — replies land in the same mailbox and count exactly the same. New members and first dispatches get welcomed on the show by first name — tell us how to read yours.

The Club Charter

Five things we'll never break.

Free to join

The daily briefing and membership cost nothing. Patrons keep the club running — by choice, never by paywall.

Honest numbers

Every claim gets its caveat. Every lever states its real cost and payback.

No guilt

Empowerment, not obligation. Miss a week? The next lever is tomorrow.

Real actions only

No performative gestures, no fabricated mail. The Dispatch runs on receipts.
