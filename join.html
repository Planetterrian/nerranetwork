How Personal works

No algorithm deciding for you: a real private podcast feed, assembled fresh every morning.

1

Pick your shows

Choose from the 26 English shows in the Personal lineup (Nerra Daily is the whole-network edition and The Age of AI is interview-driven, so neither is a segment) and order them the way you want your morning to run. Two minutes, changeable any time; edits reach the very next edition.

2

Mira builds your edition

Every morning, after the day's episodes publish, Mira, the network's AI anchor, stitches your picks into one continuous show, greets you by name, and hands off between segments. Set a location and she opens with your city's brief: the taster on Personal, the full brief plus your own topics on Personal News Network, every source named aloud.

3

Listen anywhere

You get a private RSS feed that works in Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, anywhere you already listen, added from your account in one tap. Chapter markers let you skip between your shows. Cancel in one click; your lineup stays saved.

Honest answers

What exactly arrives in my Personal feed?

One episode each morning: the day's real episodes of the shows you picked, in your order, joined by Mira's short spoken handoffs. Nothing is summarized: you hear the full episodes, minus the cross-promo tails that don't make sense mid-edition. If you set a length or turn on Skip repeats, Mira leaves out whole chapters (a story you already heard today, say) and tells you which. Chapters mark every segment so you can skip freely.

How do I add it to my podcast app?

From your account: one tap opens your feed in Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, Castro, AntennaPod or Podcast Addict. On a computer, scan the code with your phone. Any other app that takes a feed URL works too; the URL is yours alone, so sharing it shares your subscription.

How does the Local morning brief stay honest?

Weather comes from measured forecast data for your city. The local story is found with a live web search and Mira names the source out loud. On a day with nothing verifiable worth your time, she says so and moves on, no filler, ever.

Can I cancel easily?

Yes. Billing is by Stripe with self-serve cancellation, one click, no email required. Your private feed runs to the end of the paid period, and your lineup stays saved on your free account if you ever come back.

Is this AI-generated?

Yes. The shows are written and voiced with AI from the day's sourced reporting, and every episode says so on air. A person chooses each show's sources and rules and fixes what goes wrong, but no one reads every episode before it ships; every claim is checked against its source and an unverified one is marked publicly. Mira is an AI anchor and is always introduced as one. The full policy is at our AI disclosure.

What happens with my email and city?

Your email is your account and newsletter address. It's never sold, and only the services that run your account see it. Your first name, city, subjects and note to Mira exist only so Mira can greet you and build your edition: they're stored with your account, sent to the AI model that writes Mira's lines each morning (xAI, never with your email), never published, and deleted with your account. The lineup you build on this page stays in your browser until you create the account. The privacy policy has the details.
