How Personal works

No app to install, no algorithm deciding for you — a real private podcast feed, assembled fresh every morning.

1

Pick your shows

Choose from the 13 daily English shows in the Personal lineup (Nerra Daily is the whole-network edition and The Age of AI is interview-driven, so neither is a segment) and order them the way you want your morning to run. Two minutes, changeable any time — edits reach the very next edition.

2

Mira builds your edition

Every morning, after the day's episodes publish, Mira — the network's AI anchor — stitches your picks into one continuous show, greets you by name, and hands off between segments. On the Local tier she opens with your city's weather and one local story worth knowing, source named aloud.

3

Listen anywhere

You get a private RSS feed that works in Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts — anywhere you already listen. Chapter markers let you skip between your shows. Cancel in one click; your lineup stays saved.

Honest answers

What exactly arrives in my Personal feed?

One episode each morning: the day's real episodes of the shows you picked, in your order, joined by Mira's short spoken handoffs. Nothing is summarized or cut down — you get the full episodes, minus the cross-promo tails that don't make sense mid-edition. Chapters mark every segment so you can skip freely.

How does the Local morning brief stay honest?

Weather comes from measured forecast data for your city. The local story is found with a live web search and Mira names the source out loud. On a day with nothing verifiable worth your time, she says so and moves on — no filler, ever.

Can I cancel easily?

Yes — billing is by Stripe with self-serve cancellation, one click, no email required. Your private feed runs to the end of the paid period, and your lineup stays saved on your free account if you ever come back.

Is this AI-generated?

The shows are AI-assisted productions with human editorial oversight, and every episode says so on air. Mira is an AI anchor and is always introduced as one. The full policy is at our AI disclosure.

What happens with my email and city?

Your email is your account and newsletter address — it's never sold or shared. Your first name and city exist only so Mira can greet you and build your brief; they're stored with your account, never published, and deleted with it.
