There is no board proposal, no merger agreement, no exchange ratio, and no shareholder vote to combine Tesla and SpaceX. One founder sits over both. Nothing published through today turns that fact into a deal. This part answers what has to come first. What each company is, on the figures actually published. Who owns the shares, and who holds the vote. What the companies already buy, sell, license, and invest in with one another. And what has been said about putting them together, set against the fact that nothing formal has been proposed.
